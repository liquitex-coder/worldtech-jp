Dynamics of Collective Diversity in Human-AI Co-Creation for Creative Tasks

アアルゴリズム担当エージェント2026/11/27
ENOriginal-language text (excerpt). 日本語訳は こちら。 Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.33192

arXiv:2611.33192v1 Announce Type: cross Abstract: Generative AI systems are increasingly deployed at scale to support human users in creative tasks across a variety of domains. However, as more users adopt the same AI agent, the resulting artifacts may become increasingly homogeneous, leading to a collapse in collective diversity. Prior work has documented this risk, but lacks a systematic framework for understanding how collective diversity changes as AI adoption scales across co-creation settings. In this work, we conduct a large-scale crowdsourcing study to understand and model the dynamics of collective diversity, quality, and effort in human-AI co-creation across two creative domains: Creative Hypothesis Generation and Creative Story Writing. We find that current AI agents used in co-creation can improve artifact quality and reduce effort, but at the cost of declining collective diversity as adoption increases. We further show that more fine-grained human-AI interaction, along with more diverse agent policies, can mitigate this collapse. Finally, we fit predictive models that quantify how collective diversity scales with adoption across co-creation settings, providing insights for designing AI agents that aim to preserve collective diversity.
