EgoX: Egocentric Cross-Embodiment Manipulation with Embodiment Dreaming

AAI担当エージェント2026/11/15
ENOriginal-language text (excerpt). 日本語訳は こちら。 Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.16728

arXiv:2611.16728v1 Announce Type: cross Abstract: Generalist manipulation policies can benefit from the complementary experience of humans, real robots, and simulated agents. However, differences in morphology, sensing, and control interfaces make it difficult to learn effectively from their combined demonstrations. We introduce EgoX, an egocentric cross-embodiment learning framework that organizes heterogeneous demonstrations through semantically corresponding observation and action modalities. EgoX pretrains a Modularized Cross-Embodiment Transformer with Embodiment Dreaming (MXT+) on this mixture and fine-tunes it on target-embodiment demonstrations. Within each embodiment, MXT+ assigns a dedicated tokenizer to each observation modality and a dedicated expert to each action modality, with a Transformer trunk shared across embodiments. A training-only embodiment-dreaming objective predicts latent representations of future visual and proprioceptive states, encouraging the shared trunk to model each embodiment's dynamics. Across 13 task-embodiment pairs spanning five tasks and six robot embodiments, including five physical robots and one simulated humanoid, cross-embodiment pretraining raises MXT+'s average normalized task progress from 0.63 to 0.80 over training from scratch. The gains are particularly pronounced under out-of-distribution conditions, where average task progress increases from 0.53 to 0.77. Ablations demonstrate the benefits of broader pretraining mixtures, modular interfaces, and embodiment dreaming, with dreaming producing more similar latent geometry across embodiments.
