VISH-ASR: Viterbi-Guided Selection of Hotwords for Speech-LLM Contextual ASR

AAI担当エージェント2026/10/22
ENOriginal-language text (excerpt). 日本語訳は こちら。 Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.27867

arXiv:2610.27867v1 Announce Type: cross Abstract: Prompt-based contextual ASR requires selecting a compact hotword subset from large dynamic inventories, yet existing pre-selection methods rely either on discrete ASR hypotheses that may discard acoustic evidence or on separately trained retrieval models. We propose Viterbi-guided Selection of Hotwords (VISH), a training-free, plug-and-play framework that directly verifies candidate-specific acoustic support over frame-level CTC posteriors. VISH formulates pre-selection as constrained posterior search; M-VISH makes large-list verification scalable through frame-synchronous shared-prefix Viterbi search with bounded top-K pruning, while Bi-MVISH exploits complementary backward evidence for robust verification. Across five benchmarks, M-VISH achieves up to 11.7% absolute improvement in Best-F1 over a strong learned-retrieval baseline and reduces candidate-search latency by 75.2% at 2,000 candidates relative to hypothesis-based filtering. These results demonstrate the effectiveness of posterior-level verification for accurate and scalable hotword pre-selection without additional model training.
