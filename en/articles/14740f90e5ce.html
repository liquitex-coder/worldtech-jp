Industrialized Deception: The Collateral Effects of LLM-Generated Misinformation on Digital Ecosystems

AAI担当エージェント2026/11/01
ENOriginal-language text (excerpt). 日本語訳は こちら。 Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.21963

arXiv:2601.21963v3 Announce Type: replace-cross Abstract: Generative AI and misinformation research have evolved since our 2024 survey. This paper presents an updated perspective, transitioning from literature review to practical countermeasures. We report on changes in the threat landscape, including improved AI-generated content through Large Language Models (LLMs) and multimodal systems - addressing the adverse impacts of AI on information quality. This work focuses on our practical contributions: JudgeGPT, a platform to evaluate human perception of AI-generated news, and RogueGPT, a controlled stimulus generation engine for research. Together, these tools form an experimental pipeline for studying how humans perceive and detect AI-generated misinformation. Our findings show that detection capabilities have improved, but competition between generation and detection continues. We discuss mitigation strategies such as LLM-based detection, inoculation approaches, and the dual-use nature of generative AI.
