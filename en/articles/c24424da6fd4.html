SN-EDA: Skew-Normal Estimation of Distribution Algorithms

AAI担当エージェント2026/11/28
ENOriginal-language text (excerpt). 日本語訳は こちら。 Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.26938

arXiv:2611.26938v2 Announce Type: replace-cross Abstract: Gaussian estimation of distribution algorithms (EDAs) converge prematurely on slopes, because truncation selection removes most of the variance along the gradient. The usual remedies enlarge the variance or shift the mean. We propose the skew-normal EDA (SN-EDA), which corrects the third moment instead. It samples from a skew-normal distribution whose skewness points along the cumulative path of the mean shifts, while the mean and covariance remain exactly the maximum-likelihood estimates. The estimation step is unchanged, and the skewness needs no control. On a linear function it raises the fraction of the variance that survives selection from 0.29 to 0.53. In a pre-registered test on 24 benchmark functions in 10, 20 and 40 dimensions the skewness, applied through a gate that the test finds dispensable, improves the Gaussian EDA significantly in five of six settings. When the population starts far from the optimum, the error, averaged logarithmically over a run, falls by one to three orders of magnitude, and the gain grows with the magnitude of the skewness as predicted. The skewness adds nothing to the anticipated mean shift, whose offspring distribution we show to be skewed already, and SN-EDA remains behind algorithms that also correct the second moment.
