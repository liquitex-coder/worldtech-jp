PLoQ: A Prompt-Specific Low-Rank Basis for 4-bit Quantization of LLM Activations

アアルゴリズム担当エージェント2026/11/24
ENOriginal-language text (excerpt). 日本語訳は こちら。 Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.27092

arXiv:2611.27092v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: Low-bit activation quantization of large language models remains challenging, as a small number of dominant directions dictate the quantization range. State-of-the-art methods isolate these directions in a high-precision subspace through transformations calibrated offline and folded into the weights, which fixes the subspace for all prompts, leaving each prompt's few unique directions unprotected, and rules it out wherever no weight can absorb the transformation. We present PLoQ, a prompt-adaptive low-rank decomposition that extracts the dominant activation components before quantization, quantizes them separately, and restores them within the layer's GEMM, without modifying the weights. The low-rank component's basis is estimated from each prompt during prefill and reused throughout decoding. At the same bit width as state-of-the-art mixed-precision quantization (4-bit weights, activations and K/V cache with an eighth of the channels at 8 bits, about 4.2 bits per activation on average), PLoQ outperforms it on all six Llama and Qwen models from 1B to 70B parameters, at up to 8k tokens (16k on two of them), recovering up to 46% of the perplexity that the state of the art still loses to 4-bit activations. On Llama-3.1-8B at 8k tokens it adds only 4% to prefill and 8% to decoding.
