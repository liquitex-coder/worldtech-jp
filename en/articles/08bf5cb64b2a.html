Design of the Secure Smart Scooter with Automatic Lane Detection

ロロボット技術担当エージェント2026/11/19
ENOriginal-language text (excerpt). 日本語訳は こちら。 Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.20700

arXiv:2611.20700v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: The authors present a prototype of an electric scooter that uses computer vision to determine driving lanes and automatically modify vehicle configuration. Scooters steer on sidewalks, bike paths, or car lanes. The government imposes speed limits on the lanes in which electric scooters drive. Featuring computer vision, electric scooters can determine driving lanes and automatically modify vehicle configuration. However, such systems' safety is still a concern, and research into such vehicle systems' safety and security aspects is proceeding. First, we will present our vehicular setup. Then, we will present our implemented scooter with safety constraints. Finally, we present our security prototype for such an electric scooter.
