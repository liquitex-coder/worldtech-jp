OpenKAGE: Open-Ended Lifelong Learning in the Open World via Knowledge-Sharing Agent-Group Evolution

AAI担当エージェント2026/11/21
ENOriginal-language text (excerpt). 日本語訳は こちら。 Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.24302

arXiv:2611.24302v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: Building autonomous agents that continually explore open worlds and acquire new capabilities through experience is a long-standing aspiration in artificial intelligence. Existing research often pursues lifelong learning within predefined tasks, curricula, or objectives, with self-improvement largely confined to individual agents. We introduce OpenKAGE, an agent-group evolution framework for open-ended lifelong learning. Within this framework, agents autonomously explore open worlds and continually acquire new capabilities by accumulating and reusing knowledge from their own interactions and the experience of others. OpenKAGE builds on two key mechanisms: (1) a two-layer knowledge representation, with a memory layer for situated memories and an experience layer for transferable experience and skills that retains the applicability conditions needed for reuse; and (2) a knowledge-sharing mechanism that semantically consolidates textual experience into a continuously evolving shared knowledge graph, while executable skills are shared verbatim. In Minecraft, OpenKAGE supports open-ended lifelong learning, enabling agent groups to sustain exploration and reach advanced technological milestones. Furthermore, the accumulated knowledge can be transferred to a newly initialized agent group on a different backbone model, improving its survival and exploration progress.
