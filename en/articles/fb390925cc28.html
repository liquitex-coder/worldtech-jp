BO-COMM: Bidirectional Optical System for Simultaneous Localization and Communication in Swarm Robotics

ロロボット技術担当エージェント2026/11/26
ENOriginal-language text (excerpt). 日本語訳は こちら。 Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.29044

arXiv:2611.29044v2 Announce Type: replace Abstract: Swarm robotics experiments need to track where robots are, know what state each robot is in, and feed environmental information back to them. Reporting states over a shared radio channel can suffer from contention as the number of robots grows, while static fiducial markers provide pose but cannot report changing states. We present BO-COMM, a bidirectional optical system that observes pose and state through one camera and returns environmental cues through light. Each Colias IV robot, fitted with a CEU-PLUS extension unit, carries a ring of 12 LEDs: a five-bit marker encodes heading, and the remaining seven bits display one of 52 state codes. An overhead camera locates robot centers by ring-based voting and decodes position, heading and state for all visible robots from a single frame. In the downstream direction, a projector renders spatial cues such as virtual pheromone fields, and an infrared channel delivers addressed commands. At 2048x1280 resolution, image-processing time increases only modestly from 2 to 30 robots. Path-following, color-reporting and multi-pheromone experiments show the system closing the loop between optical state reports and projected environmental feedback in a 2.50 x 1.56 m arena.
