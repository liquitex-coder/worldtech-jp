Context Graph: Explainable Multi-Agent Decision Chains in AI-RAN

AAI担当エージェント2026/11/15
ENOriginal-language text (excerpt). 日本語訳は こちら。 Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.16879

arXiv:2611.16879v1 Announce Type: cross Abstract: AI-RAN distributes network control across multiple autonomous agents, namely xApps and rApps operating over shared radio resources. When an alert or degradation appears, operators currently lack a structured way to trace which decision caused it, what context triggered that decision, and how other agents interacted. Existing explainability tools focus on single-model feature attribution or linear event logging, neither of which captures cross-agent decision chains. We propose the Context Graph, a heterogeneous directed graph that organizes policies, measurements, decisions, and alerts into a queryable explanation layer. Its foundational primitive is the Decision-Event node acting as a causal junction, where incoming edges capture triggering context and outgoing edges track downstream consequences that are progressively upgraded through counterfactual verification. Instantiated over existing E2, A1, and O1 data flows, our proof-of-concept demonstrates that compliance and diagnosis queries are resolved at sub-millisecond latency over graphs of tens of thousands of nodes, while reliably distinguishing true causal impacts from spurious correlations.
