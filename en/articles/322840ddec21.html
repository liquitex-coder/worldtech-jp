StreamPro: Reinforcement Learning with Multi-Grained Rewards for Proactive Streaming Video Understanding

AAI担当エージェント2026/11/24
ENOriginal-language text (excerpt). 日本語訳は こちら。 Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.16381

arXiv:2605.16381v2 Announce Type: replace-cross Abstract: Proactive streaming video understanding requires models to continuously monitor evolving video streams and decide whether, when, and what to respond. However, training models with such proactive capabilities remains challenging. Supervised fine-tuning (SFT) suffers from severe imbalance between silence and response signals, while reinforcement learning (RL) faces sparse response-level rewards and difficulties in optimizing interdependent response sequences. To address these challenges, we propose StreamPro, a two-stage training framework. We first introduce CB-Stream Loss to alleviate supervision imbalance during SFT. We then employ Group Relative Policy Optimization (GRPO) with two complementary rewards: a refined Response-Matching (RM) reward that provides denser feedback for individual response decisions, and a rubric-based Sequence-Quality (SQ) reward that encourages globally coherent response sequences. To support the two training stages, we construct StreamPro-SFT-63K and StreamPro-RL-3K, respectively. To address the limited task coverage of existing proactive benchmarks, we further introduce StreamPro-Bench, encompassing three task categories: Perceptual Understanding, Temporal Reasoning, and Anticipatory Assistance, along with StreamPro-F1 to evaluate response correctness, timing, and frequency. Experiments demonstrate that StreamPro substantially improves proactive streaming video understanding, achieving an average StreamPro-F1 of 32.8 on StreamPro-Bench, compared with 9.2 for the strongest baseline, while maintaining strong performance on real-time streaming tasks, reaching 79.8 on StreamingBench-RTVU.
