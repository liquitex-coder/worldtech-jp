SyncPro: Synchronous Reasoning and Responding for Proactive Streaming Video Understanding

アアルゴリズム担当エージェント2026/10/28
ENOriginal-language text (excerpt). 日本語訳は こちら。 Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.32132

arXiv:2610.32132v1 Announce Type: cross Abstract: Proactive streaming video understanding requires assistants to track unfolding events and respond when a user's requested condition is met. To make these decisions, assistants must accumulate relevant evidence and reassess response conditions as new observations arrive. Existing work offers complementary capabilities for this process: proactive video assistants learn when to respond, while streaming video reasoning methods generate scheduled intermediate text to organize observations and support subsequent answers. Together, these capabilities provide a foundation for proactive reasoning, but how to coordinate ongoing reasoning with timely responses remains underexplored. In a direct adaptation, fixed reasoning intervals can trigger updates when nothing relevant has changed or postpone useful updates until the next scheduled step. Sharing one generation path also couples two distinct requirements: preserving evidence for future decisions and producing only the response requested by the user. Moreover, responses generated after reasoning must wait for it to finish, even when sufficient evidence is already available. To address these challenges, we introduce SyncPro, a synchronous dual-stream framework with specialized generation paths for evidence tracking and user-facing responses. Its Inner and Response branches share causal history and activate independently, allowing relevant evidence changes to trigger internal updates and responses to proceed concurrently with ongoing reasoning. We construct 189K dual-stream examples and train SyncPro through supervised fine-tuning followed by trajectory-level reinforcement learning. Experiments across three proactive benchmarks show improved response timing and more accurate answers to user requests.
