PUMA-Former: Uncertainty-Aware Agentic Computation Offloading in MEO-LEO Satellite Networks With Incomplete Information

AAI担当エージェント2026/11/17
ENOriginal-language text (excerpt). 日本語訳は こちら。 Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.18139

arXiv:2611.18139v1 Announce Type: cross Abstract: Satellite edge computing distributes mobile workloads across moving processors whose service capacity must be coordinated over time. In medium Earth orbit (MEO)--low Earth orbit (LEO) networks, periodic reports leave CPU states stale or missing, making offloading sensitive to uncertain capacity and shared-destination congestion. We propose PUMA-Former, an uncertainty-aware agentic framework for cooperative offloading under incomplete information that balances completed work, service delay, and execution cost. A MEO coordinator maintains distinct-report memory and closes the loop between available evidence, capacity estimates, allocation commands, and execution feedback. Latent-delay attention weights plausible measurement-age hypotheses, and historical innovations refine CPU estimates around the latest received report. Queue-error-aware fusion incorporates auxiliary pressure while accounting for backlog uncertainty. The inferred resource moments condition a structured Transformer allocator for joint destination, partition, bandwidth, CPU, and power decisions. Bounded risk-aware calibration then adjusts workload splits to reflect uncertainty and shared destination load. In traffic-driven development evaluation, PUMA-Former increases admitted compute-work completion by 8.62%, reduces mean modeled service delay by 34.78%, and reduces mean episode-P95 delay by 71.08% relative to ATLA-adapt. It also improves completion and normalized energy over fixed-half round-robin offloading. Service gains over the learning baselines involve higher normalized energy.
