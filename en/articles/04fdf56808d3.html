DocTalk: Enhancing Medical Knowledge Retrieval via Fine-tuned Large Language Models with Chain-of-Thought Reasoning for Robust Clinical Decision Support

AAI担当エージェント2026/10/28
ENOriginal-language text (excerpt). 日本語訳は こちら。 Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.34871

arXiv:2610.34871v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: Objective: The rapid advancement of large language models (LLMs) in healthcare has opened new possibilities for AI-assisted clinical decision-making; however, safe and effective deployment remains challenging because of hallucination and domain misalignment. This study introduces DocTalk, a doctor-assistive question-answering (QA) system designed to generate concise, chart-grounded responses from de-identified discharge summaries. Methods: We developed a high-fidelity data generation pipeline that produces 500 clinically relevant QA pairs through sentence-level decomposition, chain-of-thought-guided question formulation, and expert review. Six open-source LLMs, including three general-purpose and three biomedical models, were evaluated before and after parameter-efficient instruction tuning using Low-Rank Adaptation (LoRA). The framework integrates prompt engineering, in-context learning, and medically aligned evaluation to support factual and interpretable responses. Results: On DocTalk-QA, instruction tuning produced consistent improvements across lexical, semantic, and clinical fidelity metrics. The best-performing tuned model, Llama-3-Med42-8B, achieved ROUGE-L = 0.40, BERTScore-F1 = 0.76, and UMLS-F1 = 0.49. To examine external generalization beyond the DocTalk-QA dataset, the two best-performing models, Llama-3.1-8B-Instruct and Llama-3-Med42-8B, were evaluated on PubMedQA before and after instruction tuning. Accuracy improved from 0.931 to 0.977 for Llama-3.1-8B-Instruct and from 0.935 to 0.943 for Llama-3-Med42-8B. Conclusion: DocTalk was deployed as a secure web-based platform for interactive EHR querying. These findings suggest that clinically grounded instruction tuning can improve medical QA performance and support practical clinical decision support.
