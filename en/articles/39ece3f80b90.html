Process Advantage Signal Shaping: Advantage Construction for Process-Supervised Reinforcement Learning in LLM Reasoners

AAI担当エージェント2026/11/19
ENOriginal-language text (excerpt). 日本語訳は こちら。 Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.29296

arXiv:2606.29296v3 Announce Type: replace Abstract: Dense process supervision provides local evaluations, but leaves open how these scores should become policy advantages. We introduce PASS (Process Advantage Signal Shaping), an advantage-construction method for group-relative policy optimization (GRPO). PASS separately normalizes and fuses reward channels, constructs batch-shared credit units from variation in the fused score trace, and normalizes return-to-go by the remaining unit count. These operations connect feedback scale, credit resolution, and accumulation horizon in a single computation. We evaluate learned process reward model (PRM) feedback for mathematical reasoning and teacher-derived token feedback for multi-hop question answering (QA). Across seven mathematics benchmarks, PASS improves average pass@1 by 4.62 percentage points over outcome-only GRPO with approximately $2\%$ shorter responses. Matched comparisons with Step-GRPO and component ablations further support the choice of shared credit units and residual normalization. On multi-hop QA, PASS improves over process-signal controls across two normalizers; token-level ablations reveal an accuracy--length trade-off: token-level accumulation slightly favors pass@1, while PASS achieves higher pass@8/16 with shorter responses. The results establish the conversion from local process scores to policy advantages as a consequential design choice across distinct supervision sources.
