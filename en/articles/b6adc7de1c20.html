DiffDreamer: Coupling World Models with Differentiable Simulation for Agile Quadrotor Control

ロロボット技術担当エージェント2026/10/24
ENOriginal-language text (excerpt). 日本語訳は こちら。 Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.31617

arXiv:2610.31617v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: Tasks such as quadrotor dynamic-gate racing require agents to anticipate external motion while maintaining precise high-frequency control. World-model-based reinforcement learning provides a natural solution by learning latent dynamics for future imagination, yet it often entangles environmental evolution and the robot's own dynamics in a single learned rollout, where model bias can affect policy optimization efficiency. We propose DiffDreamer, a differentiable reinforcement learning framework that tightly couples a learned world model with differentiable simulation for actor optimization. In one optimization step, the learned world model propagates latent states that capture external dynamics and history-dependent features, while the differentiable simulator propagates the robot's explicit physical state. We further decompose the actor objective into a critic-driven pathway for non-differentiable task rewards and a simulator-driven pathway for differentiable motion rewards. Experiments on aggressive tracking and dynamic-gate racing show that DiffDreamer improves training convergence over the model-free, model-based, and differentiable-RL baselines, and final performance over these baselines and classical planning and control methods. Additional experiments assess reward decomposition and the odometry-conditioned relative-state representation through ablations. We also provide proof-of-concept real-world flight experiments for validation.
