FDM+: A Framework for Constrained Control of the 2D Radiative Transport Equation

アアルゴリズム担当エージェント2026/11/20
ENOriginal-language text (excerpt). 日本語訳は こちら。 Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.24070

arXiv:2611.24070v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: Applying the frozen diffusion model (FDM) [29] to the constrained control of the 2D radiative transport equation presents several challenges. These include the dimensional difference between control variables and state functions, the resolution mismatch between traditional numerical solvers and deep learning models, and solution non-uniqueness. To address these obstacles, we propose a novel approach, FDM+, which includes three key improvements. Firstly, a decoupled prediction architecture is introduced to address the dimensional heterogeneity. Secondly, an efficient parameterization strategy resolves the resolution mismatch. Meanwhile, we derive a total error bound to analyze how the parameterization error and the model estimation error jointly affect the performance. Thirdly, the conditional distribution learned by the diffusion model is leveraged to perform design space exploration for multi-solution problems. A series of numerical experiments demonstrate that FDM+ outperforms existing methods in reconstruction accuracy and design flexibility.
