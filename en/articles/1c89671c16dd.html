Neurodegenerative Differential Diagnosis with Knowledge-enhanced Structural Reasoning

AAI担当エージェント2026/11/24
ENOriginal-language text (excerpt). 日本語訳は こちら。 Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.27427

arXiv:2611.27427v1 Announce Type: cross Abstract: Accurate differential diagnosis of neurodegenerative disorders remains challenging due to overlapping clinical manifestations and heterogeneous, often incomplete multimodal observations. Existing multimodal models can integrate imaging and clinical measurements but often overlook disease-specific evidence patterns and pairwise relations among competing diagnoses. We therefore propose Knowledge-enhanced Structural Reasoning (KnowSR), which aligns multimodal patient representations with a literature-grounded knowledge bank of disease prototypes, signed evidence directions, and pairwise diagnostic relations. Specifically, in KnowSR, patient-specific knowledge alignment and target-aware pooling inject structured disease evidence, while anchored relational reasoning reconciles pairwise diagnostic margins over a sparse differential graph, thereby collectively enhancing differential diagnostic capability. Across extensive experiments, KnowSR consistently outperforms 19 baselines on patient-level six-class diagnosis under patient-disjoint five-fold evaluation. This shows that grounding patient evidence in structured medical knowledge enables more effective differential diagnosis than naive multimodal fusion alone.
