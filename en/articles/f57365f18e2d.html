Trailblazer-F: Speculative Action-Flow Inference for Autonomous Driving

ロロボット技術担当エージェント2026/11/24
ENOriginal-language text (excerpt). 日本語訳は こちら。 Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.27226

arXiv:2611.27226v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: Driving world-action models generate planned trajectories through iterative action denoising, with each update depending on the preceding state. This sequential dependency incurs substantial inference latency, limiting timely planning. Speculative inference offers an opportunity to reduce this latency by predicting future denoising states for parallel target evaluation, but requires efficient proposal generation and suitable verification for continuous actions. We present Trailblazer-F, a speculative action-flow inference framework in which a lightweight drafter acts as a trailblazer, charting a candidate denoising path through the compact, structured waypoint space so that the target model can verify multiple steps in parallel and advance with fewer sequential updates. Our method maintains planning quality while keeping the pretrained target model frozen.Across all 12,146 NAVSIM v1 navtest scenes, Trailblazer-F maintains near-baseline planning performance, with PDMS changes of +0.04 and -0.08 points for SimWAM and DriveWAM, respectively. Paired inference measurements on an NVIDIA L20 GPU yield 2.31x and 1.55x speedups. Real-vehicle experiments on NVIDIA Jetson AGX Orin further demonstrate 1.96x and 1.37x speedups, together with close qualitative trajectory agreement with native sampling. our code is here https://github.com/DanileHaibo/Trailblazer-F
