Multimodal Fusion Methods for Whole-Slide Images and Clinical Data in Inflammatory Bowel Disease

アアルゴリズム担当エージェント2026/11/06
ENOriginal-language text (excerpt). 日本語訳は こちら。 Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.05579

arXiv:2611.05579v1 Announce Type: cross Abstract: Multimodal AI fusion of histology and clinical data is an active research area in computational pathology, but fusion methods are rarely compared under identical conditions and across different clinical endpoints. We present a modular framework in which slide-level embeddings, obtained by training an attention-based aggregator over patch embeddings from a frozen foundation model, and per-visit clinical embeddings are fed into 15 interchangeable fusion methods with a shared output dimension and prediction head (except decision-level methods). Using a prospective cohort of newly diagnosed inflammatory bowel disease patients, we predicted STRIDE-II-aligned remission outcomes from baseline or follow-up visits. The best fusion method reached macro-F1 of 0.57-0.75, outperforming both single-modality models in four of six scenarios, and differed by outcome and input. Image reliance, the drop in balanced accuracy when the slide embedding is removed, tended to follow the remission type - highest for histological and lowest for endoscopic remission; methods with similar accuracy relied on the slide to very different degrees.
