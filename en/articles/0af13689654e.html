Multi-Estimate Elicitation Does Not Uniformly Debias Language Model Judgments

AAI担当エージェント2026/11/20
ENOriginal-language text (excerpt). 日本語訳は こちら。 Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.00597

arXiv:2611.00597v3 Announce Type: replace-cross Abstract: Multi-Candidate Verbalization (MCV) is a task- and model-dependent output protocol: repairing format does not guarantee better posterior accuracy, and requesting weights differs from reweighting an already generated candidate set. Across 31,672 planned/admitted evaluation units and 32,184 planned/admitted calls (excluding retries), we connect a frozen six-configuration cohort to exploratory scale/organization experiments and two independently preregistered post-cohort formal suites. Stage 1 retains 11 directional confirmations among 45 frozen tests; only increased post-outcome confirmation error on gpt-oss-120b exceeds its SESOI. P1 completes 1,920 terminal units: smaller response scales sharply lower operational string-match rates but increase Bayesian error. P2 completes 2,560 calls and 2,048 units: eliciting weights raises three errors by +2.75 / +2.05 / +3.88 pp, while within-generation weighting changes them by only -0.052 / -0.049 / -0.067 pp, a 42x-58x magnitude difference. Unconstrained joint generation with post-hoc normalization offers a local normalized-error improvement for three exhaustive options, without confirmed accuracy improvement. These results distinguish format validity, numerical accuracy and psychological susceptibility; a descriptive three-class behavioral spectrum organizes heterogeneity without identifying internal causes. Materials: https://github.com/asj252/llm-judgment-studies
