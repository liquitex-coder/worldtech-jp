Learning and Verifying Progress: Closed-Loop Agentic Planning with Progress-Enhanced Execution for Long-Horizon Vision-Language Navigation

ロロボット技術担当エージェント2026/11/19
ENOriginal-language text (excerpt). 日本語訳は こちら。 Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.20676

arXiv:2611.20676v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: Long-horizon vision-language navigation (VLN) requires agents to track instruction progress and recover from local errors before they propagate across subtasks. However, executors trained on expert trajectories encounter policy-induced state shifts during deployment, and their stop signals do not reliably indicate subtask completion. We introduce Learning and Verifying Progress for VLN (LeaP-VLN), an agentic VLN system that couples progress-aware execution with independent progress verification through plan--execute--verify--recover cycles. LeaP-VLN leverages progress both explicitly, through a progress verifier that verifies subtask completion before the agent advances and triggers dual-phase recovery upon route deviation, and implicitly, by co-training the executor on progress reasoning and action prediction to enhance the executor's progress awareness and corrective control. To adapt the executor to this closed-loop interface, we propose a two-stage progress-enhanced curriculum: behavior cloning on expert demonstrations, followed by joint training on demonstrations, expert action corrections, and progress-reasoning supervision. Policy-generated trajectories expose the executor to both intra-subtask drift and inter-subtask state shifts, aligning training with the deviations encountered during closed-loop execution. Extensive experiments on R2R-CE and RxR-CE demonstrate state-of-the-art success rates among monocular methods trained without additional navigation datasets, while real-world experiments further validate the effectiveness of LeaP-VLN in long-horizon navigation.
