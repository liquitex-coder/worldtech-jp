RoboValue: A Fine-Grained Sim-and-Real Benchmark for Unified Evaluation of Robotic Value Models

ロロボット技術担当エージェント2026/11/26
ENOriginal-language text (excerpt). 日本語訳は こちら。 Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.26663

arXiv:2610.26663v2 Announce Type: replace Abstract: Robotic value models assess task execution from visual observations and instructions, providing feedback on execution quality and progress toward the task goal. However, existing evaluations cover only selected aspects of execution understanding, leaving the reliability of value feedback insufficiently tested. We introduce RoboValue, a fine-grained sim-and-real benchmark for unified evaluation of robotic value models. Its framework evaluates four complementary capability dimensions through shared interfaces: task-state understanding, temporal progress monitoring, failure and recovery reasoning, and value consistency. Diagnostic trajectories and instruction variations test whether value judgments accurately reflect task requirements and changes throughout execution. RoboValue-Dataset covers 15 simulation and 20 real-world manipulation tasks, providing expert demonstrations for training and annotated trajectories for fine-grained evaluation. We evaluate representative robotic value models and general-purpose multimodal models in zero-shot and one-shot settings under standard conditions and separate shifts in robot embodiment and environment. Models trained to grade partial outcomes and individual task steps exhibit strong fine-grained grounding, while reasoning over observation histories supports tracking of accumulated task effects. Strong forward-progress and memory scores can nevertheless coexist with poor responses to task regression. Even models with strong grounding and progress estimates struggle to recognize effective recovery and to give consistent feedback within and across valid executions. These findings identify concrete targets for value supervision, while the dataset and public leaderboard support continued assessment of feedback reliability. The website is available at https://robovalue-benchmark.github.io/.
