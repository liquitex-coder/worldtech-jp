ClearNav: Perception-Aligned Quadrotor Flight Using Differential-Physics

AAI担当エージェント2026/10/16
ENOriginal-language text (excerpt). 日本語訳は こちら。 Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.17419

arXiv:2610.17419v1 Announce Type: cross Abstract: Autonomous quadrotor flight in cluttered environment is an key capability for tasks like inspection and search. Navigation policies trained in simulation and transferred to hardware can fly at high speed in open space, but they can still collide as obstacle clearance shrinks. We attribute most of these collisions to a single mechanism, namely motion in directions the onboard camera does not observe. Heading that is not aligned with the direction of travel and lateral excursion during obstacle avoidance both carry the vehicle toward obstacles outside its field of view. We introduce ClearNav, a navigation policy trained with two reward terms that constrain motion to the observed direction, a forward-looking heading target and a penalty on lateral velocity. A third term penalizes speed near obstacles so that an obstacle entering the field of view remains avoidable. In simulation, across 18 held-out scenarios with 2304 paired flights per policy unseen in training, ClearNav improves success by 6.8 points, cuts the collision rate by 65% relative to the state of the arts, and flies at maximum 5m/s. For the proximity term alone under matched commanded speeds, the collision reduction still holds at 70%. The effect is significant on static scenes and consistent in direction on moving obstacles, though not significant there at the suite level, showing that safer flight in clutter is possible even on the limited sensors and compute of small drones. We also validate our navigation policy in a real-world cluttered environment, showing that it enables the quadrotor to navigate through the cluttered environment successfully and safely.
