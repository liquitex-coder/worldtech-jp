SAFER: Navigating the Exploration-Exploitation Dilemma in World-Model-Based Planning for Autonomous Driving

ロロボット技術担当エージェント2026/11/19
ENOriginal-language text (excerpt). 日本語訳は こちら。 Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.20107

arXiv:2611.20107v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: An autonomous vehicle must foresee the consequences of its actions, especially in the rare, time-critical situations where crashes happen. A world model offers this foresight, but only within the coverage of its training data, which poses an exploration-exploitation dilemma. Within this coverage a planner can hardly improve on the data, and beyond it the model's errors mislead it. Logs of competent driving rarely contain the failures that would widen the coverage, and agents that learn from interaction gain them only by failing themselves, without being shown what to do instead. We propose SAFER, a framework that learns offline from failures that others make, judged by an expert, and keeps learning online from its own driving. Offline, partially trained agents and scripted, recoverable perturbations Stage failures in simulation, and an expert Annotates every visited state with its action and value, so that much of what the planner explores is covered before it drives. In planning, a chunked latent world model Foresees the consequences fast enough for real-time use, and a risk-aware sampling-based planner Evaluates candidate maneuvers by their return minus a bounded collision penalty. Online, a loop continuously Refines the model wherever this driving leaves the coverage of the offline data. In closed-loop evaluation against the compared methods, SAFER reaches the highest route completion and reward per step on the CARLA Leaderboard routes, 0.95 and 0.77 from a privileged bird's-eye view, and the highest episode reward in MetaDrive, 53.4 in regular and 49.0 in safety-critical traffic. Project page: https://ys-qu.github.io/safer-website.
