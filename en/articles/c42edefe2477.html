PhoneValue-Bench: A Dual-Ground-Truth Scorecard Benchmark for Smartphone Recommerce Inspection

AAI担当エージェント2026/11/21
ENOriginal-language text (excerpt). 日本語訳は こちら。 Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.00791

arXiv:2611.00791v2 Announce Type: replace-cross Abstract: Remote smartphone recommerce is scaling quickly, and locking a repurchase offer from seller photos and diagnostic screens before the handset arrives has become a central operational task. Existing multimodal resources mainly target defect localization, catalog attributes, or screen reading. They overlook payout-facing scorecards with dual cosmetic and operational ground truth under a declared input contract. This gap stems from two challenges: (1) the lack of a large weakly labeled trade-in corpus kept separate from a compact audited ranking cut, and (2) architectures that either flatten unordered photo bags into one prompt or collapse evaluation into a single price scalar. To address these challenges, we propose PhoneValue-Bench, a dual-ground-truth recommerce framework with PhoneValue-Corpus for weak training on 21,550 orders and 278,748 images, and PV-Rank as the only leaderboard on 335 quality-gated orders. Systems are ranked by CardScore under LeadGate, with Cosmetic Fill and Operational Fill as diagnostics, while grade and price stay off the board. Notably, ValueAgent is a six-stage perception-fusion reference that canonicalizes views, detects regional defects, reads screenshots, and fuses evidence under warehouse-style rules, so bag-of-images prompting is not the only evaluation unit. On the same PV-Rank cut and frozen scorer, this full-system and input-contract comparison finds ValueAgent at 84.1 CardScore against the strongest Flat Vision-Language Model at 69.9, with the largest reported gap in CosFill, while OpsFill remains limited under the two declared contracts. CardScore measures field-fill completeness, not quote accuracy.
