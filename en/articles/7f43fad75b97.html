Scale Anchoring via Implicit Semantics and Sequence-Consistent Loop Closure for VGGT-Based SLAM

ロロボット技術担当エージェント2026/11/07
ENOriginal-language text (excerpt). 日本語訳は こちら。 Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.06308

arXiv:2611.06308v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: Feed-forward geometric foundation models enable dense monocular reconstruction without requiring camera calibration, but independently reconstructed submaps can accumulate scale drift, and even a valid revisit can yield an inconsistent cross-submap measurement. We extend VGGT-SLAM 2.0 with fixed-root scale anchoring and short-sequence-assisted direct loop connections. SALAD selects an image pair between the initial submap and each eligible new submap; joint VGGT inference then uses learned implicit semantic-geometric cues to supply a scale-only anchor while preserving the original overlap chain. For loop closure, neighboring image pairs support a consistency check and translation-scale alignment. Each accepted candidate contributes one robust Sim(3) factor between existing center-frame nodes, without an auxiliary loop submap. A 17-sequence comparison lowers mean Sim(3) ATE on nine TUM sequences from 0.1018 to 0.0451 m relative to the VGGT-SLAM 2.0 baseline. Anchoring improves all seven no-loop module ablations. On three Clio scenes, a fixed-loop comparison favors the direct connection pipeline over the tested auxiliary-submap pipeline. A separate candidate audit exposes additional rejections of visually overlapping pairs by the short-sequence check. These comparisons distinguish the gains from long-range scale anchoring and loop connection choices from the limitations of candidate verification.
