A Semantics-First Approach to Multimodal Video Captioning with Frozen Encoders and Stabilized Reinforcement Learning

アアルゴリズム担当エージェント2026/11/27
ENOriginal-language text (excerpt). 日本語訳は こちら。 Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.33409

arXiv:2611.33409v1 Announce Type: cross Abstract: We propose the Audio-Visual Query Transformer (AVQFormer), a highly efficient architecture built upon a 2.7B parameter LLM featuring a fixed-length $K=16$ semantic alignment module, stabilized via Adaptive Self-Critical Sequence Training (ASCST) with Entropy Regularization. Multimodal Large Language Models (LLMs) often rely on global audio representations to compensate for inefficient, frame-based visual encoders. While our Phase 1 prompted evaluations demonstrate that the Multimodal Base Model achieves strong deep semantic alignment (scoring 0.889 on BERTScore F1), optimizing this architecture for legacy metrics reveals a performance gap. We explore this gap as a hypothesis we term temporal alignment friction: a conflict where global audio representations disrupt the chronological sequence of visual frames, leading to penalties under visually-focused evaluation metrics. Consequently, by stripping away the audio, our Video-Only ablation conforms more closely to this rigid syntax, achieving 63.0 CIDEr. While trailing larger models such as Qwen2.5-VL (67.4 CIDEr), this establishes a strong baseline for sub-3B parameter LLM-based models. This indicates that CIDEr, as applied to datasets such as MSR-VTT, may exhibit a bias toward visually grounded syntax, limiting its ability to fully capture multimodal semantic richness.
