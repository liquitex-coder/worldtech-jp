OpenHome: From Perception to Insight in Smart-Home Camera Videos

AAI担当エージェント2026/10/27
ENOriginal-language text (excerpt). 日本語訳は こちら。 Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.31986

arXiv:2610.31986v1 Announce Type: cross Abstract: Vision language models (VLMs) have been increasingly adopted in consumer smart-home camera systems to interpret recorded videos and provide insights. However, video clips captured from smart-home cameras are different from curated web videos dominating existing VLM benchmarks. Smart-home video clips often do not contain subtitles or narration and are captured from different viewing angles, under various lighting conditions, and in diverse scenarios. However, the capabilities of the model in this domain, under sparse visual signals and limited context, are not fully assessed by existing VLM benchmarks. To close this gap, we introduce OpenHome, a visual-only, four-option multiple-choice benchmark for evaluating smart-home video understanding. It contains 2,254 questions on 1,118 real-world video clips and organizes 10 tasks into three capability levels. Perception evaluates visible content, Reasoning evaluates causal, spatial, and temporal relationships, and Insight evaluates subject intent, supporting evidence and appropriate homeowner responses. Based on the dataset, we describe the task design, data pipeline and evaluation protocol, and report results for 32 closed-source and open-weight VLMs. The best model, Gemini 4 Argon, reaches 81.2% overall accuracy. Frontier closed-source models outperform open-weight models on every level. The best closed-source model leads the best open-weight model by 16.3% on Perception, 22.4% on Reasoning and 37.4% on Insight.
