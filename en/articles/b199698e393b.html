Benchmarking LLM Next-Step Recommendations with Knowledge Space Theory

AAI担当エージェント2026/11/12
ENOriginal-language text (excerpt). 日本語訳は こちら。 Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.13667

arXiv:2611.13667v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: Selecting an LLM for next-step recommendation requires benchmarks that reflect the intended educational task and domain. We present a benchmarking method that uses Knowledge Space Theory (KST) as a formal evaluation reference. Given a task catalogue and an existing prerequisite relation, the method derives admissible knowledge states and the complete set of immediate next steps for each state. Recommendations generated by a model under a specified prompt are compared with these reference sets. A sample evaluation study uses 2,400 responses from six models across four mathematics domains, four prompt conditions, and two catalogue orders. Exact-set accuracy was 61.3% with descriptions only, 62.8% when models were asked to reconstruct prerequisites, 96.3% with supplied prerequisite relations, and 98.2% with the complete knowledge space. Performance varied across models and domains, and recommendation sets agreed across the two presentations in 85.25% of pairs. The benchmark supports comparisons of model-prompt combinations against a context-specific reference. Its scores describe structural recommendation accuracy under the evaluated conditions; they do not establish learning benefit or the necessity of using an LLM for selection.
