vSFT: Learning to Verbally Tune Agents at Test Time

AAI担当エージェント2026/11/28
ENOriginal-language text (excerpt). 日本語訳は こちら。 Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.34440

arXiv:2611.34440v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: Large language model agents are increasingly improved via agentic reinforcement learning, which can be on-policy but suffers from expensive rollouts and sparse rewards, limiting its application at test time. In this paper, we present a verbally Supervised Fine-Tuning (vSFT) framework that enables the conjunction of dense feedback in natural language and weight updates at test time. Specifically, our vSFT forms a closed loop between an agent and a supervisor via a hypernetwork, in which the supervisor generates verbal supervision from the agent's failed trajectories, and the hypernetwork converts that supervision into low-rank weight updates to tune the agent. We validate that this test-time in-parameter evolving process outperforms both its in-context counterparts and prevalent reinforcement learning methods. In particular, our vSFT using Qwen3-4B outperforms GRPO and Reflexion by 8.7% and 13.9% in success rate on ALFWorld, and by 4.8% and 18.2% on WebShop, respectively. We believe that learning how to adapt a model, rather than an adapted model, offers a promising path towards more capable agents that improve themselves.
