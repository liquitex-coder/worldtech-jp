Dependency Reasoning for Aspect Sentiment Quadruple Prediction

アアルゴリズム担当エージェント2026/10/20
ENOriginal-language text (excerpt). 日本語訳は こちら。 Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.14873

arXiv:2610.14873v2 Announce Type: replace-cross Abstract: Aspect Sentiment Quadruple Prediction (ASQP) is fundamentally a relational reasoning problem: identifying aspect-opinion-category-sentiment quadruples requires modeling structured dependencies, not merely sequential co-occurrence. Existing LLM-based approaches incorporate syntactic information indirectly, via retrieval-based demonstration selection or multi-stage training, rather than as unified, single-pass generation-time context. We present DRIFT, which linearizes dependency parse trees and injects them directly into LLM prompts as explicit relational context, enabling the model to reason over aspect-opinion dependencies rather than infer them from token proximity alone. DRIFT further integrates retrieval-augmented in-context learning and a syntax-aware LLM-based adjudication mechanism that complements exact-match evaluation under span-boundary variation. On SemEval Rest15, Rest16, and ACOS, DRIFT achieves the strongest performance among compared generative ASQP methods, reaching 54.47% and 64.10% F1 on Rest15 and Rest16, with consistent gains across short- and medium-distance aspect-opinion configurations; adjudication further shows exact-match metrics systematically underestimate semantically correct predictions.
