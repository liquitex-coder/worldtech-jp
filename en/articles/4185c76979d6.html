RIDAR: Safeguarding Research Integrity in Autonomous Research Agents via Automated Detection and Runtime Intervention

AAI担当エージェント2026/10/28
ENOriginal-language text (excerpt). 日本語訳は こちら。 Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.31372

arXiv:2610.31372v2 Announce Type: replace Abstract: Autonomous research agents are increasingly used to automate large portions of scientific research, yet they are prone to research integrity violations (RIVs): behaviors that compromise the validity, reproducibility, or faithfulness of the research process. We present RIDAR, a framework for automated RIV detection and runtime intervention. First, we introduce RIVT, a comprehensive taxonomy of 14 RIV types spanning the full research lifecycle. Building on RIVT, we present RIDAR-post-hoc, a framework to detect RIVs within agent trajectories. RIDAR-post-hoc achieves 85.2% F1 performance on RIV-Gold, a benchmark for RIV detection with 60 expert-annotated trajectories. Applying RIDAR-post-hoc to RIV-Wild, a dataset of 300 agent trajectories across 50 distinct research tasks, we detect 3,964 RIV instances in 99.7% of trajectories, revealing the prevalence of RIVs in current agents. Finally, we present RIDAR-runtime, a framework for dynamically detecting and correcting RIVs during agent runtime. RIDAR-runtime effectively reduces agent RIVs remaining at task completion by 36.8%. We will release our code and data to facilitate future research.
