Kolpona: An Agentic Framework with Gradient-Free Neuron Programming for RTL Generation

AAI担当エージェント2026/11/28
ENOriginal-language text (excerpt). 日本語訳は こちら。 Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.33973

arXiv:2611.33973v1 Announce Type: cross Abstract: Open-weight large language models (LLMs) for register-transfer level (RTL) design are usually improved by fine-tuning on curated Verilog corpora, which needs large sets of verified designs and GPU training. We present Kolpona, an agentic framework that improves such models without gradient training. Its first component is neuron programming, a new form of weight-space programming (WSP) that writes single neurons in closed form into the weights to remove failure habits found on practice tasks, and keeps each edit only when testbench results on fresh tasks favour it. At inference time, tool-using agents propose designs, and a label-free selection stage delivers one design per task from the specification, the candidates and their simulations alone. On Qwen3.5-4B, edits to 16 neurons raise the VerilogEval v2 pass@1 of the model alone from 31.7% to 55.4%, and the full framework reaches 77.6%. On Qwen3.8-27B, Kolpona lifts pass@1 from 73.1% to 94.9% on VerilogEval v2 and from 66.0% to 92.0% on RTLLM v2.1 on a single 32 GB GPU, above every published system in our comparison. The RTL edits also carry over to Python and raise the pass@1 of the 4B model alone from 84.8% to 91.5% on HumanEval+ and from 67.7% to 77.5% on MBPP+.
