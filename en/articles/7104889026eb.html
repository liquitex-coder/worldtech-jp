RoadState: A Domain-Prior-Enhanced Vision-Language System for Industrial-Grade Lane-Level Map Updating

AAI担当エージェント2026/11/27
ENOriginal-language text (excerpt). 日本語訳は こちら。 Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.10746

arXiv:2611.10746v2 Announce Type: replace-cross Abstract: Lane-level maps provide foundational structured road information for navigation, intelligent driving, and intelligent transportation systems. Understanding road-scene semantics from structural and contextual cues is essential for reliable lane-level map updating, yet remains challenging in production due to occlusions, ambiguous lane markings, temporary obstacles, and viewpoint changes, while downstream workflows require temporally consistent, physically valid, and auditable outputs rather than free-form descriptions. We present RoadState, a production-deployed vision-language system that transforms sequential road-scene observations into structured lane-level semantics for Baidu Maps' map-updating pipeline. Its core model, RoadState-VLM, formulates road-scene understanding as autoregressive state estimation, predicting the current structured road state from the previous observation, previous symbolic state, and current observation. To improve semantic consistency and reasoning fidelity across interdependent road-state dimensions, we introduce Geometric Chain-of-Thought (Geo-CoT), which encodes a directed acyclic graph of semantic dependencies as a domain prior. On a real-world dataset, RoadState-VLM achieves 94.33% mean accuracy across six road-state dimensions, outperforming the strongest fine-tuned VLM baseline, with ablations confirming the complementary gains of Geo-CoT and temporal transition context. In production, a cascaded routing pipeline directs routine cases to lightweight models and reserves complex cases for RoadState-VLM. Deployed since March 2025, RoadState supports lane-level map updating across 360+ cities, reduces overall production costs by 30%, and maintains robust performance under stress testing with human-in-the-loop auditing.
