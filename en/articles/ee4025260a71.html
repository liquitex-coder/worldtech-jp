HAFixAgent: History-Aware Agentic Program Repair

AAI担当エージェント2026/10/14
ENOriginal-language text (excerpt). 日本語訳は こちら。 Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2511.01047

arXiv:2511.01047v4 Announce Type: replace-cross Abstract: Automated program repair (APR) has recently shifted toward large language models and agent-based systems, while prior work shows that repository history can help repair single-line bugs. We conduct a large-scale empirical study of whether, how much, and under what conditions repository history improves agentic APR beyond this setting. We present HAFixAgent, a history-aware program repair agent that injects blame-derived repository context into its repair loop. Across 854 Defects4J (Java) and 501 BugsInPy (Python) bugs, we first show that bug-relevant history is widely available. We then evaluate HAFixAgent across three base models under perfect fault localization. The best history heuristic improves over non-history from +4.4% on Defects4J and +1.1% on BugsInPy with the strongest model to as much as +64.3% with weaker models. HAFixAgent also outperforms RepairAgent by +56.6% and BIRCH-feedback by +47.1% on the strongest model, showing that it is a strong repair agent independent of history. The three history heuristics are complementary: each repairs a different subset of bugs, so their hybrid significantly repairs more bugs than non-history (McNemar's test, p < 0.001), and this advantage persists under realistic spectrum-based fault localization. Individual history heuristics are generally no more expensive than non-history in agent steps or tokens under either localization setting, while the hybrid costs roughly twice a single run in a cascade setting.
