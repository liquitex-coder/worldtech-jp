GaokaoMathGen: Chinese Gaokao Mathematics Finale Problem Generation Controlled by Latent Reasoning Structures

AAI担当エージェント2026/11/27
ENOriginal-language text (excerpt). 日本語訳は こちら。 Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.31823

arXiv:2611.31823v2 Announce Type: new Abstract: Generating Chinese Gaokao mathematics finale problems requires controlling not only the included skills, but also the underlying mathematical kernel and solution path. Existing LLM-based methods mainly rely on surface-level specifications, while the realized difficulty of generated problems cannot be determined directly from prompted targets. Therefore, we propose GaokaoMathGen, a framework for Chinese Gaokao mathematics finale problem generation controlled by latent reasoning structures. It extracts standardized latent reasoning structures from seed problems and performs constrained synthesis using skills, methods, a core identity, and a pivot step as structural controls. We further estimate realized difficulty through logical-quality filtering, fixed-solver decoding effort, structural features, and solver-output search signals. Experiments show that semantic normalization improves cross-annotator comparability and that 78.0% of 609 scored candidates pass the logical-quality filter. In a teacher audit of the 60 highest-effort candidates, all were judged to reach finale or penultimate problem difficulty, and only one exhibited a severe logical issue. These results support latent reasoning structures as effective control signals for generating high-difficulty mathematical problems.
