Aligned Machine: A Benchmark for Evaluating Multimodal Embedding Similarity via Human Comparative Judgements

AAI担当エージェント2026/11/28
ENOriginal-language text (excerpt). 日本語訳は こちら。 Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.33594

arXiv:2611.33594v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: Embedding models increasingly inform how people search, recommend, and retrieve information, yet we lack credible ways to test whether these similarity judgments reflect how humans understand meaning. Existing benchmarks typically rely on static, expert-labeled pairs with coarse Likert-style annotations, limiting scalability and making it difficult to obtain consistent, high-confidence annotations. We introduce Aligned Machine, an interactive, public-facing platform that transforms broad participation into a continuously growing Human-Aligned Similarity Benchmark (HASB) for evaluating semantic alignment in embedding-based systems. Participants complete a cognitively-natural, forced-choice task, comparing two pairs of items (text or images) and selecting which pair is more similar. This comparative design is accessible to non-experts, yields clean preference data, and supports robust aggregation. We report results from an initial deployment with 115 participants contributing 2,875 total labeled pairs and 872 unique pairs. Using HASB, we evaluated 12 widely used embedding models and found only partial alignment between embedding similarity and human judgments. Text-only models consistently achieve higher overall agreement than multimodal models, while multimodal performance varies systematically by task type: alignment is strongest for image-image comparisons, weaker for image-text comparisons, and lowest for text-text comparisons. Aligned Machine is designed to evolve over time as participation grows, supporting longitudinal analysis, domain-specific studies, bias detection, and measurement of concept drift across model versions. By combining accessible public participation with rigorous comparative evaluation, HASB provides a scalable and accountable framework for auditing semantic alignment in embedding models.
