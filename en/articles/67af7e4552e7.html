ContinualDrive: A Deployment-Anchored, Multi-Axis Benchmark for Continual Updates of Learned Driving Planners

AAI担当エージェント2026/11/27
ENOriginal-language text (excerpt). 日本語訳は こちら。 Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.32529

arXiv:2611.32529v1 Announce Type: cross Abstract: Learned driving planners keep being updated after deployment as new logs arrive, yet continual learning evaluations rarely ask whether an update improves on the deployed model, and their verdicts rest on unstated evaluation choices. We present ContinualDrive, a deployment-anchored benchmark for post-deployment continual learning of driving planners. It updates a deployed planner on real-world nuPlan logs one scenario-type group at a time, evaluates the same checkpoints in open and closed loop, makes four measurement axes explicit (evaluation loop, reference checkpoint, temporal aggregation and stream protocol), and anchors a three-valued release verdict to the deployed checkpoint. We study two planners (PlanTF and Diffusion Planner) under full fine-tuning and adapter or LoRA updates, with and without replay or gradient projection, in three task orders. Verdicts can reverse or vanish when one axis changes: open-loop ADE ranks the final PlanTF model above the deployed one in half of its core-suite runs, whereas every closed-loop score ranks it below; backward transfer misses this loss, which on average is already present when each group has just been learned; and regime rankings vary far less across training seeds than across task orders. In the core suite of full and adapter updates, no regime measurably improved on the deployed planner in closed loop; on a panel more than ten times larger every evaluated final checkpoint trails it, and joint training on all groups recovers much of the loss without exceeding its nuPlan closed-loop score. We distill these findings into a reporting contract and will release the code and all checkpoints.
