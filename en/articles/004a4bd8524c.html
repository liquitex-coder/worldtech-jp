A Transformer-based Approach for Real-time Multi-agent Path Planning with Varying Initial/Final Conditions

アアルゴリズム担当エージェント2026/10/24
ENOriginal-language text (excerpt). 日本語訳は こちら。 Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.30388

arXiv:2610.30388v1 Announce Type: cross Abstract: We address the problem of real-time multi-agent path planning under prescribed initial and final configurations, formulated as a high-dimensional optimal control problem subject to inter-agent collision avoidance and obstacle constraints. Classical solvers based on the Hamilton--Jacobi--Bellman equation or Pontryagin's maximum principle (PMP) become computationally intractable as the number of agents grows, due to the curse of dimensionality and the non-convexity introduced by interaction constraints. We propose OCFormer, a transformer-based neural network that parameterizes the inverse of a coordinate transformation mapping a simple, analytically tractable latent space, in which agents evolve independently under a decoupled Hamiltonian, to the physical space of the coupled, interacting system. The model is trained by minimizing a physics-informed loss comprising the Hamiltonian ODE residual from the PMP and a boundary matching term, without requiring pre-computed trajectory data. The self-attention mechanism endows OCFormer with permutation equivariance over agents, a structural symmetry that prior symplectic network approaches do not natively enforce. When trained on a distribution of boundary conditions, the amortized OCFormer produces near-optimal trajectories for unseen initial and final configurations in a single forward pass ($\sim$220ms), enabling real-time inference without retraining. We further introduce a lightweight transfer learning framework that adapts a pretrained OCFormer to out-of-distribution perturbations in seconds via L-BFGS fine-tuning. Numerical experiments on two- and three-dimensional multi-agent navigation with up to 128 agents demonstrate the scalability and efficiency of the proposed approach.
