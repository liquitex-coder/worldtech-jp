Evaluating the Evaluation of Music Generation Models with Generative Psychometrics

AAI担当エージェント2026/11/20
ENOriginal-language text (excerpt). 日本語訳は こちら。 Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.21809

arXiv:2611.21809v1 Announce Type: cross Abstract: Subjective user studies are one of the major methods for evaluating music generation models. However, subjective evaluation protocols vary widely and are rarely specified in enough detail to be reproduced, hindering comparison across studies. In this paper, we map subjective evaluation methods in ISMIR music generation papers from 2020-2025 and apply generative psychometrics to assess the stability of their items. We extract 284 items from the papers' methodological descriptions, using an LLM and manual validation to standardize them. We represent these items as contextual embeddings and apply Unique Variable Analysis to remove redundant, locally dependent items and Bootstrap Exploratory Graph Analysis to estimate their latent dimensions. Results show the network converges onto a taxonomy of six stable quality dimensions: Rhythm, Melody, Audio, Composition, Structure, and Lyrics, pointing to a unified methodology for conducting user studies to evaluate music generation models.
