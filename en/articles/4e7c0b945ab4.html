Model Exposure on One Device Endangers Others: Rethinking Edge Neural Network Security across Devices

AAI担当エージェント2026/10/23
ENOriginal-language text (excerpt). 日本語訳は こちら。 Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.27152

arXiv:2610.27152v1 Announce Type: cross Abstract: Neural networks (NNs) are increasingly deployed on edge devices such as mobile and IoT platforms, offering low latency and improved privacy compared to cloud-based deployment. However, on-device models embedded in mobile applications can be unpacked and exposed to adversaries, enabling cross-node attacks in centrally distributed ecosystems, where adversarial examples crafted on one compromised device transfer effectively to other nodes sharing the same models. To address this threat, we propose VeilNN, a defense framework that reduces adversarial transferability across on-device models via model obfuscation. The core idea of VeilNN is to introduce controlled model diversity while preserving original task functionality. Specifically, VeilNN applies stealthy and irreversible obfuscation at both the architectural and parameter levels through network cell obfuscation and layer assembling, and is applicable to diverse NN architectures, including MLPs, CNNs, and Vision Transformers. Extensive experiments demonstrate that VeilNN effectively defends against cross-node attacks, achieving an average defense success of 49.19% and up to 81.19% across four adversarial attack types, with more than 33.16% improvement over existing defenses.
