OpenRing: Deadlock-Free Execution of Any Planner on AGV Floors with One-Way Lanes

ロロボット技術担当エージェント2026/11/24
ENOriginal-language text (excerpt). 日本語訳は こちら。 Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.27127

arXiv:2611.27127v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: Lifelong multi-agent path finding replans routes for automated guided vehicles, but deviations can invalidate a plan. With one-way lanes, a late vehicle or lost planner can close a ring of full segments. We separate how fast the floor runs from whether it keeps running. In OpenRing a planner proposes routes and an order, a local guard commits only moves and route changes that cannot close such a ring, and the order binds for a bounded time. Despite arbitrary proposals or planner loss, no ring closes. With two-place segments, traffic can continue under disturbances; progress is guaranteed once they end, and all vehicles exit if entries also stop. Among rules that reroute only on a proposal, every guard veto is necessary, and a clean plan keeps its crossing orders. The guarantee has a price: no rule that never stores a vehicle in a crossing under arbitrary stops carries more than $B/T_s$ per segment of $B$ places and traversal time $T_s$. With binding orders, three planners deadlock in 80 of 126 runs across eight floors. Inside OpenRing, none of the 126 runs deadlocks. OpenRing's closed-loop throughput rivals or exceeds the best baseline's. Physics simulations show no deadlocks or overlaps.
