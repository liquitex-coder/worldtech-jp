When Do Rankings Beat Signs? Gradient Direction Recovery under Even Nuisance

アアルゴリズム担当エージェント2026/11/24
ENOriginal-language text (excerpt). 日本語訳は こちら。 Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.26055

arXiv:2611.26055v2 Announce Type: replace Abstract: Comparison-based optimization minimizes an objective using only ordinal feedback without numerical values. Sign-based baselines evaluate symmetric perturbation pairs in isolation, retaining only within-pair signs and discarding cross-pair orders from the same ranked batch. We ask when complete batch rankings provably beat within-pair signs under an unknown differentiable even nuisance, such as quadratic curvature. We show that reversed cross-pair comparisons cancel shared nuisance offsets without estimating a Hessian, and introduce a geometric framework characterizing three nested direction sets, namely Mirror, Reverse, and Full. Rather than curvature magnitude or condition number, we identify relative shell variation $\tau_a$, the nuisance range on a radius-$a$ sphere divided by $a\|g\|$ for gradient $g\ne 0$ in $d$ dimensions, as a key quantity governing localization. For target angular accuracy $\varepsilon$ in radians, whenever $\tau_a\le c\varepsilon/\sqrt{d}$ for a small absolute constant $c$, one-round Reverse constraints from uniform directions localize every surviving direction within angle $\varepsilon$ of the gradient using $\widetilde{O}(d+\sqrt{d/\varepsilon})$ evaluations, versus $\Theta(d/\varepsilon)$ for signs. This whole-cell guarantee needs no variation bound as input and matches a lower bound up to log factors. Conversely, over well-conditioned quadratics with unbounded curvature relative to gradient scale, rankings offer no minimax evaluation advantage over signs, even across multiple rounds. Experiments certify strict Full-over-Reverse separation, and on two ridge tasks under joint evaluation and round caps, although Full shows no established gain beyond reversed constraints, Reverse consistently attains lower terminal gaps than tuned Mirror and unit-normalized ZO-RankSGD.
