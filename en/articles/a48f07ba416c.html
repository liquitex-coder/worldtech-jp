OCTR2A: Real-Time RGB-to-BEV Trajectory Projection and Forecasting at Urban Intersections

AAI担当エージェント2026/11/21
ENOriginal-language text (excerpt). 日本語訳は こちら。 Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.24352

arXiv:2611.24352v1 Announce Type: cross Abstract: Trajectory forecasting at urban intersections is often studied assuming that reliable bird's-eye-view (BEV) motion histories are available, which in practice requires costly resources such as LiDAR, aerial views, HD maps, or calibrated infrastructure. We address this upstream perception-to-forecasting problem by reconstructing and predicting agents' BEV trajectories only from paired opposite-corner RGB cameras mounted on existing urban infrastructure at variable heights at inference time. We introduce the Opposite-Corner Trajectory Reconstruction and Anticipation Architecture (OCTR2A), a modular pipeline integrating per-view detection with class refinement, geometric cross-camera matching, transformer-based BEV projection, and egocentric GRU forecasting, trained in cascade and mainly supervised with a soft multi-scale spatial-overlap loss. Since no public benchmark provides this sensing configuration, we introduce R3ST++, a Blender-based synthetic benchmark built from SIND trajectories that provides synchronized opposite-corner views, cross-view correspondences, and BEV ground truth, while also serving as a deployment-support simulator for site-specific fine-tuning. Experiments on these datasets show that, with 12 observed and 12 predicted frames at 10\,Hz, OCTR2A achieves projection~-~forecasting ADE/FDE of 0.144/0.140~-~0.154/0.241~meters in real time on a single consumer GPU. Evaluated on ground-truth BEV histories, the forecasting module improves over the strongest state-of-the-art methods on SIND by 66.7\%/41.7\% ADE/FDE. Transfer experiments on TUMTraf-V2X show that R3ST++ pretraining supports adaptation to real-world roadside domains with different sensing configurations.
