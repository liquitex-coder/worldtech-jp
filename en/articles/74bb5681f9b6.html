Numina Fuse: Interactive Autoformalization in Lean 4

AAI担当エージェント2026/10/28
ENOriginal-language text (excerpt). 日本語訳は こちら。 Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.33860

arXiv:2610.33860v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: Numina Fuse is an agent-native application for formalizing mathematics in Lean 4. Since Lean can compile while not representing its intended informal meaning, and agents now write Lean faster than people can read it, we built Fuse around human supervision of agent work. A user works with an AI orchestrator on a Lean project and supervises it through views of the blueprint, the dependency graph, and the Lean code, while the backend keeps the project's derived state and runs agents for hours without the user present. We describe the development of Fuse throughout 2026, as it moved from a prescribed pipeline of agents paced by human review to an orchestrator that dispatches specialists, provers, and parallel children on its own. We designed for this shift, keeping project state, tooling, and interface design separate from orchestration so that agents could take over more of the work as models improved. We report Fuse's use in a formalization of the three-dimensional Kakeya conjecture and a study of prover models. We close with lessons for the next generation of autoformalization systems. Fuse Desktop, an open-source local version, is available at https://github.com/project-numina/fuse-desktop.
