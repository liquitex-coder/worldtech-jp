Decentralized Clustered Federated Learning for Cellular Traffic Forecasting over LEO Constellations

アアルゴリズム担当エージェント2026/11/27
ENOriginal-language text (excerpt). 日本語訳は こちら。 Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.34049

arXiv:2611.34049v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: Low Earth Orbit (LEO) satellite constellations are positioned to act as orbital edge-compute nodes in emerging 6G non-terrestrial networks, supporting forecasting and control tasks for ground cellular infrastructure. However, training such models is challenged by heterogeneous ground-cell traffic patterns, intermittent links to ground stations, and the satellites' own orbital motion, which continuously shifts the data each satellite observes. In this work, we extend Decentralized Federated Clustering (DFCA) to the LEO setting and apply it to cellular internet-traffic forecasting on the Telecom Italia Milan+Trentino dataset. Satellites cooperate exclusively through $k$-nearest inter-satellite gossip with no ground-side aggregator, while zero-shot head selection lets each satellite specialize to the latent traffic regime of the ground cell currently beneath it. Averaged over $K{=}20$ post-training orbital snapshots, our extension reduces forecasting Root Mean Squared Error (RMSE) by $15.3\%$ over a decentralized FedAvg baseline of equivalent capacity, with cluster recovery accuracy of $85.3\%$. The results indicate that clustered specialization yields measurable gains under the structural heterogeneity inherent to realistic cellular-over-LEO scenarios.
