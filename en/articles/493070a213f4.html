FisherDistill: Diffusion Distribution Distillation via Closed-Form Fisher Critics

AAI担当エージェント2026/11/07
ENOriginal-language text (excerpt). 日本語訳は こちら。 Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.07604

arXiv:2611.07604v1 Announce Type: cross Abstract: Recent advances in diffusion distillation have enabled high-quality image generation in only one or a few network function evaluations (NFEs). Despite their efficiency, aggressively distilled generators often retain a residual distribution gap from their multi-step teachers, motivating recent methods to introduce adversarial refinement with a learned discriminator. In this work, we ask whether such refinement necessarily requires a learned adversarial discriminator. We introduce \textbf{FisherDistill}, a simple alternative based on Fisher linear discriminant analysis. Using intermediate representations from the frozen teacher, we estimate the first- and second-order statistics of real and generated samples and derive a closed-form Fisher direction that maximally separates their feature distributions relative to within-class variation. This direction provides a lightweight refinement signal that complements the original distillation objective without requiring an additional adversarial discriminator or its optimization loop. Across multiple distillation frameworks, our approach consistently improves generation quality over their respective distilled baselines and substantially closes the gap to adversarially refined counterparts. Code: https://bhosalems.github.io/FisherDistill/
