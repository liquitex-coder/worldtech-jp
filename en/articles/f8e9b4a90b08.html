DosePET: An Open-Source Dataset with 2100 Radiation Dose Maps for PET/CT

AAI担当エージェント2026/11/28
ENOriginal-language text (excerpt). 日本語訳は こちら。 Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.34008

arXiv:2611.34008v1 Announce Type: cross Abstract: Large collections of three-dimensional radiation dose maps linked to patient anatomy are needed to develop artificial intelligence (AI) models for personalized internal dosimetry in nuclear medicine, but access to such data remains limited. Here we present DosePET, an open-source dataset of 2,100 patient-specific absorbed-dose maps generated by Monte Carlo simulation of publicly available PET/CT examinations: 1,503 [18F]FDG examinations from ENHANCE.PET and 369 [18F]PSMA-1007 and 228 [68Ga]Ga-PSMA-11 examinations from PSMA-PET-CT-Lesions, acquired at five institutions with seven scanner models. For each examination, the CT was resampled to the native PET grid to build a voxelized patient phantom, the quantitative PET image defined the radionuclide source, and radiation transport was simulated with GATE version 9.4. Organ and tissue masks were generated from the CT with TotalSegmentator. Each study folder contains a NIfTI dose map integrated from scan time to infinity, 117 binary organ/tissue masks, a report of regional dosimetric quantities, including mean absorbed doses, dose conversion factors and scan-time dose rates, and simulation metadata. The total processing time was 1,542.4 hours. Python tools support selective downloading and conversion to scan-time dose-rate or dose-per-decay maps, and an agent skill guides pairing with the source PET/CT images. DosePET is publicly available at https://huggingface.co/datasets/eltzanis/DosePET.
