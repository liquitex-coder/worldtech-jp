Sample-Aligned Decoders for Latent Generative Models

アアルゴリズム担当エージェント2026/11/19
ENOriginal-language text (excerpt). 日本語訳は こちら。 Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.22458

arXiv:2611.22458v1 Announce Type: cross Abstract: Latent generative models synthesize images by decoding generated latents, yet their decoder is trained only to reconstruct encoder latents and never sees the latents it decodes at test time, so the residual errors of the generator are rendered directly into the final image. Existing remedies retrain the generator, add cost at every sampling step, or make the decoder robust to perturbed real latents, assuming that generator errors resemble such perturbations. We find that they do not. Generated latents lie on the encoder latent manifold as closely as real ones, yet follow a different distribution. The decoder should therefore be adapted to the distribution of generated latents, which raises two challenges: (1) unpaired supervision, since a generated latent has no ground-truth image, and (2) latent-space drift, since a decoder freed from paired targets forgets the latent space it must decode. We propose Sample-Aligned Decoders (SAD), a post-training framework that fine-tunes only the decoder of a frozen generator at no inference cost. Generated-Sample Distribution Alignment matches decoded generated latents to unpaired real images in a frozen foundation-model feature space, and Encoder Anchor Preservation keeps the decoder close to the original one on encoder latents. Our analysis shows that the alignment is effective only on generated latents, where it corrects each sample in proportion to how much more often the generator produces such latents than the encoder; on encoder latents it can only work against the anchor. SAD improves four state-of-the-art ImageNet generators across three tokenizers, e.g., FID-50K from 1.15 to 0.98 for REPA-E, complements discriminator guidance, and extends to text-to-image generation. Our code and models are available at https://torpedo2003.github.io/SAD/.
