VITAL: A Framework for Validating Agentic AI in Pharmaceutical Drug Development and Clinical Trial Operations

AAI担当エージェント2026/11/20
ENOriginal-language text (excerpt). 日本語訳は こちら。 Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.22973

arXiv:2611.22973v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: Current assurance approaches do not establish whether evidence is sufficient to validate, monitor and govern agentic artificial intelligence (AI) within a pharmaceutical context of use. Plausible outputs may conceal failures in retrieval, reasoning, orchestration or human-control points, where reference standards are unavailable. We developed and operationalised VITAL (Validation by Intended use, Traceability, Autonomy and Lifecycle evidence), a validation framework for agents in clinical development, through landscape analysis, regulatory and standards mapping, expert interviews and multi-stakeholder refinement. VITAL integrates context of use, controls, behavioural validation and lifecycle evidence within a risk-adjusted framework. We applied VITAL across epidemiology and treatment-landscape analysis, site selection, enrolment rescue and trial-design trade-off support to translate autonomy, reversibility, transparency and patient impact into proportionate validation activities, evidence requirements and auditable reconstruction of system behaviour. VITAL may inform evidence requirements and lifecycle accountability for higher-risk pharmaceutical uses; evaluation should assess reproducibility across assessors, organisations and system configurations.
