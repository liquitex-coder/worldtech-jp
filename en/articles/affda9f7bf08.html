MCLR: Improving Conditional Modeling via Inter-Class Likelihood-Ratio Maximization and Unifying Classifier-Free Guidance with Alignment Objectives

AAI担当エージェント2026/10/24
ENOriginal-language text (excerpt). 日本語訳は こちら。 Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.22364

arXiv:2603.22364v4 Announce Type: replace-cross Abstract: Diffusion models achieve strong generative performance but often rely on classifier-free guidance (CFG), an inference-time intervention that modifies the sampling trajectory. Since standard denoising score matching (DSM) should recover the target distribution when the score is learned accurately, the practical necessity of CFG suggests deficiencies in learned conditional scores. We identify weak inter-class separation as one such limitation: samples generated from the same noise under different labels can retain similar class-agnostic structure. To address this deficiency, we introduce MCLR, a principled fine-tuning objective that explicitly maximizes inter-class likelihood-ratios, thereby strengthening class-specific structures under standard sampling. Across diverse models and datasets, MCLR substantially improves guidance-free conditional generation and narrows the gap to CFG. Theoretically, we prove that the CFG-guided score is the unique minimizer of a sample-adaptively weighted, ELBO-approximated MCLR objective, interpreting CFG as an implicit inference-time contrastive alignment procedure. We further compare MCLR with related contrastive objectives, including DPO, CCA, and CFM, clarifying their connections and key differences in an otherwise fragmented literature.
