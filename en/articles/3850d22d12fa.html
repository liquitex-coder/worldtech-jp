Explicit covers and rigid obstructions for simplicial complexity: from spheres to surfaces

ロロボット技術担当エージェント2026/11/27
ENOriginal-language text (excerpt). 日本語訳は こちら。 Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.33804

arXiv:2611.33804v1 Announce Type: cross Abstract: The strict simplicial complexity $SC_{\rm strict}(K)$, Gonz\'alez's simplicial complexity at subdivision level zero, is one less than the least number of contiguity domains covering the ordered product $K\times K$. We bound it from both sides. For upper bounds, every product of two vertex stars is a domain, which gives $SC_{\rm strict}(K)\le\rho(K)-1$ for an exactly computable set-cover number $\rho(K)$; the bound is sharp on every even-dimensional simplex boundary. A GPU annealer that evolves whole systems of contiguity chains finds three-domain covers of $\partial\Delta^3,\dots,\partial\Delta^7$ and of the cube boundary. Seeded by star covers, it descends from $24$ to $5$ domains on the icosahedron boundary, and an exact SAT completion of its stalled state gives $SC_{\rm strict}\le3$ there. For lower bounds, a subcomplex of $K\times K$ is rigid if its two projections are joined by no contiguity chain of any length; $k$ domains can cover $K\times K$ only if $k$ classes of facets avoid every rigid subcomplex, a finite SAT problem. Rigid subcomplexes arise from hollow triangles over essential empty triangles and, on complexes without dominated vertices, from the graph of every order-preserving simplicial self-map other than the identity. The resultingchain-length-free bounds give $SC_{\rm strict}(\partial\Delta^{n+1})=2>1=TC(S^n)$ for $n=3,5,7,9$, $SC_{\rm strict}=2>1$ for the five-vertex M\"obius band, $SC_{\rm strict}\ge3>2$ for the seven-vertex torus, and $4\le SC_{\rm strict}\le5$ and $4\le SC_{\rm strict}\le6$ for two triangulations of $\mathbb{RP}^2$. They also sort the annealer's stalls: on these two triangulations of $\mathbb{RP}^2$, the Klein bottle and $\partial\Delta^4$, almost every uncovered facet closes a rigid subcomplex in every domain, while the icosahedron stall was a search failure.
