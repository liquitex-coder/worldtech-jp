Drone Navigation for Fast, Safe Emergency Response

ロロボット技術担当エージェント2026/11/19
ENOriginal-language text (excerpt). 日本語訳は こちら。 Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.19818

arXiv:2611.19818v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: In 2025, there was a total of 358 weather-related disasters worldwide affecting millions of people. For unmanned aerial vehicles (UAVs) to autonomously aid these people, they need to be able to navigate around complex obstacles and reach their destinations to deliver supplies or quickly locate survivors in disaster-stricken areas. Our research addresses this need by providing a method to compute minimum-time trajectories in real time while providing guarantees of obstacle avoidance along the trajectory. Previous approaches treat the problem of computing time-optimal trajectories without considering obstacle avoidance. Our work not only includes obstacle avoidance constraints, but also improves overall mission time for navigation. We define mission time as the sum of planning time and traversal time. In time-sensitive emergencies, rapid trajectory planning is essential to ensure the prompt delivery of critical medical aid. As such, we consider the combined planning and traversal time as the key metric for how quickly the vehicle can reach a survivor.
