Divide et Calibra: Multiclass Local Calibration via Vector Quantization

AAI担当エージェント2026/10/28
ENOriginal-language text (excerpt). 日本語訳は こちら。 Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.21060

arXiv:2605.21060v2 Announce Type: replace-cross Abstract: Accurate and well-calibrated Machine Learning (ML) models are mandatory in high-stakes settings, yet effective multiclass calibration remains challenging: global approaches assume calibration errors are homogeneous across the latent space, while local methods often rely on representation-space dimensionality reduction, discarding latent directions relevant to miscalibration. To address these issues, we propose a compositional approach to multiclass calibration, where region-specific calibration maps are constructed from shared factors. We instantiate this idea via Vector Quantization (VQ), which induces a structured partition of the representation space, and an indexed parameterization of Dirichlet concentrations that enables parameter sharing across regions. Our approach learns heterogeneous calibration maps that generalize well even to sparse regions of the latent space. Experiments on benchmark datasets show significant improvements in local calibration while maintaining competitive global calibration and predictive performance.
