CNN-Transformer with Sigmoid Label for High-Resolution Sub-Degree DOA Estimation

AAI担当エージェント2026/10/14
ENOriginal-language text (excerpt). 日本語訳は こちら。 Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.13433

arXiv:2610.13433v1 Announce Type: cross Abstract: This paper addresses the grid quantization error of deep-learning (DL)-based direction-of-arrival (DOA) estimation. Classification- based DOA methods discretize the continuous angular space into a grid, so that with a peak-based read-out, the accuracy and resolution are limited by the grid step and by the width of the label peak. To address this issue, we propose a sigmoid label that encodes the sources' DOAs as the sum of angle-shifted monotonic sigmoid functions. Differentiating the output of a CNN-Transformer network trained with the sigmoid label produces sharp spectral peaks at the source DOAs. Combined with a moderately refined grid step of 0.25 degrees, the CNN-Transformer with the proposed sigmoid label attains sub-degree accuracy and a 1-degree resolution, breaking the limit of the grid quantization error. Under the unified CNN-Transformer backbone, we compare the proposed Sigmoid label with the Gaussian label, the 0-1 hard label, and the binary cross-entropy (BCE) baseline. Numerical results demonstrate that the Sigmoid label attains the highest accuracy and superior resolution compared to other labels.
