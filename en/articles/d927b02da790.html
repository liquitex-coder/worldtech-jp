A Variational Latent Equilibrium for Learning in Neuronal Circuits

AAI担当エージェント2026/11/17
ENOriginal-language text (excerpt). 日本語訳は こちら。 Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.09600

arXiv:2603.09600v3 Announce Type: replace-cross Abstract: Brains remain unrivaled in their ability to recognize and generate complex spatiotemporal patterns. While AI is able to reproduce some of these capabilities, deep learning algorithms remain largely at odds with our current understanding of brain circuitry and dynamics. This is prominently the case for backpropagation through time (BPTT), the go-to algorithm for learning complex temporal dependencies. In this work we propose a general formalism to approximate BPTT in a controlled, biologically plausible manner. Our approach builds on, unifies and extends several previous approaches to local, time-continuous, phase-free spatiotemporal credit assignment based on principles of energy conservation and extremal action. Our starting point is a prospective energy function of neuronal states, from which we calculate real-time error dynamics for time-continuous neuronal networks. With this, as a first contribution we provide a simple and straightforward derivation of the adjoint method dynamical equations, the time-continuous equivalent to BPTT. Importantly, additional approximations are necessary to avoid unphysical temporal non-localities. One such approximation was recently proposed in Generalized Latent Equilibrium (GLE), but enforcing temporal locality of the backpropagating dynamics induces an error in the gain that becomes problematic when processing complex signals. Thus, our second contribution is to design a learning rule for the backward weights to find a fully local (in space and time) set of equations for neuron and synapse dynamics that corrects for this gain difference. With this inclusion of backward weight learning, our theory provides a rigorous framework for spatiotemporal deep learning in the brain, while simultaneously advancing a blueprint for physical circuits capable of carrying out these computations.
