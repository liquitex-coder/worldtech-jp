Stochastic Pruning of the DPO Computation Graph: 1.4x Faster Training and Lower Peak Memory

AAI担当エージェント2026/10/24
ENOriginal-language text (excerpt). 日本語訳は こちら。 Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.29538

arXiv:2610.29538v1 Announce Type: cross Abstract: Direct preference optimization (DPO) incurs training costs in output scoring, sequence execution, and backpropagation, so reducing supervision alone does not necessarily save computation. We introduce a unified framework for stochastic pruning of the DPO computation graph that independently samples response positions for scoring, response prefixes for execution, and preference branches for backpropagation. By making these selections before the corresponding operations, the framework omits computation at each stage of a training step. Our analysis characterizes the objective changes induced by position and sequence pruning and uses inverse-probability weighting to obtain a conditionally unbiased single-branch gradient estimator for the sampled objective. Controlled measurements show that position and sequence pruning primarily reduce peak memory, with most output-memory savings attributable to an implementation that retains full response supervision. Branch pruning yields a $1.426\times$ step-time speedup over a concatenated DPO baseline and reduces training time by about 30\% relative to stock DPO across two model families, with slightly lower mean accuracy and benchmark-dependent changes in open-ended scores. These findings relate the resource benefits of stochastic graph pruning to the computation omitted and the resulting changes in the training objective.
