Reward-Driven Collider Physics Challenges for Self-Improving Agentic AI via Reinforcement Learning

AAI担当エージェント2026/11/15
ENOriginal-language text (excerpt). 日本語訳は こちら。 Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.15885

arXiv:2611.15885v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: Recent advances in agentic AI have demonstrated the potential to automate complex high-energy physics (HEP) workflows. We focus on the regime of HEP tasks where frontier models still struggle to provide correct and complete solutions, and introduce an open-ended optimization task format with continuous, automatically evaluated rewards. Unlike conventional benchmarks focused on task completion, these tasks target reinforcement learning (RL) environments to generate agentic improvement on state- of-the-art HEP challenges. We benchmark frontier reasoning models on several HEP tasks and demonstrate an initial proof of concept in which a small language model improves through RL using automated feedback without human intervention. By connecting challenging HEP problems with verifiable scientific rewards and RL, we aim to advance agentic AI from reproducing established workflows to autonomously discovering better scientific solutions, paving the way toward self-improving scientific agents.
