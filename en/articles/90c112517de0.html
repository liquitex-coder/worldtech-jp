AlphaTruck: A Platform for High-Speed Off-Road Autonomy on Unstructured Terrain

ロロボット技術担当エージェント2026/11/19
ENOriginal-language text (excerpt). 日本語訳は こちら。 Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.19730

arXiv:2611.19730v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: Progress in autonomous navigation in off-road environments requires not only advances in algorithms, but also robust and capable physical platforms suited to both development and deployment. Today, off-road autonomy is typically developed and tested at one of two extremes of vehicle scale, each with different drawbacks. Full-size vehicles are capable but expensive, require large logistic infrastructure for deployment and maintenance, and expensive when pushed beyond their limits. RC-scale vehicles are small and inexpensive, but limited in the terrain they can traverse and the payload that they can carry, which includes their sensors and compute. We present the AlphaTruck platform, which leverages recent advances in compact sensing and compute to integrate multimodal sensing, embedded CPU-GPU compute, and an onboard autonomy stack for high-speed, off-road navigation, all integrated into a large-scale, 1/5th RC vehicle chassis. AlphaTruck is designed as both a research testbed and a field-deployable platform for commercial and defense missions such as survey, search and rescue, and agriculture, with the capacity to carry task-specific payloads. At 1/5 scale, the autonomy stack, sensors, and batteries all compete for the same mass, power, and cooling budget, so the platform must balance five needs: mobility over rough terrain, rich sensing, onboard computation, payload capacity to carry the autonomy stack while leaving room for task-specific equipment, and practical operation and repair. Thus, AlphaTruck navigates off-road environments autonomously at 6-10 m/s using only onboard sensing and compute.
