CARROT: Competitive Adaptive Graph Clustering via Semi-Relaxed Optimal Transport

AAI担当エージェント2026/11/28
ENOriginal-language text (excerpt). 日本語訳は こちら。 Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.34012

arXiv:2611.34012v1 Announce Type: cross Abstract: Deep graph clustering (DGC) has emerged as a prominent paradigm for unsupervised attributed graph clustering. However, existing methods often struggle with two practical challenges encountered in real-world applications: the number of clusters $K$ is unknown a priori, and cluster sizes are frequently highly imbalanced. Methods relying on a pre-specified $K$ may fail to recover the underlying clustering structure when $K$ is misspecified, while many approaches implicitly favor balanced partitions, hindering the discovery of minority clusters. We propose CARROT, a Competitive Adaptive gRaph clustering framework via semi-Relaxed Optimal Transport, which introduces semi-relaxed optimal transport into deep graph clustering to jointly address both challenges. CARROT first pre-trains node embeddings to establish a meaningful embedding space, and then jointly optimizes embeddings and cluster assignments through an Adaptive Clustering Mechanism. The mechanism combines semi-relaxed optimal transport to adapt cluster capacities to the underlying data, a competition loss to concentrate mass onto well-supported cluster prototypes, thereby starving degenerate ones, and hard pruning to progressively eliminate them. Extensive experiments on five public benchmarks demonstrate that CARROT achieves state-of-the-art NMI and ARI, accurately recovers the number of clusters without supervision, and faithfully preserves size-imbalanced community structures.
