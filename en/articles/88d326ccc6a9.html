MetaRefine: Score Refinement with Multi-Level Features for Image Assessment

AAI担当エージェント2026/11/01
ENOriginal-language text (excerpt). 日本語訳は こちら。 Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.40487

arXiv:2610.40487v1 Announce Type: cross Abstract: An image's original dimensions, aspect ratio, and composition provide important cues that can affect its technical and aesthetic quality. Image assessment methods use resizing, cropping, padding, or patch sampling to accommodate different input sizes. However, these operations can alter or obscure original geometric and compositional cues. Existing assessment models also encode rich multi-level visual evidence that can be further extracted and exploited to improve existing models. This motivates us to combine visual evidence with original-image geometry for score refinement without changing the original scoring pipelines. In this paper, we introduce MetaRefine, a metadata-aware score refinement architecture whose lightweight residual heads predict score corrections from geometry, multi-level visual features, and the frozen model's original score. Across 100 configurations on 21 datasets, MetaRefine improves PLCC in 88 configurations and SRCC in 85, with average relative gains of 7.55% and 7.10%, respectively. It reduces MAE in 90 configurations and RMSE in 89, with average relative reductions of 23.27% and 21.93%, respectively. The median parameter overhead is only 1.11%. MetaRefine can further improve state-of-the-art (SOTA) assessment models on multiple benchmarks.
