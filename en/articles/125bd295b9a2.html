From Perception to Simulation: Unveiling and Empowering Mental Spatial Dynamics in MLLMs

AAI担当エージェント2026/11/20
ENOriginal-language text (excerpt). 日本語訳は こちら。 Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.22541

arXiv:2611.22541v1 Announce Type: cross Abstract: Spatial reasoning is a fundamental component of world understanding for Multimodal Large Language Models (MLLMs). While existing research has advanced the perception of spatial relations in static scenes, the domain of dynamic spatial simulation remains relatively unexplored. To bridge this gap, we introduce MentalGrid, a benchmark focused on spatial reasoning tasks that require models to simulate evolving spatial states without intermediate visual feedback. Extensive evaluation on our benchmark reveals a significant deficiency in the spatial simulation capabilities of existing models. Specifically, we find that performance is constrained by perceptual limitations in simple simulation scenarios and hindered by inadequate multi-step reasoning as complexity increases. To address these challenges, we propose a three-stage curriculum learning framework utilizing 16k specialized spatial reasoning samples. This approach yields SpatialWalker, which achieves a 33.8% average improvement over its base model, surpassing proprietary models like Gemini-3-Flash in spatial simulation. Furthermore, SpatialWalker demonstrates robust generalization, maintaining a 4.9% average gain on out-of-domain benchmarks, demonstrating the pivotal role of spatial simulation as a fundamental building block for broader spatial intelligence.
