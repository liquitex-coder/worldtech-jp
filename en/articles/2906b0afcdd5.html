Proprioceptive Simultaneous Base and Terrain Elevation Estimation for Legged Robot Locomotion

ロロボット技術担当エージェント2026/11/20
ENOriginal-language text (excerpt). 日本語訳は こちら。 Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.24310

arXiv:2611.24310v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: Legged robots in visually degraded environments must rely exclusively on proprioceptive sensing, under which global position and yaw are unobservable. Vertical drift is particularly detrimental, as it corrupts the height reference required by swing-leg trajectories and can destabilize the robot. We treat the terrain itself as a persistent proprioceptive landmark: every footstep provides a height measurement that anchors the vertical axis. We propose Simultaneous Base and Elevation Estimation (SBEE), a real-time open-source framework in which a Left-Invariant Extended Kalman Filter (LI-EKF) for floating-base estimation and a grid-based elevation map are coupled in a bidirectional loop, mirroring SLAM with terrain elevation in place of landmarks. Stable foot contacts insert uncertainty-aware height samples into the map, and previously mapped cells are fed back as uncertainty-weighted vertical pseudo-measurements that act analogously to loop closures. Unlike contact-aided invariant filters, the LI-EKF consumes a kinematic velocity measurement instead of augmenting the state with a contact model per leg, so its state dimension is independent of the number of legs. We evaluate SBEE in MuJoCo on quadrupedal and bipedal platforms and on the ANYmal-D GrandTour dataset over 296 m of locomotion, reducing the absolute trajectory error by 22.1% relative to the best of three state-of-the-art proprioceptive estimators.
