GuardianAgentFlow: Multi-Agent Coordination for Web Privacy Protection

AAI担当エージェント2026/11/15
ENOriginal-language text (excerpt). 日本語訳は こちら。 Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.15097

arXiv:2611.15097v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: Web agents need to use private context without unnecessarily disclosing it. Effective protection must distinguish between requests that may be sent after sanitization and those that must be withheld, while preserving the information needed for authorized tasks. We present GuardianAgentFlow, a multi-agent framework that mediates outgoing Web requests through an explicit release contract. A dedicated restriction check grounds permission in the user's instruction; a shared protection scope aligns rewriting with disclosure checking; and candidate-grounded feedback guides revision before transmission. Experiments on Web requests with human-reviewed intent labels show improved request-contract success, while controlled comparisons and role ablations identify the contributions of restriction handling, policy evidence, and candidate checking. Evaluations of action proposals from PrivacyLens and AgentDAM demonstrate complementary protection when mediation is composed with privacy-aware agents. Experiments with smaller mediation models show that the coordination benefit persists with reduced model capacity. Dedicated-server measurements show lower mediation latency than full-policy reasoning, while real-site browser tests quantify integration overhead.
