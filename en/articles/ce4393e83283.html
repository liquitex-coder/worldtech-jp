LLM-Based Threat Detection and Prevention Framework for IoT Ecosystems

アアルゴリズム担当エージェント2026/11/21
ENOriginal-language text (excerpt). 日本語訳は こちら。 Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.00240

arXiv:2505.00240v3 Announce Type: replace-cross Abstract: The rapid expansion of the Internet of Things (IoT) has introduced complex and evolving cybersecurity challenges. This paper presents a Large Language Model (LLM)-based framework for intelligent threat detection and prevention in IoT networks. The proposed architecture integrates two core components: a Detection Component that leverages fine-tuned models trained on IoT-specific datasets (IoT-23 and TON_IoT) to identify anomalous network behaviours, and a Prevention Component that generates adaptive response strategies to mitigate detected threats. The framework incorporates prompt management, local adaptation, and global refinement modules to support context-aware decision-making and continuous model-guided adaptation. Experimental validation in a Docker-based edge-cloud environment shows that BERT Small achieves 99.75% accuracy, F1-score, precision, and recall for threat detection, while the Prevention LLM achieves 98.3% accuracy in selecting suitable mitigation actions. These results highlight the potential of LLM-driven architectures to provide explainable and adaptive security solutions for next-generation IoT ecosystems.
