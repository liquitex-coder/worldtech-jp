Geometry-Aware Anisotropic Boundary Correction for Aerodynamic Simulation

AAI担当エージェント2026/06/11
ENOriginal-language text (excerpt). 日本語訳は こちら。 Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.09963

arXiv:2606.09963v1 Announce Type: cross Abstract: Aerodynamic simulation is a key component of engineering shape design, where core quantities such as the surface pressure coefficient strongly depend on flow dynamics near solid boundaries. Neural operators provide an efficient alternative to expensive Computational Fluid Dynamics (CFD) solvers. However, conventional methods treat the boundary region isotropically, failing to account for the distinct physical behaviors along the boundaries. In reality, the aerodynamic process exhibits anisotropy: along the tangential direction, flow propagates along the wall; along the normal direction, physical quantities are constrained by the wall. To explicitly model the distinct physical behaviors, we propose GeoABC, a geometry-conditioned anisotropic boundary correction framework. GeoABC leverages the boundary geometries to introduce direction-aware boundary correction into the intermediate representations of neural operators, transforming boundary geometry from static input features into a structural prior that modulates physical prediction. On 2D airfoil and 3D car tasks, GeoABC consistently adapts to multiple neural operator backbones, reducing near-boundary relative $L_2$ error by $\sim$38\% on average, narrowing the structural near-wall gap shared by mainstream neural operators, and advancing neural operators toward high-fidelity aerodynamic simulation.
