DecisionReplay: A Corpus and Offline Simulator for Ensembling Decision Models

AAI担当エージェント2026/11/28
ENOriginal-language text (excerpt). 日本語訳は こちら。 Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.34627

arXiv:2611.34627v1 Announce Type: cross Abstract: Comparing ensembles, routers and cascades requires the output of every model on every item, and its cost. Stored collections built for routing keep no per-option probabilities. We provide a dataset on which ensemble methods, routers and cascades are simulated quickly, without new model calls. DecisionReplay holds the probability vectors of 17 commercial decision models, endpoints that return a probability for every answer option, on 35 benchmarks, with the token counts, billed cost and measured latency of every call, all released. A deterministic simulator replays any policy that selects, combines or cascades them. One well-chosen model per benchmark is hard to beat: pooling all models loses 2.7 accuracy points to it, and stacking gains 1.0 points at 3.4 times its cost. The models are wrong together: two that are both wrong choose the same wrong option in 72% of cases. The best router matches that model at 64% of its cost, and certified early exit cuts the cost of a stacked ensemble by 43% without changing an answer. DecisionReplay makes it cheap and fast to test whether a new method changes these results.
