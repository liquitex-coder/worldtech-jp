MRCT: An Object-Centric Two-Layer Control Architecture for Heavy-Payload Multi-Robot Transportation

ロロボット技術担当エージェント2026/11/26
ENOriginal-language text (excerpt). 日本語訳は こちら。 Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.32502

arXiv:2611.32502v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: Cooperative transportation of heavy payloads by heterogeneous mobile manipulators requires coordinated payload motion, force distribution, and compliant physical interaction. This paper presents MRCT, an object-centric two-layer control architecture for multi-robot transportation. At the object level, a model predictive controller (MPC) generates a coordinated contact-force reference for the payload. At the robot level, whole- body impedance control (WBIC) modules on individual robots realise the reference through a confidence-based blending of velocity-tracking and impedance torques. The blending mechanism emphasises tracking when the joint-velocity error is small and increases compliance when tracking degrades because of abrupt reference changes or physical disturbances. Simulation and hardware experiments with a heterogeneous team comprising a single-arm and a dual-arm mobile manipulator transporting a near-rated table payload demonstrate accurate object tracking, regulated contact forces, and stable payload orientation. Parameter sensitivity studies, obstacle-avoidance transportation, and step-load disturbance experiments further demonstrate the effectiveness of MRCT for reliable cooperative transportation under the evaluated conditions
