How to Turn an Attention Neural Network into an Interpretable Hard-Logic Predicate Circuit: Truth-Table Predicate Extraction and State-Machine Merging under Ship-of-Theseus Replacement

アアルゴリズム担当エージェント2026/11/20
ENOriginal-language text (excerpt). 日本語訳は こちら。 Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.24381

arXiv:2611.24381v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: We convert a trained attention network as a whole, attention included, into a hard-logic predicate circuit that runs in its place. Every unit of the circuit is a 0/1 predicate on tokens, and every map between predicates is a minimised truth table. Short tables, such as the product of a query code and a key code, are minimised exactly, and wide feature-to-feature transitions become implication rules by noise-tolerant minimisation. Blocks are replaced one at a time from the output down, in the manner of the Ship of Theseus, and after each replacement the remaining soft part re-adapts to the frozen circuit. Attention selection takes two forms, a row-wise comparison of integer scores (order selection) and a learned rule (rule selection). Both are fitted on the ordinary training set alone, and only on the examples that the teacher classifies correctly, so that the circuit imitates the teacher where the teacher is right. On TweetEval-emotion, the complete circuit of DistilGPT2 reaches a test gold accuracy of 66.5 $\pm$ 1.3% with order selection and 57.4 $\pm$ 2.5% with rule selection over three seeds, against 74.7% for the teacher and 64.0% for a bag of word pieces fitted on the same tweets, and that of BERT-base reaches 70.0 $\pm$ 0.5% with order selection and 63.7 $\pm$ 1.2% with rule selection against 77.8%. The circuit needs no multiplier and no weight traffic, and its exported hardware description reproduces it bit for bit. By a synthesis-based estimate it classifies one tweet in about 1-2 $\mu$s (DistilGPT2) and 3-5 $\mu$s (BERT) on an FPGA card and in 5-9 $\mu$s on a palm-sized board whose module draws 7.5-15 W, two to three orders of magnitude faster than the network on a GPU at batch 1 (measured, 0.9-2.6 ms for DistilGPT2 including an optimised CUDA-graph baseline), at a cost of 8.5 (DistilGPT2) and 8.6 (BERT) points of gold accuracy for the synthesised seed-0 circuits.
