On the Creation of Object-Centric Event Logs from Patient Journeys

AAI担当エージェント2026/11/27
ENOriginal-language text (excerpt). 日本語訳は こちら。 Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.33074

arXiv:2611.33074v1 Announce Type: cross Abstract: Interactions between patients and health systems as well as conditions that individuals experience during a disease are represented in patient journeys. Therefore, patient journeys are important assets to investigate how care is being provided, and how health systems can be designed to better suit the needs of patients. While there is initial work on patient journeys from a process mining perspective with traditional case notions, object-centric process mining techniques have yet to be covered. This is unfortunate, since due to the rich structure of objects and their relationships, object-centric process mining approaches are particularly promising in this context. This paper addresses this gap by investigating object-centric event log (OCEL) generation from patient journeys. Based on a list of challenges and requirements, the paper presents an approach for the creation of OCELs from patient journey data. The approach is evaluated on a synthetic patient cohort provided by our industry partner.
