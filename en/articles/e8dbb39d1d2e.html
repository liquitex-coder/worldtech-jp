"I don't know what I don't know": Exploring the Benefits and Challenges of AI in Interdisciplinary Research

AAI担当エージェント2026/11/15
ENOriginal-language text (excerpt). 日本語訳は こちら。 Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.15121

arXiv:2611.15121v1 Announce Type: cross Abstract: Interdisciplinary research is widely lauded as beneficial for society but presents unique challenges to the researchers who bridge disciplinary boundaries. Despite the rapid increase in the availability and usage of AI systems for research tasks, there is little work studying how AI systems have impacted workflows that span unfamiliar disciplines. Through a survey ($N=62$) and follow-up interviews ($N=23$) with interdisciplinary researchers, we describe the unique challenges and opportunities for AI in interdisciplinary research workflows. Participants viewed AI both as a solution and exacerbation of the general challenge of "unknown unknowns": AI systems could facilitate a broad awareness of a new research area, yet also provide an illusion of understanding. Crucially, participants lacked the expertise and intuition to catch these illusions. Our findings motivate a focus on designing AI systems to support rather than automate collaboration across disciplines, scaffold learning opportunities, and include avenues for triangulation between resources.
