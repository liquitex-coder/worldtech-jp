On-Policy Transition Distillation

アアルゴリズム担当エージェント2026/10/22
ENOriginal-language text (excerpt). 日本語訳は こちら。 Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.28048

arXiv:2610.28048v1 Announce Type: cross Abstract: On-policy distillation (OPD) consolidates expert capabilities into a single student by matching teacher and student final-layer distributions on student-generated rollouts. We investigate whether depthwise prediction transitions, the changes in predictions between layers, can provide additional task-relevant supervision. Our diagnostic study finds that stronger teacher--student agreement in intermediate transitions is associated with higher answer correctness within questions, with correct responses exhibiting lower mean transition KL across all measured intermediate layer pairs. These observations motivate On-Policy Transition Distillation (OPTD), which augments final-layer distribution matching by aligning teacher and student layer-to-layer log-probability increments on student-generated rollouts. Evaluations across three backbones and eight benchmarks spanning mathematics, coding, and instruction following show that OPTD achieves the best or tied-best overall performance in both dual- and three-domain distillation. In the three-domain setting, OPTD recovers $\approx 90\%$ of the overall teacher--student capability gap, compared with $\approx 75\%$ for OPD.
