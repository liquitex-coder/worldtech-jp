Embodied Interaction Policy: A Unified Model for Full-Duplex Human-Robot Interaction

ロロボット技術担当エージェント2026/11/24
ENOriginal-language text (excerpt). 日本語訳は こちら。 Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.27327

arXiv:2611.27327v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: Real-time human-robot social interaction requires continuous coordination between speech and physical behavior. Existing systems often separate interaction agents from motion generation, complicating coordination as conversational states change. We propose \textbf{Embodied Interaction Policy (EIP)}, a unified streaming framework that couples an Audio-Interaction expert with a motion expert through layer-wise joint attention. This allows motion generation to directly access the evolving interaction context. A chunk-wise causal mask prevents access to future context, while a vocal token encodes speaking state and the semantic emotion of the response. We construct \textbf{Embodied Interaction Dataset (EI-Data)} from co-speech and dyadic interaction data and train our model in two stages to learn state-adaptive and emotion-conditioned behavior. Combined with the frozen interaction expert, EIP supports continuous robot motion during proactive responses and interruption-aware turn-taking. Quantitative evaluations demonstrate improved motion quality over representative gesture-generation baselines. Compared with a representative cascaded system, EIP achieves higher interaction success and reduces warm-start latency by 64\%. Physical deployment and user studies further demonstrate improved motion naturalness and interaction comfort.
