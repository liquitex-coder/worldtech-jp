SMSD: A Semantic Misinformation Spreader Detection Approach

アアルゴリズム担当エージェント2026/11/24
ENOriginal-language text (excerpt). 日本語訳は こちら。 Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.27661

arXiv:2611.27661v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: Misinformation spreaders on social media significantly amplify false narratives, making early detection critical to mitigate their spread. Existing approaches often overlook evolving user behavior, introduce semantic noise by ignoring user stance, and fail to handle newly emerging users effectively. To address these limitations, we propose a \underline{S}emantic \underline{M}isinformation \underline{S}preader \underline{D}etection approach (SMSD) that models misinformation propagation as a temporal, user-centric, and behavior-driven learning problem. SMSD adopts a multimodal architecture that incorporates three categories of features: user-level, network-level, and conversation propagation. A stance-aware labeling strategy is also introduced to differentiate supportive, opposing, questioning, and neutral users towards misinformation claims. Additionally, a monthly quantile analysis is introduced to simulate realistic, evolving user participation. Initial experimental results on the FibVid COVID-19 benchmark dataset demonstrate that SMSD improves recall by 45.0\%, F1 by 49.5\%, and MCC by 74.6\% under stance-aware labeling and by 17.7\%, 7.4\%, and 11.4\%, respectively, under threshold-based labeling. It consistently outperforms all baselines in both settings.
