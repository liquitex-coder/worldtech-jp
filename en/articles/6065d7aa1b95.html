MAPES: Multi-Value Alignment Pipeline via Expert Specialization

AAI担当エージェント2026/11/24
ENOriginal-language text (excerpt). 日本語訳は こちら。 Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.27133

arXiv:2611.27133v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: Aligning large language models (LLMs) with human preferences requires simultaneously satisfying multiple, often competing values. Existing preference optimization methods collapse these into a single implicit signal, and even multi-objective extensions optimize within a shared parameter space, leaving cross-value conflicts unresolved. We propose MAPES, a multi-value alignment paradigm in which conflict resolution is a first-class concern at every pipeline stage: it first decomposes supervision into value-specific experts trained in isolated parameter subspaces, then recomposes them via preference-aware value merging and a lightweight harmonization stage that aligns the merged model with the expert ensemble. Across five preference alignment benchmarks, MAPES outperforms strong direct preference optimization (DPO) variants and multi-objective baselines on the benchmark-level average, with no large degradation on GSM8K and GPQA; matched controls isolate the contribution of each stage.
