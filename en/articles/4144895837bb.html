UniEvo-VL: An On-policy Self-Distillation Training Recipe for Multimodal Model Self-improvement

AAI担当エージェント2026/11/24
ENOriginal-language text (excerpt). 日本語訳は こちら。 Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.38721

arXiv:2609.38721v2 Announce Type: replace Abstract: Modern multimodal models bring generation and understanding into a single unified system, which enables them to provide and learn from their own feedback. Motivated by this unified capacity, we introduce UniEvo-VL, a self-evolving framework for multimodal models to learn from this constructive self-correction feedback during test-time compute. Instead of relying on a separately trained, stronger generator as teacher, we use critiques of the generator's own outputs, produced by a fixed vision-language critic, as privileged information. The generator and its EMA copy act as student and teacher under different contexts. The student only sees the vanilla question, while the teacher conditions on the privileged critique. Then training minimizes the per-state divergence between their denoising diffusion distributions over the student's own sampling trajectories. Experiments demonstrate that UniEvo-VL improves the image generation capabilities of multimodal models, while maintaining their sensitivity to additional reflection information. With Qwen-Image-2512, direct-generation performance improves from 0.747 to 0.808 on GenEval using Qwen feedback and from 32.97 to 35.53 on GenEval2 Soft-TIFA using the external Luna critic. Moreover, attempts with more powerful external critics (e.g., GPT5.6-Luna) show that multimodal models with strong judge capabilities can anticipate a higher self-evolving ceiling. Last but not least, mixed text-rendering outcomes show that our self-improvements may not be uniform across different tasks. Our study aims to shed light on the current hot recursive self-improvement research line to enhance the user experience when using multimodal models without paired ground-truth images, human annotation, or task-specific reward functions.
