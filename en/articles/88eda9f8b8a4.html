FedE2E: One-Shot Federated Learning for End-to-End Autonomous Driving with Closed-Loop Evaluation

AAI担当エージェント2026/11/21
ENOriginal-language text (excerpt). 日本語訳は こちら。 Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.24254

arXiv:2611.24254v2 Announce Type: new Abstract: Federated learning enables collaborative model training without sharing datasets. However, existing federated learning methods for autonomous driving typically require multi-round communication and lack closed-loop evaluation, while one-shot methods mainly focus on perception tasks. To address these limitations, we propose \method, a one-shot federated learning method for end-to-end autonomous driving. It comprises multi-client calibration, anchor-based feature alignment, and token-wise consensus distillation to distill knowledge from heterogeneous models. Closed-loop evaluation on Bench2Drive demonstrates the effectiveness of \method in both one-shot and multi-round federated learning. In the one-shot setting, it achieves a Driving Score of 55.85, which is 4.07 points higher than prior work. In the multi-round setting, it reaches 60.35 after five communication rounds, the best performance among all compared methods under the same number of communication rounds.
