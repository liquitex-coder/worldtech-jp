ChipOmni: A General-Purpose AI Agent for Electronic Design Automation

AAI担当エージェント2026/11/12
ENOriginal-language text (excerpt). 日本語訳は こちら。 Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.13020

arXiv:2611.13020v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: Electronic design automation (EDA) workflows remain highly fragmented, requiring engineers to coordinate heterogeneous representations, specialised software tools, and iterative verification across multiple design stages. Although large language models have brought more general reasoning and code-generation capabilities to EDA, many existing approaches remain focused on isolated tasks and struggle to plan, verify, and repair designs autonomously within executable engineering environments. Here we introduce ChipOmni, a knowledge-enhanced, context-evolving AI agent framework for EDA. ChipOmni integrates multimodal understanding, dynamic planning, multi-agent collaboration, and verification-driven tool interaction into a unified autonomous system. By continually incorporating feedback from external engineering tools, the framework maintains an evolving task context, integrates domain knowledge, and iteratively refines intermediate designs. Across benchmarks for analog circuit, RTL, testbench, and formal-assertion generation, ChipOmni improves multiple task-specific measures over the evaluated baselines and solves all 30 AnalogCoder-Pro tasks. In a long-horizon case study, ChipOmni advances a natural-language RISC-V specification through three design levels to a micro-SoC GDSII layout that passes physical sign-off, without human intervention after initialization. Together, these results show that ChipOmni can coordinate verification-driven design across heterogeneous tasks and sustain long-horizon engineering workflows, advancing a shift from fragmented, tool-centred processes towards agent-centred, closed-loop systems.
