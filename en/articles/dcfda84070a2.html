A Unified Framework for Intelligent Cyber Attack Detection and Alert Correlation

アアルゴリズム担当エージェント2026/11/24
ENOriginal-language text (excerpt). 日本語訳は こちら。 Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.26824

arXiv:2611.26824v1 Announce Type: cross Abstract: A Security Operations Center (SOC) plays a critical role in detecting, correlating, and responding to modern cyber threats. However, SOCs continue to face challenges related to alert overload, fragmented detection capabilities, and limited collaboration between analysts and automated systems. These issues often result in delayed incident response, analyst fatigue, and an increased risk of overlooking sophisticated, multi-stage attacks that unfold gradually across different layers of the network. To address these challenges, this paper proposes a unified framework for network traffic classification and alert correlation through the use of signature rules, machine learning, and sequential pattern mining algorithms. The solution was evaluated in a controlled testbed environment emulating realistic, multi-stage attack scenarios, including malware exploitation, lateral movement, reconnaissance, and data exfiltration. The obtained results demonstrate the effectiveness of the proposed framework in detecting and correlating complex attack behaviors, while providing analysts with actionable insights in line with a human-in-the-loop approach to SOC operations.
