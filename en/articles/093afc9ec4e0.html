AI-Based Multi-View Echocardiographic Image Synthesis using Dynamic Shape Control

AAI担当エージェント2026/11/17
ENOriginal-language text (excerpt). 日本語訳は こちら。 Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.18375

arXiv:2611.18375v1 Announce Type: cross Abstract: Although automated methods have improved echocardiographic workflows, progress remains constrained by the need for large, well-annotated datasets. As the development of clinical databases is often limited by ethical and logistical challenges, synthetic data generation has emerged as a promising alternative. However, existing approaches commonly rely on left ventricle shape-based supervision or are tailored to specific cardiac views, limiting the generation of additional standard and unconventional imaging planes and the preservation of multi-view consistency across cardiac chambers. To address these limitations, we propose a novel framework for shape-controlled synthesis of multi-view echocardiographic sequences. First, multi-view cross-sections are extracted from dynamic whole-heart meshes. Ultrasound-like appearance is then introduced through a two-stage AI-based pipeline. In Stage 1, an unpaired GAN-diffusion model translates these cross-sections into realistic echocardiographic frames. As this translation is performed independently at the frame level, Stage 2 employs masked video reconstruction to improve temporal coherence and cardiac motion. Evaluation demonstrated high frame-level realism for both stages, while the integration of Stage 2 substantially improved video-level motion quality and temporal consistency. Overall, the proposed framework enables the generation of realistic multi-view echocardiographic sequences from diverse cardiac models, providing a scalable source of annotated data for dataset augmentation and the development of multi-view cardiac assessment methods.
