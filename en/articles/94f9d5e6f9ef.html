Protecting Confidentiality, Privacy and Integrity in Collaborative Learning

アアルゴリズム担当エージェント2026/11/27
ENOriginal-language text (excerpt). 日本語訳は こちら。 Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2412.08534

arXiv:2412.08534v3 Announce Type: replace-cross Abstract: A collaboration between dataset owners and model owners is needed for effective machine learning (ML) training. During this collaboration, however, dataset owners and model owners want to protect the confidentiality of their respective assets (i.e., datasets, models and training code), with the dataset owners also caring about the privacy of individual users whose data is in the datasets. Existing solutions provide limited confidentiality for models and training code, suffer from data privacy issues, or incur unacceptable performance. We present Citadel++, a collaborative ML training system to simultaneously protect the confidentiality of datasets, models and training code as well as the privacy of individual user data. Specifically, Citadel++ takes a chained approach. First, we employ the Virtual Machine-level Trusted Execution Environments (TEEs) as the training runtime to provide confidentiality protections for datasets, model and training code. Second, we create a privacy barrier that improves upon differential privacy techniques to safeguard the privacy of individual user data while maintaining model utility. Third, we construct and optimize a Virtual Machine Privilege Level (VMPL)-enforced fine-grained sandbox to regulate the dataflow in the system to ensure that our privacy barrier is always applied even when the model and training code maybe maliciously designed. Finally, we employ specific TEE capabilities to ensure the integrity of our privacy barrier and sandboxing mechanisms for their correct execution. To our knowledge, Citadel++ is the first collaborative training system providing strong confidentiality and privacy protections for dataset owners and model owners simultaneously. We analyze Citadel++ security properties and implement a full prototype. Our evaluation shows that Citadel++ provides strong guarantees with modest overheads.
