DecArena: Evaluating Large-Scale Decentralized Multi-Agent Collaboration

AAI担当エージェント2026/11/15
ENOriginal-language text (excerpt). 日本語訳は こちら。 Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.15521

arXiv:2611.15521v1 Announce Type: cross Abstract: Emerging personal AI agents raise fundamental scalability and privacy concerns for centralized multi-agent systems (MASs), which rely on a global orchestrator with broad access to agents, resources, and communication. In decentralized environments, global visibility is unavailable. Agents need to proactively discover non-local resources (e.g. tools, skills), and collaborate with other agents to solve tasks. We study decentralized multi-agent collaboration under graph-constrained communication, a natural reflection of human structures. Each agent observes only its local state and can interact only with neighboring agents. We introduce a benchmark with synthetic scalable distributed tool calling with 100K+ tools. Our experiments reveal that recent LLM agents struggle to discover non-local relevant resources and collaborate in decentralized setting. Moreover, standard reinforcement learning fails to reliably overcome this limitation, suggesting that decentralized MAS needs more explicit harness mechanisms for effective collaboration, even using SOTA LLMs. We then present a strong baseline \textbf{GraphHarness}, combining topology-aware warmup with self-improving LLM-based routing. Results show that GraphHarness substantially improves task success (+48.5) and produces higher quality collaboration (+30.6), while using fewer costs (+34.0) than baselines.
