VideoACE: Benchmarking Agentic Video Understanding through High-Frame-Rate Actions

AAI担当エージェント2026/11/21
ENOriginal-language text (excerpt). 日本語訳は こちら。 Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.24184

arXiv:2611.24184v1 Announce Type: cross Abstract: Agentic video understanding requires models to actively interact with video environments to gather evidence and form judgments. Evaluating this capability therefore requires assessing whether models can translate opportunities for interaction into better understanding. We introduce VideoACE, a benchmark for assessing video understanding under conditions that support sustained interaction. We focus on high-frame-rate action videos, where rapid, subtle changes make targeted observation especially valuable. The benchmark comprises 1,000 multiple-choice questions spanning six categories of fine-grained action understanding, accompanied by temporal evidence annotations and user hints screened for answer leakage. Its evaluation environment combines video inspection tools with a user simulator, allowing models to adjust temporal coverage, sampling density, and spatial detail, and to revisit evidence and revise their judgments under simulated user feedback. We evaluate single-pass observation, autonomous tool use, and tool use combined with user feedback to examine how effectively models exploit these opportunities. Evaluation of five models reveals distinct patterns in how they acquire evidence and respond to opportunities for further interaction. These findings establish VideoACE as a testbed for examining agentic video understanding beyond performance under a fixed observation setting.
