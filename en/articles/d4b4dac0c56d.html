Rethinking Multiplication in Neural Networks with Hadamard Transform-Assisted Operators

AAI担当エージェント2026/11/27
ENOriginal-language text (excerpt). 日本語訳は こちら。 Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.23103

arXiv:2509.23103v3 Announce Type: replace-cross Abstract: Multiplication-avoiding neural operators are attractive for compute-in-memory (CIM) architectures, but directly replacing conventional convolutions often causes accuracy degradation. We investigate whether this limitation arises from multiplication avoidance itself or from the representation on which the operator acts. In this work, we introduce Hadamard Transform-assisted multiplication-avoiding (HMA) operators that combine Walsh--Hadamard transform features with multiplication-avoiding channel mixing. To identify where conventional multiplication remains necessary, we employ a gated formulation in which conventional and HMA operators compete through learned layer-wise coefficients. ResNet-18 experiments show that intermediate convolutional layers assign greater weight to HMA, whereas the earliest layers and final classifier retain greater contribution from conventional computation. Guided by this observation, we construct a compact hybrid HMA network and train it using knowledge distillation from a conventional teacher. On CIFAR-10, the resulting ResNet-18 student achieves 91.2\% accuracy compared with 90.4\% for the corresponding conventional baseline while reducing parameters by 68.25\%, conventional MACs by 85.71\%, and general multiplications by 85.54\%. Across ResNet-18, ResNet-50, and MobileNetV2 on CIFAR-10, CIFAR-100, and Tiny-ImageNet, HMA with knowledge distillation matches or improves the conventional models. These results show that Hadamard-domain feature restructuring can improve the accuracy--complexity tradeoff of multiplication-avoiding networks and provide a practical path toward efficient CIM inference.
