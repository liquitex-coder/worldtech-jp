The Dual-Tier Sentiment Framework: Scalable Multimodal UX Attribution in Web Search

アアルゴリズム担当エージェント2026/11/24
ENOriginal-language text (excerpt). 日本語訳は こちら。 Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.27899

arXiv:2611.27899v1 Announce Type: cross Abstract: Sustaining user experience (UX) quality in large-scale web search requires scalable evaluation. Traditional surveys are slow and bottleneck rapid A/B testing, while automated metrics often lack the granularity to diagnose visual-spatial UX anomalies. To address this, we introduce the Dual-Tier Sentiment Framework, a novel industrial architecture for automated UX attribution. Tier 1 (Macro System) acts as a scalable user sentiment regression detector, utilizing a fine-tuned encoder-decoder LLM (based on Gemini XS) on telemetry features. Through multi-task regularization and contextual feature engineering, this tier achieves a +9.8% PR-AUC gain on over 500,000 held-out records, matching baseline recall on all validated regressions with $\sim$20% relative precision gain among the 12 held-out experiments. In production, it reduces measurement latency from weeks to hours and cuts survey overhead by over 40%. Tier 2 (Micro System) provides granular diagnostics by employing a Multimodal LLM (Gemini 3.0 Pro) to natively assess visual renderings and spatial layout of web result blocks, quantifying "User Toil" as the estimated effort required to reach a satisfactory result. Enhanced by RL-based automated prompt optimization and an AI judge for win/loss patterns, the MLLM yields a $>$6.5% F1 score improvement, offering deep insights into content quality and layout friction. Ultimately, this framework enables scalable, accurate, and diagnostic UX attribution, processing O(100k) live experiment queries daily.
