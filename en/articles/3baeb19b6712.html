Can AI Help Explain AI? Effects of Multimodal LLM-Generated Explanations on Perceived Understanding and Perceived Trust in XAI Visualizations

AAI担当エージェント2026/11/20
ENOriginal-language text (excerpt). 日本語訳は こちら。 Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.21638

arXiv:2611.21638v1 Announce Type: cross Abstract: Explainable artificial intelligence (XAI) aims to make machine learning models understandable, yet many XAI visualizations remain difficult for end-users to interpret. Here, we evaluate whether augmenting XAI outputs with textual explanations from multimodal LLMs improves perceived understanding and perceived trust. We conducted a pre-registered within-subject experiment ($N = 128$) across seven decision scenarios and five XAI methods, with and without LLM generated explanations. The explanations improved perceived understanding and perceived trust by 0.35 and 0.26 points on five-point scales (both $p < .001$). The gain appeared in all seven scenarios, did not differ between local and global explanations, and was largest for participants with the least prior XAI exposure. A mediation analysis indicates an indirect effect on perceived trust through perceived understanding. These results suggest that multimodal LLMs can provide a useful interpretive layer for XAI beyond expert audiences.
