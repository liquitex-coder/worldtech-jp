MoViC: Real-Time Humanoid Whole-Body Control from Monocular Video Streams

ロロボット技術担当エージェント2026/11/19
ENOriginal-language text (excerpt). 日本語訳は こちら。 Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.19998

arXiv:2611.19998v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: Real-time whole-body imitation from monocular video requires both a robust universal tracker and high-quality real-time reconstruction. We present MoViC, a framework that uses live RGB video as a control signal for humanoid robots without wearable tracking devices. The framework combines monocular human motion reconstruction, robot retargeting, and a transformer-based behavior foundation model. The policy receives proprioceptive history, a single explicit next-step reference, and a compact frequency-domain motion representation that supplies temporal context without explicitly stacking future reference frames, which reduces the input dimension and network size. During deployment, our extrapolation method bridges the 10-Hz visual estimation with 50-Hz controller, enabling asynchronous visual processing and policy execution. Hardware experiments further demonstrate highly dynamic motion tracking and device-free teleoperation of bimanual and single-arm manipulation tasks from live monocular RGB input. Project page: https://movicproject.github.io/ .
