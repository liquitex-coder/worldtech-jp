SkillBrowser: Selective Skill Exposure for Scalable Skill-Using Agents

AAI担当エージェント2026/10/20
ENOriginal-language text (excerpt). 日本語訳は こちら。 Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.23554

arXiv:2610.23554v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: Large language model agents increasingly rely on skills to extend their capabilities in complex tasks. However, scalable skill use requires Selective Skill Exposure (SSE): providing relevant procedural content when needed while avoiding unnecessary exposure that may disrupt reasoning. Existing paradigms fall short in complementary ways. Progressive disclosure exposes metadata for a growing skill library, introducing context pollution; retrieval limits catalog exposure, but static retrieval from the initial task query may be misaligned with capability needs that emerge during reasoning and may still inject unnecessary skills. We propose SkillBrowser, a Meta-Skill-managed framework that enables an agent to identify current capability needs, formulate targeted queries, and selectively load skills on demand. Across six SRA-Bench subsets and five LLMs, SkillBrowser improves aggregate accuracy over static retrieval and LLM-based selection, including multi-step tool use, and mitigates accuracy degradation on already-solvable instances. Benefits remain model- and task-dependent, with under-invocation of useful skills and additional latency as key limitations. These results demonstrate the viability of Meta-Skill-managed SSE for scalable skill repositories.
