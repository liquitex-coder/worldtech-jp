Domain-Tuned Diffusion Super-Resolution for 4K Facial Asset Maps

AAI担当エージェント2026/11/20
ENOriginal-language text (excerpt). 日本語訳は こちら。 Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.21890

arXiv:2611.21890v1 Announce Type: cross Abstract: Physically based facial assets require 4K albedo and displacement maps for cinematic close-ups, yet generative pipelines commonly produce them at only 1K. Naively applying photographic super-resolution is a poor fit: facial UV maps have domain-specific statistics, and displacement is a scalar height field whose errors directly distort geometry. We present a domain-tuned diffusion upscaler for 4x (1024 to 4096) facial asset super-resolution. Starting from a bilinearly upsampled map, we adapt a frozen SDXL backbone per modality with lightweight LoRA modules, while a Tile ControlNet preserves input structure as the diffusion prior restores pore- and wrinkle-scale detail. On held-out maps, our method produces sharper, more faithful detail than bilinear and off-the-shelf GAN and diffusion super-resolvers, reducing albedo LPIPS from 0.21 to 0.08 while avoiding misplaced or excessive displacement relief. These results show that lightweight domain adaptation can produce render-ready 4K facial asset maps without a custom super-resolution architecture.
