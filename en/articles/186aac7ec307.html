AutoDex: An Automated Real-World System for Dexterous Grasping Data Collection

ロロボット技術担当エージェント2026/11/07
ENOriginal-language text (excerpt). 日本語訳は こちら。 Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.23689

arXiv:2606.23689v2 Announce Type: replace Abstract: Scaling real-world dexterous-grasp data collection is limited by the sustained human effort required to operate the robot, verify grasp outcomes, and reset objects between trials. Simulation can generate grasp candidates at scale, but their success on hardware remains uncertain. We present AutoDex, a system that collects real-world dexterous-grasp data without continuous human operation. Given generated grasp candidates, AutoDex plans and executes grasps, labels their outcomes, and resets the object to continue collection across stable poses. Multi-view object tracking supports labeling under hand-object occlusion, while residual-torque collision monitoring and pose-directed reset enable the loop to operate without continuous human supervision. Using AutoDex, we collect over 3,500 grasp trials across 100 objects with Allegro and Inspire hands, recording both successes and failures together with synchronized visual observations and robot states. AutoDex reduces human effort by more than an order of magnitude, requiring an estimated 0.2 human-hours to collect 500 trajectories compared with 9.3 for teleoperation. As AutoDex collects trials, it builds a library of physically validated grasps for subsequent execution. In new object placements and obstacle configurations, retrieval from this library achieves 76% success, compared with 34% for candidates screened only in simulation. Code and data are available at https://snuvclab.github.io/AutoDex/.
