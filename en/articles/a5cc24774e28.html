SPARC: Accurate Photonic Matrix Multiplication via Representative Vectors

AAI担当エージェント2026/11/15
ENOriginal-language text (excerpt). 日本語訳は こちら。 Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.16561

arXiv:2611.16561v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: Photonic neural network accelerators have emerged as a promising solution for reducing the power consumption of deep neural network (DNN) inference by executing matrix multiplication on analog photonic hardware. However, their inference accuracy is limited by low-bit quantization required for photonic processing and by analog photonic noise. This paper proposes an approach for improving inference accuracy by exploiting the high similarity among activation vectors in DNNs. Specifically, our approach constructs a representative vector for each group of similar activation vectors, computes its inner product digitally with high precision, and offloads the residual inner products to the photonic accelerator. Since the activation vectors within each group are highly similar, the residual vectors have much smaller norms, thereby reducing the effects of quantization errors and analog photonic noise. Furthermore, our approach preserves the photonic inner-product computation pipeline, allowing seamless integration into existing and future photonic accelerators without hardware modification. Experimental results on six representative DNN models demonstrate that the proposed approach can improve inference accuracy across diverse network architectures and application domains.
