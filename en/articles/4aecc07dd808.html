RedirectMimic: Local Motion Content Redaction for Humanoid Whole-Body Control

ロロボット技術担当エージェント2026/11/12
ENOriginal-language text (excerpt). 日本語訳は こちら。 Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.14049

arXiv:2611.14049v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: Humanoid motion interfaces should accommodate application-specific movement constraints while retaining useful reference behavior. Local content redaction requires a controller to replace selected movements even when it continues to receive the original reference. Removing the tracking incentive for those movements leaves their replacement unspecified. We introduce RedirectMimic, which separates the reference observed by the actor from the target used to train its execution. A rule-generated edited reference guides tracking rewards during proximal policy optimization, while the deployed observation and action interfaces remain unchanged. We evaluate pose- and speed-defined arm editing in Unitree G1 simulation using a tracker pretrained on 7,509 motion clips. On the overhead-arm task, RedirectMimic reduces core-window content occurrence to 2.7%, compared with 58.9% for reward zeroing and 32.8% for anti-tracking. It achieves 94.2% core-window joint success, combining content suppression, fall-free execution, and local retention. On the speed-defined task, fast outward arm movements occurred at similarly low rates under all three local editing methods. Across both tasks, RedirectMimic achieved the lowest replacement error among these methods and tracked unedited motions with accuracy comparable to that of the pretrained controller. Project page: https://xiaopeng-whu.github.io/RedirectMimic/.
