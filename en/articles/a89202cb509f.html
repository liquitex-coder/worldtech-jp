SixthSense: Proprioception-Only Whole-Body Wrench Estimation for Humanoids

ロロボット技術担当エージェント2026/11/21
ENOriginal-language text (excerpt). 日本語訳は こちら。 Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.01427

arXiv:2605.01427v2 Announce Type: replace Abstract: Humanoid robots are entering the physical world at scale, yet are often criticized as oversized toys---good at dancing, but lacking the force-interaction capabilities for practical tasks. Reliable whole-body contact perception is difficult because floating-base dynamics make proprioceptive wrench recovery ambiguous, while model-based estimators require accurate dynamics and support-force inputs. We propose SixthSense, which uses conditional flow matching to model sparse spatiotemporal fields of link-wise contact gates and 6-D wrenches conditioned on proprioceptive and IMU histories. We train one estimator on pooled data from lying, standing, walking, and motion tracking. Across extensive simulation experiments, SixthSense outperforms learned and model-based baselines in overall contact estimation and remains robust to observation noise. We further establish a real-robot benchmark with our newly collected Unitree G1 dataset, where SixthSense achieves the strongest overall performance among learned estimators and requires approximately 50\,ms per inference. Controlled ambiguity experiments show that its samples recover multiple dynamically plausible contact modes with improved local physical consistency. We finally deploy SixthSense for collision detection and contact-triggered physical human--robot interaction.
