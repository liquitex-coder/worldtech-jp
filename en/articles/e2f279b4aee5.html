ProtoBridge-CLIP: Observation-Masked Finding Prototypes for Medical Image--Report Representation Learning

AAI担当エージェント2026/11/21
ENOriginal-language text (excerpt). 日本語訳は こちら。 Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.24002

arXiv:2611.24002v1 Announce Type: cross Abstract: Radiology reports distinguish affirmed findings, explicit negations, and unmentioned concepts, but conventional global image--report contrastive learning often compresses these different evidence states into a single study-level target. We introduce \model{}, which combines symmetric global image--report alignment with observation-masked finding supervision and a soft relation objective over affirmed report concepts. Text-derived finding prototypes are aligned with local visual tokens, while uncertain and unmentioned concepts are excluded from direct finding labels. We evaluate the framework on three CT benchmarks and five chest-radiograph benchmarks, together with component ablations, paired and clinical retrieval, localization, perturbation robustness, calibration, and per-finding analyses. \model{} achieves macro-AUROC values of 73.6%, 66.5\%, and 68.8% on CT-RATE, RAD-ChestCT, and INSPECT, and 80.2%, 91.4%, 78.5%, 86.5%, and 90.8% on ChestXray14, CheXpert, PadChest, RSNA, and SIIM-ACR, respectively. Relative to the matched global-only controls, the findings and relation objectives yield consistent gains across the evaluated datasets. These results demonstrate the practical value of treating observed and unobserved report concepts differently during medical image--text representation learning.
