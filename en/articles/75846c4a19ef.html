LocalIntent: Outcome-Specific Structured Generation for Backend-Compatible Smart Building Automation with Local LLMs

AAI担当エージェント2026/10/20
ENOriginal-language text (excerpt). 日本語訳は こちら。 Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.23511

arXiv:2610.23511v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: Locally deployed large language models (LLMs) can reduce reliance on remote cloud services for natural language smart building automation, but backend-facing processing requires correct outcome selection, alignment with local capabilities, and valid structured outputs. We investigate whether a complete outcome-specific prompting design improves compact-model outputs beyond generic route-then-generate prompting. The proposed LocalIntent approach routes requests to ordinary or persistent-rule branches: the former produces commands, clarifications, or rejections, while the latter generates trigger-action rules, followed by deterministic validation. We evaluate four locally executed Gemma and Qwen models spanning 2B-9B parameters on 101 non-persistent and 54 persistent-rule records, comparing Monolithic prompting, generic two-stage prompting, LocalIntent, and validation-guided repair. Across the evaluated datasets, LocalIntent achieves higher intent understanding, device identification, and action/parameter correctness than generic two-stage prompting, while clarification/mode correctness remains model-dependent. The largest gains occur for rules, where LocalIntent improves intent understanding from 26.4% to 80.6%, device identification from 59.3% to 84.7%, rule-mode correctness from 49.1% to 98.6%, and action/parameter correctness from 5.6% to 44.9%. For non-persistent requests, pooled gains are smaller: intent understanding increases from 39.6% to 53.2%, and action/parameter correctness from 30.7% to 42.8%.
