Receiver-Conditioned Clarification for Multi-Agent Food Assistance

AAI担当エージェント2026/11/21
ENOriginal-language text (excerpt). 日本語訳は こちら。 Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.23572

arXiv:2611.23572v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: Food insecurity creates a pressing need for guidance that helps households find usable food resources and assess food choices in light of their circumstances. Such guidance must connect eligibility requirements, practical access constraints, and dietary needs rather than return isolated listings or generic nutritional advice. Multi-agent food assistants can coordinate these tasks, with uncertainty indicating when clarification or verification is needed before a recommendation. However, uncertainty can be lost across agent handoffs, allowing tentative interpretations to propagate as facts and obscuring opportunities for correction. Moreover, confidence that a service requirement has been interpreted correctly is not confidence that a recommendation satisfies the user's needs; local confidence cannot simply be reused as downstream reliability. We introduce an inference-time, receiver-conditioned clarification framework that preserves shared premise identities and replays consistent interpretations across dependent agents. Each receiver exposes admissible decisions and remaining evidence obligations, so question relevance reflects both decision changes and missing support. Information questions and user-confirmable fallback adjustments are evaluated together with their anticipated follow-up interactions under shared budgets. This structure connects clarification to jointly supported plans for resource navigation, nutritional reasoning, and supply-side planning.
