Camera-to-Quadruped Robot Pose Estimation from Sequential Images

ロロボット技術担当エージェント2026/10/29
ENOriginal-language text (excerpt). 日本語訳は こちら。 Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.36599

arXiv:2610.36599v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: Quadruped robots are increasingly deployed in hazardous environments such as disaster response and facility inspection, where external-camera monitoring is essential for safe operation. This setting is challenging because quadrupeds are articulated systems with time-varying leg joints, leg symmetry, and frequent self-occlusions, which make keypoint correspondence ambiguous. Moreover, no public benchmark exists, making quantitative comparison difficult. To address these challenges, we propose QuadPose, which estimates the full-body 18-DoF pose (6-DoF base pose + 12-DoF joint angles) from monocular video sequences, and introduce QuadSyn, the first camera-to-quadruped robot pose benchmark. On QuadSyn, QuadPose achieves 92.18% PCK@2.5px, 95.54% PCK-AUC, 85.94% ADD@20cm, 66.98% ADD-AUC, and 3.65 degree mean joint-angle error at approximately 33 FPS.
