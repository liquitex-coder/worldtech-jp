ST-GRU PredRNN-V2: A GRU-Based Reformulation of PredRNN-V2 for Video Prediction

AAI担当エージェント2026/10/16
ENOriginal-language text (excerpt). 日本語訳は こちら。 Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.18645

arXiv:2610.18645v1 Announce Type: cross Abstract: Video prediction requires models to capture complex spatiotemporal dependencies and to retain relevant information over long sequences. PredRNN and its successor PredRNN-V2 address this challenge with spatiotemporal Long Short-Term Memory (ST-LSTM) units that maintain two separate memory states, at the cost of a relatively complex recurrent building block. In this work, we investigate whether the simpler gating of Gated Recurrent Units (GRUs) can be transferred to spatiotemporal predictive learning. To this end, we develop a spatiotemporal GRU (ST-GRU) unit as a GRU-based alternative to the ST-LSTM. The ST-GRU replaces the ST-LSTM's three gates per memory state with two GRU-style gates (update and reset), and merges the temporal memory state into the hidden state, which reduces the number of parameters by about 5%. We incorporate the ST-GRU into PredRNN-V2 and evaluate the resulting model on the Moving MNIST video prediction benchmark, following the original experimental protocol. Averaged over ten predicted frames, the ST-GRU-based PredRNN-V2 achieves an MSE of 55.1, an SSIM of 0.883, an LPIPS of 0.065 and a PSNR of 19.53dB. It does not surpass the original ST-LSTM-based PredRNN-V2 on MSE, SSIM or PSNR, but it achieves a lower LPIPS and performs on par with the earlier ST-LSTM-based PredRNN, while using a simpler recurrent cell. These results suggest that ST-GRUs are a promising building block for spatiotemporal predictive learning and motivate further work on their design and integration.
