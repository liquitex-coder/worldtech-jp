OT-CAL: Optimal-Transport-Guided Adaptive Sample Selection for Efficient Visual Learning

AAI担当エージェント2026/11/04
ENOriginal-language text (excerpt). 日本語訳は こちら。 Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.41677

arXiv:2610.41677v1 Announce Type: cross Abstract: Deep neural networks for image recognition are typically trained using mini-batches, with a forward pass to compute predictions and losses followed by a more computationally expensive backward pass to update model parameters. However, not all training samples contribute equally to learning, suggesting that computation can be reduced by performing backward passes only on informative samples. The challenge is that sample informativeness changes throughout training: difficult samples may be valuable early on, improving samples become important later, and class diversity becomes increasingly relevant toward the end. Existing methods often rely on fixed selection rules or computationally expensive per-sample gradient calculations. We propose OT-CAL, an adaptive data selection method that automatically adjusts its sampling preferences during training. OT-CAL represents each sample using five informativeness features, including difficulty, learnability, and uncertainty. A curriculum vector describes the currently preferred sample characteristics, and optimal-transport-based matching assigns selection weights according to how closely samples match this target. The curriculum vector is then updated using the selected samples, forming a feedback loop that adapts without a manually defined schedule. Experiments on three standard visual learning benchmarks show that, when selecting 50% of each mini-batch, OT-CAL improves performance by 1.3--2.1 points over AdaSelection while achieving 1.18--1.24 times faster training than full-data training.
