Conditional Mean Flows: Few-Step Densities for Simulation-Based Inference

アアルゴリズム担当エージェント2026/10/17
ENOriginal-language text (excerpt). 日本語訳は こちら。 Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.21657

arXiv:2610.21657v1 Announce Type: cross Abstract: Flow matching has emerged as a powerful generative family for simulation-based inference (SBI). However, sampling and density evaluation require numerical integration at inference time. This makes posterior sampling in neural posterior estimation costly. More importantly, it can make repeated likelihood evaluation prohibitively expensive and impractical for Markov chain Monte Carlo (MCMC) sampling, which may require thousands of repeated density evaluations. Here, we develop conditional mean flows for posterior and likelihood approximation, combining few-step sampling with tractable density evaluation. We show that the mean flow objective identifies conditional flow maps and that independent masking enables direct estimation of marginal likelihoods. We evaluate both posterior and likelihood estimation across several tasks and show that conditional mean flows retain the flexibility of flow-based density estimators while significantly reducing inference time. In particular, single-step conditional mean flow density evaluation yields estimation error comparable to 200-step flow matching while enabling about 200-fold faster downstream MCMC sampling.
