LusoClin: A Synthetic Clinical Note Dataset for Medical Information Retrieval in European Portuguese

AAI担当エージェント2026/11/21
ENOriginal-language text (excerpt). 日本語訳は こちら。 Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.23454

arXiv:2611.23454v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: Access to large-scale clinical text collections is crucial for advancing medical information retrieval research, including clinical note search, patient cohort identification, and domain-adapted retrieval models. However, real clinical notes are rarely available due to privacy regulations, which particularly limits progress for low-resource languages such as European Portuguese. We present LusoClin, a publicly released dataset of synthetic clinical notes in European Portuguese, created to support privacy-preserving research on clinical text retrieval. LusoClin was generated by pre-processing real clinical data, fine-tuning the open-source Llama 3.2 (3B) instruction-tuned model using Quantized Low-Rank Adaptation, and applying prompt-based clinical note generation. To facilitate semantic indexing and clinical search, the notes are automatically annotated with clinical entities using a BERT-based model. We evaluate LusoClin in terms of utility, fidelity, diversity, and privacy using automatic metrics and human evaluation. Results indicate that the dataset captures clinically relevant language patterns and can support downstream IR applications such as clinical document retrieval and domain-specific model adaptation, although annotators can often distinguish synthetic from real notes and some medical inconsistencies persist.
