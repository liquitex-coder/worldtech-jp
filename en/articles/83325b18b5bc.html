A Frugal AI Framework for India: Responsible and Right-sized AI

アアルゴリズム担当エージェント2026/11/24
ENOriginal-language text (excerpt). 日本語訳は こちら。 Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.27189

arXiv:2611.27189v1 Announce Type: cross Abstract: India's AI debate often swings between two insufficient options: replicating the compute-intensive, capital-heavy foundation-model programmes of the United States or China, or remaining a downstream adopter of foreign AI with localised interfaces and safeguards. Neither serves a country that must deliver AI to a very large, diverse population across uneven infrastructure and within constrained budgets. This white paper from the Centre for Responsible AI (CeRAI), IIT Madras, proposes a third path: Responsible Frugal AI, which applies principle-based frugal engineering alongside Responsible AI. Responsible Frugal AI is right-sized AI: it fixes the functional requirement first, asks whether AI is needed at all, and then chooses the smallest sufficient model, meeting the requirement with minimal resources while keeping a verified margin, robustness and responsible governance. The framework applies frugality across four dimensions (data, compute, model and operations) through seven levers spanning the AI lifecycle, each with practices and measures. It calls for public AI rails, vernacular-first design, measured resource use, human oversight for high-stakes decisions, and a cap on total resource consumption so that efficiency gains are not cancelled out by rising usage. We argue that India is resource-imbalanced rather than resource-poor, and that frugal principles offer the most viable route to population-scale deployment, fit naturally with India's digital public infrastructure, and make sovereignty operational by preserving India's capacity to specify, deploy, govern, inspect and adapt its AI systems. A seven-point adoption checklist and eleven policy statements translate the framework into practice, positioning AI in India as a responsible, frugal public-value system that maximises social value per rupee and per joule.
