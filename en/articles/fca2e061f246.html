Formal Explanations for Linear Temporal Logic over Finite Traces

AAI担当エージェント2026/10/28
ENOriginal-language text (excerpt). 日本語訳は こちら。 Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.35169

arXiv:2610.35169v1 Announce Type: cross Abstract: Linear Temporal Logic over Finite Traces (LTLf) is widely used in Artificial Intelligence to specify desired temporal behavior and determine whether finite traces satisfy or violate it. Explaining these outcomes is essential for debugging and trust. However, existing approaches typically capture either sufficient reasons or counterfactual causes, without clarifying their relationship. We introduce a native characterization of abductive (AXps) and contrastive (CXps) explanations directly over finite traces, based on locking and unlocking time-indexed events. We prove its equivalence to the classifier-based formulation, and show that CXps coincide with a recently proposed temporal causality semantics for LTLf. This characterization enables an efficient implementation based on the enumeration of minimal unsatisfiable cores and minimal correction sets of ASP programs. Experiments on real-world event logs demonstrate the effectiveness of the approach.
