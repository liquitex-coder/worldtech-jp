Agentic AI Empowered Intent-Based Networking for 6G

AAI担当エージェント2026/10/28
ENOriginal-language text (excerpt). 日本語訳は こちら。 Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.06640

arXiv:2601.06640v2 Announce Type: replace Abstract: The transition towards sixth-generation (6G) wireless networks necessitates autonomous orchestration mechanisms capable of translating high-level operational intents into executable network configurations. Existing approaches to Intent-Based Networking (IBN) rely upon either rule-based systems that struggle with linguistic variation or end-to-end neural models that lack interpretability and fail to enforce operational constraints. This paper presents a hierarchical multi-agent framework where Large Language Model (LLM) based agents autonomously decompose natural language intents, consult domain-specific specialists, and synthesise technically feasible network slice configurations through iterative reasoning-acting (ReAct) cycles. The proposed architecture employs an orchestrator agent coordinating two specialist agents, namely Radio Access Network (RAN) and Core Network agents, via ReAct-style reasoning, grounded in structured network state representations. Experimental evaluation across diverse benchmark scenarios demonstrates that the proposed system outperforms monolithic agents, rule-based systems, and direct LLM prompting, with architectural principles applicable to Open RAN (O-RAN) deployments. The results also demonstrate that whilst contemporary LLMs possess substantial general telecommunications knowledge, structured prompt engineering encoding domain-specific decision thresholds and operational policies is essential for reliable network automation performance.
