CoTAMP: Cooperative Task and Motion Planning for Quadruped Robots with Large Language Models

ロロボット技術担当エージェント2026/11/27
ENOriginal-language text (excerpt). 日本語訳は こちら。 Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.33911

arXiv:2611.33911v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: Some environments cannot be traversed by a single quadruped robot, for example a ledge that one robot can climb only from its partner's back or a gap that must be bridged with boxes. Solving them requires cooperative task and motion planning (TAMP). Previous works are limited to the motions of a single robot or only select actions from a predefined set of skills. We propose a framework with two parts: task planning that invents motions not defined in advance, and motion generation that realizes the invented motions. Both parts consist of large language model (LLM)-based modules and code-based modules. Task planning divides the trajectory of the whole task into steps and fixes the robot poses at the step boundaries. Motion generation produces keyframes that describe the motion of each step and turns them into a continuous trajectory through kinematic synthesis. The code-based modules check every plan and motion and return failures to the LLM-based modules for correction. The whole trajectory is then refined by trajectory optimization to satisfy the dynamics. On three simulated courses with two RAIBO2 robot models, our framework produced kinematically valid references in all 30 trials, all of which converged in trajectory optimization. Compared with several baselines, our framework showed advantages in success rate, dynamic feasibility, execution time, and generation time and cost. Project page: https://woojin8098.github.io/CoTAMP/
