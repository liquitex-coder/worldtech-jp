Residual-Guided Lightweight Reinforcement Learning for Risk-Aware Trajectory Selection with a Frozen World Model

AAI担当エージェント2026/11/20
ENOriginal-language text (excerpt). 日本語訳は こちら。 Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.22998

arXiv:2611.22998v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: In automated driving, world models provide future scene predictions for evaluating candidate trajectories, but fixed selection rules may not capture scene-dependent trade-offs. We propose a residual-guided reinforcement learning (RL) framework for lightweight trajectory selection with a frozen world model and a fixed candidate bank. The framework uses proximal policy optimization (PPO) to fine-tune a joint policy over candidate selection and bounded trajectory residuals. A context-conditioned ranking network learns candidate preferences from feedback obtained by evaluating local perturbations of the selected trajectories. At inference, the selector combines learned preference and predicted risk ranks using fixed weights and returns the selected candidate trajectory without modification. Fine-tuning updates 16,106 parameters across the actor and critic, while the deployed selector retains only the 11,725-parameter ranking network, with the residual branch and critic used exclusively during training. In open-loop evaluation on the nuScenes validation set, the method reduces average Euclidean trajectory error (L2) by 3.99% relative to the RiskWorld model baseline without RL-based selection. Under matched budgets for feedback accesses and actor updates, residual exploration reduces L2 by 0.83% relative to the categorical PPO variant trained without residuals. The method also maintains lower L2 than RiskWorld when risk features are removed or shuffled.
