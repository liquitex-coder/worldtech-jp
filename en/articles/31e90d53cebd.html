Towards Accurate UAV Image Perception: Guiding Vision-Language Models with Stronger Task Prompts

AAI担当エージェント2026/11/15
ENOriginal-language text (excerpt). 日本語訳は こちら。 Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.07302

arXiv:2512.07302v2 Announce Type: replace-cross Abstract: UAV image perception with VLMs aims to identify and localize targets based on semantic and spatial task prompts. However, small, densely distributed objects and cluttered backgrounds hinder these tasks, particularly when concise prompts provide insufficient guidance. Although task-specific fine-tuning improves image-text alignment, it requires large-scale annotations and model-specific updates, increasing adaptation costs across models and tasks. To address this, we propose AerialVP, a task-adaptive, training-free agent that adapts VLMs through prompt enhancement rather than parameter optimization. AerialVP dynamically adapts its enhancement strategy to the original prompt's specificity, selectively incorporating image-derived semantics, spatial positions, and object relationships. These cues provide discriminative descriptions and spatial constraints, leveraging VLMs' pretrained alignment and reasoning capabilities to improve image-text alignment and reduce target ambiguity. Specifically, an LLM-based Task Engine coordinates modular tools for prompt analysis, visual information extraction, and prompt fusion. The enhanced prompt and original image are then provided to the unchanged backend VLM for prediction. This prompt-centric strategy enables training-free adaptation across diverse open-source and proprietary VLMs. For systematic evaluation, we introduce AerialSense, a unified benchmark covering Aerial Visual Grounding, Aerial Visual Reasoning, and Aerial Visual Question Answering across diverse UAV scenes and imaging conditions. Experiments demonstrate consistent improvements across all three tasks and evaluated VLMs. AerialVP also achieves competitive performance against LoRA-based instruction fine-tuning, validating prompt-centric adaptation. Our work is available at https://github.com/lostwolves/AerialVP.
