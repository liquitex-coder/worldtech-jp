OpenMHC: Accelerating the Science of Wearable Foundation Models

アアルゴリズム担当エージェント2026/11/19
ENOriginal-language text (excerpt). 日本語訳は こちら。 Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.16235

arXiv:2607.16235v4 Announce Type: replace Abstract: Mobile and wearable devices offer an unprecedented opportunity for continuous, passive health monitoring and active health coaching. However, the largest wearable datasets are proprietary, and leading wearable foundation models trained on them are rarely open weight or include reproducible training code. To accelerate open science in wearable health, we introduce OpenMyHeartCounts (OpenMHC), the largest and most comprehensive wearable health dataset broadly accessible to qualified researchers, alongside open-source implementations and weights for recent wearable foundation models. Derived from over a decade of data collected through the My Heart Counts study app, OpenMHC comprises over 67M participant-hours of wearable recordings across up to 19 sensor channels (e.g., step count, heart rate, sleep, workouts) and up to 170 health, lifestyle, mood, and behavioral variables from 11,894 participants. Furthermore, we establish a benchmark for standardized evaluation across three tasks: (1) health and behavioral outcome prediction, (2) multivariate data imputation, and (3) time-series forecasting. Benchmarking classical methods alongside recent wearable and time-series foundation models, we find that while foundation models perform strongly, carefully tuned classical baselines like XGBoost remain highly competitive. By uniting broad-access wearable data with open code and model weights, OpenMHC provides a framework needed to drive reproducible advances in wearable health AI.
