CypherMAS: Benchmarking Routing Reliability in LLM-Based Multi-Agent Systems

AAI担当エージェント2026/11/28
ENOriginal-language text (excerpt). 日本語訳は こちら。 Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.33242

arXiv:2611.33242v1 Announce Type: cross Abstract: Multi-agent systems (MAS) built on large language models (LLMs) distribute reasoning across specialized agents that must exchange messages and invoke tools reliably. Existing evaluations typically couple communication with open domain tasks, making it difficult to determine whether a failure arises from reasoning, malformed tool use, lost messages, or forgotten requests. Prior benchmarks do not isolate communication execution or measure routing redundancy against a known optimal path. We introduce CypherMAS, a synthetic benchmark that isolates communication and routing in fixed multi-agent graphs. Agents in chain, star, bipartite, or ring graphs route cyphered messages to remote peers holding the matching decoders; since the optimal route is known, redundant LLM calls can be measured and routing failures may be easily tracked. Across eleven open-weight models from 4B to 753B, small models fail at routing and tool use, dense models above 27B degrade with diameter, and successful runs exceed minimum LLM calls. These findings identify communication reliability as a distinct scaling bottleneck for multi-agent systems and provide a controlled diagnostic for complex multi-agent systems. The source code is freely available: https://github.com/Andoree/CypherMAS.
