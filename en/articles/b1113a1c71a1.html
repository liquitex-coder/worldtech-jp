Auditable Financial Knowledge Graphs: Schema-Driven Construction and Intrinsic Quality Evaluation

AAI担当エージェント2026/11/24
ENOriginal-language text (excerpt). 日本語訳は こちら。 Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.27835

arXiv:2611.27835v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: Financial documents combine narrative disclosures, accounting metrics, corporate structures, management commentary, and time-dependent facts, making them difficult to represent using conventional chunk-based retrieval-augmented generation systems. This paper presents an auditable, schema-driven methodology for constructing temporal financial knowledge graphs from page-delimited documents parsed using a general purpose document intelligence service. Overlapping document windows undergo phased large language model extraction of structural entities, quantitative observations, and domain-specific relationships. Candidate graph elements are validated against predefined schemas and consolidated through scope-aware exact, normalized, fuzzy, embedding-based, and LLM-assisted entity resolution. Temporal merge constraints, deterministic edge deduplication, conflict-aware property management, and fine-grained provenance preserve issuer identity, reporting-period validity, and traceability to source evidence. The contributions of this work comprise the proposed methodology for financial knowledge graph construction, the creation of an evidence-grounded temporal knowledge graph from heterogeneous financial documents, and an intrinsic framework for evaluating the quality of the resulting graph. The evaluation framework assesses seven weighted dimensions, two hard validation gates, and structural diagnostics. By separating graph-quality assessment from downstream retrieval and answer generation, the framework supports systematic diagnosis and iterative refinement of the constructed graph.
