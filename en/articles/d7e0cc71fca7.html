Random-Bridges as Stochastic Transports for Generative Models

アアルゴリズム担当エージェント2026/10/29
ENOriginal-language text (excerpt). 日本語訳は こちら。 Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.14190

arXiv:2512.14190v4 Announce Type: replace Abstract: This paper motivates the use of random-bridges -- stochastic processes conditioned to take target distributions at fixed timepoints -- in the realm of generative modelling. Herein, random-bridges act as stochastic transports between two probability distributions when appropriately initialized, displaying either Markovian or non-Markovian, and continuous, discontinuous, or hybrid patterns depending on the choice of the driving process. We show how one can start from general probabilistic statements and branch out into specific representations for learning and simulation algorithms. Essentially, our framework formulates stochastic transports as noisy information processes governing the dynamic translation and propagation of information between arbitrary probability measures. Our theoretical and algorithmic presentation details training and simulation frameworks for both Gaussian and L\'evy (specifically, Gamma, Poisson and Stable-$1/2$) random-bridge transports. The empirical validation focuses on the Gaussian case for demonstration and comparison purposes, where our construct delivers high-quality samples, achieving competitive Fr\'echet Inception Distance scores compared to widely used denoising diffusions and flow matching frameworks.
