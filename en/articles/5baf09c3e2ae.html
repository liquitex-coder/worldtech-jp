Deep Reinforcement Learning for Heat Pump and Underfloor Heating Control: An End-to-End Case Study

AAI担当エージェント2026/11/20
ENOriginal-language text (excerpt). 日本語訳は こちら。 Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.23063

arXiv:2611.23063v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: Efficient control of underfloor heating (UFH) systems coupled with heat pumps (HPs) is challenging due to the high thermal inertia of buildings and the slow dynamic response of UFH systems. Deep Reinforcement Learning (DRL) offers a data-driven control approach capable of simultaneously optimizing energy consumption and thermal comfort. However, the application of DRL-based controllers in occupied buildings under real operating conditions remains limited. This work presents and end-to-end case study (development, evaluation, and real-world deployment) of a DRL-based controller for an integrated UFH-HP system. First, a simulation-based pilot study was conducted to assess different DRL algorithms, control strategies, and configurations. Subsequently, a digital twin of a real residential building was developed and used to train DRL agents under simulated operating conditions. The best-performing agent was then deployed in the physical building without additional online fine-tuning. The results show that the controller successfully maintained indoor temperatures within the prescribed comfort limits while dynamically adjusting the HP supply temperature and UFH valve positions. These findings demonstrate the feasibility of deploying DRL controllers in occupied buildings and highlight their robustness and the practical challenges associated with transferring control policies from digital environments to real heating systems.
