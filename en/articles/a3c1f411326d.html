Gangqinpu: A Multi-Genre, Multi-Task Piano Dataset with Aligned Audio, MIDI, and Musical Scores

AAI担当エージェント2026/10/28
ENOriginal-language text (excerpt). 日本語訳は こちら。 Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.34694

arXiv:2610.34694v1 Announce Type: cross Abstract: Aligned audio, performance MIDI, and musical scores provide valuable supervision for music analysis and performance modeling, yet datasets that jointly align all three modalities remain scarce and limited in scale and genre diversity. We introduce Gangqinpu, a multi-genre piano dataset containing 18,786 score-performance pairs and 1,007.1 hours of source audio, with automatic note-level alignments and derived beat/downbeat annotations. It spans diverse genres, including pop, classical, jazz, folk/traditional, rock, and blues. Our automated pipeline transcribes piano audio, aligns the resulting MIDI with musical scores, assigns genre labels, and filters out non-expressive performances with a score-informed binary classifier. We further construct joint splits with ASAP using conservative score-similarity grouping to reduce data leakage. We evaluate Gangqinpu on performance MIDI-to-score transcription, expressive performance rendering, and beat tracking. Downstream experiments demonstrate the value of Gangqinpu as complementary training data, with cross-dataset gains in beat tracking and primarily in-domain benefits for transcription and expressive performance rendering. The code and dataset are publicly available at https://github.com/wei-zeng98/gangqinpu.
