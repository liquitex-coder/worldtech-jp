What Does Matching a Human Intervention Effect Establish? Benchmark Checks for LLM Simulations

AAI担当エージェント2026/11/24
ENOriginal-language text (excerpt). 日本語訳は こちら。 Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.27186

arXiv:2611.27186v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: LLM simulations are validated by equivalence with human effects, yet a zero reference passes whenever the human 90 percent interval lies inside the margin, as the human benchmark shows before simulation. When the zero reference fails, planned precision gives independent simulations the margin up to which passing is a 5 percent test of response. In an exploratory 70-experiment reanalysis, 72 percent of GPT-4's $\pm$0.20 SD passes were zero-reference passes; GPT-4 passed 16 contrasts the zero reference failed (1.58 expected without response). In a preregistered audit, Claude Sonnet 5.5 passed three intention targets, all zero-reference passes, and the registered belief-gradient control; norm beliefs changed 0.065 SD against humans' 0.351 (zero-reference check added after unblinding; norm beliefs emphasized after inspection). Registered tests detected no overestimation; average-effect intervals excluded overestimation above 0.08 SD. A group audit's precision floor is 1.33 facts against a 2.9-fact effect. Report the zero-reference threshold beside every equivalence decision.
