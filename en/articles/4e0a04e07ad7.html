RoSci: Exploring Robustness in Science VLMs

AAI担当エージェント2026/11/24
ENOriginal-language text (excerpt). 日本語訳は こちら。 Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.27750

arXiv:2611.27750v1 Announce Type: cross Abstract: Recent advancements in Vision-Language Models (VLMs) enable significant progress in scientific tasks. However, these models are generally assumed to be presented with complete and accurate information, overlooking the challenges of erroneous/missing information in wild scenarios. To address this gap, we introduce the concept of Scientific Model Robustness, which defines a model's ability to detect anomalies, assess solvability, and perform self-calibration when faced with Information Error (inconsistencies) or Information Loss (insufficiency). Specifically, we present RoSci-Bench, a diagnostic benchmark of 1,220 samples using a seed-variant strategy across six degradation types. To address VLM fragility, we further propose RoSci-Agent, a framework that employs a Parse-Verify-Decide pipeline to vet premises before reasoning. The evaluated VLMs sometimes answer questions whose premises are invalid or incomplete. RoSci-Agent achieves a higher Overall Robustness Score than the evaluated standalone VLMs. RoSci-Bench gives the multimodal reasoning community a common test of when models answer, request clarification, or reject a scientific question.
