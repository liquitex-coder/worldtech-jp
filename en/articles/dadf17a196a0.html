RoboColiseum: Co-evolution of Simulation, Evaluation, and Models

ロロボット技術担当エージェント2026/11/24
ENOriginal-language text (excerpt). 日本語訳は こちら。 Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.27042

arXiv:2611.27042v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: Simulation-based evaluation of embodied models requires calibrated interactions, diverse tasks, and explicit execution constraints. We present RoboColiseum, a platform combining system identification, composable physics and rendering, and multidimensional benchmarking. Recorded-command replay characterizes robot dynamics and object interactions, while a shared interface connects rigid and deformable simulation to rendering. Across 22 paired real--simulation robot--task configurations, task scores have a reported Pearson correlation of 0.9686. Eight evaluation tracks span instruction following, spatial reasoning, general manipulation, robustness, dexterous manipulation, precision manipulation, whole-body coordination, and execution across robot embodiments. Budget-aware scoring combines task progress, resource use, and completion rewards. We report 12 selected Season~I policies and all 11 systems on the public Season~II leaderboard, comprising eight Agent systems and three VLA policies. Agent2Act connects language models to robot-control execution; the VLA study uses mixed-task supervised fine-tuning. The results reveal substantial variation across tracks within each evaluation category. RoboColiseum connects simulation development with capability-oriented evaluation and provides a foundation for extending robot interactions and model assessment.
