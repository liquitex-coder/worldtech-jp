Parameter-Free Dynamic Regret under Heavy-Tailed Noise

AAI担当エージェント2026/11/27
ENOriginal-language text (excerpt). 日本語訳は こちら。 Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.27073

arXiv:2607.27073v4 Announce Type: replace-cross Abstract: We study online convex optimization on a compact convex domain of diameter $D$ in which the learner observes, at each round, one stochastic subgradient and no loss values. The observations are conditionally unbiased, and the noise has an unconditional $p$-th moment at most $\sigma^p$ for an unknown $p\in(1,2]$; for $p<2$ its variance may be infinite. For a comparator sequence of path length $P_T$ we write $\Lambda_T=1+P_T/D$, and we show that the minimax expected dynamic regret is, up to numerical constants, \[ \min\Bigl\{GD\sqrt{T\Lambda_T}+\sigma DT^{1/p}\Lambda_T^{(p-1)/p},\ GDT\Bigr\}, \] where $G$ bounds the subgradients. The lower bound holds on every compact convex domain of positive diameter. An algorithm that combines AdaGrad learners restarted at different frequencies through exponential weights with a data-dependent learning rate attains this rate for each fixed $p>1$, without knowing $G$, $\sigma$, $p$, or $P_T$: the constant of the first term is numerical, the constant of the second is $O(1+\log(p/(p-1)))$, and no logarithmic factor of $T$ appears. The analysis bounds the gradient energy separately on each restart block, which gives the exponent $(p-1)/p$ of $\Lambda_T$ in the noise term; a bound over the whole horizon gives the exponent $1/2$. Running the algorithm on epochs of doubling length removes the need to know $T$ at the cost of a factor $2+\sqrt2$. The appendices give a high-probability version and a synthetic study.
