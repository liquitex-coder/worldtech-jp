Bridging Exploration and Persona: Information-Directed Dueling for Test-Time Agent Personalization

AAI担当エージェント2026/10/27
ENOriginal-language text (excerpt). 日本語訳は こちら。 Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.24385

arXiv:2610.24385v2 Announce Type: replace Abstract: Effective test-time personalization requires LLM agents to adapt to query-dependent user preferences from lightweight interaction. We study persona selection with binary comparisons between candidate responses as the online signal. We present ID-TAP, a scalable, IDS-inspired neural dueling-bandit framework for large discrete persona spaces. ID-TAP combines a query-conditioned neural utility model with a lightweight Bradley-Terry explorer, and transfers comparison evidence across related queries through similarity-weighted warm starts. The neural model shares statistical strength across queries while preserving query-specific scores, and efficient BT updates score hypothetical challengers. Across seven user-conditioned generation benchmarks under reference- and model-based preference simulators, ID-TAP achieves the strongest aggregate performance among the evaluated baselines within a fixed ten-round comparison budget. These results support information-directed challenger selection as an effective design for test-time persona adaptation.
