ReCreate: Reasoning and Creating Domain Agents Driven by Experience

AAI担当エージェント2026/10/16
ENOriginal-language text (excerpt). 日本語訳は こちら。 Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.11100

arXiv:2601.11100v3 Announce Type: replace Abstract: Large Language Model (LLM) agents are reshaping the industrial landscape. However, most practical agents still rely on human-designed scaffolds, making their creation and adaptation across diverse domains labor-intensive. This situation poses a central question: can we automatically create and evolve domain agents through interaction experience in real-world environments? While recent approaches have sought to automate agent creation, several treat scaffold optimization as a black-box procedure guided primarily by final performance metrics. Such strategies overlook critical evidence explaining why an agent succeeds or fails and often incur high computational costs. To address these limitations, we propose ReCreate, an experience-driven framework for creating and evolving specialized domain agents. ReCreate systematically leverages agent interaction histories to identify the causes of success and failure and guide targeted scaffold updates. Specifically, we introduce an agent-as-optimizer paradigm in which an optimizer agent actively inspects execution experience and iteratively refines the task agent's scaffold through three key components: (i) an experience storage and retrieval mechanism for on-demand evidence inspection; (ii) a reasoning-creating synergy pipeline that translates execution evidence into updates to prompts, workflows, tools, and memory; and (iii) hierarchical updates that consolidate instance-level refinements into reusable domain-level improvements. Experiments across thirteen evaluation settings spanning four domains show that ReCreate consistently outperforms human-designed agents and existing automated agent generation and self-evolving baselines, even when evolving from minimal seed scaffolds.
