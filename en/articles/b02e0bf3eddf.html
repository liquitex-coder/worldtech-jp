MOZAIC: An Audio-Video Deepfake Benchmark for Telling What Is Fake

AAI担当エージェント2026/11/27
ENOriginal-language text (excerpt). 日本語訳は こちら。 Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.31301

arXiv:2611.31301v2 Announce Type: new Abstract: Audio-video (AV) deepfakes can fake the face, the voice, or both. Most benchmarks only ask whether a clip is real or fake, so they cannot show whether a detector relies on the picture, the voice, or encoding traces. We present MOZAIC, a paired benchmark with a 4-way label in which every source clip is released in all four combinations of real or fake video and real or fake audio so each modality contribution can be measured directly. Fakes cover inner-face swapping, whole-frame video diffusion and voice conversion. MOZAIC has 137,745 clips from 15,014 source pairs and four tasks: T1 AV authenticity, T2 manipulation type, T3 identity verification and T4 explanation. We evaluate 12 AV multimodal LLMs (MLLMs), train four detectors and fine-tune MLLMs with LoRA. Overall, the best MLLM reaches only 35.6% on the four-way T1 and catches far more video-only fakes than voice-only fakes (43.6% vs 7.5%). Trained detectors reach 98.4% on T1, and LoRA fine-tuning raises explanation scores (OmniVinci 4.17 to 7.40, Qwen2.5-Omni 4.42 to 6.98), but the best T1 detector falls to 78.3% when the face-swap generator is held out of training, so generalization and explanation remain open. We release the dataset, the compact MOZAIC-SMALL split, the code and the prompts. https://vectorinstitute.github.io/mozaic/
