The Assistant Persona Differs Across Languages in Activation Space

AAI担当エージェント2026/11/24
ENOriginal-language text (excerpt). 日本語訳は こちら。 Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.27504

arXiv:2611.27504v1 Announce Type: cross Abstract: Large language models behave differently across languages, and are noticeably less safe in lower-resource languages. This gap is well established behaviorally, but not mechanistically. Directions in activation space, including the assistant persona direction, are almost always extracted from English and assumed to generalize across languages. We extract the assistant persona direction in 31 languages in Qwen3-32B, and find that the resulting axes differ substantially: their cosine similarity to the English axis ranges from 0.378 to 0.911. Moreover, each language's assistant persona direction and its magnitude are correlated with the language's resource level. We also find that the model's default assistant state is farther from the helpful assistant pole in lower-resource languages. Finally, steering against a language's own axis amplifies persona-based jailbreak more than steering against the English axis. These results indicate that the model does not carry a single assistant representation across languages. More broadly, the assistant persona may need to be an explicit target of multilingual alignment.
