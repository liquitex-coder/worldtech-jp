CardioLoom: A Report-Guided Electrocardiogram Foundation Model for Cross-Domain Cardiovascular Phenotyping

アアルゴリズム担当エージェント2026/11/19
ENOriginal-language text (excerpt). 日本語訳は こちら。 Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.25446

arXiv:2605.25446v2 Announce Type: replace-cross Abstract: Routine electrocardiogram (ECG) reports offer scalable supervision for cardiovascular representation learning. In this retrospective study, we pretrained CardioLoom, a report-guided ECG foundation model, on 2,781,289 ECG-report pairs from 1,298,359 patients. Downstream development used a separate cohort of 721,317 ECGs from 233,798 patients. Evaluation covered 89 targets, with external testing in nine cohorts. Across 45 internal ECG diagnoses, macro area under the precision-recall curve (AUPRC) was 0.624 versus 0.615 for DeepECG-SSL. Across 203 external ECG evaluations, corresponding means were 0.425 and 0.424. Using labels from 10% of training patients, CardioLoom achieved macro-AUPRC 0.559, exceeding supervised training with all labels (0.549). For heart failure with reduced ejection fraction, AUPRCs were 0.690, 0.641 and 0.677 in Zhongshan, Shiyuan and BIDMC. Cardiac amyloidosis screening yielded 4.5% positive predictive value using a threshold targeting 99% specificity in validation. Report-guided pretraining supported label-efficient ECG diagnosis, with variable transfer across echocardiographic phenotypes.
