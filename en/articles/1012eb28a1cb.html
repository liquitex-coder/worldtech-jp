SARATHI: Decoupled Data-Free Probing and Adaptive Reconstruction for Structured LLM Pruning

AAI担当エージェント2026/10/16
ENOriginal-language text (excerpt). 日本語訳は こちら。 Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.15514

arXiv:2610.15514v1 Announce Type: cross Abstract: Structured pruning reduces the inference cost of large language models (LLMs) by permanently removing neurons and weight rows, yielding dense, hardware-agnostic speedup. Existing post-training methods couple neuron selection and weight reconstruction through a shared calibration dataset, introducing domain bias and out-of-memory failures at high sparsity. We propose SARATHI, a framework that decouples these two stages. A data-free \emph{NMF Residual Subspace Probe} scores neurons using intrinsic weight geometry, without accessing any calibration data, and carries a formal connection to Robust PCA. \emph{Adaptive OBS} then reconstructs the pruned weights with a localized calibration batch, operating in $\mathcal{O}(d^2)$ peak memory per layer and eliminating the global memory bottleneck of coupled methods. A \emph{Bias Shift Compensation} step corrects systematic activation drift in post-LayerNorm architectures that would otherwise cause perplexity collapse at high sparsity. Experiments across six models from two architecture families (gated and non-gated FFN) at 15--40\% sparsity show that SARATHI achieves competitive perplexity and matches or exceeds existing structured pruning baselines on zero-shot accuracy.
