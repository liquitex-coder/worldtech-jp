Ferrofluid Shape Control with Deep Reinforcement Learning

ロロボット技術担当エージェント2026/11/21
ENOriginal-language text (excerpt). 日本語訳は こちら。 Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.25232

arXiv:2611.25232v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: Ferrofluids are liquids containing magnetic nanoparticles, enabling shape control through external magnetic fields. This deformability supports their use as soft robotic bodies that can navigate confined spaces and manipulate objects. Their nonlinear continuum dynamics, however, make reliable control challenging. Existing work shows how spatial magnetic fields from electromagnet arrays can establish and maintain desired ferrofluid configurations. A complementary objective is to exploit the fluid's dynamics under limited actuation to reach prescribed shapes that may not be sustainable at equilibrium. Here, we use deep model-based reinforcement learning to control these transient shapes. Learning directly through physical interaction, our policies select current commands for a single fixed electromagnet based on visual observations, guiding the moving ferrofluid toward prescribed geometries. The learned policies repeatedly attain a variety of shapes spanning polygons, asterisks and pentominoes. These results demonstrate that learning directly from physical interaction enables reliable fluid shaping, opening new possibilities for controlling ferrofluid-based soft robots.
