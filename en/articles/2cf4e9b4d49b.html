ScoutRoute: Efficient Mixture-of-Experts Routing Using Internal Language Model Signals

AAI担当エージェント2026/10/20
ENOriginal-language text (excerpt). 日本語訳は こちら。 Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.23443

arXiv:2610.23443v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: Efficient expert routing is a fundamental challenge in Mixture-of-Experts (MoE) systems, particularly for heterogeneous collections of independently trained Large Language Mod els (LLMs). Existing routing approaches typically rely on jointly trained gating networks, prompt-based predictors, or computationally expensive multi-model inference, limiting their flexibility and efficiency. This paper presents ScoutRoute, a lightweight post-hoc routing framework that predicts expert competence using internal language model signals extracted from a single forward pass through an inexpensive scout model. Hidden-state representations are used to estimate cross-model competence through lightweight logistic probes, while logit-derived confidence measures are incorporated within a domain-aware routing strategy for efficient expert selec tion without modifying the underlying language models. ScoutRoute isevaluatedon fiveopen-weightlanguagemodels across fourbenchmark datasets covering mathematical reasoning, code generation, commonsense reasoning, and multidis ciplinary knowledge. Experimental results show that ScoutRoute consistently outperforms individual routing signals while requiring only a single scout-model inference. The proposed competence probes also achieve superior calibration compared with conventional confidence measures, demonstrating that internal language model representations provide transferable information for accurate and efficient expert routing across heterogeneous language models
