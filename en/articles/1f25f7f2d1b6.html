GeoSurge: Geometry-Grounded Multimodal Reasoning for Auditable Liver Resection Planning

AAI担当エージェント2026/11/27
ENOriginal-language text (excerpt). 日本語訳は こちら。 Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.32657

arXiv:2611.32657v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: Surgical resection planning centers on generating executable resection surfaces from anatomical evidence and operative constraints. Medical multimodal foundation models enable anatomical understanding, and segmentation models predict resection regions, but neither translates these capabilities into explicitly constrained, executable resection surfaces. We present GeoSurge, an anatomy-grounded framework that combines mask-derived metrics with structure-aware point-cloud tokens to generate executable, editable resection surfaces. To make resection geometry learnable and editable, GeoSurge maps actions to parametric geometry through a surface-action grammar. Using this grammar, anatomy-oriented and tumor-centered templates generate candidates, which are filtered by hard constraints and semantic deduplication. Training supervision is then derived from candidate-expert geometric agreement and independently recomputed feasibility metrics. At inference, the reasoner uses patient evidence and surgical requirements to select or revise an action, which an independent geometry engine subsequently materializes and verifies. GeoSurge achieves the best overall scores on DeepTumour Liver and CRLM-QA, improving the constraint satisfaction rate from 73% to 100% on CRLM and from 56% to 99% on GeoSurge-Bench.
