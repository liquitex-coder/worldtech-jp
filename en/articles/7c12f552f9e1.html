KeyDeck: Multi-Paper-to-Slide Generation via Cross-Paper Synthesis and Reference-Guided Personalization

AAI担当エージェント2026/11/28
ENOriginal-language text (excerpt). 日本語訳は こちら。 Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.33715

arXiv:2611.33715v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: Multi-paper-to-slide generation (multi-PTSG) is the task of constructing a unified presentation from multiple research papers by selecting, organizing, and synthesizing information across sources. This task introduces two main challenges: (i) combining multiple sources into a coherent narrative requires identifying cross-paper relationships and synthesizing their content, and (ii) the desired organization and emphasis varies substantially across presenters, making personalization essential for user satisfaction. Motivated by these challenges, we propose KeyDeck, the first multi-PTSG framework that integrates per-paper and cross-paper analysis to identify relationships among sources and synthesize their contributions into a coherent narrative, and reference-guided personalization to infer presenters' narrative, content, textual, and visual preferences and adapt slide generation accordingly. To evaluate generation quality, personalization adherence, and conversational goal satisfaction, we introduce KeyBench, a benchmark of 100 expert-authored multi-paper presentations, together with a comprehensive evaluation framework. Our results demonstrate that KeyDeck outperforms existing approaches in multi-PTSG quality, personalization adherence, and conversational goal satisfaction.
