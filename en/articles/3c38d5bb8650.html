DINO-FIL: Multi-Layer Transformer Fusion for Generative Inpainting Forgery Localization

AAI担当エージェント2026/10/17
ENOriginal-language text (excerpt). 日本語訳は こちら。 Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.21780

arXiv:2610.21780v1 Announce Type: cross Abstract: We propose DINO-FIL, a transformer-based framework for pixel-level localization of generative image forgeries. The method leverages multi-layer representations from a pretrained DINOv3 ViT-H+/16 encoder combined with a lightweight fusion decoder to capture both semantic structure and fine-grained texture inconsistencies introduced by image editing. By aggregating intermediate transformer features across depths, the model improves sensitivity to subtle, spatially localized artifacts that are often missed by standard single-layer approaches. We evaluate DINO-FIL in a cross-dataset setting, training on B-Free and testing on Beyond the Brush, CocoGlide, TGIF, and SAGI-D. The model achieves strong generalization, outperforming existing baselines on average and on four of five evaluated datasets, while maintaining a high true negative rate on pristine images.
