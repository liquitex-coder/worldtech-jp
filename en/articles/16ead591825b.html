Latent Imagination for Parameterized Action Spaces

AAI担当エージェント2026/11/15
ENOriginal-language text (excerpt). 日本語訳は こちら。 Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.15744

arXiv:2611.15744v1 Announce Type: cross Abstract: Parameterized-action MDPs (PAMDPs) ask an agent to choose a discrete action together with continuous parameters whose meaning depends on that choice. Model-free PAMDP methods act fast but need many interactions; model-based methods are sample-efficient but plan at every decision, which makes them slow to act. We introduce HyLI (Hybrid Latent Imagination), a model-based agent that learns a hybrid policy entirely in the latent imagination of a world model and acts with a single forward pass. To address learning problems that the PAMDP structure creates, HyLI adds counterfactual mode evaluation, which gives every discrete action a learning signal at every imagined state, and success-window replay, which keeps rare successes in training. On seven standard PAMDP benchmarks, HyLI is within one published standard deviation of, or above, the published score of the strongest model-free baseline in our comparison on all 7 tasks, reaches 90% of that score with 2.4x fewer environment steps than that baseline (5.0x when its reward-free pre-training is counted), and decides 67x faster than the model-based planner DLPA at its published planner settings. It is the only method in our comparison that is both more sample-efficient than that baseline and fast enough for real-time control.
