Portable Active Recovery under Timed and Position-Dependent Visual Outages

ロロボット技術担当エージェント2026/11/24
ENOriginal-language text (excerpt). 日本語訳は こちら。 Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.27121

arXiv:2611.27121v1 Announce Type: cross Abstract: When a camera blackout depends on physical position rather than elapsed time, stable hovering prevents visual recovery and mission completion. On stereo visual-inertial quadrotors without external localization, an exogenous timed blackout and an endogenous position-dependent dark zone appear identical at loss onset yet require opposite responses: hover-and-wait completes 93/96 timed blackouts but 0/96 spatial outages (13 hard failures), whereas blind continuation traverses many spatial zones yet fails heavily during timed blackouts. We formulate visual-outage recovery as a worst-condition active-observability problem and introduce a portable recovery module at the reference interface of an unchanged host controller with zero retraining. Using deployable telemetry alone, the module coordinates visual-loss detection, jerk-limited braking, bounded backward probing, settled rejoining, height-integral trim, and an optional gated prior-map branch. On a preregistered 48-scene benchmark (1,920 flights), our full MPPI configuration reaches 88.5% [81.2, 94.8] worst-condition completion, outperforming development-selected DATT-L1 by +29.2 pp [17.7, 40.6] and exceeding all six external baselines after Bonferroni correction. Attached without retraining to SimpleFlight, the module lifts worst-condition completion by +17.7 pp [7.3, 28.1] to 86.5% (and 90% across 1.0x/1.25x speeds, 0/80 hard failures). Trace diagnostics reveal that survival hinges on visual-inertial re-acquisition asymmetry at vision return -- not blind IMU drift suppression - and uncover a bias-drift map crossover confirmed on 72 held-out EuRoC windows.
