BOP-Refer: Benchmark for Monocular 3D and 2D Object Grounding

AAI担当エージェント2026/11/28
ENOriginal-language text (excerpt). 日本語訳は こちら。 Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.34862

arXiv:2611.34862v1 Announce Type: cross Abstract: We introduce BOP-Refer, the first benchmark for monocular 3D and 2D grounding of everyday and industrial objects sourced from 9 diverse BOP datasets. We pair images from these datasets with VLM-generated and human-verified referring expressions, and derive accurate ground-truth 3D and 2D bounding boxes of the referred objects from their 3D models and 6DoF pose annotations. Our systematic evaluation of 17 frontier VLMs across the major proprietary and open-weight families (Gemini, GPT, Claude, Qwen, Gemma) shows that the best model, Gemini 4 Carbon, reaches 72.6% AP_IoU2D in 2D but only 12.4% AP_IoU3D in 3D, despite the more lenient IoU thresholds in 3D. The predicted 3D boxes are usually centered on the target objects in the image but often off in depth by more than the object's size, suggesting that metric scale estimation, which needs reliable object size priors, is currently by far the main source of error.
