From Rule-based to Agentic: LLM Agent based Real-time Control of Urban Drainage Systems for Flooding and Sewer Overflow Mitigation

AAI担当エージェント2026/11/27
ENOriginal-language text (excerpt). 日本語訳は こちら。 Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.32046

arXiv:2611.32046v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: Real-time control (RTC) for urban drainage systems (UDS) remains hampered by sparse in-network sensing. While heterogeneous multi-modal meteorological early warnings often contain decision-critical information for UDS RTC, none of the existing approaches can exploit such externally-sourced information within the control workflow. Here we introduce AgenticAI-Control, an agentic artificial intelligence framework able to assimilate multi-source meteorological early warnings in RTC. Two coupled large language model agents are integrated to generate control strategies by retrieving meteorological early-warnings and fusing them with domain engineering expertise. Validated via simulation studies of a combined sewer catchment in eastern China across 10 real-world rainfall events, the AgenticAI-Control achieved 69.1-90.6% reduction in aggregated flood and overflow volumes compared to the rule-based baseline control. Rigorously standardized prompt engineering and modular workflow constraints substantially compress performance disparities among four distinct LLMs. Our work establishes AgenticAI as a viable tool to unify multi-source meteorological monitoring and practitioner domain knowledge, surmounting longstanding monitoring limitations of UDS RTC and delivering cost-efficient, robust intelligent control solutions for sensor-sparse sewer infrastructures.
