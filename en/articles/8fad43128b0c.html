Quantum-JEPA: Quantum Born Priors for Latent-Action JEPA World Models

AAI担当エージェント2026/10/22
ENOriginal-language text (excerpt). 日本語訳は こちら。 Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.27921

arXiv:2610.27921v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: A single observation can admit several plausible futures, making uncertainty an essential part of world modelling. However, quantum generative models remain underexplored for this task. We introduce Quantum-JEPA, a latent-action JEPA framework that learns distributions of future representations using a conditional quantum Born prior. Circuit measurements generate binary latent actions, which a shared predictor maps to alternative future representations. We train the quantum prior through the quality and diversity of these predictions using a sampled energy objective. Our model learns distinct continuations on constructed multimodal tasks and captures latent-action correlations that support coordinated multi-agent futures. On D3IL, a benchmark of diverse human demonstrations, our trained models generate multiple obstacle-crossing routes from a common starting point without observed motion. On the controlled tasks, our classical and quantum prior implementations improve prediction validity and distributional fidelity over the evaluated variational baselines. Our quantum priors combine competitive predictive quality with a parameter-efficient sampling structure: they avoid an exponentially sized categorical output layer while supporting correlated latent actions. We further validate finite-shot parameter-shift training, including training with readout noise. Unlike most prior work, which focuses on simulation, we also verify the feasibility of inference with trained priors on a real QPU. These results establish a trainable quantum component for JEPA future prediction and a basis for exploring quantum-enhanced world models at larger scales.
