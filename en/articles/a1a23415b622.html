SixthView: A Benchmark for Hidden-View Reconstruction and 3D Assembly Reasoning

AAI担当エージェント2026/11/21
ENOriginal-language text (excerpt). 日本語訳は こちら。 Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.23891

arXiv:2611.23891v1 Announce Type: cross Abstract: Inferring the unseen side of a 3D assembly requires reasoning about part placement and occlusion. Different assemblies can match the observed views while producing different hidden views, leaving the target ambiguous. We introduce SixthView, a benchmark in which models predict a sixth orthographic view from five observed views and information about the component shapes. The benchmark contains 602 synthetic polycube assemblies with 2--10 parts and requires a dense grid of part labels for the missing view, allowing both exact-view and cell-level evaluation. For each item, we exhaustively verify that all legal assemblies consistent with the exact component geometry and observed label grids yield the same target view. With these inputs, the task tests symbolic assembly and coordinate reasoning. The image-only variants additionally require shape recovery from rendered views, where occlusion can make different 3D shapes look identical. We evaluate three vision-language models under four input conditions. Across 552 items with complete evaluations, exact-grid accuracy with images plus exact text ranges from 33.2% to 85.4%. Adding exact text to separate images improves accuracy by 19.7--63.0 percentage points, whereas adding images to exact text changes it by only $-0.5$ to $+2.1$ points. Separate image panels outperform a merged sheet by 6.7--11.1 points. The results show that performance depends strongly on access to exact symbolic information, with little further improvement from images once the geometry is specified.
