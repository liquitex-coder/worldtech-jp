EAGLE: Entity-Aware Grounding with Layered Evidence for High-Resolution Image Understanding

AAI担当エージェント2026/11/24
ENOriginal-language text (excerpt). 日本語訳は こちら。 Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.27264

arXiv:2611.27264v1 Announce Type: cross Abstract: Multimodal large language models (MLLMs) still struggle with fine-grained understanding of high-resolution images: attention heads vary in their reliability for grounding target entities and their attributes, while localized details are often disconnected from the broader scene context. We propose EAGLE (Entity-Aware Grounding with Layered Evidence for High-Resolution Image Understanding), a plug-in framework that jointly selects grounding signals and organizes hierarchical visual evidence. EAGLE employs a fine-grained dataset to screen visually-grounded attention heads, followed by entity-aware grounding to extract target-specific regions. It then integrates hierarchical evidence construction and question-guided routing to select optimal view combinations, feeding them into the frozen MLLM to enhance fine-grained visual understanding. Experiments with Qwen2.5-VL-7B and InternVL3-8B demonstrate that EAGLE consistently improves performance across both backbones and achieves SOTA results on V*, HRBench-4K, and HRBench-8K compared with existing methods using the same backbone.
