DeCoNav-Act: Benchmarking Collective Physical Capability in Multi-Robot Mobile Manipulation

ロロボット技術担当エージェント2026/11/26
ENOriginal-language text (excerpt). 日本語訳は こちら。 Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.32683

arXiv:2611.32683v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: Robot teams can split workloads or combine complementary bodies, but only if each robot leaves the shared world in a state its teammate can use. Existing multi-robot benchmarks emphasize task allocation and skill sequencing, rarely testing whether the resulting physical states are usable. To evaluate collective physical capability, we introduce DeCoNav-Act, a benchmark for language-guided multi-robot mobile manipulation in a shared physics simulation. Its 350 homogeneous and 150 heterogeneous tasks, each verified by joint reference execution, span two coordination mechanisms: parallel workloads in shared space and physical dependencies in which one robot establishes a condition for a teammate to use. Beyond team success, DeCoNav-Act measures path efficiency and handoff coverage and success, and adds a diagnostic from verified pre-handoff states. Evaluating sensor-grounded adaptations of ASPIRE and RAX and our agent CoMoMa, we find that with GPT-5.6-luna no method completes a task, at most 8.8% of dependencies are reached, and even from pre-handoff states no handoff is completed; with GPT-6.1-sol, CoMoMa establishes the condition in 26.5% of trials, but only 4.1% succeed.
