ScienceBuddy-PV: A Verifier-Governed Recursive Improvement Protocol for Panvascular Intervention Research

AAI担当エージェント2026/10/16
ENOriginal-language text (excerpt). 日本語訳は こちら。 Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.14523

arXiv:2610.14523v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: ScienceBuddy-PV adapts the ScienceBuddy recursive-in-recursive design to panvascular intervention research through a structured evidence ledger, device-vessel ontology, abstention contract and retained failure bank. Recursive harness optimization and outer-loop model learning, including reinforcement learning, are specified but not executed. We audit a fixed component, rather than the complete agent system, using separate ARCADE syntax and stenosis tasks (1,000 training, 200 validation and 300 test images per task). A shared task-masked convolutional model was trained once per representation setting; its parameters and the heuristic support gate were then held fixed, with the stenosis threshold selected on validation data. On the locked test sets, syntax Dice was 0.475 (95% CI 0.459-0.490) and stenosis Dice was 0.231 (95% CI 0.214-0.249). Stenosis pixel AUROC was 0.957 and AUPRC was 0.180 at prevalence 0.00975. The gate accepted 76.7% of stenosis-frame outputs versus 100% for a score-only comparator. In a constructed mask audit, it accepted 44.3% of reference candidates and rejected 55.7%, exposing the cost of abstention. Acceptance does not establish correctness or clinical safety. This public-data component audit makes support and abstention measurable and provides a reproducible protocol for future collaborative agent studies. It does not demonstrate recursive improvement, patient-level validity or clinical device recommendations.
