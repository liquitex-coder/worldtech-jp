Find the Fungible: Variable-Resolution Tokenization that Compacts Semantics with a Granularity Dial as a VLM "Catalyst"

AAI担当エージェント2026/11/27
ENOriginal-language text (excerpt). 日本語訳は こちら。 Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.18690

arXiv:2610.18690v3 Announce Type: replace-cross Abstract: Efficient vision-language models should preserve a useful representation of the whole image, even under tight budgets. However, aggressive pruning or indiscriminate merging can erase critical details and disrupt the spatial structure. We thus propose Varest, which maximizes semantic fungibility: tokens describing coherent content can be summarized together with minimal information loss. Object boundaries distilled from a visual foundation model guide grouping, while text localization protects details needed for reading. At lower budgets, Varest merges the image into coarser semantic regions, while maintaining full spatial coverage. After early language decoder layers process this representation, a learned selector prunes visual tokens that later layers no longer need, reducing repeated access to already integrated information. Across three LLaVA backbones and 13 benchmarks, Varest outperforms 10 training-free and 5 trained baselines in mean performance retention at every evaluated budget, preserving 86.10-92.33% of uncompressed performance using only 5.56% of tokens. This SOTA performance further generalizes to modern VLMs like Qwen3.8-27B.
