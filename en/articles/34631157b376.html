Making Evaluation Explicit Is Not Enough: Supervising the Evaluation Process in Generative Reward Models

AAI担当エージェント2026/11/27
ENOriginal-language text (excerpt). 日本語訳は こちら。 Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.32161

arXiv:2611.32161v1 Announce Type: cross Abstract: Generative reward models (GRMs) make language-model evaluation more explicit by generating critiques, rubrics, or intermediate analyses before predicting a final preference. However, explicit evaluation does not necessarily imply a reliable evaluation process: under outcome-only supervision, a model may reach the correct final preference while producing weak or unsupported intermediate judgments, turning the evaluation into a post-hoc rationalization. We introduce STEP-GRM, a framework that directly supervises the intermediate evaluation process of rubric-based GRMs. In addition to conventional preference supervision, STEP-GRM provides structural guidance for rubric construction and local supervision for criterion-level judgments, encouraging the model to learn not only the correct final preference but also a more reliable process for reaching it. Extensive experiments show that STEP-GRM achieves strong performance on standard reward-modeling benchmarks while improving the consistency and correctness of intermediate evaluations. Moreover, reward signals produced by STEP-GRM lead to better downstream policy optimization, demonstrating that more reliable evaluation processes can translate into practical gains beyond reward-model accuracy.
