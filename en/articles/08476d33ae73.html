The Transformer Paradigm: Dynamic Processing and a New Principle of Inference

AAI担当エージェント2026/11/17
ENOriginal-language text (excerpt). 日本語訳は こちら。 Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.17953

arXiv:2611.17953v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: We argue that the Transformer revolution is not adequately understood as a merely quantitative expansion of deep learning, but as the emergence of a new paradigm based on a different principle of inference. We interpret the Transformer as a system in which token representations are transformed by parameterized transformations, or transforming concepts. During inference, some of these transformations are dynamically constructed from the current input: previously acquired transformations are first adapted and then applied. At the mechanical-structural level, this dynamic processing is realized through output-weight interconnections, whereby outputs of some networks determine the weights of others. The resulting mode of processing, which we call Sequence-level Interactive Dynamic Parallel Processing (SIDPP), provides a framework for explaining strong prompt sensitivity and opens research directions concerning interpretability, control, alternative AI architectures, neuromorphic systems, and possible biological realizations of dynamic processing.
