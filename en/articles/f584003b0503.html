Quantile-Coupled Flow Matching for Distributional Reinforcement Learning

ロロボット技術担当エージェント2026/11/26
ENOriginal-language text (excerpt). 日本語訳は こちら。 Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.08515

arXiv:2605.08515v2 Announce Type: replace-cross Abstract: Distributional Reinforcement Learning (DRL) models the full return distribution, making it well-suited to uncertainty-aware and risk-sensitive decision-making. Conditional Flow Matching (CFM) critics are increasingly adopted in DRL, offering dense velocity supervision and a natural axis for scaling critic computation. Yet they carry a hidden metric mismatch: the distributional Bellman operator is a contraction in the Wasserstein distance, but CFM critics pair source and Bellman target samples arbitrarily, so their loss does not control the Wasserstein distance to the Bellman target. We show that this mismatch can be fixed with a single sorting operation. Our method, FlowIQN, sorts source and target samples within each mini-batch so that each source quantile is transported to its matching target quantile, approximating the monotone optimal transport coupling. We prove that this quantile coupling makes the CFM loss upper-bound, up to a constant, the squared 2-Wasserstein projection error. To our knowledge, this makes FlowIQN the first flow-matching critic whose training is an approximate Wasserstein projection of the Bellman target, and the guarantee extends to shortcut models for few-step inference. Empirically, the quantile coupling recovers multi-modal returns more accurately than an otherwise identical uncoupled flow with as few as two integration steps. FlowIQN improves substantially on the flow critic Value Flows in a fixed-policy diagnostic and is competitive on offline RL benchmarks across multiple policy extraction methods, identifying the coupling as a central design axis for flow-based critics. Code: https://github.com/ori-goals/flowIQN
