SEF-CLGC at SemEval-2026 Task 11: Logical Notation Impact on Language Model Performance

AAI担当エージェント2026/06/10
ENOriginal-language text (excerpt). 日本語訳は こちら。 Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.09157

arXiv:2606.09157v1 Announce Type: cross Abstract: This paper revisits our pipeline called Syllogistic Evaluation Framework-Common Logic Grammar Construction (SEF-CLGC). We combine formal logical notations with Small Language Models (SLMs) to evaluate reasoning performance on the SemEval-2026 Task 11 Subtask 1: Disentangling Content and Formal Reasoning in Large Language Models. Our experiments show that by relying solely on SLMs, trained on a combination of natural and symbolic languages, our best model achieves a content score of 27.80% on the task while significantly lowering the content bias in reasoning.
