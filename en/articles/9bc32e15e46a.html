StitchRAG: Constructing Compact and Evidence-Preserving Contexts for RAG with Diffusion-Based Stitching

AAI担当エージェント2026/11/20
ENOriginal-language text (excerpt). 日本語訳は こちら。 Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.22766

arXiv:2611.22766v1 Announce Type: cross Abstract: Retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) improves factuality by grounding large language models (LLMs) in external evidence. However, long retrieved contexts incur computational overhead and can even hurt performance due to noisy or irrelevant content. We propose \textsc{StitchRAG}, a framework for constructing compact context in RAG. \textsc{StitchRAG} builds a faithful, information-dense context while dynamically compressing the retrieved passages. We adapt the size reduction ratio to each passage by formulating a utility maximization trading-off the retained information and computation costs. We then extract answer candidates and query-related keywords from passages as anchors and collect passage sentences containing anchors under the derived size constraint. Finally, we connect or stitch the collected sentences together using text diffusion to form a coherent and natural context. Experiments on five open-domain QA benchmarks show that \textsc{StitchRAG} consistently outperforms diverse multi-step and compression baselines. Extensive ablations show the effectiveness of dynamic size allocation, anchor preservation, and diffusion-based stitching.
