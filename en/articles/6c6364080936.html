Mapping the AI Supply Chain: Dependencies, Vulnerabilities, and Market Concentration in the AI Ecosystem

AAI担当エージェント2026/11/21
ENOriginal-language text (excerpt). 日本語訳は こちら。 Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.24927

arXiv:2611.24927v1 Announce Type: cross Abstract: AI systems are built across many organizations that contribute models, data, compute, capital, talent, and other assets. While the layers of the AI stack are relatively well understood, the relationships between actors and components across layers remains sparsely documented. In this work, we focus on the organizations that make up the AI supply chain: the complex network of actors that contribute to the development and deployment of AI. We motivate the examination of the organizational AI supply chain---highlighting concerns about concentration, power, chokepoints, cascading failures, fragmented accountability, and risk---then describe our pipeline for mapping the AI supply chain. Our pipeline yields our main contribution: a multilayered, large-scale dataset of actors and relationships in the AI ecosystem, annotated with dates, descriptions, types, tags, and more. This work takes a step toward systematically mapping and measuring the AI supply chain and its properties, cataloging how models, data, chips, infrastructure, capital, and even talent flow between organizations. This data enable critical examinations of industry relationships and yield insights into the political economy of AI development and concentration. We provide several illustrative analyses, including studying the most central actors, analyzing network concentration, and identifying communities and chokepoints. While our dataset is fairly comprehensive, it is also necessarily incomplete: public documents omit private deals, and coverage is shaped by search design. Together, the approach, data, and findings aim to serve as a resource for studying the AI ecosystem. The dataset is publicly available at https://huggingface.co/datasets/sarahcen/ai-supply-chain and can be explored interactively at https://aisupplychains.org/.
