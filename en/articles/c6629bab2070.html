Modeling the Frictional Contact of Continuum Robots with Environments through Linear Complementarity Problem

ロロボット技術担当エージェント2026/11/17
ENOriginal-language text (excerpt). 日本語訳は こちら。 Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.18546

arXiv:2611.18546v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: Accurate friction modeling in continuum robots is essential for precise motion control and path planning. However, current approaches make predictions only for the mechanism's internal friction that is limited by the boundary forces of the Coulomb friction cone, which fail to characterize the static friction and motion hysteresis. In this paper, we propose a novel friction model based on complementarity constraints under the Coulomb friction assumption. This model predicts various friction effects caused by environmental interactions, including both static and sliding frictions and hysteresis behavior, as well as the corresponding robot deformation induced by these interactions, which are crucial for the practical motion planning and control of continuum robots. Additionally, real-time implementation of this model can be achieved, meeting the demand for computational efficiency in robotic applications. Experimental validations demonstrated that this model is able to reduce the maximum tip position error from 45.4 mm in the frictionless model to 5.85 mm in our friction-included model with a 287.9 mm-long tendon-driven continuum robot.
