SecID: An LLM-Guided Framework for Threat-Aware Secondary Asset Identification with Quantitative RTL Analysis

AAI担当エージェント2026/11/28
ENOriginal-language text (excerpt). 日本語訳は こちら。 Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.33453

arXiv:2611.33453v1 Announce Type: cross Abstract: The growing complexity of modern hardware designs is making security assurance increasingly difficult. One of the foundational steps in pre-silicon security verification is identifying security assets. While primary assets are often evident from design functionality, the internal signals that can facilitate their compromise are much less apparent. Complex data- and control-flow dependencies create many such internal security-critical locations, whose exploitation depends on the threat-specific attack mechanism---these are the secondary assets. To precisely identify these, we propose SecID, a multi-agent framework that combines deterministic RTL analysis with bounded dynamic evaluation and LLM reasoning to identify threat-specific secondary assets. Each asset decision is supported by functional influence, threat features, and explicit attack mechanisms. We demonstrate SecID on seven open-source SoC and IP designs, including openMSP430, NEORV32, and CV32E40P. The experimental results are corroborated through comparison with prior work, fault simulation, and formal analysis, and further evaluated through pipeline- and final-stage ablation, threshold-sensitivity studies, and final-stage repeatability analysis. Together, these evaluations show that SecID can identify highly threat-relevant secondary assets with respect to a given primary asset, providing a scalable path toward hardware-security verification.
