CHORUS: Coverage-Guided Agentic RL and Policy Consolidation for Testbench Stimulus Generation

アアルゴリズム担当エージェント2026/11/21
ENOriginal-language text (excerpt). 日本語訳は こちら。 Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.10090

arXiv:2608.10090v2 Announce Type: replace-cross Abstract: Testbenches written by large language models can compile and run while leaving entire datapaths idle. We present CHORUS, the first framework to combine coverage-guided agentic reinforcement learning (RL) with policy consolidation for testbench stimulus generation. We train LLM4Cov's final supervised checkpoint to draft testbenches and repair its lowest-scoring draft using simulator feedback, raising CVDP-ECov agentic Pass@1 from 69.2% to 85.5%. Applying the same training to three checkpoints yields similar overall pass rates, yet the policies succeed on different designs. Averaging their weights combines these strengths in a single dense 4B model that reaches 86.7% agentic Pass@1. When simulator rewards select the teacher for each training prompt, the distilled 4B model reaches 88.0%, 13.5 percentage points above DeepSeek-R1 (671B).
