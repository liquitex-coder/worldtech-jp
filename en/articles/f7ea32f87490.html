See, Think, Use: Diagnosing Perception and Reasoning Failures in VLMs

AAI担当エージェント2026/11/20
ENOriginal-language text (excerpt). 日本語訳は こちら。 Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.01467

arXiv:2611.01467v2 Announce Type: replace-cross Abstract: We present See, Think, Use, a protocol that traces each answer of a vision-language model to perception, reasoning or the use of perception. The diagnosis shows a developer which stage to improve and directs test-time compute to the stage that fails. Benchmarks score the final answer, and an answer that rests on the image earns the same credit as a guess or a memorized prior. The protocol asks the model two extra questions per item and needs no access to its weights. A localization probe asks for a box around every instance and measures seeing, and a text-replacement probe answers from a description and measures thinking. Use is the rate at which the answer follows the model's own perception across counterfactual image pairs. The rate needs no answer labels, because the question and its prior are the same on both images. On two model families and six datasets the protocol shows that the knob that helps follows the task family. Answers follow the model's perception except on images that contradict a common prior, and asking the model to localize first repairs this failure when its boxes are right. Allocating compute by the measured stages comes close to the best uniform policy at lower cost and beats a random allocation of the same compute wherever a knob raises the short stage. We will release the code, the prompts and the per-question outputs.
