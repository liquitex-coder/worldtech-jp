InterConRank: Joint Node Centrality in Interconnected Heterogeneous Networks

AAI担当エージェント2026/11/28
ENOriginal-language text (excerpt). 日本語訳は こちら。 Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.34763

arXiv:2611.34763v1 Announce Type: cross Abstract: Two nodes with identical connections within their own layer can differ in importance if their neighbors in another layer occupy different positions in the network. We introduce InterConRank, a centrality algorithm that computes a separate node ranking for each layer of an interconnected network. Directed dependencies specify which layers supply scores to each receiving layer. InterConRank normalizes each type of link separately and updates all layers together, so a score can cross into a layer in one iteration and propagate along its internal links in the next. We prove convergence of this joint update and evaluate the resulting rankings on four scholarly networks. On all four networks, InterConRank gives the highest rank correlations with both h-index and total citations for nodes in the collaboration layer.
