Training in Deployment: Human-in-the-loop Online Post-Training for Robot Manipulation

ロロボット技術担当エージェント2026/10/23
ENOriginal-language text (excerpt). 日本語訳は こちら。 Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.29264

arXiv:2610.29264v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: Large pretrained robot foundation models provide strong priors for manipulation, yet adapting them to target tasks with offline teleoperated demonstrations often fails to cover the contact-rich, recovery, and precision-critical states encountered during deployment. Existing methods mitigate this issue by augmenting offline demonstrations with human-in-the-loop data or online reinforcement learning. Despite this, offline post-training adapts slowly because it cannot immediately absorb deployment experience, while online RL typically relies on value functions or auxiliary actor-critic models, increasing both training complexity and instability. We present TIDE (Training In DEployment), a human-in-the-loop online post-training system. TIDE introduces a provenance-conditioned supervised learning framework that updates the policy online from human interventions and policy-generated rollouts collected during deployment, without a learned critic, reward model, or action scorer. Across four real-world bimanual manipulation tasks, TIDE improves milestone-normalized task progress and binary full-task completion over the warm-start policy and matched additional offline demonstrations, and performs comparably to the fixed-policy collection baseline while making updated checkpoints available during deployment. Each task-specific online post-training session, from the start of deployment rollouts until the final checkpoint is available, completes in less than one hour while learning and checkpoint handling run asynchronously.
