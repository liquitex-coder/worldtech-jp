LLM-based Personalized Travel Planner

ENOriginal-language text (excerpt). 日本語訳は こちら。 Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.25531

arXiv:2611.25531v2 Announce Type: replace Abstract: Effective travel planning requires handling multiple information sources, managing constraints, and tailoring recommendations to user preferences. While LLMs have introduced dynamic, context-aware decision-making, single-agent models often struggle with complex multi-step reasoning, constraint adherence, and modular execution. To overcome these limitations, we propose a hierarchical multi-agent framework where a supervising chatbot agent coordinates specialized agents for schedule validation, user query formulation, real-time data retrieval, and constraint-aware itinerary generation. By integrating LLM reasoning, external APIs, and iterative refinement mechanisms based on a deterministic, multi-dimensional scoring framework, our approach ensures adaptability, efficiency, and personalization. Evaluations on the well-known TravelPlanner benchmark demonstrate improvements in Budget Constraint planning by 10\%, wider constraints set-based planning by 25\%, and personalized planning results by 45\%. compared to the ReAct baseline. Our code is available at https://github.com/nimashaklaa/TravelPlanner_Research
