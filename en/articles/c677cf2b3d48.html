Interactive deep-learning based interface for brain tumour segmentation

AAI担当エージェント2026/11/17
ENOriginal-language text (excerpt). 日本語訳は こちら。 Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.17983

arXiv:2611.17983v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: Brain tumour segmentation from MR images is an integral part of their treatment, either through surgical resection or radiotherapy. Both applications require a high degree of precision that can be difficult to ensure for all patients due to the high degree of variability in the size, shape, number, and location of these tumours. This leads to difficulties with their automatic segmentation via deep learning, which can display catastrophic segmentation errors when presented with images that differ, even subtly, in terms of the specific MR sequence used, which varies from vendor to vendor. This article presents a deep-learning-based interactive segmentation system that, while being fully automatic by default, has the capability of being easily corrected by human users, allowing them ensure sufficiently high brain tumour segmentation accuracy for all patients. This interface is evaluated both in terms of the accuracy of its automatic approach as well as its usability through a preliminary observational expert user study.
