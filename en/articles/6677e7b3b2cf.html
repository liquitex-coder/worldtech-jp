PhysScope: Agentic Multimodal Physics Reasoning with Grounded Visual Memory

AAI担当エージェント2026/11/20
ENOriginal-language text (excerpt). 日本語訳は こちら。 Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.23010

arXiv:2611.23010v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: Vision-language models (VLMs) can correctly interpret physics diagrams yet apply constraints outside the states or conditions in which they hold, causing premise errors to propagate through subsequent calculations. We introduce PhysScope, an inference-time visual harness that supports self-correction of these scope mismatches without updating model weights. PhysScope enables the model to link physical scopes directly to its visual observations and explicitly constructs derived views to support reasoning. The harness imposes explicit constraints on state distinctions, conditions, and physical scopes during model reasoning, and checks agent-proposed revisions against sources and scopes. Accepted revisions invalidate registered dependents, thereby maintaining scope-aware working memory. On PhysAlign, PhysScope achieves higher solving scores than direct solving across all six evaluated VLMs. With Gemini-3.8-flash, it outperforms Canvas-CoT by 9.34 percentage points and substantially improves solving accuracy on the out-of-distribution (OOD) PhysUniBench benchmark. Ablation studies support explicit scope management and dependency maintenance as design principles for multimodal physics reasoning agents.
