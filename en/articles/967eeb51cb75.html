Specifically, FrameCheck 1) guides the draft with professional examples that report a change of similar magnitude, 2) flags judgments that professionals would not use and causes the input does not give, and 3) removes them by rule, without a model call. In experiments on 17 open LLMs and 3M drafts, FrameCheck removes almost all unsupported framing from drafts and brings the judgment rate close to that of the writers of 10-K filings, the annual reports that US firms file with regulators. Code is available at github.com/seunghan96/framecheck.
