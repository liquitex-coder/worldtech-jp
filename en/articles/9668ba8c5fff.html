X-RIC: An Agentic RAN-Intelligent Controller Benchmark for Enhancing Reinforcement Learning

AAI担当エージェント2026/10/17
ENOriginal-language text (excerpt). 日本語訳は こちら。 Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.22269

arXiv:2610.22269v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: The O-RAN ALLIANCE introduces architectural flexibility through disaggregation and AI-driven control. While the RAN Intelligent Controller (RIC) enables centralized optimization, current AI approaches lack reasoning capabilities and explainability. We propose eXplainable RIC (X-RIC), a novel framework integrating agentic AI into O-RAN architectures. X-RIC combines reactive Reinforcement Learning (RL) with strategic agentic reasoning through: (i) automated Behavioral Cloning (BC) using domain heuristics for sample-efficient training, (ii) a Telecom Language Model (TLM), a transformer-based reasoning module providing contextual abstraction and interpretability, and (iii) closed-loop co-adaptation within the Near-RT RIC control loop enabling continuous policy refinement. To ensure deployment readiness, we introduce BARX (Benchmarking Agentic Reasoning for RAN), a domain-specific evaluation framework assessing agentic control across latency, compliance, and determinism metrics. We validate X-RIC on SLA-aware network slicing in simulation and O-RAN-compliant testbed deployments. X-RIC achieves 89% operational cost reduction while maintaining SLA compliance, converging 75% faster with 50% fewer training steps and 46% lower variance.
