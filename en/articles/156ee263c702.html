EviDARL: Evidence-Centric Dual-Agent Reasoning via Alternating Reinforcement Learning for Multi-Table Question Answering

AAI担当エージェント2026/11/20
ENOriginal-language text (excerpt). 日本語訳は こちら。 Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.22310

arXiv:2611.22310v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: Despite advances in LLM-based table reasoning, most studies focus on single-table settings. Multi-table QA requires multi-hop reasoning over complex table relationships and evidence dispersed across long contexts, often without verifiable intermediate traces. To address these challenges, we introduce EviDA, an evidence-centric dual-agent framework comprising an Evidence Construction Agent and a Reasoning and Answering Agent. The Evidence Construction Agent generates and executes table-manipulation programs over complete tables to produce question-specific evidence and verifiable intermediate traces, simplifying subsequent reasoning. The Reasoning and Answering Agent then performs multi-hop reasoning over this evidence to derive final answers. We further introduce EviDARL, which combines agent-specific fine-grained rewards with parameter-efficient alternating reinforcement learning to improve collaboration between the agents. By optimizing each agent in turn while holding the other fixed, EviDARL enables bidirectional adaptation between evidence construction and answer reasoning. Experiments on MMQA and TQA-Bench demonstrate that EviDARL substantially outperforms competitive open-model and multi-table reasoning baselines under constrained model and training budgets.
