ProMORNA: An End-to-End Generative Framework for Full-Length mRNA Design

AAI担当エージェント2026/10/24
ENOriginal-language text (excerpt). 日本語訳は こちら。 Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.01513

arXiv:2605.01513v2 Announce Type: replace-cross Abstract: Designing therapeutic messenger RNA (mRNA) requires generating full-length transcripts that optimally balance intracellular stability and translation efficiency. While generative models have advanced mRNA design, existing approaches rely on region-wise generation, expensive inference-time search, or target-specific model updates. To overcome these limitations, we introduce ProMORNA, a zero-shot, end-to-end generative framework for full-length mRNA design. ProMORNA aligns the design process with modern Large Language Model training pipelines: it utilizes a BART-style architecture pre-trained on over 6 million natural protein-mRNA pairs, followed by a two-stage post-training process comprising cold-start supervised fine-tuning and multi-objective reinforcement learning. By standardizing and aggregating relative advantages at the individual metric level with domain-specific stabilization, our post-training stage directly aligns the generative policy with conflicting biological constraints while avoiding direct scalarization of raw objective scores. In silico evaluations demonstrate that ProMORNA pushes the Pareto frontier of predicted stability and translation efficiency beyond standard baselines and GEMORNA, a leading state-of-the-art method. In three independent in vitro Fluc2P experiments, Candidate 2 achieved a 12-72h reporter-activity AUC 3.33 times that of the conventional Benchmark (Holm-adjusted $P=0.01285$), while its estimated 1.29-fold advantage over the reported best GEMORNA candidate remained exploratory. These results support ProMORNA as a promising framework for generating functionally active full-length mRNA candidates.
