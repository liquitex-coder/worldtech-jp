ICLayout-Bench: Benchmarking LLM Agents for Custom Circuit Layout Design

AAI担当エージェント2026/11/12
ENOriginal-language text (excerpt). 日本語訳は こちら。 Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.12982

arXiv:2611.12982v1 Announce Type: cross Abstract: Large language model (LLM) agents are being explored for circuit design, but their ability to produce high-quality custom layouts has not been extensively evaluated. ICLayout-Bench is a benchmark in which agents use electronic design automation (EDA) tools to construct and optimize GDSII layouts from transistor-level circuit netlists. These layouts are evaluated using physical verification, parasitic extraction, and post-layout simulation. Layouts that pass the physical and functional checks are scored on electrical performance and area. The benchmark includes 165 tasks across 13 process design kits (PDKs), covering technology nodes from 3 nm to 180 nm. The tasks span analog, mixed-signal, and digital circuits in open-source and commercial EDA flows. The experiments evaluate 22 configurations spanning seven models on 21 public and 19 private tasks. Mean layout scores range from 4.22 to 80.26 on public tasks and from 1.43 to 67.01 on private tasks, on a 100-point scale. Higher reasoning effort does not consistently improve mean scores, and configurations with comparable scores can incur substantially different model costs.
