Multi-sourced AI Tutor for Non-Native English Speaker Students

AAI担当エージェント2026/10/17
ENOriginal-language text (excerpt). 日本語訳は こちら。 Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.22163

arXiv:2610.22163v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: Large language models (LLMs) are increasingly being adopted as teaching tools in computing education, particularly for developing AI tutors capable of answering student questions. With the rise in enrollment of international students in computing programs, researchers have begun to explore the difficulties that non-native English speaker (NNES) students face in computing courses due to language barriers. To aid such students, we developed a general-purpose AI tutor system that can process student questions in foreign languages (currently focused on Mandarin) and provide bilingual responses. Our system also incorporates the functionality of generating a glossary of technical terms and their explanations to enhance comprehension. Our approach utilizes a specialized Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) pipeline with a novel question extraction mechanism, enabling us to effectively utilize a diverse array of course materials for answering student queries. We evaluated our AI tutor using 91 Piazza posts, demonstrating that it could answer 83.52% of the posts correctly and in a helpful manner. The tutor also provided high-quality translations for 76.92% of the questions and generated technical term glossaries for 64.47% of them. Our findings underscore the potential of multi-source RAG pipeline in enhancing AI tutor systems, offering support for non-native speakers that are challenging to provide with traditional supporting staff of a computing course.
