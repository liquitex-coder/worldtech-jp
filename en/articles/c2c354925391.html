VRLLM: Turning LLMs into Powerful Tabular Classifiers

アアルゴリズム担当エージェント2026/11/19
ENOriginal-language text (excerpt). 日本語訳は こちら。 Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.22308

arXiv:2611.22308v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: Large Language Models (LLMs) have shown strong performance on text, but whether pretrained LLMs are effective for large-scale tabular classification remains unclear, as many previous works demonstrate that LLMs' tabular data classification performance falls behind tree-based models or neural architectures designed for tabular data. At Visa Research, we managed to fine-tune a pretrained Llama 3.2 1B LLM on billions of rows of labeled tabular data and make it outperform a wide array of tabular classifiers and even our competitive production deep learning model. For ease of reference, we refer to the model as the Visa Research LLM (VRLLM). In this paper, we share how we achieved this exciting result, covering data preparation, training recipe, ablation studies of design choices and deployment validation. We also benchmark frontier LLMs on the same task and analyze their prediction behavior across model families and generations. Our interpretability experiments suggest that VRLLM learns feature-specific predictive associations, while component ablations indicate that the earliest two FFN layers contribute to the emergence of linearly decodable task information. Our paper provides an engineering recipe and a focused empirical case study on adapting compact LLMs to industrial tabular prediction tasks.
