DGF-Asym: Adaptive Peer Weighting for Collective Estimation under Noisy and Faulty Sensing

AAI担当エージェント2026/11/19
ENOriginal-language text (excerpt). 日本語訳は こちら。 Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.22532

arXiv:2611.22532v1 Announce Type: cross Abstract: Collective estimation under unreliable sensing requires robots to adaptively balance personal observations and social information from peers. We propose DGF-Asym, a decentralized collective estimation method extending the DeGroot-Friedkin model with asymmetric peer weighting based on retrospective predictive performance. The method supports consensus formation without prior sensor-noise statistics or explicit fault models while keeping inter-robot communication asynchronous. We evaluate DGF-Asym in simulated collective navigation under noisy observations and time-varying sensor faults. Compared with three baselines, it produces more stable estimates and more reliably sustains the decision criterion, at the cost of longer decision times. However, overall performance depends on fault ratio, group size, and influence-threshold selection. Our results demonstrate the potential of adaptive peer weighting for stable model-free collective estimation under unreliable sensing.
