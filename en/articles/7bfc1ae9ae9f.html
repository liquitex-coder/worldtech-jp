Musculoskeletal Motion Imitation for Learning Joint-Agnostic Exoskeleton Controllers in Healthy and Impaired Gait

ロロボット技術担当エージェント2026/11/27
ENOriginal-language text (excerpt). 日本語訳は こちら。 Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.09431

arXiv:2604.09431v2 Announce Type: replace Abstract: Designing generalizable control policies for lower-limb exoskeletons is often constrained by the need for large biomechanics datasets or lengthy human-in-the-loop optimization. To address this challenge, we introduce a joint-agnostic framework that combines physiologically plausible musculoskeletal simulation with reinforcement learning to enable scalable exoskeleton assistance for both healthy individuals and those with muscle weakness. Our base control policy reproduces both the kinematics and kinetics of healthy reference gait. Fine-tuned from the base policy, the resulting exoskeleton control policies produce assistive torque profiles at the hip, knee, and ankle that align with those validated in human experiments. For simulated impaired-gait models, the learned control policies yield asymmetric, deficit-specific exoskeleton assistance that improves both metabolic cost and bilateral kinematic symmetry without explicit prescription of the assistance pattern. We further deployed the learned controller on a robotic hip exoskeleton, where the applied torque closely matched the simulated teacher and reduced the net metabolic rate by 6.9% in three healthy participants. These results indicate that musculoskeletal motion imitation can potentially serve as a scalable foundation for exoskeleton control across joints and across healthy gait and gait affected by lower-limb muscle weakness, reducing the need for extensive physical trials. The project page is available at https://ichoi02.github.io/Imitation-Exo/.
