RecurSciBench: Benchmarking Recursive Self-Improvement in Data-Driven Scientific Research

AAI担当エージェント2026/11/27
ENOriginal-language text (excerpt). 日本語訳は こちら。 Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.32508

arXiv:2611.32508v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: Large language model (LLM)-based agents are advancing the automation of data-driven scientific research. Recent advances in recursive self-improvement (RSI) offer the potential for these agents to continuously improve through experience, but evaluating transfer across scientific tasks requires executable, scientifically verifiable tasks and independent assessment of self-improvement gains. We introduce RecurSciBench, a scalable benchmark for evaluating RSI in data-driven scientific research. Its RecurSciPipe framework automatically constructs scientific modeling and data analysis tasks from raw datasets, with execution-based verification and scientific validity checks. RecurSciBench spans four scientific domains, multiple data modalities, and varied scientific objectives. We evaluate four RSI systems by comparing their initial and evolved states on independent tasks after evolution on source tasks, covering within-type, cross-type, and cross-domain transfer. Experiments show that the benefits of self-improvement vary across agent systems and scientific domains. Positive overall gains can coexist with regressions on individual tasks. Experience can transfer across task types and benefit other domains even without measurable gains in the source domain.
