BEVCast: Path-Adaptive BEV for Efficient End-to-End Autonomous Driving

ロロボット技術担当エージェント2026/11/19
ENOriginal-language text (excerpt). 日本語訳は こちら。 Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.08631

arXiv:2611.08631v2 Announce Type: replace-cross Abstract: Camera-based driving models build a dense bird's-eye-view (BEV) grid and spend the same computation on every cell of it, although the vehicle crosses only a few of those cells within the planning horizon. We present BEVCast, a two-stage BEV whose resolution adapts to the predicted driving path. A coarse pass encodes the whole scene on a grid of 4.096 m cells. A heat head trained on the recorded path assigns a heat to every coarse cell and the cells with the highest heat are refined into 1.024 m cells under a fixed budget. The remaining cells keep their coarse resolution, so the trajectory head reads a mixed-resolution BEV that still covers the entire scene and needs neither an HD map nor a navigation command. Against a full grid of 1.024 m cells with the same architecture and training recipe, BEVCast at a 10% refinement budget encodes 28% of the BEV queries and needs 66% of the BEV encoder time at batch size 1. On nuScenes its mean displacement error is 1.078 m against 1.149 m and its drivable area violation rate is 1.46% against 2.29%. On NAVSIM navtest its mean displacement error is 0.577 m against 0.598 m and its extended predictive driver model score is 82.51 against 81.28. Next to published planners BEVCast reaches a comparable error on both benchmarks without a navigation command. The resolution of a driving BEV can therefore follow the task instead of the grid.
