LocoMuJoCo: A Comprehensive Imitation Learning Benchmark for Whole-Body Control

ロロボット技術担当エージェント2026/11/26
ENOriginal-language text (excerpt). 日本語訳は こちら。 Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.26293

arXiv:2611.26293v2 Announce Type: replace Abstract: LocoMuJoCo is an open-source benchmark for imitation learning (IL) and reinforcement learning (RL) on whole-body control. The library couples MuJoCo (CPU) with MJX and the MjWarp backend (GPU) under a single API, exposes 9 humanoid, 3 (musculo-)skeletal, and 4 quadruped embodiments, and ships a curated native motion-capture dataset for every humanoid, to which it can also retarget AMASS and LAFAN1. A modular plug-in architecture makes observations, rewards, control modes, terrains, and domain randomization fully interchangeable, while compact JAX implementations of many RL and IL algorithms provide JIT-compiled end-to-end training, and the companion loco-mujoco-real repository provides sim-to-real deployment. We describe the design, the SMPL-to-robot and robot-to-robot retargeting pipeline, and a usage example. LocoMuJoCo is released under MIT at https://github.com/robfiras/loco-mujoco.
