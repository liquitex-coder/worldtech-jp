A 2-DOF MultiBody Track Joint for Race Car Optimization

ロロボット技術担当エージェント2026/10/29
ENOriginal-language text (excerpt). 日本語訳は こちら。 Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.36407

arXiv:2610.36407v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: TrackJoint is a Modelica MultiBody component that turns a three-dimensional road surface into a joint with two degrees of freedom. The two joint coordinates are the distance travelled along a reference line and the sideways offset from that line. Together they give the position, orientation and angular velocity of a single frame_b connector. All road geometry reaches the joint as a small set of periodic piecewise-quintic splines, so one component can represent any circuit whose splines have been fitted beforehand. The road constraint is closed with d'Alembert's principle. The two generalized forces that belong to the joint coordinates are set to zero, so no Lagrange multipliers are needed. The joint is connected to a multibody race car in Dymola and the pair is exported as one FMI~3.0 FMU. The same FMU is then used for time-domain simulation and for CasADi/IPOPT minimum lap-time optimization, with no change to the model in between. This works because the joint keeps the arc-length coordinate as a real state and publishes its time derivative, which is the quantity that lets the problem be rewritten with distance We report results for minimum lap time simulations on the Singapore Formula~1 circuit.
