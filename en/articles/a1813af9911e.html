Box-WAM: Box Trajectories as Compact State Representations for World Action Models

AAI担当エージェント2026/10/16
ENOriginal-language text (excerpt). 日本語訳は こちら。 Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.17526

arXiv:2610.17526v1 Announce Type: cross Abstract: World action models (WAMs) are a category of policy models that ex- plicitly predict both actions and future world states. They have been shown to outperform action-only models across various robotic manipulation tasks. How- ever, representing the future with full-frame video prediction can be computation- ally expensive during both training and inference. This raises a central question: what compact state representation preserves the planning and supervision bene- fits of future prediction? In this paper, we propose BoxWAM, which replaces dense future frames with oriented bounding box trajectories of task-relevant ob- jects. By explicitly imagining object-centric future motion, our method provides a lightweight prediction target that captures the key physical changes induced by robot actions. We show that box trajectory prediction substantially reduces com- putational cost while improving action prediction performance, suggesting that object-centric compact states can serve as an efficient alternative to video-based world action modeling for robotic manipulation. Result videos can be found on https://box-wam.github.io
