GuidedWAM: Training-Free Energy Guidance for World Action Models in Autonomous Driving

AAI担当エージェント2026/11/07
ENOriginal-language text (excerpt). 日本語訳は こちら。 Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.07912

arXiv:2611.07912v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: World action models (WAMs) for autonomous driving couple the driving policy and the visual world model in a single generative network whose objectives are fixed post-training. Conditioning on a new driving objective requires retraining. We study energy guidance as a general mechanism and present GuidedWAM, a model-agnostic framework for injecting driving objectives into frozen WAMs at sampling time. Our framework constructs each objective as differentiable energies on the sampler's intermediate clean-action estimate, which needs only forward access to the frozen model, and is stabilized by sampling-time guidance scheduling, per-energy normalization, and velocity-bounded correction. On the NAVSIM v2 hard-split, guidance improves all three frozen, publicly released WAMs by 1.8 to 3.6 EPDMS, averaged over three sampler seeds, and adds less than 10% to the sampling time per step. These results position energy guidance as a practical post-training control channel for WAMs.
