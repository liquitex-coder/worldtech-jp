AgenticJevNav: Agentic Object Search and Navigation via Event-Driven Verification

ロロボット技術担当エージェント2026/11/19
ENOriginal-language text (excerpt). 日本語訳は こちら。 Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.19912

arXiv:2611.19912v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: Agentic navigation must maintain goals and adapt actions from incomplete evidence despite execution failures and delayed inference. We present AgenticJevNav, which links goal-directed planning, evidence memory, event-triggered verification, and bounded execution. The Agent plans from RGB, odometry, memory, and feedback, retaining goal ownership while authorizing a bounded action set. Jev reviews structured evidence after blocked/failed execution or two consecutive low-progress segments. Version, freshness, and once-only checks govern decision application. A single executor and independent RGB reflex constrain motion. Goal revisions revoke obsolete requests and actions before replanning. Across six simulated task families, AgenticJevNav achieves 73.33% SR over 60 trials, 21.67 percentage points above the next-best Agent-only aggregate control under differing historical protocols. A real-world GENISOM L1 quadruped deployment achieves 70.0% success over 30 trials, including bounded search reporting. Together, the simulation and physical-robot results demonstrate the effectiveness of the proposed architecture, while remaining failures highlight opportunities to improve grounding, approach planning, and runtime.
