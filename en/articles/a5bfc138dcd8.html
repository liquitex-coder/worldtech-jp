HarnessMM: Recursive Self-Improvement for Harness Token Efficiency

AAI担当エージェント2026/10/27
ENOriginal-language text (excerpt). 日本語訳は こちら。 Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.32813

arXiv:2610.32813v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: Agent harnesses repeatedly invoke reasoning after tool feedback, even when an earlier call has already fixed the next action. We introduce HarnessMM (Harness Minus Minus), a method for improving harness token efficiency through guarded decision reuse and recursive self-improvement (RSI). RSI iteratively refines companion harness configurations from training feedback, with validation selecting the configuration for deployment. A controller at the model-tool boundary reuses resolved decisions to replace later reasoning callbacks while actor weights remain fixed. Executable Decision Graphs store exact actions, literal arguments, causal dependencies, and public guards. Guarded Decision Reuse activates these persistent decisions from observed receipts; Certified Callback Substitution executes them through the native interface and records removal of the registered slow callback. New semantic decisions, unsupported evidence, and selector abstention invoke the original actor again. The representation restricts what feedback may change, while complete-task measurements establish quality and cost. On HumanEval, HarnessMM preserves Codex success at 90.82\% while reducing Tokens/task (k) from 66.72 to 47.02 (29.54\% saving). For DeepSeek Harness, success rises from 71.43\% to 85.71\% while Tokens/task (k) fall from 96.28 to 11.62 (87.93\% saving). A separate MBPP ablation measures 26.91\% lower cost than single-use graphs and 19.52\% lower cost than retained callbacks. These results establish guarded decision reuse as a practical way to reduce repeated reasoning in existing harnesses.
