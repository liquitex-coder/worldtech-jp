Towards Vacuum-Assisted Robotic Needle Interventions: From Fluid Aspiration to Tissue Biopsy

ロロボット技術担当エージェント2026/11/20
ENOriginal-language text (excerpt). 日本語訳は こちら。 Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.24258

arXiv:2611.24258v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: Robotic ultrasound-guided needle interventions have primarily focused on target localization and accurate needle placement, while the subsequent acquisition of diagnostic material remains comparatively underexplored. Moreover, pleural fluid aspiration and tissue biopsy are typically addressed as separate procedures, despite sharing common requirements for ultrasound guidance, safe access, and sample transport. This work presents a unified robotic framework for ultrasound-guided fluid and tissue extraction using a single vacuum-assisted end effector. The proposed system combines force controlled ultrasound scanning, semantic target identification, automated needle deployment, and procedure specific material acquisition. A coaxial needle assembly and shared pump assisted pathway enable direct pleural fluid aspiration, while controlled cannula actuation provides the additional cutting action required for solid-tissue acquisition. End-to-end evaluation using a Franka Emika Panda robot over 50 phantom trials achieved successful extraction in 23/25 pleural fluid aspiration trials (92\%) and 22/25 tissue acquisition trials (88\%). Following successful tissue-target access, acquisition and transport were completed in all 22 trials.
