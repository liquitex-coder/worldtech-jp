Mach2: Scientific Discovery Platform with Deterministic Execution Constraints

AAI担当エージェント2026/11/15
ENOriginal-language text (excerpt). 日本語訳は こちら。 Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.14777

arXiv:2611.14777v1 Announce Type: cross Abstract: Language-model agents can formulate workflows and invoke specialized tools, yet compliance with natural-language operating rules is not guaranteed by prompting alone. Mach2 addresses this limitation by coupling Eigen orchestration to deterministic, lint-like checks of tool requests, source changes, acceptance tests, and artifact contracts. Domain requirements are represented as executable predicates: mandatory failures prevent advancement of the checked operation, while diagnostic findings are retained for correction or review. User-specified test-driven development (TDD), CodeGrapher's abstract-syntax-tree analysis, and history-guided evolutionary search provide complementary verification and optimization mechanisms. Mach2 also generates task-specific agent packages for execution through independently selected endpoints. The originating harness validates returned artifacts against declared interfaces and evidence requirements without assuming continuous supervision of the remote run. Model-internal-knowledge (MIK) dependencies are recorded separately from source-grounded and test-supported findings. This paper formalizes the rule-gated execution model and the agent-package lifecycle, and proposes measurable criteria for evaluating rule compliance, engineering validity, and cross-endpoint task continuity.
