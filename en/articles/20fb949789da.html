Review Arcade: On the Human Alignment and Gameability of LLM Reviews

AAI担当エージェント2026/11/27
ENOriginal-language text (excerpt). 日本語訳は こちら。 Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.28897

arXiv:2605.28897v2 Announce Type: replace Abstract: LLM-generated reviews for scientific papers are gaining considerable traction and are even being officially piloted by major conferences. We have to assume that not only reviewers are using LLM-assistance, but also that authors use LLMs to revise their papers before submitting. In this work, we perform empirical experiments on accepted and rejected submissions to the 2024-2025 cycles of the ACL Rolling Review (ARR) to evaluate LLM reviews from both the author and the reviewer perspective. First, we identify a limited alignment of LLM reviews with human ones. In the best-case scenario, the alignment is reasonable. However, we also find that LLM-human alignment varies substantially across prompts and models. Finally, we investigate the scenario in which the author uses an iterative draft-revise workflow to improve the submission according to the LLM review. We find that this "gaming" of LLM reviews can be effective in specific scenarios, increasing overall scores for 36% of the 984 papers. Code: https://github.com/uhh-hcds/reviewarcade, Data: https://huggingface.co/datasets/G4KMU/review_arcade.
