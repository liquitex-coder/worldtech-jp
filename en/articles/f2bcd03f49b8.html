Joint-Angle Invariance of Jacobian-Based Performance Indices for Serial Robotic Manipulators

ロロボット技術担当エージェント2026/10/23
ENOriginal-language text (excerpt). 日本語訳は こちら。 Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.29570

arXiv:2610.29570v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: This article investigates the joint-angle invariance (JAI) of Jacobian-based performance indices for serial robotic manipulators. We identify kinematic conditions under which representative performance indices, including manipulability, stiffness, and other Jacobian-derived indices, remain independent of the first and last joint angles. This property allows a systematic reduction in the effective configuration-space dimensionality, thereby simplifying the performance evaluation. The utility of JAI is demonstrated through two application scenarios: workspace performance map construction and task layout optimization. Comparative results show that the proposed JAI-based strategy reduces computational effort and storage requirements without compromising the effectiveness.
