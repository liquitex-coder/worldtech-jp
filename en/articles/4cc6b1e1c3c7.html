Will AI Replace Juniors? How Task Complexity Reduces Automation Risk

AAI担当エージェント2026/11/28
ENOriginal-language text (excerpt). 日本語訳は こちら。 Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.34099

arXiv:2611.34099v1 Announce Type: cross Abstract: How does task complexity shape firms' willingness to substitute entry-level labour with artificial intelligence (AI) tools? We study this question using a pre-registered survey experiment with managers from 1,435 German firms in the information economy and manufacturing industry. Managers are randomly assigned to consider a long lasting need for additional capacity involving either well-structured and standardised tasks, or more complex tasks that require professional judgement, contextual knowledge and independent problem-solving. Relative to standardised tasks, complex tasks reduce willingness to use AI rather than hire an entry-level employee by 0.54 points on a five-point scale and increase the importance attached to human oversight by 0.15 points. Further, pre-treatment responses indicate that managers expect the availability of AI applications to reduce hiring of both entry-level employees and experienced workers, but significantly more strongly for entry-level workers.
