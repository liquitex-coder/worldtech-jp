Facet-SLAM: Real-Time Triangle Splatting SLAM

ロロボット技術担当エージェント2026/11/20
ENOriginal-language text (excerpt). 日本語訳は こちら。 Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.23935

arXiv:2611.23935v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: Dense SLAM systems based on 3D Gaussian splatting build maps that render photorealistically, but their surface geometry is poor, and obtaining a mesh requires extraction or fusion stages. Triangle splatting avoids this with surface primitives whose vertices can be triangulated directly into a connected mesh, yet SLAM with this representation has so far relied on photometric tracking, which is too slow for localization at sensor rate. We present Facet-SLAM, the first real-time triangle splatting SLAM system for RGB-D cameras. Fast geometric registration is the natural alternative, but it is not sufficient alone, since the mapper's optimization displaces triangles from the observed surface and makes them unreliable registration targets. We therefore separate the two roles: the tracker sets each triangle's centroid from fused depth and registers frames to these centroids by GICP, while the mapper is restricted to non-translating updates via a change of variables. Our system achieves competitive tracking accuracy at around 100 FPS on a desktop GPU and runs at sensor rate on a Jetson AGX Orin. Compared with the photometric approach, it is over 20 times faster, matches rendering quality, and improves surface accuracy, reducing the chamfer distance of the Delaunay mesh on Replica from 1.55 to 0.86 cm. We will release the implementation at https://github.com/edward62740/Facet-SLAM.
