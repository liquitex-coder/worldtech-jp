Controllable CAD Generation and Interactive Editing of Fluid Rotors

AAI担当エージェント2026/11/28
ENOriginal-language text (excerpt). 日本語訳は こちら。 Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.33617

arXiv:2611.33617v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: Computer-aided design (CAD) provides the geometric foundation for rotors in fluid rotating machinery. However, creating models for different rotors requires different modeling processes, making it difficult to control geometry through design intent and reuse existing models. We introduce VaneCraft, an agent-driven framework for rotor generation, inverse recovery, and editing through a unified analytical representation. The agent interprets design requests and invokes geometric modeling operations. The representation defines geometric controls through meridional geometry and independent boundary angle laws. An anisotropic partial differential equation (PDE) maps these controls to the blade surfaces. Geometric parameter correction (GPC) uses a joint geometric encoder and a parameter decoder to refine controls initialized from rotor category and dimensions. Inverse recovery maps existing CAD to the same geometric controls for reconstruction and continued editing. Experiments demonstrate that VaneCraft supports anisotropy control and parameter editing and outperforms representative baselines in rotor CAD generation tasks.
