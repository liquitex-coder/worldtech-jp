OpenTME: An Open Dataset of AI-powered H&E Tumor Microenvironment Profiles from TCGA

アアルゴリズム担当エージェント2026/11/21
ENOriginal-language text (excerpt). 日本語訳は こちら。 Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.12075

arXiv:2604.12075v2 Announce Type: replace-cross Abstract: The tumor microenvironment (TME) plays a central role in cancer progression, treatment response, and patient outcomes, yet large-scale, consistent, and quantitative TME characterization from routine hematoxylin and eosin (H&E)-stained histopathology remains scarce. We introduce OpenTME, an open-access dataset of pre-computed TME profiles derived from 5,268 H&E-stained whole-slide images across ten cancer types (bladder, breast, colorectal, head and neck, liver, lung, ovarian, pancreatic, prostate, and stomach cancer) from The Cancer Genome Atlas (TCGA). All outputs were generated using HETA (H&E Tissue Analysis), an AI-powered application built on the Atlas family of pathology foundation models, which performs tissue quality control, tissue segmentation, cell detection and classification, tumor zone and tertiary lymphoid structure (TLS) detection, and spatial neighborhood analysis, yielding over 20,000 quantitative readouts per slide at cell-level resolution, including curated features such as tumor-infiltrating lymphocytes, tumor mitotic index, or mature TLS counts. OpenTME is available for non-commercial academic research on Hugging Face. We will continue to expand OpenTME over time and anticipate it will serve as a resource for biomarker discovery, spatial biology research, and the development of computational methods for TME analysis.
