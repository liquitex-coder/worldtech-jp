FlashReFocus: Interactive Image Refocusing in Seconds

AAI担当エージェント2026/11/27
ENOriginal-language text (excerpt). 日本語訳は こちら。 Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.23297

arXiv:2610.23297v4 Announce Type: replace-cross Abstract: Single-image refocusing changes the focal plane and depth of field of a photograph after capture. Users compare focal choices on one photograph, so content stability across edits matters alongside quality and speed. Recent generative methods rerun large diffusion models for every edit, even when they first restore an all-in-focus image, so content can change inconsistently between edits. Yet only this restoration is highly ill-posed and benefits from a generative prior, whereas rendering blur is largely determined by depth and the focal plane. We therefore propose FlashReFocus, which runs diffusion once per photograph and renders each edit with a lightweight network. A one-step diffusion deblurrer with a decoder refiner recovers fine detail efficiently while balancing fidelity and perceptual quality. The renderer uses an explicit defocus map for control and defocus-bias attention to promote information exchange between tokens with similar blur magnitudes. Because every edit reuses the same reconstruction, in-focus content stays consistent across edits, and editing the map enables tilt-shift and two-plane effects. We further collect 3CReal, a benchmark of 89 pairs from three camera-lens systems, to evaluate generalization to unseen optics. For zero-shot bokeh rendering on EBB! Val176 and 3CReal, FlashReFocus leads the evaluated methods in fidelity and perceptual quality. At 1536x1536, a complete refocus takes 2.45 s and each subsequent edit 0.46 s on one RTX PRO 6000.
