Learning Curves and Prediction Persistence to Under-Regularization of Spectral Algorithms in Large Dimensions

アアルゴリズム担当エージェント2026/11/21
ENOriginal-language text (excerpt). 日本語訳は こちら。 Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.23212

arXiv:2604.23212v2 Announce Type: replace-cross Abstract: We study how prediction risk changes as regularization is reduced for spectral algorithms when the sample size $n$ and dimension $d$ satisfy $n\asymp d^\gamma$ for fixed $\gamma>0$. For inner-product kernels on the sphere, under a source condition with smoothness $s\geq0$ and a signal nondegeneracy condition, we characterize the conditional excess risk up to constant factors at each fixed admissible scale $\lambda\asymp d^{-u}$, $u>0$, and at interpolation. The resulting risk orders describe the effects of source smoothness, filter qualification, and transitions between spectral blocks. They identify when the conditional excess risk retains the minimax risk order as regularization is weakened from an optimal scale to interpolation, and when an interval before interpolation exhibits a polynomially larger risk. We also compare the kernel risk with a population sequence risk for the same spectral filter, using a deterministic regularization scale bounded below by order $n^{-1}$. Finally, for noninteger $\gamma$, we extend the risk characterization for kernel ridge regression to kernels on general domains under spectral scaling, hyper-contractivity, and tail diagonal concentration conditions, together with the source and signal conditions.
