Structured Extraction of Diffraction Data from Covalent Organic Frameworks

AAI担当エージェント2026/11/21
ENOriginal-language text (excerpt). 日本語訳は こちら。 Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.23680

arXiv:2611.23680v1 Announce Type: cross Abstract: Powder X-ray diffraction (PXRD) is the primary structural evidence for covalent organic frameworks (COFs) and many other materials. Yet decades of measured patterns exist only as images in published figures. Automatic recovery is difficult, because PXRD figures vary widely in format, from a single curve to crowded stacks with labels, insets, and neighboring plots that challenge traditional computer vision. This work presents a pipeline in which a vision large language model (LLM) interprets the layout of each figure, locating the diffraction plot and its curves and sketching a coarse trace of each, while classical computer vision (CV) refines every trace to pixel-level accuracy. Applied to 3,954 COF articles, the pipeline produces 11,445 calibrated diffraction curves, released in the open database COF-PEAK. Compared with a hand digitization of 60 panels, extracted peak positions differ by a median of only 0.07 degrees. Whereas papers usually report only a few peak angles, which limits further analysis, the digitized curves enable tracking how peak prominence, a possible marker of crystallinity, changes with synthesis conditions. Vision LLMs and classical CV can thus turn such figures into reusable experimental data.
