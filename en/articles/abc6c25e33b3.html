ELARSpeech: A 30K-Hour Dataset Across 675 Underrepresented Languages for Long-Tail Multilingual Speech Technologies

AAI担当エージェント2026/10/23
ENOriginal-language text (excerpt). 日本語訳は こちら。 Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.19388

arXiv:2610.19388v2 Announce Type: replace-cross Abstract: Massively multilingual speech modeling has made remarkable progress in recent years, yet existing training resources and benchmarks still underrepresent much of the linguistic long tail. To address this gap, we introduce ELARSpeech, a large-scale multilingual speech resource comprising 30,202 hours of speech across 675 underrepresented languages, curated from the Endangered Languages Archive. Combining human-annotated and unlabeled recordings, the corpus spans diverse recording conditions, domains, and language families. Beyond corpus construction, our experiments show how ELARSpeech supports extending ASR and TTS capabilities to diverse underrepresented languages, with further benefits from pseudo-labeling unlabeled data. At the same time, our speech representation analysis reveals remaining limitations in transferring existing multilingual models to the linguistic long tail. Together, ELARSpeech provides both a resource and a testbed for extending multilingual speech technology further into the linguistic long tail. We publicly release the dataset, all trained ASR and TTS models, and the complete data construction pipeline to facilitate research on long-tail multilingual speech technologies.
