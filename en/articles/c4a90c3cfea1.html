arXiv:2608.08101v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: Generative AI has emerged as one of the most transformative forces in modern artificial intelligence, reshaping how we create, imagine, and interact with digital content. From photorealistic images to coherent text, from immersive videos to novel molecular structures, generative models now power applications that were once confined to science fiction. This book is designed to guide readers through the foundational principles, mathematical underpinnings, and practical architectures that underpin this revolution.
