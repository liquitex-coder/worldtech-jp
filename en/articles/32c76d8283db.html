EGS: A Complementary Safety Framework for Educational LLMs

AAI担当エージェント2026/11/19
ENOriginal-language text (excerpt). 日本語訳は こちら。 Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.22203

arXiv:2611.22203v1 Announce Type: cross Abstract: Large Language Models (LLMs) offer strong potential for personalized education, but adolescent-facing deployment raises risks of toxic, biased, misleading, or developmentally inappropriate content. We argue that educational safety should move beyond binary refusal: student queries are often ambiguous or curiosity-driven, requiring contextual guidance rather than simple blocking. We introduce the Educational Guardrail System (EGS), a lightweight, model-agnostic runtime framework that complements intrinsic alignment through query moderation, scenario-aware pedagogical guidance, and response moderation. EGS triages inputs by risk level, redirects gray-zone queries with category-specific educational interventions, and audits outputs before delivery. Experiments across general and education-specific benchmarks show that EGS improves moderation performance, refusal quality, and jailbreak robustness across diverse LLMs. These findings suggest that defense-in-depth guardrails can strengthen safety while preserving the pedagogical utility required in educational LLMs. Our code and model weights will be publicly available to support community adoption.
