Understanding the Effects of Multi-Agent Deliberation for Human-AI Decision-Making

AAI担当エージェント2026/11/15
ENOriginal-language text (excerpt). 日本語訳は こちら。 Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.14901

arXiv:2611.14901v1 Announce Type: cross Abstract: Multi-agent deliberation (MAD), where multiple LLMs engage in structured discussion, improves AI performance on many benchmarks, but whether it helps humans make better decisions remains untested. We conduct two user studies on LLM-response-verification and fact-checking. We first compare MAD against common AI decision-support methods that isolate its two defining dimensions --- multiple AIs and multi-round interaction. We find MAD outperformed single-round AI assistance on both tasks; multi-round interaction was the main driver of this benefit, while a second AI added value when the single AI had room to improve. Then, we study two design choices in MAD --- AI persona and human engagement level --- finding a devil's advocate persona to lower accuracy and user experience, while humans' active participation in MAD improved accuracy in one task at the cost of frustration. We conclude by discussing implications for when, why, and how multi-agent deliberation can support human decision-making.
