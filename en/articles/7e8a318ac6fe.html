PriTabICL: Differentially Private Tabular In-Context Learning with Task-Aligned Cluster-Wise Synthesis

アアルゴリズム担当エージェント2026/11/21
ENOriginal-language text (excerpt). 日本語訳は こちら。 Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.25073

arXiv:2611.25073v1 Announce Type: cross Abstract: Tabular in-context learning (TabICL) enables large language models (LLMs) to predict target records using a handful of labeled demonstrations in their prompts. However, these demonstrations often contain sensitive personal information. Differential privacy (DP) offers principled protection, but noise-based methods introduce substantial noise, distorting the feature-label relationships essential for accurate prediction, especially as data dimensionality grows. In this work, we study DP data synthesis for generating privacy-preserving TabICL demonstrations. We identify two limitations of directly applying general-purpose DP synthesis to entire datasets: modeling a global distribution can obscure local structure and minority patterns in heterogeneous data, while measuring broad attribute dependencies under a limited privacy budget can reduce the accuracy available for relationships most relevant to prediction. More broadly, global statistical fidelity may fail to preserve heterogeneous structures needed by downstream predictive and generative models. To address these challenges, we propose PriTabICL, a general framework compatible with existing DP synthesis algorithms. It partitions records into DP clusters and synthesizes each independently, using a fixed feature-target workload to prioritize prediction-relevant statistical relationships. At inference, each query is routed to a DP cluster, and demonstrations are sampled from its synthetic dataset without additional privacy loss for the original records. Experiments on four real-world datasets demonstrate the effectiveness of our methods across heterogeneous datasets. In particular, one of our instantiations, PriTabICL-PrivSyn, improves over global PrivSyn in 8 out of 12 settings and outperforms existing private TabICL approaches in nearly all evaluated settings. Open-source code is available.
