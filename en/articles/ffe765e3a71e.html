AhaPro: A Process-level Rebus benchmark for probing Aha moments in LLM reasoning

AAI担当エージェント2026/11/27
ENOriginal-language text (excerpt). 日本語訳は こちら。 Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.31268

arXiv:2611.31268v1 Announce Type: cross Abstract: Existing research on Aha moments in large language models has largely focused on reflective expressions or final revision outcomes, leaving their role in the visible reasoning process insufficiently characterized. Rebus puzzles encode answers through character forms, spatial arrangements, and homophony. Reinterpreting key details may trigger Aha moments and shifts in the reasoning process and candidate answers. We introduce AhaPro, a process-level benchmark that uses rebus puzzles to examine how models reorganize evidence and revise answers through Aha moments. The framework represents CoT as sequences of candidate answers, evidence, and verification states, using hierarchical metrics to assess conflict detection, state improvement, and subsequent adoption. Across eleven models, we observe frequent failures to adopt better states discovered during Aha moments, alongside adopted revisions that leave state quality unchanged or reduce it. Models also detect conflicts without subsequently achieving an effective revision. Further paired interventions suggest that prior reasoning context may hinder effective revision after conflict detection, while multi-branch search highlights answer selection as a bottleneck between correct-answer discovery and successful correction.
