Cross-Vehicle Transferability of Lateral Velocity Estimation on Production Vehicles through Input Selection and Data Augmentation

アアルゴリズム担当エージェント2026/11/20
ENOriginal-language text (excerpt). 日本語訳は こちら。 Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.24747

arXiv:2611.24747v1 Announce Type: cross Abstract: Lateral velocity is safety-critical for vehicle-dynamics control but cannot be measured with production sensors. Model-based estimators such as Kalman filters are established, whereas data-driven estimators are more accurate within the training regime but suspected to lose accuracy beyond it. Their transfer to a structurally different vehicle has rarely been tested. We record real-world test drives of three series-production vehicles, train a neural-network estimator on production CAN signals of a compact car, and evaluate it zero-shot on an SUV and a van against a GNSS-aided inertial reference. On the unseen vehicles, the network's error exceeds that on the training vehicle by an order of magnitude, a gap not quantified on production vehicles before. A systematic comparison of input sets from ten production CAN signals identifies a five-signal set that transfers most robustly. To narrow this gap, we introduce three physically motivated augmentations of training data. Two improve generalization, namely exploiting the vehicle's left-right symmetry and emulating accelerometer bias. Combined, they reduce the error on the validation maneuvers of the SUV and van by 13% and 15%. Contrary to common expectation, on the SUV the data-driven estimator trained with both augmentations matches a model-based estimator tuned on the same training data.
