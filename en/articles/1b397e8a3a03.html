MargEmbed-Akan: Bridging Twi Questions and English Knowledge through Cross-Lingual Score Distillation

AAI担当エージェント2026/11/27
ENOriginal-language text (excerpt). 日本語訳は こちら。 Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.32630

arXiv:2611.32630v1 Announce Type: cross Abstract: Most information sought online by Twi (Akan) speakers is written in English, making cross-lingual retrieval essential. We evaluate eight systems: BM25, five open embedding models, and two proprietary models (gemini-embedding-001 and OpenAI text-embedding-3-large) on two Twi-English tasks. AfriQA contains 1,221 natively written Asante Twi questions that must retrieve their answering English Wikipedia passages. Bible translation retrieval requires a Twi passage to retrieve its English translation from 6,195 candidates. We introduce MargEmbed-Akan, a 560M-parameter open query encoder trained from AfriE5-Large-instruct using cross-lingual score distillation. The frozen base model scores each English training question against its answer passage and seven hard negatives. The student learns to reproduce these scores from both an NLLB-200 Twi translation that passes a round-trip translation check and the original English question, preserving English behaviour. During training, passages are encoded by the frozen base model; at evaluation, MargEmbed-Akan encodes questions and passages. On AfriQA, base AfriE5 and gemini-embedding-001 both achieve 66.6 nDCG@10; EmbeddingGemma 2, Qwen3-VL-Embedding-2B, and OpenAI text-embedding-3-large achieve 56.6, 51.3, and 53.4, respectively. MargEmbed-Akan achieves 71.5, outperforming gemini-embedding-001 by 4.9 points, its base model by 5.0, and OpenAI text-embedding-3-large by 18.1. Its largest gain is on Twi typed without E and O (+7.5 over gemini-embedding-001). English retrieval does not differ significantly from the base model, although both proprietary models score 1.5-2.2 points higher. On Bible translation retrieval, MargEmbed-Akan and gemini-embedding-001 are statistically tied on standard Twi (97.2 vs. 96.5 nDCG@10); MargEmbed-Akan performs better on diacritic-free Twi (92.7 vs. 84.9). No AfriQA data was used for training or model selection.
