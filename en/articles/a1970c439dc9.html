Hybrid Mechanical and Agentic Specification Inference

AAI担当エージェント2026/11/17
ENOriginal-language text (excerpt). 日本語訳は こちら。 Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.10005

arXiv:2605.10005v3 Announce Type: replace-cross Abstract: Adoption of formal verification has long been hindered by the cost of manual specification. To prove high-level properties, one must also write contracts for supporting functions, many of which are mechanical and low-level. Specification inference automatically derives such contracts from the program. We present a hybrid of mechanical and agentic inference for Move, a smart contract language, integrated into its verifier, the Move Prover. A large language model (LLM) agent drives inference and verification through a tool that calls the Move Prover and derives specifications from code by weakest-precondition (WP) analysis. We evaluate our approach on 26 targets, 24 of them extracted from public Move functions of the Aptos framework that have no specifications, under a frontier model and a mid-tier model of the same family (Sol 5.6 and Terra 5.6). With and without WP, the agent completes over 90% of its runs, so agentic inference is effective on its own. Adding WP lowers its cost by 11-21% overall and by 40-64% on targets without loops.
