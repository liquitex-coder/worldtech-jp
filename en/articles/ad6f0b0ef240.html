Annealed Gibbs Measures with Data-Driven Interaction Potentials on Hierarchical Structures

アアルゴリズム担当エージェント2026/11/19
ENOriginal-language text (excerpt). 日本語訳は こちら。 Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.30064

arXiv:2606.30064v2 Announce Type: replace Abstract: We construct annealed Gibbs measures on continuous interaction parameters and binary spins on Cayley trees of order $k\geq 1$, with coupling functions determined by an empirical loss. The interaction parameters and spins are distinct random variables: the former determine edge couplings, while the latter represent binary labels. The training data specify the coupling function and the joint Gibbs specification, rather than automatically selecting a unique Gibbs measure on interaction parameters. We derive necessary and sufficient compatibility conditions for the finite-volume joint densities. These conditions take the form of nonlinear integral equations for boundary laws and characterize the splitting annealed Gibbs measures constructed here. For $k=1$, the translation-invariant equations reduce to positive compact integral operators. Under the stated hypotheses, they have a unique normalized positive solution. For $k=2$, an explicit empirical-loss kernel reduces the translation-invariant equations to polynomial equations. In this case, we show that hierarchical learning systems may exhibit phase-transition phenomena: for certain empirical kernels on Cayley trees order two, multiple Gibbs measures on spins emerge beyond a critical inverse temperature, corresponding to distinct equilibrium prediction regimes. For a selected annealed Gibbs measure, we distinguish its marginal on interaction parameters from its conditional Gibbs measures on spins at fixed interaction parameters. Our results provide a new perspective on energy-based learning, where data do not merely determine an optimal model through minimization but define an entire probabilistic landscape of possible inference states.
