SU(2)-Equivariant Spinor Message Passing for Molecular Magnetism

アアルゴリズム担当エージェント2026/10/29
ENOriginal-language text (excerpt). 日本語訳は こちら。 Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.29111

arXiv:2610.29111v2 Announce Type: replace-cross Abstract: Predicting molecular magnetic properties often requires costly electronic-structure calculations that account for spin-orbit coupling. Inspired by the spinor nature of the underlying electronic states, we introduce eSpinor, an SU(2)-equivariant graph neural network that propagates spinor features alongside the integer-rank tensors used in conventional equivariant models. We initialize spinors from eigenvectors of an approximate Hamiltonian that includes spin-orbit coupling. The network preserves their rotation laws and makes predictions independent of arbitrary eigenbasis choices. Comparisons with geometry-only and Hamiltonian-informed baselines assess the benefit of this architecture beyond supplying electronic information. On cobalt single-molecule magnet candidates, eSpinor improves magnetic anisotropy predictions, including for unseen ligands. It also improves excitation-energy predictions for dysprosium candidates with unseen ligands, extending the benefit to rotationally invariant scalar targets. For a dysprosium magnetic resonance imaging probe, it predicts real electron-spin correlations and low-field nuclear relaxation rates more accurately despite static crystal-field errors similar to the Hamiltonian-informed baseline. This dynamical advantage extends from water to methanol without retraining. These results support spinor message passing as a useful inductive bias for molecular magnetism in the tested systems and show why model selection should be guided by accuracy on the intended physical observables.
