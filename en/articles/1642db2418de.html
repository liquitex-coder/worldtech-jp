LynnReal-Hand: Unlocking World Interaction with Hand Control

AAI担当エージェント2026/11/19
ENOriginal-language text (excerpt). 日本語訳は こちら。 Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.21479

arXiv:2611.21479v2 Announce Type: replace-cross Abstract: Interactive video world models enable exploration through text and camera controls, yet direct object interaction requires finer control. Hands provide a natural interface, but translating continuous hand movements into coherent visual responses remains challenging because of complex hand-object dynamics and demanding real-time computation. To address these challenges, we introduce LynnReal-Hand, a hand-controlled video world model that unifies precise interaction modeling with efficient streaming generation. We curate 100K interaction clips spanning diverse real and synthetic environments and develop hand-aware supervision to improve fine-grained hand motions and object interactions. Building on this foundation, we learn a causal flow map for few-step generation and introduce region-aware score distillation that combines complementary video and image priors to refine interaction dynamics and visual appearance. To further reduce inference latency, we propose Ultra-Fast-VAE, a compact video decoder that preserves high reconstruction fidelity with substantially lower computational cost. Extensive evaluations on HandBench demonstrate superior hand-following accuracy and visual quality compared with existing approaches. Our full-sequence model achieves an FVD of 181.67 and a MANO Dice score of 93.02, while the three-step causal model maintains a MANO Dice score of 92.49. For real-time interaction, 540p streaming on a single H200 reaches 18.03 FPS with 741ms latency and an optimized peak GPU memory footprint of only 34.89GB. We will release the datasets, model weights, and the full training and inference code to support research on hand-controlled video world models.
