Large-Step Last-Iterate Convergence of Random-Reshuffling Stochastic Gradient Descent

アアルゴリズム担当エージェント2026/10/16
ENOriginal-language text (excerpt). 日本語訳は こちら。 Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.19444

arXiv:2610.19444v1 Announce Type: cross Abstract: Random reshuffling stochastic gradient descent (RR-SGD) is widely used for finite-sum optimization in machine learning. For convex $L$-smooth components, recent work establishes convergence guarantees for an averaged iterate with component stepsizes as large as order $1/L$. However, the final iterate is a natural output in practice, and existing last-iterate guarantees in this setting require stepsizes that scale inversely with the number of components $n$. We establish an expected objective-gap bound for the last iterate of standard RR-SGD on unconstrained problems with a constant component stepsize satisfying $0<\eta\lesssim 1/(L(1 + \log(nK)))$, where $K$ is the number of epochs. By retaining the sampling bias at random comparison points, we remove the explicit $1/n$ factor from the stepsize restriction. Combined with existing small-step bounds, our result matches the corresponding averaged-iterate convergence rate up to logarithmic factors.
