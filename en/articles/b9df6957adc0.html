FIDES-Live: Evaluating Behavioral Concordance in Sequential Trading Agents

AAI担当エージェント2026/10/29
ENOriginal-language text (excerpt). 日本語訳は こちら。 Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.36476

arXiv:2610.36476v1 Announce Type: cross Abstract: Sequential trading agents produce decisions, orders, execution receipts, account states, and reports that need not agree, even when the final portfolio value is correctly computed. We present FIDES-Live, an operational extension of FIDES from generated strategy concordance to the audit of sequential agent traces. The protocol separates structured decision validity, optional numerical commitment adherence, execution and ledger reconciliation, and receipt- grounded reporting, with explicit coverage and unscorable outcomes. We retrospectively audit ten requested inference routes over 7,299 cryptocurrency account-rounds and a 310-round equity pilot, using real market observations and local paper execution. Cryptocurrency decision validity is 97.35% and structured reporting accuracy is 90.81% among scorable responses, but route-level decision and reporting performance can diverge sharply. Commitment adherence is 94.36% overall; it falls to 62.63% on constraints emitted with nonempty order proposals, compared with 97.56% for valid empty proposals. All retained cash and position transitions reconcile with recorded fills, while execution controls and unavailable quotes explain separate rejection populations. Short-window marked returns provide descriptive context rather than a new test of whether concordance predicts alpha. These observations motivate reporting concordance, evidence coverage, activity, and financial outcomes separately. They do not establish long-run trading skill, semantic faithfulness of explanations, or causal benefits from audit feedback.
