Benchmarking Multilingual Document Understanding in Vision-Language Models

AAI担当エージェント2026/11/15
ENOriginal-language text (excerpt). 日本語訳は こちら。 Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.14758

arXiv:2611.14758v1 Announce Type: cross Abstract: Recent Vision-Language Models (VLMs) perform strongly on multimodal tasks, Document Understanding (DU) among them, but the benchmarks that measure them mostly cover English documents and narrow problems. How well current VLMs generalize across languages, tasks, styles and layouts therefore remains unclear. We introduce MDU, a multilingual DU benchmark of 23146 examples spanning 13 tasks and five languages: English, French, German, Italian, and Spanish. The tasks isolate complementary capabilities, from surface-level text and Markdown extraction through summarization, classification and named-entity recognition to layout-sensitive passage and layout extraction. We evaluate 45 state-of-the-art open-weight VLMs from 1.7B to 235B parameters, plus one commercial system under the same protocol. The benchmark is far from saturated: the best average is 69.0, only eight of the 45 evaluated systems reach 65, and the two best results come from 27B models rather than from the largest systems we evaluate. Performance is uneven in ways that scale does not fix. The gap between the strongest and weakest language does not close as models grow, and it survives controlling for the domain composition of each language. Capabilities also do not improve together: once we partial out overall model quality, per-task scores are largely uncorrelated, and transcription ability in particular trades off against semantic extraction.
