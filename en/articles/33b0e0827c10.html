Can MLLMs Reason over Surround-View Driving Videos? A Multi-View Autonomous Driving QA Benchmark

AAI担当エージェント2026/11/15
ENOriginal-language text (excerpt). 日本語訳は こちら。 Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.15225

arXiv:2611.15225v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: Surround-view video understanding in autonomous driving is a crucial capability for Multimodal Large Language Models (MLLMs) to achieve reliable driving reasoning. Real-world driving often requires information beyond the front-view camera. Existing MLLMs primarily focus on front-view information, while their spatio-temporal understanding and reasoning abilities remain unknown. To address this gap, we propose MVDriveBench, a high-information-density video QA benchmark for autonomous driving, characterized by synchronized surround-view inputs, spatio-temporal understanding, and reasoning-chain-oriented evaluation. We design 12 categories of QA tasks that cover the complete reasoning pipeline from low-level visual perception to high-level driving decision-making, enabling a systematic evaluation of MLLMs surround-view spatio-temporal understanding and reasoning capabilities in complex driving environments. We conduct a comprehensive evaluation of 23 open-source, closed-source, and expert MLLMs on MVDriveBench. The results show that current MLLMs still exhibit insufficient surround-view spatio-temporal reasoning ability. Meanwhile, closed-loop evaluations of specialist models in AlpaSim reveal a correlation between surround-view spatio-temporal reasoning capabilities and closed-loop driving performance, highlighting the importance of comprehensive multi-view understanding for reliable vehicle control.
