Cybflight: An Embedded Rust Autopilot for Aerial Robotics Research

ロロボット技術担当エージェント2026/09/29
ENOriginal-language text (excerpt). 日本語訳は こちら。 Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.31232

arXiv:2609.31232v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: Bringing an aerial robotics method from simulation to flight should not require rebuilding a mature autopilot or adding a companion computer. Cybflight is an open-source embedded Rust research autopilot whose typed, replaceable interfaces connect hardware access, perception, state estimation, trajectory planning, and control. This modular development and compile-time optimization workflow is demonstrated with replaceable Rust implementations of model predictive contour-tracking control (MPCTC) and incremental nonlinear dynamic inversion (INDI) running on one STM32H743 without a companion computer. Using this configuration, the vehicle reaches 12.38 m/s during indoor flight, while an outdoor flight using global navigation satellite system (GNSS) position updates reaches 31.4 m/s. These flights show that running demanding estimation and nonlinear control entirely on a flight-controller microcontroller need not come at the expense of a modular autopilot structure.
