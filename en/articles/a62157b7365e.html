Creative Product Design under Brand Image Constraints

AAI担当エージェント2026/11/17
ENOriginal-language text (excerpt). 日本語訳は こちら。 Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.17762

arXiv:2611.17762v1 Announce Type: cross Abstract: Industrial design requires creative visual exploration while satisfying constraints that are often product-specific and implicit. Existing text-to-image (T2I) approaches can generate creative designs through concept blending or multi-agent self-enhancement, but they provide limited mechanisms for ensuring that generated designs remain consistent with such constraints. We focus on brand image as a representative design constraint and propose a framework for brand-aware redesign generation. Given a product name, the framework constructs a brand image survey report that summarizes product and market information, derives a creative direction from the report, and integrates this direction into a self-enhancement loop in which generated images are critiqued and prompts are revised. We evaluate the framework on 50 redesign tasks across five product categories, comparing it with Naive T2I, IP-Adapter/IT-Blender, and unconstrained self-enhancement under the same T2I backbone. By a VLM-as-Judge protocol to measure creativity and brand identifiability, the proposed framework achieves a more favorable trade-off between the two objectives, with particularly clear gains in highly constrained categories such as automotive and motorcycle, where the plausible design space is relatively limited compared with that of other product categories. These results suggest that dynamically eliciting and textualizing brand image can guide creative product design generation under brand image constraints.
