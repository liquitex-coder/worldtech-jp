Hybrid Quantum-Classical Sequence Models for Sample-Efficient Energy Regression in Simulated Calorimeter Showers

アアルゴリズム担当エージェント2026/11/17
ENOriginal-language text (excerpt). 日本語訳は こちら。 Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.17354

arXiv:2611.17354v1 Announce Type: cross Abstract: Energy reconstruction from electromagnetic calorimeter data underpins particle identification, event reconstruction and precision measurements in high-energy physics. Machine learning already performs well on this task, yet two questions remain open: how much the choice of data representation matters, and whether hybrid quantum-classical models add value in practice. We compare classical and quantum-enhanced models for incident positron energy reconstruction on the CaloGAN dataset, a public benchmark of 100,000 simulated electromagnetic shower events spanning 1 to 100 GeV. Eight architectures, namely SVR, ANN, CNN and LSTM together with their quantum counterparts, are trained on three calorimeter representations: engineered physical features, calorimeter images, and longitudinal shower sequences. Models that follow the longitudinal development of the shower reach the highest accuracy, which points to the value of physically informed representations. The hybrid QLSTM gives the lowest reconstruction error, with an RMSE of 0.294 GeV. Under matched-data conditions with only 1,500 training events, the QLSTM improves on the classical LSTM by 52.7% and also surpasses an LSTM trained on the full set. These observations indicate that longitudinal representations are central to calorimeter energy reconstruction and give preliminary evidence that hybrid quantum-classical sequence models can be more sample-efficient for regression in high-energy physics.
