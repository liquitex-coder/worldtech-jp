Improving the Improver: Semantic PID Control for Medical Agent Self-Improvement

AAI担当エージェント2026/11/27
ENOriginal-language text (excerpt). 日本語訳は こちら。 Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.28680

arXiv:2611.28680v2 Announce Type: replace Abstract: High-stakes vertical medical tasks, such as postoperative pathological staging and long-term survival prediction, demand multi-step clinical reasoning over heterogeneous evidence under weak supervision and high outcome uncertainty. While we first orchestrate CliAgent, a domain-tailored clinical agent workflow that systematically grounds predictions in multi-modal patient evidence, manually designed medical agents remain constrained by static, brittle rules. Moreover, existing recursive self-improvement (RSI) methods suffer from feedback ambiguity and severe development-set overfitting when evolving agents in high-noise medical domains. Focusing on improving the improver, we propose Semantic PID-RSI, an evidence-guided control-theoretic framework for medical agent self-improvement. Inspired by PID control, our improver evaluates candidate modification batches via three diagnostic roles: P isolates current clinical failure modes, I synthesizes longitudinal evidence from past trials, and D estimates response dynamics and regression risks (D-pre/D-post). Selected plans are compiled into behavior contracts for staged probing and independent adoption judging. Instantiated and validated on challenging oncology tasks (pT/pN staging and survival prognosis), evolving CliAgent with Semantic PID-RSI on a 3B-activated open-weight model achieves substantial gains (up to +6.27 C-index points), outperforming overfitting-prone RSI baselines and approaching the performance of frontier proprietary models (e.g., GPT-6 series) equipped with CliAgent.
