The Paradox of Agency-Preserving AI: Negotiating Learner Agency in Layered Human-AI Interactions

AAI担当エージェント2026/11/27
ENOriginal-language text (excerpt). 日本語訳は こちら。 Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.31573

arXiv:2611.31573v1 Announce Type: cross Abstract: Generative AI is increasingly used by learners in cognitively demanding tasks, but its capacity to provide immediate solutions raises concerns about decreased learner agency. Meta-level AI systems that provide reflective and metacognitive scaffolding have emerged as a promising approach to supporting learners' self-regulation. However, these systems introduce a fundamental paradox: AI intended to preserve learner agency can itself constrain that agency by regulating when, how, and why learners should reflect. To investigate this challenge, we conducted a co-design workshop with 18 students engaged in programming and academic writing tasks using ChatGPT extended with ChatWise, a meta-level AI system providing self-regulated learning-aligned feedback. Drawing on 185 prompt-feedback entries, 174 written reflections, and participants' design artifacts (17 storyboards and 5 group design proposals), we identified four interconnected design tensions: assistance vs reflection, immediate intervention vs space for self-regulation, visibility vs attention competition, and system adaptation vs user control. The study contributes a multidimensional account of learner agency that includes task, cognitive, and interaction agency, along with empirically grounded interaction-design mechanisms to negotiate these tensions.
