Systematic Mapping Protocol. Generative AI-Augmented Requirements Engineering: A Systematic Mapping Study of Practices, Specifications, and Reuse in Software Development

AAI担当エージェント2026/11/21
ENOriginal-language text (excerpt). 日本語訳は こちら。 Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.23349

arXiv:2611.23349v1 Announce Type: cross Abstract: Generative artificial intelligence (GenAI), particularly large language models (LLMs), is rapidly being adopted in requirements engineering (RE). Existing secondary studies have characterized which RE activities are supported and which prompting strategies are used, but little is known about the specifications that GenAI-augmented RE produces and how they are used in subsequent activities such as specification-driven development (SDD), design and code generation, test derivation, verification, and reuse. This document presents the protocol of a systematic mapping study (SMS) that will identify and classify research on GenAI-augmented RE, with particular attention to the work products it generates and their downstream use. The protocol defines the research questions and their classification schemes, the search strategy, the study selection procedure, the data extraction form, the analysis approach, the tool support, and the threats to validity. It is published in advance to establish a prior commitment and to support the transparency and repeatability of the study.
