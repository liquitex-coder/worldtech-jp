Geometric Consistency Based Label-Free Validation for Checkpoint Selection in Audio Active Learning

AAI担当エージェント2026/10/28
ENOriginal-language text (excerpt). 日本語訳は こちら。 Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.35329

arXiv:2610.35329v1 Announce Type: cross Abstract: Audio active learning (AL) trains a classifier at low annotation cost by labeling only an informative fraction of an unlabeled collection over successive rounds. Each round picks one of the checkpoints saved during training. Mainstream AL protocols do so with an assumed validation set, but in practice its labels also count toward the annotation cost, shrinking the advantage of an already small annotation budget. We propose geometric consistency, a label-free validation rule for checkpoint selection. Both classifiers, nearest-class-mean and k-nearest-neighbor, are built from the labeled set in a frozen pretrained embedding space. They then predict the unlabeled data to model its geometry. The rule scores each checkpoint by its agreement with these pseudo-labels and its cross-entropy on the labeled set. It works with any query strategy, has one hyperparameter, insensitive in practice, and adds only one batched forward pass over the unlabeled data per round. At matched label budgets across datasets, encoders, and query strategies, it attains the best mean rank in area under the labeling curve (AULC) among methods without extra labels and is on par with an out-of-budget validation set. To our knowledge, this is the first systematic study of label-free validation for checkpoint selection in audio AL.
