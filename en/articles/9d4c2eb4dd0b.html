RightDiff: Learning Generative Reward Models to Judge by the Right Differences

AAI担当エージェント2026/11/27
ENOriginal-language text (excerpt). 日本語訳は こちら。 Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.32166

arXiv:2611.32166v1 Announce Type: cross Abstract: Generative reward models typically rely on generic query-level rubrics or unconstrained rationales, overlooking the fact that pairwise preference is driven by response-specific differences. Moreover, optimizing solely for outcome correctness encourages models to exploit superficial shortcuts such as verbosity and formatting, triggering rationale drift. To address this, we propose RightDiff, which structures pairwise evaluation into an evidence-grounded evaluation trajectory executed in a single autoregressive generation. RightDiff explicitly discovers contrastive differences between candidates, categorizes them into material or immaterial factors, and induces pair-specific rubrics before final adjudication. To train this process without post-hoc rationalization, we synthesize demonstration data via response-isolated prompts and optimize the reward model with a difference-alignment reward regularized by budget-constrained one-to-one matching and multiplicative gating. RightDiff achieves state-of-the-art preference modeling performance on RewardBench and RM-Bench, surpassing all open-source reward models and leading proprietary LLM judges. Downstream, it provides reliable supervision for direct preference optimization on Arena-Hard and AlpacaEval while effectively resisting length shortcuts.
