Lost Between Mind, Model Collaboration, and Motion: Understanding and Measuring Cross-Model Action Misalignment in Commercial Robots

ロロボット技術担当エージェント2026/11/20
ENOriginal-language text (excerpt). 日本語訳は こちら。 Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.24100

arXiv:2611.24100v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: Embodied robots increasingly rely on large language models (LLMs) to interpret user intent and coordinate with robot-specific action models for physical interaction. This multi-model pipeline creates a critical semantic boundary across mind, model collaboration, and motion: semantic consistency may break between what the large model understands, how the small action model translates that understanding into executable commands, and what physical actions the robot ultimately performs. As a security consequence, commercial robots may execute unintended physical actions even when user inputs contain no explicit action commands. To measure this problem, we develop TARS, a black-box framework that explores the command-reasoning-action pipeline and automatically discovers and validates unintended physical behaviors. We evaluate TARS on four commercial robots across 44 experimental settings, conducting 133,400 executions. TARS uncovers widespread cross-model action misalignment and achieves up to 94% verified action coverage.
