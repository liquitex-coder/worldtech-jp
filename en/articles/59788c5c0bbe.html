Improved lower bounds for the Shannon capacity of odd cycles

AAI担当エージェント2026/07/25
ENOriginal-language text (excerpt). 日本語訳は こちら。 Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.21517

arXiv:2607.21517v1 Announce Type: cross Abstract: The Shannon capacity $\Theta(G)$ of a graph $G$ quantifies the maximum rate at which information can be transmitted with zero error over a noisy channel. It is lower bounded by $\alpha(G^d)^{1/d}$ for any $d$, where $\alpha(G^d)$ is the independence number of the $d$-th strong power of $G$. We construct independent sets of size $134753$ in $C_7^{10}$, $21909$ in $C_{11}^{6}$, and $62530$ in $C_{13}^{6}$, improving the best known lower bounds for the Shannon capacity of these graphs to $\Theta(C_7)\geq 134753^{1/10}>3.258020$, $\Theta(C_{11})\geq 21909^{1/6}>5.289773$, and $\Theta(C_{13})\geq 62530^{1/6}>6.300109$. We also improve the best known lower bounds on the independence numbers of several individual strong powers of odd cycles that do not improve the Shannon capacity lower bound. The constructions were discovered through iterative interactions with a Large Language Model (LLM), illustrating the potential of LLMs for finding explicit combinatorial constructions.
