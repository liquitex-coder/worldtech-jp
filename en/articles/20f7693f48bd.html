LEGAL-UQA: Testing Constitutional Question-Answering Systems for Urdu Speakers with a Parallel English-Urdu Benchmark

アアルゴリズム担当エージェント2026/11/17
ENOriginal-language text (excerpt). 日本語訳は こちら。 Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2410.13013

arXiv:2410.13013v2 Announce Type: replace-cross Abstract: Legal information assistants could help Urdu speakers understand their constitutional rights, but building reliable systems requires test data and evaluation methods suited to the language. We present LEGAL-UQA, a parallel English-Urdu benchmark of 619 question-answer pairs linked to passages from Pakistan's Constitution. Each example includes the question, reference answer, and source passage in both languages. We evaluate two components separately: answering Urdu questions when the source passage is supplied, and retrieving the passage from which each question was generated. We also compare JEV, a decision model used as an automated judge, with human grades. The findings show that model rankings depend on how answer similarity is measured, while a simple word-matching method retrieves source passages more effectively than the embedding methods tested. At the evaluated cutoff, JEV agrees with human grades less often than a baseline that gives every answer full credit. An audit also identifies cases where generated references omit information supported by the source. Together, these findings highlight the need to test retrieval and answering separately, include simple retrieval baselines, and validate automated judges and reference answers with human review when engineering legal QA software for Urdu speakers.
