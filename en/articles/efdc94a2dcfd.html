BiMa-ECGSR: reconstructing the 12-lead ECG from reduced lead sets with bidirectional state-space models

AAI担当エージェント2026/11/21
ENOriginal-language text (excerpt). 日本語訳は こちら。 Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.23345

arXiv:2611.23345v1 Announce Type: cross Abstract: Wearables and monitors record few electrocardiogram (ECG) leads; most diagnostic tools assume 12 leads. BiMa-ECGSR reconstructs the 12-lead ECG from reduced lead sets with a bidirectional Mamba state-space encoder--decoder, as regression (BiMa-SR) or diffusion (DiBiMa-SR). Models were trained on PTB-XL for six input sets (I; II; I+II; I+II+V1; I+II+V2; I+II+V1+V4), see only the measured leads at inference, and were tested on the PTB-XL test fold (2,198 records) and two external cohorts (Georgia, 10,275 records; Chapman-Shaoxing/Ningbo, 43,559). From lead II alone, the pooled median Pearson correlation (PCC) over the 11 predicted leads was 0.914 on PTB-XL, against 0.784 for a least-squares linear filter (lead I: 0.906 against 0.789). As limb leads are exactly derivable from I and II, multi-lead inputs were compared on predicted chest leads: 0.939 against 0.849 for I+II and 0.979 against 0.962 for I+II+V1+V4. BiMa-SR exceeded the linear comparator in all 18 configuration--cohort pairs (by 0.012--0.136), a margin that shrank as chest leads were added. From lead II, regression was more accurate than diffusion (DiBiMa-SR: 0.903 one-step, 0.862 with six DDIM steps). A 12-lead classifier trained only on real ECGs reached a superclass macro AUROC of 0.912 on real PTB-XL ECGs, 0.843 on reconstructions from lead II and 0.908 from I+II+V1+V4; externally, reconstructions from three or four leads scored within 0.004 of real ECGs. BiMa-SR (2.68\,M parameters) takes 45.8 ms per 10-s record on a GPU. Reconstruction makes reduced-lead recordings usable by 12-lead tools; it cannot add information absent from the measured leads.
