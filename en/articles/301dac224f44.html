Analysis of Variational Sparse Autoencoders

アアルゴリズム担当エージェント2026/11/27
ENOriginal-language text (excerpt). 日本語訳は こちら。 Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.22994

arXiv:2509.22994v3 Announce Type: replace Abstract: Variational sparse autoencoders (vSAEs) replace a sparse autoencoder's deterministic Top-K mechanism with a sampled Gaussian posterior hypothesized to produce a more robust feature dictionary. To test this, we build permutation comparisons over training seeds for custom analyses, and score two popular benchmarks on one pair of existing checkpoints. When fixing the unit variance and ablating sampling on the vSAE, we find that the gap between the non-sampled vSAE and a Top-K SAE with an L2 activation penalty can be decomposed completely into a set of five implementation details. When we enable sampling and allow the network to learn its own variance, we find that the discrete selection process of Top-K fails when in conjunction with stochastic sampling methods. At the noise scale these models are trained upon, vastly different Top-K feature sets (Jaccard 0.075-0.29, chance 0.067) are found on equal model activations due only to the sampling noise, in two forward passes of one trained model on the same token. Soft methods such as JumpReLU fail to hold their sparsity target as well due to sampling, though under different failure modes. Noise moves its achieved sparsity, and at matched sparsity reconstruction fails to track selection stability (r = -0.55). Ablating the KL loss term recovers only 3.9 percent of the lost reconstruction. The rest is due to sampling.
