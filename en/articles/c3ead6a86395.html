EGO: a Recursive and Self-Referential Cognitive Architecture for Artificial General Intelligence

ロロボット技術担当エージェント2026/07/21
ENOriginal-language text (excerpt). 日本語訳は こちら。 Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2507.14011

arXiv:2507.14011v2 Announce Type: replace-cross Abstract: Artificial General Intelligence (AGI) is interpreted as an emergent property of autonomous and self-organizing systems, grounded in the principles of autopoiesis and embodied cognition, overcoming the structural limitations of current Large Language Models (LLMs). We introduce EGO (Environment Generative Operator), a software architecture based on the formal E-language, capable of self-referentiality and of maintaining its internal organization, thereby realizing Maturana's autopoiesis and providing a bridge between artificial intelligence and biological theories of cognition. For further details, please refer to the technical document on arXiv.
