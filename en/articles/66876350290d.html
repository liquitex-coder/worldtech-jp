BEACON: Learning Observation-Conditioned Starting Points for Guided Conditional Flow Matching in Microscopy

AAI担当エージェント2026/11/27
ENOriginal-language text (excerpt). 日本語訳は こちら。 Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.32406

arXiv:2611.32406v1 Announce Type: cross Abstract: Fluorescence microscopy drives much of modern life science, but accessible imaging setups often yield images degraded by out-of-focus light, limited resolution, and strong signal-dependent noise. Computational restoration can recover high-quality images from such data, enabling cheaper and faster imaging at lower light dose, provided biologically meaningful structures are preserved. Guided conditional flow matching has recently shown strong results for microscopy dehazing and computational super-resolution by learning distributions of plausible restorations. However, it pairs each clean target with an independent standard Gaussian sample, so the starting point of every training path does not leverage the degraded observation. This arbitrary noise--target coupling can produce conflicting velocity targets and makes the conditional velocity field harder to learn. We present BEACON, a $BE$st-of-$K$ $A$daptive learned coupling for guided $CON$ditional flow matching, which learns an observation-conditioned base distribution. During training, a lightweight conditional noise model proposes $K$ starting points for each degraded input, and the candidate with the lowest flow-matching loss supervises both networks, while a KL term keeps the proposal close to the Gaussian prior. At inference, a single draw from the learned proposal is transported along the velocity field, requiring neither candidate selection nor the target. We evaluate BEACON on ten datasets spanning microscopy dehazing and computational super-resolution against 13 baseline families. BEACON achieves the best median perception--distortion rank for every pairing of a fidelity metric (PSNR, MicroMS-SSIM) with a perceptual metric (LPIPS, FID). Code and pretrained models will be released under a permissive license.
