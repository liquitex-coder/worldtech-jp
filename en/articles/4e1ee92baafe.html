Data-Efficient Backdoor Attacks on Code Models via Trigger-Neutralized Counterfactual Scoring

AAI担当エージェント2026/10/27
ENOriginal-language text (excerpt). 日本語訳は こちら。 Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.32334

arXiv:2610.32334v1 Announce Type: cross Abstract: Pre-trained code models are exposed to backdoor poisoning, in which crafted training samples make the model misbehave on a trigger. A capable attacker minimizes the number of such samples, since each adds cost and exposure and a lower poisoning rate leaves a smaller footprint for defenses. Which samples to poison therefore matters, but existing data-efficient selection methods target image classification, offer no scoring mechanism for autoregressive generation, and have not been studied on code. We propose Trigger-Neutralized Counterfactual Scoring (TNCS), which isolates a trigger's contribution to the target behavior by comparing the scoring model's output on a triggered input with its output on a trigger-neutralized counterfactual, covering classification and generation alike. TNCS attains higher attack success rates than the baselines at very low poisoning ratios, across three CodeXGLUE tasks and five trigger families, without degrading clean-task performance, and its margin grows as the ratio decreases. It is the only method effective on every trigger family, and its selected poison set transfers to mismatched victims.
