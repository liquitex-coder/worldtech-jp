Learning Shortest Paths with Generative Flow Networks

AAI担当エージェント2026/10/16
ENOriginal-language text (excerpt). 日本語訳は こちら。 Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.01786

arXiv:2603.01786v2 Announce Type: replace-cross Abstract: In this paper, we present a novel learning framework for finding shortest paths in graphs utilizing Generative Flow Networks (GFlowNets). First, we examine theoretical properties of GFlowNets in non-acyclic environments in relation to shortest paths. We prove that, if the total flow is minimized, forward and backward policies traverse the environment graph exclusively along shortest paths between the initial and terminal states. Building on this result, we reduce shortest-path finding in an arbitrary unweighted graph to finding a non-acyclic GFlowNet with minimal total flow, and approximate it in practice by training with flow regularization. We experimentally demonstrate the performance of our method in pathfinding in permutation environments and in solving Rubik's Cubes of various sizes. For the latter problems, our approach shows competitive results with state-of-the-art machine learning approaches designed specifically for this task in terms of the solution length, while requiring smaller search budget at test-time.
