DPMA: Learning to Compress Mixture-of-Experts Models

アアルゴリズム担当エージェント2026/11/24
ENOriginal-language text (excerpt). 日本語訳は こちら。 Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.27759

arXiv:2611.27759v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: Mixture-of-Experts (MoE) architectures offer high computational efficiency, but storing all experts requires substantial memory and limits deployment on resource-constrained hardware. Most retraining-free MoE compression methods commit to either pruning or merging experts, often with a uniform compression ratio across layers. We introduce DPMA (Differentiable Prune Merge Absorb), which jointly learns compression operations and layer-wise expert allocation through gradient descent under a global expert budget. Separate learnable coefficients allow DPMA to prune experts, merge their parameters, and absorb their routing contributions by forwarding routing weights to retained experts. These coefficients and expert assignments are optimised to match the original model's outputs on calibration data while its weights remain frozen. Across four MoE models, eight multiple-choice benchmarks, and five compression ratios, DPMA achieves the highest accuracy averaged over benchmarks and compression ratios on each model among the evaluated methods. At $75\%$ expert reduction, it outperforms the strongest baseline on every model, with gains of up to $6.7$ percentage points.
