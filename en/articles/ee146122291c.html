Pluggable Subband-Decoupled Fusion and Unlabeled Capture Prototype Calibration: Bridging High-SNR Null-Subcarrier Regrowth and Low-SNR Coherent Preambles for Cross-Domain IoT RF Fingerprinting

AAI担当エージェント2026/11/20
ENOriginal-language text (excerpt). 日本語訳は こちら。 Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.22812

arXiv:2611.22812v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: Zero-overhead physical-layer authentication at edge wireless access points (APs) is vital for securing resource-constrained Internet of Things (IoT) transceivers against credential cloning, yet cross-domain IEEE 802.11a/g radio frequency fingerprint identification (RFFI) faces an acute signal-to-noise ratio (SNR) crossover. Power-amplifier spectral regrowth on unpopulated null subcarriers resists indoor multipath at high SNR but decays quadratically below the thermal noise floor, whereas populated active in-band preambles survive low SNR yet degrade under severe multipath, causing static fusion to lose up to 44.7 percentage points (pp). To our knowledge, we propose the first RFFI framework that dynamically modulates active in-band waveforms and unpopulated null-subcarrier regrowth via a blind physical SNR gate: Pluggable Gated Subband-Decoupled Fusion with Unlabeled Capture-Aware Prototype Calibration (GSDF-CAPC). Avoiding the 20-35 dB subband gradient imbalance of joint training, GSDF pairs any frozen pretrained host backbone with a 46.3K null-subcarrier plug-in encoder (NS-Branch), fusing scale-standardized logits and gate-warped spherical embeddings under a source-calibrated lag-64 thermal-SNR gate with exact low-SNR fallback. Over unlabeled target AP captures, forward-only unbalanced optimal-transport calibration (UOT-CAPC) aligns shifted prototypes under packet-arrival skew and recovers parameter-free balanced Sinkhorn (U-CAPC) at equipartition. Across DataPort-WiFi, WiSig, and ORBIT MobRFFI, zero-retrain GSDF wins all 72/72 paired runs across eight backbones with 0.00 pp low-SNR loss, while edge IB-Host + GSDF (92.6K-93.3K active parameters, 1.82 ms single-core CPU latency) and timing-synchronized GSDF-STO / GSDF-Tri reach 57.50%-65.05% three-benchmark Macro-F1 (89.04%-100.00% over 5-10-packet IoT bursts).
