Refine, Don't Reinvent: RASA-Bench for Source-Grounded Hindi Prose Refinement

AAI担当エージェント2026/11/28
ENOriginal-language text (excerpt). 日本語訳は こちら。 Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.29750

arXiv:2611.29750v2 Announce Type: replace-cross Abstract: For a producer of Hindi creative prose, drafting is cheap and editing is not: a machine-written episode, story, or essay reads as under-written and flat, laced with translationese, and it sometimes quietly alters what the source says. RASA-Bench targets that editing task across three Hindi prose forms, measuring how well a model refines an under-written AI draft, a task distinct from writing from scratch. Across 2,050 drafts and eight refiners (16,400 rewrites), a four-layer protocol credits a rewrite only after it passes a faithfulness check against its own source, then ranks the survivors by holistic editorial preference through per-form Bradley-Terry fits whose centered coefficients are standardized before equal-weight averaging. Staying faithful proves to be a decisive test that behaves differently by form: gate-failure rates range from 9.1% on short stories to 29.5% on audio fiction, and the drift that disqualifies a rewrite shifts from events and identity in narrative to claims and evidence in essays. A 600-case human audit puts gate agreement at 0.925 (0.91 to 0.94 across forms), whereas subjective preference is form-sensitive and least settled for essays, among human readers no less than the model judge. The benchmark, evaluation protocol, and Hindi prose diagnostics are available at https://abdur75648.github.io/rasa-bench.
