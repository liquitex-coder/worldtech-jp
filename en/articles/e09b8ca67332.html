Recalling the Past to Answer the Present: Context-Aware Multi-Evidence Reasoning for Streaming Video Question Answering

AAI担当エージェント2026/11/28
ENOriginal-language text (excerpt). 日本語訳は こちら。 Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.34545

arXiv:2611.34545v1 Announce Type: cross Abstract: Vision-language models (VLMs) have achieved remarkable progress in video understanding, yet existing video question answering (VQA) benchmarks mainly evaluate models in offline settings. Real-world streaming scenarios instead require models to answer context-dependent queries by retrieving and reasoning over evidence distributed across the video history. We introduce StreamRecall, a benchmark for context-aware Multi evidence reasoning in streaming video QA. StreamRecall requires models to retrieve, associate, and reason over temporally scattered evidence to answer queries at the appropriate moment. It contains 4,983 questions over 462 videos, with context-aligned query times and fine-grained temporal evidence annotations. We further propose the Evidence Utilization Cascade to diagnose model reasoning through evidence localization, grounding, chain completion, and answer derivation. To enable efficient long-term streaming reasoning, we present EviStream, a training-free causal visual memory mechanism that preserves reasoning-critical evidence under a fixed KV budget. Experiments reveal substantial gaps between current VLMs and realistic streaming requirements, establishing StreamRecall as a foundation for developing evidence-aware streaming video intelligence.
