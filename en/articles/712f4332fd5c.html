Agentic-Priors: Using Large Language Models to Address the Cold Start Problem in Preference Elicitation

AAI担当エージェント2026/10/17
ENOriginal-language text (excerpt). 日本語訳は こちら。 Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.21938

arXiv:2610.21938v1 Announce Type: cross Abstract: An agent that learns a user's preferences from their choices faces a cold start problem: before any choices are observed, it must rely solely on a prior. Informative priors are usually fitted to historical choice data, which new tasks and deployments lack. To address this, we present Agentic-Priors, which lets agentic systems use the implicit knowledge of large language models (LLMs) instead. Specifically, an LLM states a new user's trade-offs in interpretable terms, such as willingness to pay or value of time, which we map to a prior over multinomial logit coefficients. This prior is then refined by a Bayesian learner as choices are observed. Across seven choice datasets, Agentic-Priors improves cold start accuracy over an uninformed prior by 5-40 percentage points. It also outperforms AutoElicit, a state-of-the-art LLM prior-elicitation method, on three datasets and loses on one. It is up to 18 points more accurate than in-context learning, and never significantly worse. Against hierarchical Bayes models fitted to surveys of increasing size, our approach is worth roughly 6-40 surveyed users, in terms of log-loss, so its value is concentrated in a deployment's first users.
