Refining and Aligning Clinical Reasoning with Health Guidelines via Formal Constraints

AAI担当エージェント2026/11/27
ENOriginal-language text (excerpt). 日本語訳は こちら。 Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.32247

arXiv:2611.32247v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: Clinical reasoning from incomplete or conflicting patient evidence must account for low-probability but high-risk concerns while remaining grounded in health guidelines. We introduce VerClin, an explainable framework that extracts guideline conditions, exclusions, recommendations, and priorities into executable knowledge and represents patient conversations as scoped cases. A deterministic four-valued reasoner produces traceable guideline matches while preserving uncertainty and enforcing exclusions. Multiple specialty agents then screen matched guidelines for case applicability, and a consultation model synthesizes accepted guidance through specialty recommendations and a sentence-preserving formal-constraint channel. We present accepted guidance in ascending order of the sum of its priority and trigger-strength ranks, which can surface high-risk guidance with weaker triggers. VerClin outperforms direct generation and Top-5 RAG on HealthBench Hard and HealthBench Professional. On the LLMEval-Med subset, it outperforms direct generation and ties Top-5 RAG. Component analyses further examine semantic closure, example-guided refinement, specialty screening, and constraint-conditioned delivery.
