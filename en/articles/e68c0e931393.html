GenCoord: Executable Commitments for Embodied LLM Agents under Private Information

AAI担当エージェント2026/10/14
ENOriginal-language text (excerpt). 日本語訳は こちら。 Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.22055

arXiv:2608.22055v3 Announce Type: replace Abstract: Embodied language-model agents must coordinate interdependent actions while each holds private task information. In dialogue-based coordination, models generate messages and interpret replies to decide what each agent should do, spending inference before the team acts. We propose GenCoord, a decentralized protocol that makes the division of work itself the message. A proposal pairs the sender's skill path with its partner's continuation in Short DSL, a compact language for expressing joint work. Compatible paths form commitments linking the sender's delivery to the partner's work after verified receipt. We check fulfillment separately from original-task success. Complete requests also let a deterministic receiver proceed without another model call. In Minecraft, trained local 0.8B agents match the task success of controlled free-form communication while reducing median commitment time by 68.2%. The same-field full-key JSON control approaches free-form output length and delay, indicating that the amount generated matters even when messages are structured. With the same prompted DeepSeek model, both protocols complete nearly all tasks, but dialogue generates 2.8 times as many output tokens. Multistep commitments improve held-out-template success from 91.3% to 98.1% with fewer decisions. These results point to a design principle for embodied cooperation: make the division of work the message, and spend inference on choosing and revising the work.
