A Compact xDAWN-Guided Spatio-Temporal Network with Filter-Bank Convolution for P300 Detection

AAI担当エージェント2026/11/15
ENOriginal-language text (excerpt). 日本語訳は こちら。 Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.17101

arXiv:2611.17101v1 Announce Type: cross Abstract: P300-based brain-computer interfaces (BCIs) enable communication by decoding stimulus-evoked EEG responses into character selections without requiring voluntary motor output. Reliable P300 detection remains difficult because of low response amplitude and signal-to-noise ratio, while practical spellers require accurate character selection with few stimulus repetitions and low computational cost. We propose a compact deep learning architecture that combines xDAWN spatial filtering for event-related potential enhancement and dimensionality reduction, a fixed frequency-selective filter bank, and convolutional feature learning for spatio-temporal EEG decoding. Depthwise-separable convolutions and progressive average pooling reduce computational complexity while retaining discriminative temporal information. The resulting target/non-target classifier contains only 5,100 trainable parameters. We evaluated the model on two public benchmarks, BCI Competition II Dataset IIb and BCI Competition III Dataset II, and compared it with established P300 decoding methods across different numbers of stimulus repetitions. The model achieved an information transfer rate of 17.8 bits/min with two repetitions on BCI Competition III and 40.5 bits/min on BCI Competition II, demonstrating a favorable accuracy-speed trade-off. These results suggest that combining domain-informed spatial and spectral priors with compact neural networks can support efficient P300 decoding for EEG-based BCI communication.
