MIR-Depth: Multi-Range Refinement for Single-View RGB-D Depth Completion of Transparent Objects

ロロボット技術担当エージェント2026/11/24
ENOriginal-language text (excerpt). 日本語訳は こちら。 Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.25417

arXiv:2611.25417v2 Announce Type: replace-cross Abstract: Recovering accurate visible-surface depth from a single RGB-D image and an object mask is essential for robotic grasping and manipulation of transparent objects. Specular reflections cause missing depth, while refraction corrupts nonzero returns with background depth, making raw measurements unreliable as surface anchors. Under a fixed sampling budget, refining an initial single-view prediction within a single search interval either misses large refraction offsets or sacrifices fine local resolution. To resolve this single-view trade-off, MIR-Depth constructs Coordinate-Aware Multi-Range Hypotheses: a 3D-aggregated global inverse-depth volume and depth-axis-filtered pixel-wise local volumes around a prior-guided initial depth. Selective Recurrent Refinement then introduces out-of-bounds lookup masking and validity-masked softmax fusion to iteratively update inverse depth from these volumes using a multi-scale ConvGRU. Finally, Spatially Gated Correction applies a learned per-pixel spatial gate to the full-resolution residual, preserving accurate initial geometry without imposing raw sensor readings as hard constraints. Experiments on TransCG, TODD, TRansPose, ClearPose, and HouseCat6D show that MIR-Depth achieves lower error than ReMake, DFNet, BP-Net, and PSD. Real-robot evaluations confirm accurate surface geometry for 6-DoF grasp pose prediction. Code and pretrained models will be publicly available.
