Flying over The Uncertain Nature (FORTUNE): Intelligent and Humanistic 3D Path Planning for Low-Altitude Collaboration

ロロボット技術担当エージェント2026/11/24
ENOriginal-language text (excerpt). 日本語訳は こちら。 Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.03408

arXiv:2608.03408v2 Announce Type: replace Abstract: Low-altitude intelligent agents are driving demand for timely, socially responsible collaborative sensing in dynamic urban environments. Yet jointly handling heterogeneous spatio-temporal sensing demands, real-time uncertainty, and human-centered constraints in a unified multi-uncrewed aerial vehicle (UAV) framework remains challenging. We study 3D multi-UAV path planning and joint task assignment under heterogeneous spatio-temporal uncertainty of ground points of interest (PoIs). Unlike studies assuming static, known PoIs, we formulate a unified model capturing persistent (Type-I), temporally predictable (Type-II), and emergent unpredictable (Type-III) demands. Following the low-altitude-for-good principle, we explicitly model altitude-dependent societal and environmental costs, including noise and public safety risks, jointly optimizing sensing effectiveness and socially compliant operations. To solve the resulting large-scale mixed-integer nonlinear problem, we propose FORTUNE, a hierarchical offline-online framework. Offline, a Transformer-based predictor forecasts Type-II activation windows, while an enhanced sparrow search algorithm (ES2A) with priority-aware decoding and danger-aware evolution generates globally coordinated flight plans. Online, lightweight trajectory refinement accommodates new Type-III PoIs while preserving global mission coherence. Experiments on real-world traffic and synthetic datasets show FORTUNE consistently outperforms state-of-the-art benchmarks, validating effectiveness, scalability, and practical applicability in dynamic low-altitude systems.
