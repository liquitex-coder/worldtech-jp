RoboEvo$^2$: Recursive Robot Self-Improvement via Harness-Policy Co-Evolution

AAI担当エージェント2026/11/28
ENOriginal-language text (excerpt). 日本語訳は こちら。 Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.34675

arXiv:2611.34675v1 Announce Type: cross Abstract: Robots should adapt to unfamiliar environments and acquire new skills through interaction, then execute those skills efficiently with practice. Vision-language-action policies provide efficient control, but often need additional human demonstrations to handle unfamiliar settings; reasoning agents can discover solutions using broad prior knowledge and robot-control APIs, but repeated deliberation and repair are costly. Inspired by human learning through reasoning and practice, we propose RoboEvo$^2$, a framework for recursive harness-policy co-evolution. In Policy-to-Harness (P2H) evolution, policy failures guide a coding agent to search complementary Code, Code+ICL, and Code+Policy routes, consolidating verified solutions into reusable skills, routing strategies, and experience memory. In Harness-to-Policy (H2P) evolution, verified trajectories provide supervised training, while Counterfactual Preference Distillation (CPD) teaches the policy to favor successful corrections over failures from the same starting state. The improved policy provides stronger motor skills for subsequent search, while its remaining failures redirect harness evolution, closing the reciprocal loop. Through recursive co-evolution without additional human demonstrations, RoboEvo$^2$ improves standalone success from 11.0% to 84.1% and hybrid success from 30.4% to 90.5% on LIBERO-PRO, with similar gains and reduced agentic costs on RoboTwin C2R. Further experiments demonstrate autonomous task acquisition, cross-embodiment harness transfer, and 95.0% hybrid success on the Unitree G1-D. Our models and code will be publicly released.
