InterGenEHR$^+$: An Interactive Framework for Natural Language-Conditioned Generation of Longitudinal Patient Trajectories

AAI担当エージェント2026/11/28
ENOriginal-language text (excerpt). 日本語訳は こちら。 Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.33788

arXiv:2611.33788v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: Objective: To develop and validate InterGenEHR$^+$, an interactive framework for natural language-conditioned generation of synthetic tabular electronic health records (EHRs) and longitudinal trajectories for scenario-based evaluation of machine learning models. Methods: An optional large language model (LLM) parser converts clinical requests into structured constraints. Conditional variational autoencoders and diffusion models generate single-time-point records, while diffusion models generate longitudinal trajectories. We evaluate parsing, distributional fidelity, imputation, clinical-constraint adherence, mortality prediction, and clinical plausibility using a multi-institutional cohort of 874 patients receiving venovenous extracorporeal membrane oxygenation (VV-ECMO). Results: Gemma 3 4B with few-shot prompting achieves the highest parsing performance with a low spurious slot rate. IGE-TabDDPM and IGE-PMVAE perform well in single-time-point generation; IGE-CSDI, IGE-FGTI, and IGE-MTSCI lead on different longitudinal fidelity metrics. Across five random seeds and three classifiers, synthetic cohorts retain predictive utility for mortality prediction on real test data. Clinician ratings support trajectory plausibility and alignment with requested scenarios. Conclusion: InterGenEHR$^+$ supports clinician-specified generation of EHR trajectories with statistical fidelity and downstream predictive utility in the evaluated VV-ECMO cohort. Extension to other clinical settings requires further validation. Significance: The framework supports scenario-based evaluation of machine learning models in data-sparse critical care settings.
