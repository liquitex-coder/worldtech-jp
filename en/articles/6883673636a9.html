Diffusion Tree Sampling for Inference Time Adaptation of Crystal Generative Models

アアルゴリズム担当エージェント2026/11/17
ENOriginal-language text (excerpt). 日本語訳は こちら。 Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.17462

arXiv:2611.17462v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: Diffusion-based generative models have become strong priors over plausible crystal structures, but turning that prior into promising candidates requires steering toward several conflicting properties at once. Fine-tuning is expensive and produces a new model for every new objective, while differentiable guidance is not applicable to many real-world tasks. We instead adapt these generative models at inference time using Diffusion Tree Sampling (DTS), a Monte Carlo tree search procedure over the denoising trajectory. We make two technical contributions on top of DTS that are particularly relevant for AI-for-science applications. First, we show how to use existing property-conditional generative models (e.g., models fine-tuned for classifier-free guidance) as guided proposals inside DTS, without distorting the target distribution. Second, we extend DTS to multi-objective sampling, so that a single search produces samples spanning an entire Pareto front. We instantiate both adaptations on de novo crystal generation: improving the stability of crystals generated by different models, using pretrained conditional models to generate crystals belonging to rare space groups or with high bulk modulus, and generating crystals along a Pareto front of high dielectric constant and high bandgap materials. Across these tasks, DTS-based inference-time adaptation successfully improves model outputs without modifying the models themselves.
