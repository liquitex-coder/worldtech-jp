A Negotiation-Aware Hybrid LLM Framework for Intent Interpretation and Service Discovery

AAI担当エージェント2026/11/01
ENOriginal-language text (excerpt). 日本語訳は こちら。 Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.40161

arXiv:2610.40161v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: Increasingly, users express their needs as natural-language intents rather than structured requests. These intents may be incomplete, high-level, or internally conflicting. A conflicting intent either violates fundamental properties of the cloud paradigm or states mutually inconsistent requirements. Classical composition approaches are designed for structured, low-level, and complete requests. They cannot interpret such intents, discover suitable services from provider catalogs, or negotiate conflicting requirements with users. To address this gap, we propose a negotiation-aware framework for intent interpretation and service discovery. The framework first refines the natural-language intent and dynamically detects conflicts. It then structures the intent into a provider-independent TOSCA template. For each specification, it retrieves candidate service offers through deterministic matching grounded in cloud provider offerings. Some conflicts arise during interpretation. Other requirements prove unsatisfiable during discovery. Both are resolved through iterative natural-language negotiation with the user. We evaluate the framework on 70 diverse intents against single-shot and enhanced-prompting baselines. It improves recall and F1 for both conflict detection and service discovery. It also eliminates hallucinated offers and resolves conflicts at a cost proportional to their number.
