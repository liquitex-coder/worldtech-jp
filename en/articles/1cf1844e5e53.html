OmniScore-Bench: Evaluating Vision-Language Models on Musical Score Reading

AAI担当エージェント2026/11/17
ENOriginal-language text (excerpt). 日本語訳は こちら。 Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.17870

arXiv:2611.17870v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: Vision-language models (VLMs) can answer questions about musical scores, but their ability to recognise notation, recover its structure and interpret its musical meaning requires separate evaluation. Existing optical music recognition (OMR) resources establish detection and transcription measurements; a transcription score alone leaves the strengths and bottlenecks of a VLM score reader unresolved. We introduce OmniScore-Bench, an evaluation of OMR capabilities in VLMs through 3,000 questions spanning six aspects and 24 task subtypes. Each question specifies a visible scope, reference annotations and an answer schema, enabling automatic evaluation from symbol localisation to musical consistency and evidence-linked question answering. Our assessment of twelve VLMs reveals operation-specific capability profiles and different transcription leaders across excerpt, single-page and multipage tasks. Controlled analyses examine the roles of score images, auxiliary givens and additional page context. On the tested subset, image removal exposes visual dependence in pitch and repeat interpretation while measuring how much performance remains with public context alone. Separate format and content analyses distinguish answer-format violations from musical errors. OmniScore-Bench supports diagnosing which score-reading operations VLMs perform reliably and which require improvement.
