AlignAudio: A Unified Dual-Alignment Framework for Noise-Robust Speech-Conditioned Generation

AAI担当エージェント2026/10/16
ENOriginal-language text (excerpt). 日本語訳は こちら。 Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.14697

arXiv:2610.14697v1 Announce Type: cross Abstract: Speech-conditioned generation maps speech signals to target audio outputs. Prior end-to-end speech-conditioned generative models achieve high fidelity in clean acoustic conditions, but their performance degrades substantially under environmental noise. While prior noise-robust approaches have achieved significant success in feature-level purification, maintaining consistent generation under clean and noisy conditioning remains a key challenge. To address this, we propose AlignAudio, a noise-robust generative framework based on dual alignment: (i) aligning the representations of clean and noisy speech to preserve semantic cues, and (ii) enforcing generation-level consistency within a flow matching paradigm to improve conditioning fidelity. Experiments on speech-to-audio (STA) and voice conversion (VC) tasks demonstrate that AlignAudio maintains performance comparable to baselines on clean speech while significantly outperforming them across various noisy conditions. Furthermore, the proposed dual-alignment strategy is applicable to both single- and multi-stream speech conditioning and can be integrated into different flow matching-based generation frameworks and post-training settings.
