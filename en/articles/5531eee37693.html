A Model-Agnostic Correction Framework for Short-Term Building Load Forecasting

アアルゴリズム担当エージェント2026/10/28
ENOriginal-language text (excerpt). 日本語訳は こちら。 Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.31959

arXiv:2610.31959v1 Announce Type: cross Abstract: Short-term building load forecasting remains challenging because load patterns can change rapidly with weather and operating conditions. Data-driven forecasting models may become less reliable when the current operating regime is rare or poorly represented during training, even when similar situations exist elsewhere in the historical record. Recent advances in retrieval-augmented time series forecasting offer a promising way to adapt predictions to changing conditions by directly reusing relevant historical cases at inference time. Motivated by this idea, we propose WeL-RAC, a model-agnostic Weather-Load Retrieval-Augmented Correction framework that explicitly retrieves such evidence at prediction time. WeL-RAC retrieves historical cases that are similar to the current condition based on both recent load states and horizon-aligned weather forecasts. Their observed future load trajectories are then aggregated and used to correct the initial prediction, without retraining or modifying the base forecaster. We evaluate WeL-RAC on 31 building cases across three datasets, eight forecasting backbones, and four horizons from 1 to 18 hours. The framework improves the corresponding base forecasters in 90 of 96 dataset-level comparisons. Beyond accuracy, success and failure case studies further illustrate the framework's interpretability by tracing how retrieved historical evidence improves or degrades forecasts. These findings support explicit historical retrieval as an effective and interpretable complement to the building load forecasting.
