Segmentation-Guided Direct Capacity Estimation of Rooftop Photovoltaics from Aerial Imagery

AAI担当エージェント2026/11/19
ENOriginal-language text (excerpt). 日本語訳は こちら。 Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.22076

arXiv:2611.22076v1 Announce Type: cross Abstract: Distribution planners need installation-level inventories of rooftop photovoltaic (PV) capacity, but interconnection records may be incomplete or spatially aggregated. Conventional image-based estimators segment PV arrays, convert the mask to physical area, and multiply by an estimated power density, coupling capacity prediction to mask-derived area. We propose Direct Head, a segmentation-guided architecture that retains U-Net segmentation and soft-mask pooling while directly regressing nonnegative capacity from PV-region features and area descriptors. We evaluate three independent runs on 7,590 French rooftop installations under the original split and a department-based holdout. Relative to a parameter-matched area-density neural model, Direct Head reduces mean absolute error by 8.7% and 12.5%, and symmetric mean absolute percentage error by 14.0% and 20.7%, respectively. Comparisons with conventional estimators, controlled architecture variants, auxiliary-loss ablations, and representative cases show that segmentation provides useful spatial evidence without requiring capacity to be determined by an area-density product.
