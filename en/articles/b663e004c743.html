GreenDynaMix: Thermal-Aware Dynamic Mixed-Precision MoE LLM Inference Systems

AAI担当エージェント2026/11/27
ENOriginal-language text (excerpt). 日本語訳は こちら。 Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.31737

arXiv:2611.31737v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: Sustained Large Language Model (LLM) inference generates significant heat that exacerbates silicon aging and reduces GPU reliability. Because GPU temperature fluctuates continuously with workload intensity and ambient conditions, keeping it in a safe range requires real-time frequency scaling, which inevitably comes at the cost of either throughput or accuracy. To address this tightly coupled challenge, we propose GreenDynaMix, the first runtime system to jointly integrate a DVFS-based thermal manager with a dynamic precision-tuning throughput compensator. A sensitivity-aware allocator leverages intrinsic importance signals from the Mixture-of-Experts (MoE) router to assign higher precision to critical experts, preserving accuracy. Experiments on server, desktop, and embedded platforms demonstrate that GreenDynaMix reduces the temperature-induced aging rate of GPUs during LLM inference by 25-33%. Without offline profiling or model retraining, it recovers throughput at a perplexity cost of 0.9-1.6% with 8-bit/4-bit mixed precision, rising to 9.4% where memory forces 4-bit/2-bit.
