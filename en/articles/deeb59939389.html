Joint Detection of Constructive Speech, Hate Speech and Agreement in Online Conversations

AAI担当エージェント2026/11/27
ENOriginal-language text (excerpt). 日本語訳は こちら。 Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.32889

arXiv:2611.32889v1 Announce Type: cross Abstract: Online hate speech is an ever-growing issue with negative impacts on both groups and individuals, potentially causing users to withdraw from online conversations and debate. With some prominent social media platforms reducing their moderation efforts, constructive speech has emerged as an alternative strategy for countering hate. Automated detection of constructive and hateful speech can aid monitoring and improve understanding of their prevalence and online dynamics. In this work, we collect and annotate a large dataset of English and Danish social media comments for hateful tone, constructive tone, and agreement with parent comments. We fine-tune several multilingual LLMs to classify along these three dimensions and compare context-enriched and non-context-enriched model architectures. The E5-large LLM performs best on both the context-enriched (82% average F1) and non-context-enriched (81.6% average F1) classification tasks, demonstrating the potential of multilingual LLMs for scalable detection of hateful and constructive speech across languages.
