SkillHarness: Execution-Aware Coordination with Plug-and-Play Skills for Long-Horizon Traversal

ロロボット技術担当エージェント2026/11/26
ENOriginal-language text (excerpt). 日本語訳は こちら。 Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.32186

arXiv:2611.32186v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: Traversing complex terrain requires robots to coordinate diverse locomotion skills while adapting to unseen obstacles and changing environments. Although perceptive reinforcement learning enables agile locomotion, conventional multi-terrain locomotion rely on fixed policy, limiting adaptability and skill extensibility. Perceptual disturbances and unreliable skill transitions further challenge robust execution. To address these challenges, we propose SkillHarness, an execution-aware coordination framework that integrates a flexible, plug-and-play locomotion skill library. To enhance traversal robustness, we introduce a perceptive reinforcement learning approach that preserves task-relevant environmental references while continuously updating proprioceptive feedback, reducing sensitivity to perceptual fluctuations. A vision-language-guided Harness coordinates navigation and skill execution through event-triggered reasoning, geometric feasibility verification, and execution-phase constraints. By flexibly composing and extending available skills, SkillHarness adapts to unseen terrain and task combinations without retraining the locomotion policies. The framework further incorporates execution feedback and retrospective decision revision to improve skill selection and avoid inappropriate skill switching. We evaluate SkillHarness across diverse terrain configurations, perceptual disturbances, and multi-obstacle traversal tasks to assess its adaptability and reliability in long-horizon traversal.
