Boiling Down Uncertainty: Deep Generative Scenario Search with Decision-Guided Evolution Strategies for Two-Stage Stochastic Optimization

AAI担当エージェント2026/11/27
ENOriginal-language text (excerpt). 日本語訳は こちら。 Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.32573

arXiv:2611.32573v1 Announce Type: cross Abstract: One of the most common models for handling uncertainty is two-stage stochastic programming. Choosing the right number of scenarios is a trade-off between the uncertainty that can be represented and computational cost. A recent method, Deep Generative Scenario Search, generates synthetic scenarios and improves them by learning from the decisions they induce. The original approach used a black-box gradient estimate, since decisions are piecewise constant across scenarios. This paper provides the missing analytical foundation by deriving the gradient in closed form from recourse duality and an adjoint solve of the regularized reduced problem. The decision-focused direction, with a heuristic direction as a backup when duals are unavailable, defines the sampling subspace of a guided evolution strategy rather than being used as a direct update. For any guiding subspace, the expected update is a descent direction of the smoothed objective, so an inaccurate guide adds variance while the expected step still descends. For six problem families, both guided estimators outperform the baseline. The decision-focused version reduces the mean gap by three-quarters, and on the two families with the most integer variables the reduced sets beat the exact solver's two-hour incumbent in most runs, using a fifth of the time.
