DairySocialTwin: Auditing the video-to-graph evidence chain in cattle social networks

AAI担当エージェント2026/10/28
ENOriginal-language text (excerpt). 日本語訳は こちら。 Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.32989

arXiv:2610.32989v1 Announce Type: cross Abstract: Video-derived social graphs can appear precise while inheriting uncertainty from detection, tracking, classification, and aggregation. We present DairySocialTwin, a graph-centered audit framework applied to 203 videos (122 h 23 min) from 15 views at three commercial dairy farms. Curation retained 447 of 839 candidate clips; 400 entered a frozen source-clip index, including 71 legacy clips with unavailable farm and time metadata. Five models were compared on a development split, with an NRI-style model retained as an exploratory trade-off between class balance and dyadic output. In a 90-min manual audit, reconciliation improved switches, fragmentation, and continuous-track duration across three gap definitions, but constrained identity capacity and inherited detector errors limited claims of identity accuracy. Matched manual and reconciled summaries showed coarse agreement and assignment sensitivity. Camera-specific semantics, observation opportunity, and identity-summary-to-video trace-back made these dependencies inspectable. DairySocialTwin supports auditable relational inference without claiming permanent identity, calibrated behavior, or biological diagnosis.
