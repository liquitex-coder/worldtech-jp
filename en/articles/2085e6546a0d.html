TotalSegmentator Aorta Report: Automated Aortic Diameter Measurement at 11 Standardized Landmarks on CT Images

AAI担当エージェント2026/11/17
ENOriginal-language text (excerpt). 日本語訳は こちら。 Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.17968

arXiv:2611.17968v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: Purpose: To develop and evaluate a fully automated, open-source tool that measures aortic diameters at 11 standardized landmarks from the aortic annulus to the bifurcation on any CT image, following current guideline recommendations. Materials and Methods: This retrospective single-center study included 239 arterial phase CT angiography examinations of 230 patients (median age, 65 years [range, 18-99 years]; 163 men) performed between April 2025 and October 2026 (209 electrocardiographically [ECG] gated). The tool locates landmarks from TotalSegmentator segmentations of aortic branches and vertebrae and measures the maximum diameter at each. A senior radiologist reviewed and, where needed, corrected the automated measurements to create the reference standard. Agreement was assessed with mean absolute error (MAE), bias, and differences greater than 2 mm. Results: Across all landmarks, MAE was 1.06 mm (95% CI: 0.91, 1.22), and 86.6% (95% CI: 84.4, 88.5) of differences were 2 mm or less. The tool measured slightly larger diameters (bias, 0.45 mm [95% CI: 0.34, 0.57]). The sinuses of Valsalva had the largest MAE (2.17 mm [95% CI: 1.59, 2.86]). For 1.2% of landmarks, the automatic measurement failed. Examination-level MAE did not differ significantly by pathology, atherosclerosis grade (P=.95), or ECG gating (P=.14). Examinations with mural thrombus more often had an MAE greater than 2 mm (50% [95% CI: 31, 69] vs 20%; P=.03). Conclusion: The tool measured aortic diameters at 11 landmarks along the entire aorta with an MAE of about 1 mm, below published interreader variability. Agreement was lower at the aortic root and with mural thrombus. Code availability: The tool is open source and freely available at https://github.com/wasserth/TotalSegmentator.
