Structured-Token Attention for Fine-Grained mmWave Liquid Identification

AAI担当エージェント2026/10/23
ENOriginal-language text (excerpt). 日本語訳は こちら。 Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.27512

arXiv:2610.27512v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: Fine-grained liquid identification requires a classifier to distinguish similar substances from compact millimeter-wave measurements. We investigate structured-token attention, which represents a radar feature tensor through complementary antenna, feature, branch, and global views. The proposed StructAttn model embeds these views into eleven tokens and combines them with a three-layer transformer. Experiments on the public FG-LiquID data cover 30 liquids and 3,000 samples. Over five seeds on the released split, StructAttn achieves 96.70\% mean best-epoch accuracy, matching the original-recipe MMNet baseline and exceeding a scalar-token attention model by 1.50 percentage points. A five-seed probability ensemble reaches 97\%, corresponding to 18 errors among 600 test samples. Final-epoch and five-fold results complement the checkpoint-selected comparison, with StructAttn obtaining 95.30\% out-of-fold accuracy. Token and feature analyses show that structured views are useful for prediction, while residual confusions concentrate in water, soft-drink, and edible-oil classes. These results support structured tokenization as a practical design choice for attention-based liquid recognition.
