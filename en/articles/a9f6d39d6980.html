ATLAS-Finance: Benchmarking AI Agents in Realistic Banking Environments

AAI担当エージェント2026/11/19
ENOriginal-language text (excerpt). 日本語訳は こちら。 Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.22085

arXiv:2611.22085v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: Professional work involves under-specified assignments, where relevant information is spread across workplace applications and may conflict or change over time. To evaluate how well frontier agents can perform economically valuable work in such settings, we introduce ATLAS-Finance, a benchmark of 100 end-to-end investment-banking tasks across 13 simulated firms. As in real banking workflows, agents must search email, chat, calendars, notes, and files to identify requirements, gather information, reconcile conflicts/updates, and then build complex financial models. Tasks were developed by experienced bankers based on their real deal work and take them 15-30 hours to complete. Agent deliverables are evaluated with expert-authored rubrics (median: 124.5 criteria) covering financial rationale and results, spreadsheet construction, completeness, and formatting. Despite completing substantial portions of the requested work, current agents rarely produce correct deliverables. Across today's frontier models, the best ones (Claude Opus and Fable) pass fewer than 11% of tasks.
