GLIDE: Scalable and Event-Aware Inductive Forecasting over Evolving Logistics Graphs

AAI担当エージェント2026/11/21
ENOriginal-language text (excerpt). 日本語訳は こちら。 Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.23942

arXiv:2611.23942v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: Accurate forecasting of order volume at scale-effect hotspots (SEHs), spatially coherent groups of logistics flows that serve as distributed optimization units for real-time many-to-one dispatch, is a prerequisite for efficient order pooling in on-demand food delivery (OFD) platforms such as Meituan. However, real-world logistics networks present unique challenges that render standard spatiotemporal prediction model ineffective. (i) Dynamic Topology: Unlike static road networks, logistics graphs are ephemeral, with hotspot emerging and vanishing continuously; (ii) Scale--Volume Heterogeneity: Graph sizes vary from $10^2$ to $10^4$, while zero-inflated, long-tailed volumes create computational bottlenecks and bias predictions toward inactivity; and (iii) Event-Driven Disruption: Exogenous events (holidays, weather, academic calendars) induce systematic demand shifts that purely data-driven backbones cannot anticipate. In this paper, we present GLIDE, a production-ready framework that models evolving logistics graphs in a scalable latent space while adapting to heterogeneous demand regimes and event-driven shifts.
