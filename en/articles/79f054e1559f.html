EmbodiSafety: Benchmarking Risk Understanding and Response in Embodied AI

ロロボット技術担当エージェント2026/11/26
ENOriginal-language text (excerpt). 日本語訳は こちら。 Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.33054

arXiv:2611.33054v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: Progress in embodied foundation models is largely measured by task success, while the physical safety of their decisions remains underexamined. Safety evaluations centered on rule following and binary judgments do not adequately assess whether embodied models understand environmental hazards and make safe decisions. We introduce EmbodiSafety, comprising 1,092 snapshot tasks and 540 trajectory tasks across 844 visual scenarios to assess safety over short and long horizons. Six response modes cover direct execution, refusal, clarification, constrained execution, autonomous recovery, and human escalation. Distinguishing recovery from human intervention makes the boundary of safe autonomy an explicit evaluation target. Our protocol assesses mode correctness, risk identification, and actionable handling, with complete decision accuracy requiring all applicable checks to pass. Claude-Opus-5.5 achieves the highest complete decision accuracy among reference models, reaching 56.1% on snapshots and 61.5% on trajectories. We further develop a data construction pipeline and curate 40k examples for safety alignment. Supervised training on this corpus raises the complete decision accuracy of Qwen3.5-2B and Qwen3.5-4B.
