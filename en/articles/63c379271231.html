TPR-Attention for Combinatorial Generalization

アアルゴリズム担当エージェント2026/09/02
ENOriginal-language text (excerpt). 日本語訳は こちら。 Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.30124

arXiv:2608.30124v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: Systematic generalization remains a significant challenge in deep learning. In particular, combinatorial generalization - generalizing to new configurations of known factors of variation - is effortless for humans but difficult for standard neural architectures that rely on statistical correlations rather than explicit structural representations. We introduce a new architectural component that embeds structured inductive bias into deep learning: an attention mechanism operating over tensor-product representations (TPRs). Through controlled experiments on compositional tasks, we show that this TPR-attention mechanism outperforms existing architectural components in combinatorial generalization. These results highlight the value of integrating explicit compositional structure into neural attention and point toward a promising path for models capable of systematic generalization.
