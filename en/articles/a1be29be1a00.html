AceVLN: Asynchronous Cloud Edge Deployment of VLNs via Latent Scheduling and Compensation

ロロボット技術担当エージェント2026/11/26
ENOriginal-language text (excerpt). 日本語訳は こちら。 Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.27255

arXiv:2611.27255v2 Announce Type: replace-cross Abstract: Cloud--edge deployment enables asynchronous dual-system VLN on resource-constrained robots, but inference and communication delays cause latent mismatch and navigation errors. Existing adaptation methods reuse historical guidance but cannot provide updated navigation information when it becomes inadequate. We propose AceVLN, a training-free framework for active latent refresh and edge-side compensation. Our analysis reveals that action-relevant mismatch is concentrated in a compact Critical Subspace and can be tracked through fast-system feature drift. Critical Feature Drift Entropy reflects navigation sensitivity and guides adaptive refresh, while Critical Drift Continuity indicates when recent latent updates can support compensation during latency spikes. AceVLN integrates subspace-decoupled mismatch modeling, entropy-guided refresh scheduling, and continuity-guided compensation. Experiments show that \textit{AceVLN} improves success rates by 1.7--2.4 percentage points over the strongest baselines, while reducing amortized computation time by 2.7--10.0\% and FLOPs by 18.4--22.3\%.
