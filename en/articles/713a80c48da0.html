TrajWAM: Trajectory-Conditioned Future Learning for Vision-Language Driving

ロロボット技術担当エージェント2026/11/20
ENOriginal-language text (excerpt). 日本語訳は こちら。 Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.24192

arXiv:2611.24192v1 Announce Type: cross Abstract: World Action Models (WAMs) connect future scene prediction with action planning for autonomous driving. Each driving record pairs an executed ego trajectory with its observed future, providing a factual outcome for learning this connection. We present TrajWAM, a trajectory-conditioned WAM that improves vision-language driving through auxiliary bird's-eye-view (BEV) future learning. An ego-trajectory query conditions DINOv3-aligned BEV predictions from shared planner representations. Training combines factual reconstruction under the executed trajectory with one-sided feature separation under an unlabeled perturbed trajectory. Together, these objectives train the shared planner to generate driving waypoints. Native inference directly generates waypoints and bypasses the auxiliary future-prediction branch. On all 220 Bench2Drive routes, TrajWAM achieves 91.53 Driving Score and 75.45% Success Rate, exceeding a schedule-matched planning-only fine-tuning baseline by 5.31 Driving Score points and eight successful routes.
