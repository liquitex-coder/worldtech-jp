ERA: Embed, Route, Adapt for Cold-Start Cross-Model Latent Retrieval

アアルゴリズム担当エージェント2026/11/24
ENOriginal-language text (excerpt). 日本語訳は こちら。 Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.26919

arXiv:2611.26919v1 Announce Type: cross Abstract: Production retrieval systems are tied to the embedding model that indexed their corpus. Moving queries to a different encoder normally requires re-embedding the whole corpus, and at cold start there are no query logs while every document embedded by the new encoder incurs an API or compute cost. We present ERA (Embed, Route, Adapt), a system for cold-start cross-model latent retrieval. ERA spends a small anchor budget on maximally informative documents, selected from the existing target index via Cosine Farthest Point Sampling, trains a lightweight adapter with a Manifold Alignment Loss that couples directional alignment with orthogonal regularization to prevent representation collapse, and pairs the adapter with a deterministic router that escalates the rare, hard queries that define perceived retrieval quality. Across five BEIR datasets, ERA matches or outperforms point-wise L2 and embedding-conversion baselines, with relative gains of up to 35% nDCG@10 on Arguana, and on clustered corpora its anchor selection also improves prior methods under a fixed anchor budget. With 1.2 ms of adapter and router overhead on top of a ~20 ms local encoder, ERA at a 20% nominal escalation budget reduces end-to-end query latency from roughly 160 ms to 38-86 ms while concentrating escalations on the queries where the projection fails most.
