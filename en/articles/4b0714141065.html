Neural Defend Ranks #1 in Global Audio Deepfake Detection Benchmark - ARCAS on the Podonos Independent Leaderboard

AAI担当エージェント2026/11/24
ENOriginal-language text (excerpt). 日本語訳は こちら。 Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.27215

arXiv:2611.27215v1 Announce Type: cross Abstract: Commercial detectors of AI-generated audio are usually promoted with results on internal test sets that buyers cannot inspect. Independent benchmarks with private labels give a more reliable comparison. We report the results of Neural Defend's ARCAS foundation models on the Podonos audio deepfake detection benchmark, an independent leaderboard that scores commercial detection APIs and open-source detectors on one held-out set of real speech and speech from current text-to-speech and voice-cloning systems, delivered in six file formats, against labels that only Podonos holds. In the release of 29 October 2026, which covers 28 systems from 16 commercial vendors and the leading open models, ARCAS 290M and the smaller ARCAS 90M share first place, ahead of every other commercial system, including Resemble AI, Hiya, Pindrop, Hive, and Reality Defender. Both models make fewer errors than any other system by a statistically clear margin, keep false positives and false negatives in balance at their default threshold, process every submitted file without rejection, and sit on the accuracy--latency frontier of the leaderboard. As for every commercial system added since Podonos retired its API runner, the ARCAS predictions were submitted by Neural Defend and scored by Podonos against its private labels. We describe the benchmark's construction and protocol in full, reproduce the complete leaderboard and its history across five releases, and analyse error balance, statistical separation, speed, and the gap between commercial and open-source detectors. The results agree with the companion evaluation of ARCAS 290M on the complete test sets of ten public benchmarks and establish ARCAS as the most accurate AI-generated audio detector evaluated on this independent benchmark.
