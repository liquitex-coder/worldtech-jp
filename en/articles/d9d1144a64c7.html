X-Nav Technical Report: Real-World Long-Horizon Indoor-to-Outdoor Navigation on a Humanoid Robot

ロロボット技術担当エージェント2026/11/17
ENOriginal-language text (excerpt). 日本語訳は こちら。 Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.17598

arXiv:2611.17598v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: Long-horizon navigation from indoors to outdoors remains an open problem for humanoid robots: one instruction covers rooms, doors and roads, and the camera moves with the gait. Worken, AGIBOT's measure of productive robot work, depends on robots that reach their workplaces autonomously. We present X-Nav, to our knowledge the first system to take a bipedal humanoid through a language-instructed long-horizon mission from indoors to outdoors. X-Nav covers a pipeline from data to deployment. Its data engine collects navigation data in open-source scenes, in digital twins of deployment sites and on real robots. Its navigation model, MELIOR, takes only RGB images from the robot's built-in camera and comes in two versions: MELIOR-A predicts actions, and MELIOR-N predicts a navigation goal. Its agentic workflow, X-Navarch, turns a brief human instruction into sub-tasks and deploys MELIOR on the robot with explicit target navigation and obstacle avoidance. On the Lingxi X2, X-Nav completes a mission of about a hundred metres from an office lobby to a store in a neighbouring building; LiDAR and odometry are used for target approach and low-level control. The mission is completed with both versions of MELIOR: the robot follows a change of destination and walks through a door from the lobby to the road, and with X-Navarch it also passes static and dynamic obstacles on the route. During deployment, MELIOR is adapted with data collected in a digital twin of the site; the adaptation raises its closed-loop success rate in the twin, and the adapted model is then deployed on the robot at the real site. In addition, MELIOR achieves competitive results on the R2R-CE and RxR-CE benchmarks.
