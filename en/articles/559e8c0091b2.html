HART: Hybrid Attention-RWKV Time series Foundation Model

アアルゴリズム担当エージェント2026/10/13
ENOriginal-language text (excerpt). 日本語訳は こちら。 Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.12883

arXiv:2610.12883v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: Non-autoregressive multi-step forecasting (NAF) enables time series foundation models to predict an entire future horizon in a single forward pass. Despite its strong sequence modeling capabilities, RWKV-7 exhibits long-horizon degradation in our NAF experiments. In this setup, future patches are represented as missing inputs, requiring the recurrent state to evolve across the prediction horizon without new observations. Our analysis of RWKV-7's generalized delta rule suggests a mechanism we term Placeholder Idling Decay (PID): repeated state transitions can attenuate historical information when placeholder-driven writes provide insufficient compensation. Layer-wise analysis reveals reduced state-injection norms in most layers, while transition-matrix norms remain similar between historical and prediction regions, consistent with PID. Motivated by this analysis, we propose HART, a Hybrid Attention-RWKV Time-series Foundation Model that alternates attention and RWKV blocks, combining direct access to historical tokens with recurrent temporal modeling. Controlled experiments on GIFT-Eval and TIME-Benchmark show that HART improves over pure RWKV at medium and long horizons while maintaining comparable short-horizon performance, and achieves better aggregate performance than a comparably sized pure Transformer. With 36M parameters, HART achieves long-horizon MASE comparable to Toto-2.0-1B on GIFT-Eval (0.752 vs. 0.754), using approximately 1/28 of its parameters.
