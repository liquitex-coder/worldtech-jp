PURA: Video Token Pruning via Unified Spatiotemporal Redundancy Assessment

AAI担当エージェント2026/11/28
ENOriginal-language text (excerpt). 日本語訳は こちら。 Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.34521

arXiv:2611.34521v1 Announce Type: cross Abstract: Video large language models incur substantial inference costs from long visual token sequences. Existing video token compression methods mitigate these costs by exploiting spatial redundancy within frames and temporal redundancy across frames. However, many approaches impose predefined processing orders or fixed allocation ratios for spatial and temporal compression, limiting their ability to adapt to the varying balance of spatiotemporal redundancy across videos. To address this limitation, we propose PURA, a training-free method that prunes visual tokens through unified spatiotemporal redundancy assessment. For each token, PURA constructs spatial and temporal reference candidates and selects the reference with the lowest representation error across the two dimensions. The representation error is combined with token importance to determine each token's removal cost. To avoid the time overhead of removing the lowest-cost token one by one, we introduce Coordinated Pruning, which iteratively groups mutually compatible low-cost removals into batches for simultaneous execution and updates the pruning state after each batch until the target budget is reached. Extensive experiments on three video large language models and five video understanding benchmarks demonstrate that PURA achieves a favorable trade-off between task performance and inference efficiency.
