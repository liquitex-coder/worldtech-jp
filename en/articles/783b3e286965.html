AIRO: A Streaming-Based Machine Learning Framework for Threat Detection and Security in Industrial Internet of Things

アアルゴリズム担当エージェント2026/10/24
ENOriginal-language text (excerpt). 日本語訳は こちら。 Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.30713

arXiv:2610.30713v1 Announce Type: cross Abstract: Modern Industrial Internet of Things (IIoT) im- plementations now connect edge gateways, cloud dashboards and business applications. This improves visibility and remote monitoring. However it simultaneously exposes industrial traffic to various cyber threats including scanning, web attacks and Structured Query Language (SQL) injection (SQLi), phishing- driven credential theft, and command-and-control (C2) activity. Although rule-based tools remain useful for detecting known signatures, their efficacy decreases with minimally altered attacks or when alerts require manual investigation. This paper presents AIRO (Autonomous Intelligence-Driven Cybersecurity Risk Re- duction System), an efficient framework for IIoT monitoring and response. AIRO combines Apache Kafka streaming, attack- specific feature extraction, machine learning (ML) inference, confidence-based decision logic and risk reduction measures such as Internet Protocol (IP) blocking, account suspension and traffic limiting. We developed four specialized datasets for C2 beaconing, reconnaissance, SQLi, and phishing to evaluate AIRO. These datasets include noisy and overlapping characteristic distribu- tions to minimize label leakage. The best-performing models, primarily Gradient Boosting and LightGBM, achieve F1-scores up to 0.9229 while ensuring low-latency from event input to mitigation
