RVLM-X: Towards a Universal VLM Harness

AAI担当エージェント2026/11/27
ENOriginal-language text (excerpt). 日本語訳は こちら。 Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.31755

arXiv:2611.31755v1 Announce Type: cross Abstract: Harnesses for vision-language models (VLMs) have grown with the models' capabilities, notably for images far larger than those seen in training, where downscaling loses small details. Most of them wrap the VLM in a fixed procedure, such as a zoom tree, that decides in advance where it looks and how the evidence is combined. These procedures make up for the weaknesses of older models by keeping them on a task-specific path, but with frontier models that reliably plan and use tools, they can lower accuracy. We propose RVLM-X, a task-generalizable recursive VLM harness that runs unchanged across photographs, charts and maps. Following the recursive inference of Recursive Language Models, the VLM programmatically controls its own visual context in a code REPL that holds the full-resolution image and calls itself recursively on regions that it chooses. With a cost-efficient frontier model as the base of every method, RVLM-X is more accurate than a single call on all 7 public benchmarks and has the highest pooled accuracy. Because existing benchmarks mostly test whether a model finds one small detail, we also introduce MapGenBench, procedurally generated maps whose questions combine fine-detail perception with multi-step reasoning. There, RVLM-X answers 71.4% of held-out questions against 55.5% for the strongest baseline. Overall, our findings suggest that as VLMs plan and use tools more reliably, letting the model write its own visual search works better than fixing one in advance.
