MADJEPA: Enhanced Multi-Stage Joint-Embedding Predictive Architecture for Medical Anomaly Detection

AAI担当エージェント2026/11/27
ENOriginal-language text (excerpt). 日本語訳は こちら。 Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.25194

arXiv:2611.25194v2 Announce Type: replace-cross Abstract: Anomaly detection in medical images is a complex task, requiring the development of a robust model using only healthy patient data. Without access to labeled data, modern approaches typically employ self-supervised learning to obtain a model of normality. Since world models promise to build strong internal representations of their environment, we consider them as suitable candidates for the medical anomaly detection environment, namely medical investigations of healthy patients. To this end, we propose MADJEPA, a medical anomaly detector based on an enhanced multi-stage joint-embedding predictive architecture (JEPA). We design a multi-stage training pipeline to produce a robust detector: (1) image-based JEPA (I-JEPA) training aims to learn a strong representation of normal training data; (2) target encoder distillation aims to modulate over-generalization of I-JEPA representations to abnormal data; (3) pseudo-anomaly classification aims to calibrate anomaly predictions by learning a tight boundary around the normal data manifold. To demonstrate our design, we carry out comprehensive experiments on six datasets covering various medical image domains (e.g. MRI, OCT, X-ray, histopathology), comparing our MADJEPA with numerous state-of-the-art methods. The results show that our carefully designed world model, MADJEPA, attains competitive results, ranking first on three datasets. Our ablation studies further confirm the utility of each training stage, indicating that I-JEPA alone is not sufficient to obtain competitive results in medical anomaly detection. We publicly release our code at https://github.com/Sabina085/madjepa.
