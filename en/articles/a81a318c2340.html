RePace: Reasoning about Demonstration Speedup for Faster Vision-Language-Action Policies

AAI担当エージェント2026/11/28
ENOriginal-language text (excerpt). 日本語訳は こちら。 Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.34864

arXiv:2611.34864v1 Announce Type: cross Abstract: Since vision-language-action (VLA) policies can inherit the slow pace of human demonstrations, accelerating these demonstrations before fine-tuning can lead to faster VLA policies without an additional speed-selection module at deployment. Several offline speedup methods have focused on identifying segments that require less precision and assigning predefined speedup factors to the segments. However, even within a single segment, a speedup factor suitable for steady motion may be too high when precise alignment is required. To this end, this paper presents RePace, an offline reasoning framework that determines how much to accelerate each part of a demonstration. For the same recorded movement, RePace measures how much each candidate speedup factor shortens the sequence and compares the resulting observation-action pairs used for training. Given the task instruction, a pretrained language model uses the visual context and original motion of successive windows to interpret these candidate retiming effects and select a speedup profile for the complete episode. Experimental results demonstrate that RePace outperforms the baselines in overall task execution efficiency on both LIBERO and RoboCasa-Kitchen. On all three tasks with a gripper-equipped Franka Research 3 arm, RePace matches or exceeds the acceleration baselines in success rate and completes successful trials faster.
