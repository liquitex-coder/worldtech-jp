ReDence: A Hybrid Agentic and System One Model Framework for Rapid, Evidence-Grounded Detection of Social Engineering Scams

アアルゴリズム担当エージェント2026/11/24
ENOriginal-language text (excerpt). 日本語訳は こちら。 Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.30963

arXiv:2610.30963v2 Announce Type: replace-cross Abstract: Detecting online social engineering (SE) scams often requires examining website workflows, verifying claims against external sources, and connecting evidence across multiple pages. Existing detectors provide limited support for these tasks and rarely produce actionable evidence to support their decisions, leaving analysts to manually investigate flagged websites, which inevitably delays detection and subsequent takedown of these attacks. To address this gap, we present ReDence, a hybrid framework for automated, evidence-grounded scam investigation, detection, and reporting across diverse SE scam categories. ReDence combines two locally deployed, fine-tuned models: a multimodal agentic Investigator (Qwen3.5-4B) for investigative planning, evidence interpretation, and report generation, and a specialized System One model (Tev1) that handles binary and multiple-choice decisions, including browser action selection and evidence assessment, significantly reducing response generation overhead while preserving the Investigator's reasoning capabilities for complex tasks. The reports produced by ReDence also document investigative actions, collected evidence, and the reasoning behind each decision. Our evaluation shows that ReDence has a detection accuracy of over 96%, outperforming existing detectors while being at least four times faster. When deployed on a real-world URL stream over the period of 28 days, ReDence identified 4,042 new SE scam websites across 17 categories, of which at least 71% were missed by all four popular anti-scam blocklists evaluated. Feedback from twelve domain providers indicated that ReDence's investigative reports helped analysts recognize the scams documented in reported websites, understand the supporting evidence, and validate cases more efficiently. We open-source ReDence to aid automated, evidence-grounded scam investigation and reporting.
