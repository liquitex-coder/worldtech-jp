Safe-FCL: Continual Risk Awareness and Fine-Grained Safety Aggregation for Federated Continual Learning of LLMs

アアルゴリズム担当エージェント2026/10/16
ENOriginal-language text (excerpt). 日本語訳は こちら。 Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.19968

arXiv:2610.19968v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: Federated fine-tuning enables large language models (LLMs) to learn collaboratively from distributed client data, but malicious client updates can compromise global model safety. As federated tasks evolve, changing malicious update patterns challenge the ability of existing defenses to maintain reliable risk detection over time. To address this challenge, we propose Safe-FCL, a framework integrating continual risk awareness with fine-grained safety aggregation for federated continual learning of LLMs. Specifically, the Continual Risk Awareness (CRA) mechanism learns emerging risk patterns across sequential tasks and constrains detector updates using a protective subspace derived from historical features, mitigating forgetting of previously learned malicious patterns while adapting to new risks. To translate risk awareness into aggregation-level safety control, the Fine-Grained Safety Aggregation (FGSA) mechanism combines client-level risk assessments from CRA with module-level deviations from historical update behavior to adaptively regulate aggregation contributions, suppressing potentially malicious updates while minimizing unnecessary restrictions on benign updates. Extensive experiments demonstrate that Safe-FCL outperforms current SOTA methods, improving global-model Rule and MD-Judge scores in federated continual learning by 0.41% and 0.64%, respectively, while increasing average risk recognition accuracy by 2.01%.
