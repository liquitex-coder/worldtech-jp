Adaptive Electromagnetic Transient-level Digital Twins for Data Centers via In-Context Learning with LLMs

AAI担当エージェント2026/11/27
ENOriginal-language text (excerpt). 日本語訳は こちら。 Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.32882

arXiv:2611.32882v1 Announce Type: cross Abstract: The rapid growth of artificial intelligence (AI) workloads has significantly increased data center (DC) power demand. Frequent operational and configurational changes, such as load surges caused by dynamic workload allocation, can induce electromagnetic transients (EMTs) whose accurate prediction is critical for reliable DC operation and maintenance. Although high-fidelity EMT solvers are widely adopted for transient analysis, their computational cost limits large-scale and multi-scenario studies. Digital twins address this computational bottleneck by emulating the behavior of a physical system and enabling fast prediction in place of expensive high-fidelity simulation. However,data-driven digital twins are non-adaptive and fail to generalize once the DC configuration changes. To address this limitation, this paper proposes an adaptive EMT-level digital twin for DCs based on in-context learning (ICL) with large language models (LLMs). We formulate the EMT prediction task as a system identification problem under parametric uncertainty and develop a GPT-2-based framework that adapts to unseen DC configurations at inference time from a small set of EMT trajectory samples, without model retraining. The proposed framework is validated on three representative EMT scenarios and benchmarked against three baseline non-adaptive twins. Results demonstrate superior prediction accuracy, improved adaptability to unseen operating conditions, and substantial computational speedups compared to high-fidelity EMT simulation.
