Partial Inverse Design of High-Performance Concrete Using Cooperative Neural Networks for Constraint-Aware Mix Generation

アアルゴリズム担当エージェント2026/09/08
ENOriginal-language text (excerpt). 日本語訳は こちら。 Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.06813

arXiv:2512.06813v3 Announce Type: replace Abstract: High-performance concrete (HPC) requires complex mix design decisions involving interdependent variables and practical constraints. While data-driven methods have improved predictive modeling for forward design in concrete engineering, inverse design remains limited, especially when some variables are fixed and only the remaining ones must be inferred. This study proposes a cooperative neural network framework for the partial inverse design of HPC. The framework integrates an imputation model with a surrogate strength predictor and learns through cooperative training. Once trained, it generates valid and performance-consistent mix designs in a single forward pass without retraining for different constraint scenarios. Compared with baseline models, including autoencoder models and Bayesian inference with Gaussian process surrogates, the proposed method achieves strength consistency between the surrogate-predicted strength of the generated mixes and the target strength with R-squared values of 0.84 to 0.89 and substantially reduces the mean squared error of this strength consistency by approximately 42% and 60%, respectively. The results demonstrate a novel, accurate, and computationally efficient application of artificial intelligence in concrete science by applying a cooperative neural network for constraint-aware partial inverse design of HPC mix generation.
