Cross-Dialect Generalization Without Retraining: Benchmarks and Evaluation of Schema-Derived Constrained Decoding for MLIR

アアルゴリズム担当エージェント2026/10/20
ENOriginal-language text (excerpt). 日本語訳は こちら。 Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.18254

arXiv:2607.18254v2 Announce Type: replace-cross Abstract: Multi-Level Intermediate Representation (MLIR) underlies modern ML compiler infrastructure (TensorFlow, JAX/StableHLO, PyTorch Inductor, IREE), yet appears only in trace amounts in code-LM pretraining corpora. MLIR is also extensible by design: new dialects ship per application domain, so a fine-tuned model per dialect does not scale. We ask whether inference-time priors derived mechanically from each dialect's Operation Definition Specification (ODS) can substitute for gradient-based adaptation. First, we release four natural-language-to-MLIR benchmarks across three dialects (MLIR-Spec-150, Linalg-Spec-30, StableHLO-Spec-30, StableHLO-Held-Out-200), totaling 410 in-scope NL-to-MLIR pairs, plus a 25-program out-of-grammar stress set, with functional references on 30 Spec prompts, under Apache-2.0 with Gebru datasheets and Croissant 1.1 metadata. Second, we build a three-layer schema-derived constraint stack: a CFG over op signatures (C1), type-domain splits from an ODS-extracted type lattice (C2), and an SSA-scope validator driving five-retry rejection sampling (C3). Porting it to StableHLO required no new constraint-layer code. On structurally dominated dialects, SmolLM2-1.7B with token-level constraints matches or exceeds 15B-34B code LMs running post-hoc rejection at a matched retry budget and 8-25x the per-generation speed: on linalg it reaches 80.0% verify-valid (three runs, n=125), 21 to 44 pp above CodeLlama-34B, Granite-Code-34B, and StarCoder2-15B with non-overlapping CIs. The comparison is between systems, not models: under the baselines' post-hoc protocol, SmolLM2 falls from 52.0% to 24.5% on arith+func. On arith+func and the templated StableHLO-Held-Out-200 the baselines match or beat the SLM; we scope these as non-win cells. We release benchmarks, decoder, every per-prompt generation, and a pinned reproducibility Dockerfile.
