Federated Soft Poly-Level Learning Over a Distributed Network with Guaranteed Convergence

AAI担当エージェント2026/11/17
ENOriginal-language text (excerpt). 日本語訳は こちら。 Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.17722

arXiv:2611.17722v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: Multi-level learning/optimization, e.g., bilevel optimization, has been recognized for its versatile applicability across a broad array of domains, spanning from signal processing to machine learning. However, most existing works focus on bilevel and trilevel optimization. In contrast, multi-level optimization problems involving four or more hierarchical levels, which we term poly-level learning (PLL) problems, remain largely unexplored.In addition, in practical PLL, data may be distributed across multiple nodes over a distributed network. Nevertheless, extant studies on PLL predominantly focus on the non-federated setting, necessitating the collection of data to central servers and thereby posing potential privacy risks. To this end, we propose a Federated Poly-Level Learning (FPLL) framework over a distributed network to tackle the PLL problems with soft constraints in a federated manner. FPLL hinges on a novel non-linear cut, i.e., $L$-cut, for the level-wise relaxation to the PLL problems. Additionally, the proposed federated algorithm is designed with adjustable parameters that allow for a flexible trade-off between its computational complexity and performance efficiency, enabling users to tune it according to specific application requirements. We also carry out non-asymptotic analysis to derive the iteration complexity for the proposed FPLL to achieve the $\epsilon$-Pareto stationary point. Extensive experiments have been undertaken to elucidate the superior efficacy of the proposed method.
