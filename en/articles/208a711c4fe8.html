ExtremeDrive: A Real-World Dataset and Benchmark for Critical Driving

ロロボット技術担当エージェント2026/11/19
ENOriginal-language text (excerpt). 日本語訳は こちら。 Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.20574

arXiv:2611.20574v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: Existing autonomous driving datasets and benchmarks provide extensive resources for routine driving, yet real-world data covering safety-critical scenarios and extreme environmental conditions remain limited. To address this gap, we present ExtremeDrive, a real-world dataset and benchmark covering emergency maneuvers, complex traffic interactions, and adverse conditions such as slippery or snow-covered roads, alongside routine driving. The dataset comprises 589 driving scenes curated from a larger pool of real-world driving recordings, with calibrated and aligned multisensor data and reviewed annotations of road structure and traffic participants. We further establish a benchmark to evaluate planned trajectories through simulated vehicle execution, measuring safety-related metrics and driving performance. Experiments at equal training set sizes show that incorporating critical driving data reduces trajectory prediction errors and improves mean critical-success rates in critical scenarios. ExtremeDrive provides a unified resource for learning and evaluating autonomous driving in safety-critical scenarios and extreme environmental conditions.
