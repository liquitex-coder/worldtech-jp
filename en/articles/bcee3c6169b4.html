Efficient World-Model Planning via Physics-Guided Search Distillation

ロロボット技術担当エージェント2026/11/20
ENOriginal-language text (excerpt). 日本語訳は こちら。 Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.24171

arXiv:2611.24171v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: World models support autonomous driving by evaluating candidate trajectories through predicted future scene evolution. However, accurate future prediction does not ensure road-compliant execution; when all candidates violate road boundaries, re-ranking cannot select a feasible trajectory. We propose Physics-Guided Search Distillation (PGSD), a single-view planning framework that combines future representations and execution feedback for verified beyond-pool repair through three components: (1) Execution-Aware Selection checks ego-vehicle road compliance under simulated execution and selects the highest-scoring feasible trajectory; (2) a Clearance-Aware Search Teacher generates beyond-pool corrections and selects demonstrations with larger minimum road-boundary clearance during execution to buffer imitation errors; and (3) a Future-Conditioned Repair Student predicts one correction from rewarder features and execution feedback for single-rollout verification. The pretrained planner and rewarder remain frozen; no future scenes are generated online. On NAVSIM v1, single-view PGSD achieves 90.97 PDMS, exceeding all compared prior single-view planners and retaining 73.2% of the search teacher's PDMS gain over execution-aware selection. On NAVSIM v2 navhard, it reaches 38.96 EPDMS, improving on WorldDrive by 11.24 points (40.5%). The student replaces up to ten search rollouts with a single verification rollout per repair trigger. HUGSIM Hard examples provide qualitative evidence of zero-shot transfer.
