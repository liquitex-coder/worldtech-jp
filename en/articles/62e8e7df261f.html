Grounding Object-to-Object Hazard Reasoning into 3D Risk Fields for Task-Conditioned Safety

ロロボット技術担当エージェント2026/11/17
ENOriginal-language text (excerpt). 日本語訳は こちら。 Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.17566

arXiv:2611.17566v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: Safety in robotics does not solely depend on geometric constraints, but also on semantics: the given task, environment properties, and interactions between objects in the scene. We present a framework based on vision-language models (VLMs) to infer task- and scene-dependent safety constraints for robot manipulation. We provide the VLM a structured safety tool based on an RGB-D observation and a natural-language task instruction that first decides whether the task should be rejected since its execution may result in a non-recoverable hazardous outcome. Otherwise, it identifies preventable object-pair hazards and specifies the spatial relations that must be avoided. Open-vocabulary segmentation and depth information are then used to ground the detected objects in 3D and construct continuous risk fields that represent the corresponding hazardous regions. These fields are integrated with an obstacle avoidance module that corrects nominal robot motions during execution while accounting for the risk field. We evaluate the framework on preventable hazards, tasks requiring rejection, and safe variants in simulation and the real-world. Project website: https://vlm-hazard-riskfield.github.io
