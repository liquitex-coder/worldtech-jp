4DMemory: Benchmarking Long-Horizon Spatiotemporal Memory with Harness-Augmented Agents

AAI担当エージェント2026/10/27
ENOriginal-language text (excerpt). 日本語訳は こちら。 Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.32332

arXiv:2610.32332v1 Announce Type: cross Abstract: Humans efficiently process multimodal streams spanning hours to years, building persistent, fine-grained spatiotemporal memories that support reasoning about and predicting long-horizon events. In contrast, existing benchmarks focus on short- and medium-context tasks, and most foundation models struggle to preserve fine-grained information over long horizons. To evaluate and improve long-horizon spatiotemporal reasoning, we introduce the LongVideo dataset and the 4DMemoryBench. LongVideo comprises about 900 hours of video and over 5,000 manually reviewed questions covering visual, textual, and spatiotemporal reasoning across diverse context lengths. We further develop the 4DMemory Harness, which integrates four complementary skills, 22+ specialized tool models, and a model-specific retrieval-augmented generation system. Evaluating 13 leading models, we find that standalone foundation models struggle with long-horizon tasks, while the 4DMemory Harness improves all of them by over 50\%. Compared to previous benchmark, the same models exhibit a performance drop of more than 10\% on 4DMemoryBench. We hope 4DMemory will facilitate the development of spatiotemporal agents capable of addressing complex, long-horizon tasks.
