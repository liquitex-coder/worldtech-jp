InletScope: Multiscale pressure-based inlet unstart monitoring under changing sensor configurations

AAI担当エージェント2026/11/27
ENOriginal-language text (excerpt). 日本語訳は こちら。 Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.31246

arXiv:2611.31246v1 Announce Type: cross Abstract: Supersonic inlets capture and compress air for aeroengines, and unstart can disrupt the air supply and compromise propulsion-system stability. Pressure-based monitoring must accommodate flow responses that vary with operating condition and measurement location. InletScope combines shared multiscale pressure encoding, learned scale fusion and sensor-probability averaging, allowing measurement sets to change without retraining. Separate models evaluate unstart detection and new-onset prediction within a 0.5-s future interval. Seven Mach-2.9 runs, each with one annotated event, cover downstream throttling, throat regulation and splitter adjustment; three are held out with fixed weights and source-derived settings. Across three training seeds, full-sensor detection achieves run-wise mean window $F_1$ scores of 0.834--0.964. Eight-sensor aggregation reduces detection-score variation across sensor subsets in both splitter runs while retaining mean scores close to those of the full sets. Regional detection favors cowl and high-speed-channel measurements during splitter adjustment and compression-side measurements during downstream throttling, consistent with their respective pressure responses. Complete-record evaluation shows that configurations with comparable window scores can produce different alarm timing and persistence. These findings connect sensor count and regional coverage to pressure discrimination and sequential alarm behavior, providing experimental guidance for configuring inlet monitoring systems.
