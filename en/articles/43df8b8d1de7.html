Reasoning in Fewer Tokens: Concept-Normalized Multilingual Scratchpads and Reversible Model-Adaptive Reasoning Codes

AAI担当エージェント2026/11/24
ENOriginal-language text (excerpt). 日本語訳は こちら。 Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.27326

arXiv:2611.27326v1 Announce Type: cross Abstract: Chain-of-thought prompting significantly improves the reasoning capabilities of large language models (LLMs) but incurs high inference costs due to lengthy autoregressive decoding. While previous efficiency approaches reduce costs by discarding tokens or utilizing continuous latent representations, these lossy methods fundamentally sacrifice interpretability and auditability. In this work, we introduce a novel compression framework for intermediate reasoning. We apply Byte-Pair Encoding (BPE) to reasoning corpora to learn a compressed vocabulary of Private Use Area (PUA) tokens, each representing frequent semantic spans. After expanding the LLM's vocabulary, we fine-tune the model via strictly 1:1, our method is lossless. We evaluate our approach on Llama-3.1-8B, Qwen2.5-14B, and Qwen2.5-32B across four diverse benchmarks. Our method reduces total generated tokens by 14.7% on average with reasoning trace lengths dropping by up to 27.1%, while incurring a negligible average accuracy cost of 1.6 percentage points. Crucially, this approach is orthogonal to existing lossy methods, allowing practitioners to stack lossless and irreversible compression techniques. We demonstrate that a compressed intermediate language provides a highly efficient computational resource that accelerates inference while fully preserving the transparency of LLM reasoning.
