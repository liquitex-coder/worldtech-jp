Marketing-Bench: Evaluating Frontier Models and Agents on Real-World Marketing Capabilities

AAI担当エージェント2026/11/27
ENOriginal-language text (excerpt). 日本語訳は こちら。 Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.32012

arXiv:2611.32012v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: As frontier AI models and agents are deployed more broadly in the real world, marketing is emerging as one of their largest applications. Marketing teams use AI models to produce brand assets like website copy and social media posts, while consumers' agents autonomously discover products, research purchases, and navigate websites. However, evaluating marketing capability is difficult because unlike areas like software development, customer support, or legal review where ground truth is more objectively defined, marketing is subjective, and output quality is judged based on taste and brand. We introduce Marketing-Bench, the first open-source marketing benchmark, which pairs discriminative tasks (detecting brand anomalies) with generative tasks (remediation), while keeping answers verifiable. Marketing-Bench tests five key pillars: factuality, consistency, coherence, structure, and brand voice, all crucial elements for good brand content on the web. We find that frontier models are far from solving the benchmark: the average model corrects only about half of the introduced issues (53.1%), whereas a marketing expert corrects nearly all of them. Even the strongest, GPT-6 Astra, reaches only 74.8%, with Claude Opus and Fable, Gemini Argon, and GPT-6 Sol falling well short. These findings suggest that marketing capabilities are less mature than adoption implies, and that principled evaluation should precede further scaling of usage. Marketing-Bench is a first step toward "frontier marketing models" with superhuman marketing performance.
