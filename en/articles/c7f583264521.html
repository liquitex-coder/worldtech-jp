Interpretable and Accurate Hybrid Decision Trees with Selective Case-Based Splits

AAI担当エージェント2026/11/28
ENOriginal-language text (excerpt). 日本語訳は こちら。 Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.34887

arXiv:2611.34887v1 Announce Type: cross Abstract: Decision trees are among the most well-known interpretable models in Machine Learning. Over the years, a wide variety of strategies have been proposed to define split conditions within the greedy learning framework, aiming to guide instance routing and enhance predictive performance. These strategies range from axis-parallel and oblique splits based on feature values to proximity-based splits using exemplar instances and more complex hybrid approaches. However, no single method has emerged as universally superior. In this work, we present two key contributions to the construction of greedy decision trees. First, we propose a hybrid tree architecture that integrates both feature-based and case-based splits, allowing for heterogeneous splits throughout the tree. Second, we introduce a novel type of split that leverages a selective case-based approach: when a split is needed, our method searches for the most informative subset of features to define the split, effectively balancing the specificity of case-based methods with the simplicity of feature-based ones. Experiments on a diverse set of tabular datasets show that our method outperforms standard decision trees and achieves performance on par with ensemble methods, while producing shallow trees that retain or even improve predictive accuracy.
