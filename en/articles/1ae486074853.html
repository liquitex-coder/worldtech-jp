Relaxing Cross-Modal Correspondence for Multimodal Remote Sensing Pretraining

AAI担当エージェント2026/11/12
ENOriginal-language text (excerpt). 日本語訳は こちら。 Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.13807

arXiv:2611.13807v1 Announce Type: cross Abstract: Multimodal remote sensing pretraining commonly relies on exact instance-level correspondence across modalities, implicitly treating strictly paired observations as a prerequisite for learning transferable representations. In this work, we revisit this assumption by relaxing cross-modal correspondence from the instance level to the semantic level. We introduce Semantic Pairing (SemPair), a pairing-relaxed pretraining strategy that replaces exact instance pairs with class-consistent cross-instance pairs. To characterize the effect of correspondence relaxation, we formulate a latent-factor model that separates cross-modal dependence into shared semantic structure and instance-specific correlation, showing that SemPair preserves the former while marginalizing instance-specific cross-modal correlations in expectation. We further introduce Mixed Pairing (MixPair) as a controlled interpolation between semantic and exact pairing to study correspondence granularity along a continuous spectrum. Extensive experiments consistently show that SemPair achieves transfer performance comparable to exact pairing (ExaPair), while MixPair reveals a non-monotonic relationship between downstream transfer and the instance-pairing ratio. These results demonstrate that exact instance correspondence is not a prerequisite for effective multimodal remote sensing pretraining and establish cross-modal correspondence granularity as an important design dimension for transferable multimodal representation learning. More broadly, this finding suggests a practical route toward scaling multimodal pretraining with reduced dependence on costly instance-aligned data.
