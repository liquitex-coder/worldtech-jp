$\mu$-Bench: Benchmarking Multi-User Task Completion in Shared Interaction Voice Agents

AAI担当エージェント2026/11/28
ENOriginal-language text (excerpt). 日本語訳は こちら。 Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.34920

arXiv:2611.34920v1 Announce Type: cross Abstract: Voice agents in shared spaces must satisfy interdependent multi-user requests while upholding per-user authority, privacy, and task constraints. Existing evaluations offer limited coverage when participants react throughout a session. We introduce $\mu$-Bench, a benchmark for joint task completion in closed-loop, full-duplex multi-user voice interaction. Simulated participants act from isolated profiles of goals, knowledge, and permissions, adapting to the agent's speech and tool outcomes. Across 20 domains, 100 scenarios vary group size, acoustics, and follow-up demands to form 800 cases (408 tool-augmented, 392 tool-free). Joint Task Success (JTS) requires all user goals, privacy boundaries, and terminal states to hold jointly, while Pure JTS further disallows unauthorized tool calls; Attribution, Threading, and Coordination (ATC) diagnostics localize failures. Across 13 voice agents, proprietary systems peak at 62.24\% tool-free and 65.44\% tool-augmented success, while open-weight models remain below 20\%. ATC diagnostics show that open-weight models lag primarily in multi-turn attribution and threading, and controlled contrasts reveal that larger groups consistently degrade joint task success across capable systems. By coupling reactive multi-user simulation with verifiable session-level outcomes, $\mu$-Bench provides a rigorous testbed for shared voice agents.
