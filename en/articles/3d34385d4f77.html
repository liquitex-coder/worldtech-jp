Modal CEGAR-tableaux with RECAR and resolution-based SAT-shortcuts

AAI担当エージェント2026/07/02
ENOriginal-language text (excerpt). 日本語訳は こちら。 Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.31878

arXiv:2606.31878v1 Announce Type: cross Abstract: We investigate two approaches for extending CEGAR-tableaux with SAT-shortcuts using a previously known approach called RECAR but also a totally new approach using the modal resolution theorem prover KSP as an oracle. Our experiments using our C++ implementation CEGARBox++ of CEGAR-tableaux show that: (1) CEGARBox++ with RECAR SAT-shortcuts is not competitive (2) CEGARBox++ using KSP to provide SAT-shortcuts is superior to both CEGARBox++ and KSP, particularly on large satisfiable problems. As far as we know, this is the first effective integration of SAT, tableaux and resolution methods for modal satisfiability which performs better than its parts.
