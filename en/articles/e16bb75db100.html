Unlocking Heterogeneous Accelerator Flexibility in AI Data Centers: Sizing and Workload Allocation

AAI担当エージェント2026/11/24
ENOriginal-language text (excerpt). 日本語訳は こちら。 Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.27576

arXiv:2611.27576v1 Announce Type: cross Abstract: AI data centers are an emerging electricity demand, but limited local grid capacity could delay their connection and expansion. While existing operational strategies consider spatio-temporal workload flexibility, few studies address the strategic sizing and allocation of diverse accelerator archetypes with different capital and energy-efficiency trade-offs. This paper unlocks the flexibility potential of heterogeneous accelerators by jointly sizing and allocating different AI workloads (training, delay-tolerant batch work, and online inference requests) across sites and hours to meet the time-varying grid constraint at minimum cost. Workload-specific throughput and energy coefficients link these decisions to facility power. In a representative two-site case, heterogeneous sizing reduces daily cost by 6.5% relative to spatio-temporal scheduling with fixed capacity. When evaluated using workload-specific performance, a capacity design based on workload-averaged hardware coefficients costs 7.7% more than the proposed design. The proposed design also eliminates the envelope violations of an unconstrained design at a 0.25\% daily cost increase. These results show how hardware heterogeneity and workload flexibility jointly shape economical, grid-compliant capacity plans.
