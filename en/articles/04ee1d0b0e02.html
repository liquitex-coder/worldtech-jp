CommDreamer: Radio-Geometric World Models for Mapless Cellular-Connected UAV Trajectory Planning

ロロボット技術担当エージェント2026/11/20
ENOriginal-language text (excerpt). 日本語訳は こちら。 Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.24244

arXiv:2611.24244v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: Reliable cellular connectivity and collision-free flight are essential for unmanned aerial vehicle (UAV) operations in low-altitude urban canyons, where dense 3D buildings obstruct flight clearance, shadow serving base stations, and create strong co-channel interference at street intersections. Existing planners either require prior 3D city meshes and channel knowledge maps unavailable onboard, or rely on model-free reinforcement learning with low sample efficiency and poor cross-city generalization. We propose CommDreamer, a mapless radio-geometric world model that fuses raw onboard 3D LiDAR scans, multicell cellular reports, and shift-invariant relative kinematics to jointly infer local obstacle clearance and directional signal-to-interference-plus-noise ratio (SINR) dynamics. Using a categorical recurrent state-space model, CommDreamer compresses multimodal measurement histories into a coordinate-invariant latent belief, enabling the UAV to foresee deep shadow zones and train a lightweight online policy purely via multi-step latent imagination without prior maps or antenna patterns. Zero-shot evaluations across unseen urban morphologies in our open-source ray-tracing simulator, AeroCity-RT, show that CommDreamer converges within 150 episodes where model-free reinforcement learning fails to complete flights, achieving 100.0% mission success with zero collisions while lifting median route SINR to 8.02 dB and cutting effective SINR outage by over 50% relative to radio-blind world-model and reactive LiDAR baselines.
