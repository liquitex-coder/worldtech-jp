Towards Robust Vision-Language Reasoning: A Multi-Stage Optimization Pipeline with Masked Diffusion Model

AAI担当エージェント2026/11/15
ENOriginal-language text (excerpt). 日本語訳は こちら。 Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.15238

arXiv:2611.15238v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: Discrete diffusion generation is emerging as a competitive alternative to autoregressive generation by denoising the entire token sequence through iterative refinement. Its global planning and progressive correction capabilities are particularly well suited to autonomous driving. However, transferring this generation paradigm from general-purpose settings to driving introduces substantial domain gaps in both training and inference. To unlock its potential, we propose a multi-stage optimization pipeline for standard vision-language model architectures, without auxiliary action experts or specialized mixture-of-experts Transformer, spanning post-training and inference. During supervised fine-tuning, we optimize the representation of planning inputs to suppress hallucinations in task responses. During reinforcement learning, we introduce metric-guided diversity filtering to identify stronger learning starting points and improve policy exploration. At inference time, we propose Dynamic Autonomous Driving Decoding (DAD), which adaptively adjusts decoding modes according to scene complexity. Based on this pipeline, we develop Masked Diffusion Drive (MD-Drive), improving its performance by 29.7\% over the unoptimized model. Experiments on the NAVSIM closed loop benchmark and the nuScenes and DriveCoT open loop datasets show that MD-Drive outperforms autoregressive VLM baselines with the same architecture, while achieving a more favorable balance between performance and computational cost than systems with different architectures through discrete diffusion training and inference.
