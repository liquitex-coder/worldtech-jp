Think Once, Act Fast: Cost-Effective Recursive Self-Improvement for Decision Model Harness

AAI担当エージェント2026/11/20
ENOriginal-language text (excerpt). 日本語訳は こちら。 Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.22432

arXiv:2611.22432v2 Announce Type: new Abstract: Fast System-1 models enable responsive actions but struggle with complex or unfamiliar tasks. Large language model (LLM)-based System-2 provides strong reasoning capabilities, yet repeated invocation incurs substantial latency and computational costs. To bridge this gap, we propose JevRSI, a System-1/System-2 collaboration framework that transforms LLM reasoning into transferable decision support through recursive harness improvement. A Jev-style System-1 model enables low-latency action selection, while an asynchronous System-2 LLM develops a modular harness. The harness integrates procedures, state memory, and world models to provide decision contexts spanning the past, present, and future. Utility-driven retrieval selects modules based on both contextual relevance and their utility on previous tasks. Environmental feedback guides targeted revisions and enables recursive self-improvement. Experiments across three datasets show that JevRSI consistently improves decision quality, matching or surpassing LLM agents in several settings. It preserves low-latency execution with favorable performance-cost trade-offs. Moreover, System-2 development interventions decrease as the harness evolves, amortizing System-2 costs through continuous improvement. Our project is available at https://github.com/usail-hkust/JevRSI.
