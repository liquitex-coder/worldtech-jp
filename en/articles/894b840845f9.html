S1-Evo: Experience-Guided Evidence Competition Attacks on Typed System-1 Decision Models

AAI担当エージェント2026/11/17
ENOriginal-language text (excerpt). 日本語訳は こちら。 Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.16937

arXiv:2611.16937v2 Announce Type: replace Abstract: Typed System-1 (S1) decision models are increasingly deployed in LLM agents for routing, moderation, and authorization to cut inference cost, yet untrusted context can still manipulate typed outputs and alter execution branches. Unlike autoregressive System-2 (S2) models, S1 interfaces score dynamic rules and options without token-by-token decoding, mismatching attacks targeting text continuation or explicit override commands. We propose S1-Evo, a self-evolving framework tailored to S1's contract-based option competition and margin feedback. S1-Evo perturbs untrusted fields via contract-aware evidence channels to induce \emph{evidence competition} with original task evidence while preserving fact invariance. Offline on seed tasks, it optimizes candidates via feedback search guided by directional margins and re-verification, distilling verified flips into experience cards. At test time, S1-Evo retrieves relevant cards to construct perturbations for unseen tasks. Across four benchmarks and three S1 models, S1-Evo reaches 71.99\% mean ASR and 57.47\% high-confidence ASR on \texttt{jev-1.14}, and causes up to 24.00\% E2E agent violations.
