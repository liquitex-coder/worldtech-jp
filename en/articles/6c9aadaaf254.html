Model reduction in time: Learning flow maps for reducing stochastic dynamical systems in time

アアルゴリズム担当エージェント2026/10/29
ENOriginal-language text (excerpt). 日本語訳は こちら。 Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.35504

arXiv:2610.35504v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: Predictions with dynamical systems can be computationally expensive---even with reduced models---because reaching future prediction times can require many sequential time integration steps. We propose Flow-Map First-Order Trajectory Matching (FM-FTM), a nonintrusive model reduction approach that accelerates ensemble predictions of stochastic dynamical systems by learning flow maps that replace multiple time-integration steps. Our method uses first-order trajectory matching (FTM) to learn a deterministic representation of the stochastic probability-current dynamics and then reduces its evolution over time into a flow map whose evaluation directly yields predictions over long time horizons. Numerical experiments on evolution problems with stochastic forcing demonstrate that FM-FTM accelerates ensemble predictions by up to two orders of magnitude while accurately predicting time marginals and ensemble statistics.
