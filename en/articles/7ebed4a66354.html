F3-AGI v4.0: Bioenergetically-Gated Active Inference, Metaplastic Preconditioning, and Residual Program Synthesis for Sample-Efficient Abstract Reasoning

AAI担当エージェント2026/11/12
ENOriginal-language text (excerpt). 日本語訳は こちら。 Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.13325

arXiv:2611.13325v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: Few-shot abstract reasoning on ARC-AGI requires inducing algorithmic transformations from 2 to 5 demonstration pairs without task-specific pretraining. Because few-shot demonstrations under-constrain the hypothesis space, program synthesis suffers from the Rashomon effect (predictive multiplicity): heuristic search finds spurious programs with low training error that fail on test inputs, while unconstrained library growth degrades latency. We introduce F3-AGI v4.0, a CPU-native neuro-symbolic and active inference architecture comprising three mechanisms: (1) F3-Metabolic-Optim, combining mistake-gated plasticity, Fisher-EMA preconditioning, and virtual ATP gating; (2) Adaptive Complexity-Guided Program Synthesis (AACR) with a Cycle-3 ResidualPatchSynthesizer and smallest-pair early rejection; and (3) a 512D L2-normalized random projection driving Expected Free Energy (EFE) exploration with dead-signature pruning. Across three empirical studies: (i) on a synthetic 5-task class-incremental stream without replay, F3-Metabolic-Optim achieves 91.67% final accuracy with 9.17% forgetting, skipping 93.47% of updates, cutting L1 plasticity cost by 65.7%, and running 2.19x faster than EWC; (ii) on the 400-task ARC-AGI-1 public evaluation set (offline CPU, zero LLM calls), F3-AGI v4.0 solves 57/400 tasks (14.25% pass@2; 56/400 pass@1) with a mean latency of 1.62 s/task, decomposed into 19/50 (38.00%, 1.19 s/task, a 42.5x paired speedup over Horizon 2) on the 50-task calibration slice and 38/350 (10.86%) on the 350 strictly uninspected hold-out tasks (with 7 autonomous Cycle-3 patch solutions); and (iii) in a local 5-game (15-level) ARC-AGI-3-inspired interactive suite, our agent completes 15/15 levels with 89.44% level-weighted RHAE (90.72% unweighted).
