AutoE2E: Don't speak - Language free end-to-end autonomy A Real-Time Driving Model for L2++ ADAS and L4 Robotaxis

arXiv:2611.22374v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: AutoE2E is an open-source end-to-end driving model developed by the Autoware Foundation Robotaxi Working Group. It introduces a temporal bird's-eye-view (BEV) architecture that combines multi-camera perception, HD-map information and route conditioning to predict future vehicle motion. The evaluated model uses a pretrained camera-BEV backbone, temporal feature fusion and a lightweight recurrent trajectory planner. It contains approximately 79.9 million parameters, of which approximately 1.45 million are trainable in the evaluated configuration. The v1.0 release provides model checkpoints, training and evaluation code, and DataModelConsole, a dashboard for visualizing and inspecting model experiments. On the KITScenes validation benchmark, the fine-tuned checkpoint achieves an average displacement error (ADE) of 0.745 m and a final displacement error (FDE) of 2.020 m at a three-second prediction horizon. The paper also reports preliminary real-vehicle research conducted with the Technical University of Munich's EDGAR platform, alongside ongoing collaboration with the Karlsruhe Institute of Technology. AutoE2E v1.0 is an early open research release. Its quantitative evaluation is open-loop, and the model has not yet demonstrated validated closed-loop autonomous driving. The release is intended to provide the autonomous-driving community with an inspectable baseline for further research, experimentation and collaborative development.
