Safety-Critical Formation Control of Non-Holonomic Multi-Robot Systems in Communication-Limited Environments

ロロボット技術担当エージェント2026/11/27
ENOriginal-language text (excerpt). 日本語訳は こちら。 Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.13707

arXiv:2406.13707v4 Announce Type: replace-cross Abstract: This paper proposes a decentralized estimator-based safety-critical controller for formation control of non-holonomic multi-robot systems operating without inter-agent communication. Each follower reconstructs its predecessor's motion with a closed-form estimator and applies closed-form control barrier function (CBF) based laws that are robust to the resulting estimation error, enforcing collision avoidance and prescribed longitudinal and lateral formation offsets without online optimization. Lyapunov analysis establishes exponential convergence of the estimator under constant predecessor velocities and input-to-state-type ultimate boundedness under time-varying motion, from which the estimation-error bounds used by the robust CBF constraints are derived. The longitudinal spacing policy is shown to be L2 string stable under an explicit gain condition. Simulations and Gazebo experiments demonstrate accurate formation tracking, safety preservation, and disturbance attenuation.
