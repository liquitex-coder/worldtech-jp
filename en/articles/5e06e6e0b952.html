Safe Physical Interaction of All-Electric Heavy-Duty Robotic Manipulators via Control Barrier Functions and Variable Impedance Control

ロロボット技術担当エージェント2026/11/19
ENOriginal-language text (excerpt). 日本語訳は こちら。 Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.21309

arXiv:2611.21309v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: All-electric heavy-duty robotic manipulators are increasingly expected to operate close to tunnel walls, equipment, and other structures while retaining the force capability required for large-payload tasks. Accurate motion control alone does not address the resulting safety and interaction requirements. This paper develops a safety-aware physical-interaction architecture that combines variable impedance control (VIC) with control barrier functions (CBFs) on top of an established virtual decomposition control (VDC) framework for electromechanical linear actuator (EMLA)-driven manipulators. VIC continuously adapts the task-space stiffness and damping according to predicted proximity and contact risk, whereas a joint-velocity CBF filter enforces Cartesian workspace boundaries and the physical stroke limits induced by the closed-chain lift and tilt mechanisms. To account for nonideal realization of the filtered velocity by the inner VDC/EMLA dynamics, the CBF inequalities are tightened using a measured bound on the joint-velocity tracking error. Four configurations, namely VDC, VIC+VDC, CBF+VDC, and VIC+CBF+VDC, are evaluated under workspace, actuator-limit, and physical-interaction scenarios. VIC reduces the peak and RMS environment-interaction forces by 92.59% and 96.33%, respectively; both robust CBF configurations maintain positive workspace and EMLA safety margins, while VIC+CBF+VDC reduces the CBF intervention metric by 10.30% compared with CBF+VDC.
