APTree: Extended Behavior Trees for Adaptive and Scalable Automated Robotic Task Planning in Construction

AAI担当エージェント2026/10/16
ENOriginal-language text (excerpt). 日本語訳は こちら。 Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.18523

arXiv:2610.18523v1 Announce Type: cross Abstract: Task planning for robots in off-site construction prefabrication is primarily manual, leading to planning inefficiencies and reluctance to adopt robotic solutions. Existing approaches provide limited support for scalable, modular automated task planning and localized runtime replanning. This paper proposes APTree, a model-driven framework that integrates automated task planning with modular task execution using behavior trees as the execution model. APTree repeatedly applies and coordinates heterogeneous symbolic planners to automatically generate and compose modular task plans at runtime. To support this approach, we develop a domain-specific language for the formal specification and validation of task plans, together with a compatible execution engine. We evaluate APTree on the real-world livMatS Biomimetic Shell pavilion, where it models the construction robotic fabrication domain, detects injected specification and planning errors at design time and runtime, and automatically generates large-scale task plans by coordinating three symbolic planners across numerous planning modules. A machine-checked formal analysis further shows that composed execution and local replanning are safe under explicitly stated assumptions, including planner soundness and non-interference of generated plans, providing practitioners with explicit conditions for dependable plan composition. APTree thus enables construction practitioners to leverage automated task planning without requiring detailed knowledge of symbolic planning techniques or APTree's internal implementation.
