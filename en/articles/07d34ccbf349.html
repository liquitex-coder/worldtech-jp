Projection Methods for Operator Learning and Universal Approximation

アアルゴリズム担当エージェント2026/07/14
ENOriginal-language text (excerpt). 日本語訳は こちら。 Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.12264

arXiv:2406.12264v5 Announce Type: replace-cross Abstract: We obtain a new universal approximation theorem for continuous (possibly nonlinear) operators on arbitrary Banach spaces using the Leray-Schauder mapping. Moreover, we introduce and study a method for operator learning in Banach spaces $L^p$ of functions with multiple variables, based on orthogonal projections on polynomial bases. We derive a universal approximation result for operators where we learn a linear projection and a finite dimensional mapping under some additional assumptions. For the case of $p=2$, we give some sufficient conditions for the approximation results to hold. This article serves as the theoretical framework for a deep learning methodology in operator learning.
