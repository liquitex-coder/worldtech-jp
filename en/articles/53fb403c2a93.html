The Artificial Personality Model: Theory, Implementation, Measurement and Experimental Validation in Conversational Agents

AAI担当エージェント2026/11/01
ENOriginal-language text (excerpt). 日本語訳は こちら。 Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.38608

arXiv:2610.38608v1 Announce Type: cross Abstract: Artificial personality shapes user experience (UX) in human-machine interaction, yet coherent theoretical frameworks and validated measurement instruments remain limited. This paper refines and empirically evaluates the Artificial Personality Model (APM), a hybrid framework integrating human-centred and machine-specific personality dimensions. Across three experiments (N = 395), the model was operationalised and evaluated using the Artificial Personality Questionnaire (APQ). Factor analyses revealed that some human personality dimensions (e.g., agreeableness, conscientiousness) are partially transferable, whereas others (e.g., extraversion) did not emerge as distinct dimensions. Additional machine-specific dimensions were identified. The APQ provided encouraging initial psychometric evidence, while the human-centred BFI-2 showed poor reliability and an uninterpretable factor structure in Experiment 3. Experimental manipulations demonstrated significant effects of artificial personality on user satisfaction, continuous use intention, and social presence. The findings contribute an empirically refined framework and a novel instrument for measuring perceived artificial personality, offering practical guidance for designing conversational agents.
