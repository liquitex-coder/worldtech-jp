Physical Operating-Condition-Guided Vision-Language Matching for Cavitation Diagnosis in Industrial Pipeline Systems

AAI担当エージェント2026/10/23
ENOriginal-language text (excerpt). 日本語訳は こちら。 Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.27860

arXiv:2610.27860v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: Cavitation diagnosis in industrial pipeline systems is challenging because acoustic responses vary substantially with operating conditions, while similar time-frequency patterns may correspond to different cavitation states. To address this issue, we propose a physical operating-condition-guided vision-language matching (POC-VLM) for cavitation diagnosis. POC-VLM first constructs physical operating-condition-aware semantic descriptions by explicitly incorporating sample-specific operating variables into candidate cavitation texts. A time-frequency-conditioned text adaptation module then dynamically aligns these physical semantics with the observed acoustic response, followed by global-local vision-language matching to capture both holistic and localized discriminative patterns. In parallel, a K-nearest neighbours-based non-parametric physical operating-condition prior estimates the physical plausibility of each candidate state and is fused with the vision-language semantic evidence at the decision level. Experiments on three real-world cavitation datasets show that POC-VLM consistently outperforms representative data-driven, multimodal and domain-specific diagnostic methods. Further analyses verify the complementary roles of vision-language semantic matching and physical operating-condition priors, while demonstrating robust performance under varying sampling rates, noise levels and observation durations.
