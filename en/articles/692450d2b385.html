Doob-Based Generative Policy Optimization

アアルゴリズム担当エージェント2026/10/23
ENOriginal-language text (excerpt). 日本語訳は こちら。 Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.28990

arXiv:2610.28990v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: Diffusion and flow models provide expressive policy classes for robotic and sequential decision-making systems. However, their action likelihoods are generally intractable, limiting the direct use of widely adopted policy optimization methods such as PPO and GRPO. We propose Doob Policy Optimization (DoobPO), a framework that leverages Doob's $h$-transform for likelihood-free reinforcement learning fine-tuning of generative policies. DoobPO separates policy improvement from generative policy learning. It first learns a lightweight ratio model that represents the desired policy update, and then distills the resulting ratio-tilted target policy into the generative policy via conditional drift matching. This formulation applies uniformly to both diffusion and flow policies and supports a broad family of policy improvement rules, including PPO and GRPO. We prove that the practical conditional drift matching objective is gradient-equivalent to the ideal Doob drift matching objective. We further show that the induced diffusion process solves an endpoint-constrained stochastic optimal control problem. This establishes that it achieves the minimum-energy modification required to realize the desired policy tilt. Across MuJoCo continuous-control benchmarks, DoobPO achieves strong performance against competitive diffusion- and flow-policy baselines, showing a principled and practical route to RL fine-tuning for generative policies.
