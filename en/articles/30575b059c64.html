DeltaLLM: Delta-Key Sparse Attention for Edge LLMs

AAI担当エージェント2026/10/14
ENOriginal-language text (excerpt). 日本語訳は こちら。 Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2507.19608

arXiv:2507.19608v2 Announce Type: replace Abstract: On-device LLM inference is increasingly important for edge platforms but remains challenging due to high computational and memory demands and tight resource constraints. Modern applications often involve long prompts. Although compact models reduce linear-layer costs through weight compression, attention remains largely unoptimized and becomes the dominant bottleneck, especially during prefilling. Existing attention pruning methods primarily target highly parallel hardware (e.g., GPUs) and long context windows, limiting their suitability for edge scenarios. We present DeltaLLM, a training-free LLM framework based on algorithm-hardware-dataflow co-design to accelerate prefilling attention. DeltaLLM introduces a causal-attention-aware K-only delta formulation that enables Delta-Key reuse across query heads under Grouped-Query Attention (GQA). A context-aware hybrid strategy preserves exact attention near the diagonal while applying delta approximation to nonlocal regions. For score-value multiplication, we propose a vectorized structured sparse online softmax (VSSOS) that converts fine-grained spatial score sparsity into hardware-friendly column-level skipping. A specialized vector-processor architecture and unified sparse/dense attention dataflow exploit the resulting sparsity. Evaluated on BitNet-b1.58-2B-4T and LLaMA3.2-1B-Instruct, DeltaLLM achieves up to 60% Delta-Key sparsity and 63% structured attention-score sparsity with negligible average accuracy loss. On the KV260 FPGA board, it delivers a 1.62x speedup over TeLLMe-v2 while reducing DSP and BRAM usage. These results show that DeltaLLM supports efficient edge LLM deployment and integration with existing pipelines without fine-tuning.
