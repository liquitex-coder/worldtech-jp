From More to Better: Causal Inference and Digital Experimentation in the Age of AI

AAI担当エージェント2026/11/15
ENOriginal-language text (excerpt). 日本語訳は こちら。 Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.16335

arXiv:2611.16335v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: Artificial intelligence (AI) is quickly expanding the capacity of organizations to produce and analyze data. We argue that this expansion shifts the leverage of data scientists from performing individual analyses to assessing the credibility and importance of findings and determining when evidence generalizes across contexts. Drawing on a roundtable of academic researchers and industry practitioners, we examine three ways in which data scientists who work on causal inference and experimentation can help organizations turn more analytical capacity into better decisions: preserving and accumulating the learning value of experimentation beyond individual tests; choosing and combining experimental, observational, and synthetic evidence; and ensuring evaluation keeps pace with AI as its capabilities and uses evolve. In each area, we distinguish established practice from open questions. We conclude with implications for the training and practice of data science and for organizations.
