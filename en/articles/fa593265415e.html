A Framework for AI-Ready Data in Earth and Environmental Science

AAI担当エージェント2026/11/24
ENOriginal-language text (excerpt). 日本語訳は こちら。 Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.27313

arXiv:2611.27313v1 Announce Type: cross Abstract: Artificial intelligence (AI), specifically machine learning (ML), is increasingly integral to Earth and environmental research, yet the suitability of current research data infrastructures for supporting AI workflows remains poorly defined. While the FAIR principles provide a foundation for scientific data management, they do not explicitly address several requirements arising when datasets are used for training, evaluating, and deploying AI models. This article presents a practically motivated framework for assessing and improving AI-readiness of research data within distributed Earth and environmental data infrastructures. The framework was developed through a structured, community-driven process, beginning with a review of existing literature and readiness frameworks combined with feedback from a limited survey of scientists applying ML methods. Its main aspects were shaped by a multidisciplinary expert group representing data providers, infrastructure developers, data managers, and AI practitioners. It identifies 25 criteria covering data preparation, data quality, documentation, access, and supervised learning targets. The criteria emphasize aspects particularly relevant to AI applications, including provenance, treatment of missing data, bias documentation, uncertainty characterization, machine-actionable metadata, efficient access formats, and the quality and reproducibility of target generation. Application to a broad range of representative environmental use cases highlights how the relevance of individual criteria varies by application. We argue that AI-readiness should be understood primarily as a property of transparency and fitness for purpose rather than as a fixed level of data quality. The framework lays a foundation for communication and community-driven action to advance AI-ready data and support next-generation AI-enabled scientific workflows.
