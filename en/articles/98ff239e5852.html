Ego2Ego: Generative Visual Perspective Taking Across Collaborators

ロロボット技術担当エージェント2026/11/24
ENOriginal-language text (excerpt). 日本語訳は こちら。 Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.27459

arXiv:2611.27459v1 Announce Type: cross Abstract: Visual perspective taking, the ability to infer what another person sees from their position, is fundamental to human collaboration. However, prior works reduced perspective taking to state estimation, geometric reconstruction, or discrete question answering, whereas existing cross-view video generation methods either inferred the target view from the input video alone or required an external camera that observes the target person. We introduce generative visual perspective taking: Given an ego person's egocentric video, a scene point cloud, and a collaborator's camera trajectory, the task is to generate the continuous egocentric video from the collaborator's perspective. This task is challenging given extreme viewpoint shifts, dynamic occlusions, and sparse visual overlap. We address these challenges with Ego2Ego -- a set of backbone-agnostic geometric modules that provide a pretrained video diffusion model with the 3D scene geometry shared by both interactants. Ego2Ego renders dense, temporally consistent scene coordinate maps into every view as a shared spatial reference, aligns cross-view tokens in 3D ray space through relative geometric attention, and recovers regions outside the ego person's current view from the ego person's visual memory. Experiments on the CoMind dataset across three video diffusion backbones show that Ego2Ego consistently improves visual quality, Level-2 scene layout accuracy, and collaborator motion, outperforming state-of-the-art cross-view video generation baselines on every backbone. By generating the collaborator's egocentric video more accurately, Ego2Ego brings visual perspective taking to human-robot collaboration, where it can help infer what a human collaborator sees and does, and can turn single-person recordings into synchronised multi-perspective videos for robot learning.
