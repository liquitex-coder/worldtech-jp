RTPolicy: Real-Time (32.9 Hz) Multi-View Visuomotor Policy for Robotic Manipulation on Edge Devices

ロロボット技術担当エージェント2026/11/03
ENOriginal-language text (excerpt). 日本語訳は こちら。 Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.41625

arXiv:2610.41625v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: Low inference latency is critical for closed-loop robotic manipulation, especially under asynchronous inference, where action chunks must be updated promptly to correct accumulated deviations. However, existing visuomotor policies rely on computationally expensive visual backbones, resulting in high inference latency and deployment overhead. In this paper, we propose RTPolicy, a multi-view visuomotor policy that attains accurate manipulation with high inference efficiency. Its core design combines view-aware feature fusion with visual compression to aggregate complementary information across camera views into a compact token representation. The representation conditions a flow-matching action generator with the robot state and flow time, reducing the cost of action generation. Extensive experiments demonstrate that this design reduces the computational cost of visual representation and action generation while preserving visual information required for manipulation. On the RoboTwin~2.0 single-task clean evaluation across 50 tasks, RTPolicy attains a mean success rate of 52.26\%, outperforming the strongest leaderboard baseline $\pi_0$ (46.4\%). We further evaluate RTPolicy under asynchronous streaming inference on a real bimanual platform, where RTPolicy attains a mean success rate of 72.0\% and sustains real-time inference at 30.35 ms (32.9 Hz) on the RDK S600 and 149.72 ms (6.7 Hz) on the RDK S100, both in INT16. Project Page: https://d-robotics-ai-lab.github.io/RTPolicy.project.
