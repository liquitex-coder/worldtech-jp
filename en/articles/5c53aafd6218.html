Gandalf: Flash Shall Not Die! Increasing Flash Storage Lifetime for KV Cache Offloading in LLM Serving

アアルゴリズム担当エージェント2026/10/24
ENOriginal-language text (excerpt). 日本語訳は こちら。 Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.30791

arXiv:2610.30791v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: KV caching reduces the serving cost of large language models (LLMs), but the growing KV cache footprint is increasingly exceeds GPU and host-memory capacity. Therefore, people use SSD to expand the KV cache storage capacity with high I/O bandwidth. However, in production LLM workload, such as multi-round conversation and LLM agents, the total amount of KV cache writes significantly exceeds SSD capacity, shortening SSD lifetime from years to months. To improve SSD lifetime, a natural solution is to reduce the KV cache writes by gating the KV cache writes via historical access patterns. However, that significantly lowers the KV cache hit rate (and increase the LLM serving cost) since KV caches from different attention types might have identical historical access pattern but different future access patterns. Our insight is that, to reduce KV cache writes without hurting KV cache hit rate, one need to not only consider the access pattern, but also application-level information such as the attention type of KV cache. Leveraging this observation, we propose Gandalf , the first SSD KV cache offloading system with SSD life time awareness. Gandalf prioritize the admission of full-attention KV cache into SSD over the cache generated by other types of attention, which significantly increases SSD lifetime without sacrificing cache hit rate. Our evaluation shows that Gandalf extends the SSD lifetime by 7.3X on average, while having negligible impact on cache hit rate across multiple workload traces and wide range of hardware capacity. Gandalf design can be extended to other flash-based storage, such as High Bandwidth Flash (HBF).
