LLM-Enhanced Deep Reinforcement Learning with Human-in-the-Loop Calibration for Real-Time Optimal Power Flow

AAI担当エージェント2026/11/15
ENOriginal-language text (excerpt). 日本語訳は こちら。 Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.15003

arXiv:2611.15003v1 Announce Type: cross Abstract: Real-time optimal power flow (RT-OPF) requires fast and reliable decision-making under time-varying operating conditions. However, many practical operating preferences and safety related requirements are difficult to encode using conventional numerical rewards alone, which limits the effectiveness and controllability of deep reinforcement learning (DRL) for RT-OPF. To address this issue, this paper proposes an LLM-enhanced DRL framework with human-in-the-loop (HITL) for RT-OPF. The proposed method augments the physics-based reward with sparse language evaluative feedback during offline training, so that semantic operational requirements can be incorporated into policy learning. To improve reliability in RT-OPF, a selective HITL calibration mechanism is further introduced to inspect and correct questionable LLM judgments before they continuously shape the learning process. Case studies on the IEEE 30-bus and IEEE 118-bus systems show that LLM feedback improves policy safety over vanilla TD3, while further HITL calibration achieves the best overall performance and more stable training behavior. These results demonstrate the effectiveness of combining language guidance and expert calibration for RT-OPF.
