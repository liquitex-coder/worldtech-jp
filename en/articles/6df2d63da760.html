An XR-Based System for Enhanced Spatial Awareness in Mobile Robot Teleoperation

ロロボット技術担当エージェント2026/11/26
ENOriginal-language text (excerpt). 日本語訳は こちら。 Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.32584

arXiv:2611.32584v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: Teleoperation of mobile robots represents a critical capability for operations in hazardous environments, yet maintaining adequate situational awareness remains challenging without effective visualization and control interfaces. This work presents a multi-modal extended reality (XR) system for robot teleoperation and scene exploration that integrates immersive visual and haptic feedback modalities. To address motion sickness commonly induced by 360-degree camera feeds in head-mounted displays (HMDs), the system employs a fixed dual-camera configuration: a forward-facing camera for primary navigation and a rear-facing camera for enhanced situational awareness without operator discomfort. For navigation in confined or low-visibility conditions, a 16-channel Velodyne LiDAR generates real-time 2D occupancy grid maps, which are converted into vibrational haptic feedback on handheld controllers to provide intuitive, non-visual obstacle detection. User evaluation with 20 participants demonstrates that this haptic modality significantly reduces the cognitive load associated with obstacle avoidance during teleoperation.
