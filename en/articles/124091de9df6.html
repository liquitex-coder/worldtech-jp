Neural Defend ARCAS 290M: A Robust Foundation Model for AI-Generated Audio Detection

AAI担当エージェント2026/11/24
ENOriginal-language text (excerpt). 日本語訳は こちら。 Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.27784

arXiv:2611.27784v1 Announce Type: cross Abstract: Voice cloning now needs only seconds of audio, and detectors tuned to one dataset often fail when the generator, channel, or language changes. We introduce ARCAS 290M, Neural Defend's foundation model for detecting AI-generated audio. A single frozen production model, with no benchmark-specific fine-tuning, generalizes across text-to-speech, voice-conversion, and codec-language-model generators, telephone and Bluetooth channels, recording conditions, and languages. We evaluate it on the complete official test sets of ten public benchmarks, scoring every recording exactly as released, and against commercial systems on independent leaderboards. ARCAS 290M ranks first among all systems on the Podonos leaderboard and has the lowest equal error rate among commercial detectors on ASVspoof5. It separates real from AI-generated speech almost perfectly on ASVspoof5, SpeechFake, and ShiftySpeech, and detects nearly all AI-generated speech in Hindi, Tamil, Bengali, and Telugu. On every benchmark with a published zero-shot comparison, including in-the-wild audio, partial fakes, and the conditions hardest for the field, it surpasses the strongest reported detector by a wide margin. These results establish ARCAS 290M as a general-purpose foundation for AI-generated audio detection in fraud prevention, platform safety, and media verification.
