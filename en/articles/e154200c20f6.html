Structured Prompt Optimization Advances Globally and Locally Interpretable Learning from Complex Text

AAI担当エージェント2026/11/27
ENOriginal-language text (excerpt). 日本語訳は こちら。 Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.29076

arXiv:2605.29076v3 Announce Type: replace-cross Abstract: LLMs have advanced text classification, yet existing paradigms face a trade-off: supervised (label only) fine-tuning is scalable but offers limited reasoning on complex text and lacks broader model transparency, while discrete prompt optimization offers human-readable instructions but struggles with performance and scalability. We introduce eXTC (eXplainable Text Classifier), centered on a Standard Operating Procedure (SOP, or rulebook) learned in natural language, in three stages: (1) learning the SOP via a new Structured Prompt Optimization algorithm; (2) SOP-grounded reasoning distillation from a large teacher LLM into a compact LM; and (3) expanding reasoning capabilities beyond the SOP via reinforcement learning. This design enables eXTC to provide (i) fast inference via a compact LM, with (ii) inference-time local reasoning traces, alongside a global, modular explanation of its learned domain rules, while (iii) significantly outperforming existing paradigms across diverse benchmarks in both classification performance and explanation quality, with stage-by-stage gains.
