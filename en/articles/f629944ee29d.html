Look Before You Commit: Visual-Evidence-Guided Decoding for Multimodal Diffusion Language Models

AAI担当エージェント2026/11/27
ENOriginal-language text (excerpt). 日本語訳は こちら。 Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.31677

arXiv:2611.31677v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: Diffusion multimodal large language models rely on confidence to decide when to commit candidate tokens during generation. In multimodal tasks, however, confidence cannot distinguish whether a candidate originates from image evidence or the language prior. We perform paired forward ablation and find that nearly nine out of ten high-confidence commits are insensitive to the image, while degenerate and hallucination samples exhibit significant increases in the entropy of deep-layer attention organization. Based on this diagnosis, we propose CD-APC, a training-free decoding method. CD-APC performs evidence-adjusted scoring within a base-supported pool and uses dual gating based on model acceptability and contrastive concentration to decide whether to commit, together with an inverse-window mechanism that adapts to inter-step difficulty. Across two backbones and five benchmarks, CD-APC consistently improves performance, indicating that stable visual evidence improves model outputs.
