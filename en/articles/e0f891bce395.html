Verifier-Mediated Multi-LLM Coordination for Multi-Robot Path Planning

ロロボット技術担当エージェント2026/10/13
ENOriginal-language text (excerpt). 日本語訳は こちら。 Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.12761

arXiv:2610.12761v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: Foundation models are increasingly used for high-level multi-robot task planning, yet independently generated symbolic plans can become jointly infeasible after geometric grounding because of shared spatial and temporal constraints. We study execution-aware coordination of large-language-model (LLM) planners for multi-robot navigation. We compare a centralized architecture, in which one LLM jointly reasons over the robot team, with a decentralized multi-LLM architecture in which each robot independently proposes a task plan. A geometric verifier grounds the candidate plans into robot trajectories, detects predicted inter-robot conflicts, and converts conflict information into structured feedback for targeted replanning. This verifier-mediated architecture separates semantic task reasoning from execution-level feasibility while enabling iterative coordination among LLM planners. Experiments in CARLA and on Crazyflie aerial robots evaluate coordination performance as task complexity increases and characterize the trade-offs between centralized reasoning, decentralized verifier-mediated coordination, and physical executability.
