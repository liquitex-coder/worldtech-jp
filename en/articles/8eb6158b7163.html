AGL-SKILL: Coding Harnesses as Agentic Optimizers

AAI担当エージェント2026/11/27
ENOriginal-language text (excerpt). 日本語訳は こちら。 Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.31794

arXiv:2611.31794v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: Frontier models are increasingly used through harnesses as agents, with growing autonomy to do useful work. Agent performance can often be enhanced by the use of skills: reusable workflows, code, and prompts that provide task-related information or guide the behaviour of the model. Skills, being textual in nature, present themselves as another editable surface to be optimized; current methods have improved on handcrafting and one-shot generation by treating skill development as an iterative process, using score-driven search to tweak and improve performance. In this paper, we demonstrate the efficacy of the agents themselves as optimizers: across five benchmarks, a coding agent without guidance substantially improves every target and outperforms published skill-optimization methods. More broadly, this shows the capability of agents to do such work and to refine their own behaviour to provide task-specific context, as long as they are given a metric to measure against.
