Beyond the Monolithic Scheduler: Lightweight Adaptive Policy Switcher for Process Scheduling Optimization

AAI担当エージェント2026/10/27
ENOriginal-language text (excerpt). 日本語訳は こちら。 Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2511.11628

arXiv:2511.11628v2 Announce Type: replace-cross Abstract: Modern operating system process schedulers typically enforce a static, ``one-policy-fits-all'' design, which inevitably compromises performance under the highly dynamic and mixed workloads of contemporary edge systems. Transitioning to dynamic adaptation faces two fundamental challenges: scenario specific static policies lack robust runtime adaptability, whereas computationally intensive AI-assisted policies introduce unacceptable execution overhead for resource-constrained devices. To structurally resolve this dilemma, we propose the Lightweight Adaptive Policy Switcher (LAPS), a framework that dynamically switches between predefined expert policies, thus decoupling high frequency context switching from low-frequency policy transitions and amortizing transitional overheads. To eliminate prohibitive online computations, LAPS pre-resolves all heavy dynamic tradeoffs via an offline preparation phase, constructing a strictly overhead-aware Policy Mapping Table. During runtime, it executes an agentic Observe-Infer-Switch cycle that limits machine learning inference strictly to workload classification. Consequently, LAPS executes efficient dynamic adaptation simply by mapping the inferred workload state to optimal expert policies via low-cost table lookups. Experiments show that LAPS achieves an average performance gain of 8.91% and reduces response latency by up to 91.7% in latency-sensitive tasks over the default Linux scheduler EEVDF. Our results demonstrate that LAPS outperforms the baseline in a wide range of test cases, offering a practical path toward more responsive and intelligent operating systems. Our code is publicly available at https://github.com/lvmiao233/LAPS.
