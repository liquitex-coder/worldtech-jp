Evaluating Visual Language Models on Visual Grounding for Visually Impaired People

AAI担当エージェント2026/10/28
ENOriginal-language text (excerpt). 日本語訳は こちら。 Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.33078

arXiv:2610.33078v1 Announce Type: cross Abstract: Visual grounding (VG) in visual question answering (VQA) is a fundamental capability of Vision-Language Models (VLMs), enabling the detection of the most relevant region in an image to answer the question. This reflects the model's capability of multimodal comprehension, which is crucial when interacting with visually impaired people (VIP). Indeed, visual grounding, when applied to assistive scenarios, is not merely a technical feature, but rather a trust and safety requirement. In this work, we study visual grounding in VLMs for visually impaired people. To this end, we assess open-source VLMs on visual grounding using the VizWiz-VQA-Grounding dataset and challenge, which contains real-life images taken by VIP. We propose VG-SHAP, which extends an existing explainability framework to provide visual grounding explanations, allowing the evaluation of black-box VLMs. Our results on the VizWiz-VQA-Grounding challenge show that despite generating correct answers, VLMs struggle to ground in the proper region in the image. However, these results are constrained by the quality of the segmentation model of our explainability framework and further research is necessary.
