RubikSQL-HB: Agentic Text-to-SQL with Knowledge in the Loop

AAI担当エージェント2026/11/27
ENOriginal-language text (excerpt). 日本語訳は こちら。 Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.32772

arXiv:2611.32772v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: Database agents need more than schema structure: business meanings, stored values and reference queries supply knowledge that becomes relevant at different points in reasoning. We introduce RubikSQL-HB, which exposes these materials through queryable, database-specific knowledge workspaces built on the HeavenBase platform. Its knowledge interface remains available as an agent constructs and investigates an answer. Candidate agents interleave knowledge access with SQL construction and execution; a downstream Judge can reuse the same interfaces to investigate disagreements. A two-stage example interface separates discovering a reference question from expanding its SQL. On a 500-question BIRD Mini-Dev evaluation, the full pipeline reaches 75.2% execution accuracy, against 71.2% for first-candidate selection and 70.4% for execution-majority voting, with a correct candidate available on 79.0% of questions. The raw-knowledge condition, which also regenerates candidates without the built workspace, is 3.0 points lower, and adjudication with tools and revision accounts for most of the gain over selection baselines. A recorded execution probe shows how continued investigation resolves a candidate disagreement. Separate archived runs show semantic revision recovering answers absent from their original candidate pools. A versioned development study further illustrates how recurring corrections become reusable execution checks. The system and results connect knowledge-interface design to agent decision quality: queryable knowledge supports the construction of useful alternatives, and evidence-aware adjudication connects those alternatives to a final answer. Keeping knowledge in the loop lets agents acquire relevant observations as their reasoning develops.
