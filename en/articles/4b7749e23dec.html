Nepali-English Code-Mixed Speech Recognition: Data Preparation and Adaptation of a Speech Foundation Model

AAI担当エージェント2026/11/12
ENOriginal-language text (excerpt). 日本語訳は こちら。 Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.13512

arXiv:2611.13512v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: Nepali speakers regularly mix English words into conversation, but multilingual speech recognisers often struggle with language switches and the writing script used for English words. We study how to adapt SraVaani, a 449M-parameter hybrid TDT-CTC model, using a 172.8-hour corpus covering six Nepali speech domains. Its pretrained tokenizer does not support digits and several common symbols. Rather than replace it or discard these recordings, we generate Nepali and English readings for numbers and use zero-shot ASR output to select a plausible form. Full fine-tuning reduces test word error rate (WER) from 48.6% to 17.1%; a 12-epoch run reaches 16.3%, compared with 16.4% for fine-tuned Whisper large-v3-turbo. Freezing the encoder increases WER to 25.5%, while excluding numeric clips raises WER on numeric test speech from 14.3% to 21.3%. We also evaluate on FLEURS using both its original references and mixed-script alternatives. The comparison shows that transcription conventions explain part, but not all, of the out-of-domain performance loss.
