AI Buckingham: Agentic Discovery of Dimensionless Scaling Laws Across Physical Regimes

AAI担当エージェント2026/11/28
ENOriginal-language text (excerpt). 日本語訳は こちら。 Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.33961

arXiv:2611.33961v1 Announce Type: cross Abstract: Classical dimensional analysis defines admissible dimensionless representations but does not determine which combinations capture the dominant physical dependence, whether it changes across physical regimes, or when dependent-variable rescaling is required. We present AI Buckingham, an agentic framework for autonomous dimensionless discovery from natural-language descriptions of physical problems and observational data. It couples reasoning and planning by a large language model (LLM) with predefined symbolic and numerical tools. Literature-based knowledge retrieval supports dimensional checking, gradient-based clustering identifies physical regimes, and mutual information provides an objective, reparameterization-invariant criterion for identifying dominant dimensionless groups. Explicit numerical criteria determine the group count and assess simplification. Across ten physical systems spanning fluid dynamics, magnetohydrodynamics, thermal convection and additive manufacturing, AI Buckingham recovers established similarity representations and resolves regime-dependent scalings. For the longstanding challenge of predicting turbulent drag on rough surfaces, it identifies informative reparameterizations of surface statistics that improve equivalent sand-grain roughness estimates for engineering-oriented drag modelling.
