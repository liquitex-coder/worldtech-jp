Movie2Game-Bench: Holistic Evaluation of Film-to-Game Adaptation Beyond Playability

AAI担当エージェント2026/11/21
ENOriginal-language text (excerpt). 日本語訳は こちら。 Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.24669

arXiv:2611.24669v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: Adapting a film into a game requires selecting source elements and translating them into player actions, rules, and feedback. We introduce Movie2Game-Bench (M2G-bench), a benchmark for evaluating film-to-game adaptation beyond playability. M2G-bench combines general evaluation, movie-specific evaluation, and playtesting by three GUI model players. The protocol connects source content to gameplay steps and implementation evidence, capturing both content preservation and interactive realization. The benchmark comprises 22 films and 658 source-specific reference items. Evaluating 14 models on the same films with their selected primary harnesses reveals complementary strengths in game quality, adaptation, and player experience. A shared weakness concerns playable sequences: this category has the lowest content-presence mean in all 14 models, averaging 37.9 out of 100 compared with 54.8 for iconic elements. Among judgments of retained emotionaldevelopment content, narrative choices account for 60.0% and scenario operation for 7.2%. These findings identify two linked priorities for film-to-game adaptation: preserving source situations that can support gameplay and connecting retained content to player actions and consequences.
