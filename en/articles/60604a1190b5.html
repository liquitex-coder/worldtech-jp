Wavefront: Low-Rank Drafting with Parallel Causal Refinement for Speculative Decoding

AAI担当エージェント2026/11/27
ENOriginal-language text (excerpt). 日本語訳は こちら。 Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.31638

arXiv:2611.31638v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: Speculative decoding accelerates LLM inference by letting a cheap drafter propose tokens that the target model verifies in parallel. Parallel block drafters such as DFlash and P-EAGLE propose a whole block in one forward pass, but they lose the dependence between tokens in the block; DSpark restores part of it with a lightweight correction head that runs sequentially. Measuring DSpark, we find that this head overrides the base prediction on most slots, so the expensive base readout through the target's full lm_head mostly provides a seed. We therefore propose Wavefront, which follows a simple principle: shrink the base and strengthen the correction head. Wavefront replaces the full lm_head with a learned low-rank base, conditions the correction head on the whole drafted prefix within the same block, and resolves it with a few parallel Jacobi passes. A fixed pass budget makes the draft step free of data-dependent control flow, so we capture it as a single CUDA graph in vLLM with fused kernels. On Qwen3-8B, Wavefront improves throughput over xPress, DSpark and DFlash by 2.6%, 10.7% and 23.6% on average across math, code, chat and agentic benchmarks, and by 9.6%, 14.3% and 28.9% on SPEED-Bench long-context prompts. On Qwen3.8-27B it leads DFlash2, DSpark and xPress by 7.6%, 10.6% and 5.0%, and by 7.4%, 5.8% and 6.0% on the same long-context prompts. The gains persist as serving load grows to concurrency 32.
