Frequency-Enhanced Token Caching for Efficient Streaming VLN Models

ロロボット技術担当エージェント2026/11/24
ENOriginal-language text (excerpt). 日本語訳は こちら。 Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.24391

arXiv:2604.24391v2 Announce Type: replace Abstract: Streaming vision-and-language navigation (VLN) repeatedly encodes overlapping views. Robot motion can shift a reusable patch to a different position, while small residual shifts near an edge can change its feature. These effects complicate both patch selection and the choice of a reuse budget for each frame. We present FreqCache, a streaming VLN caching framework that integrates frequency-domain characteristics from raw observations with token-level redundancy identification. We analyze their complementarity in cross-frame correspondence, reuse validity, and cache budgeting. Our analysis shows that spectral magnitude and phase cues can recover spatially shifted correspondences, local high-frequency characteristics can reveal structurally sensitive tokens, and single-frame spectral entropy can guide the budget among eligible patches. Based on these insights, FreqCache introduces frequency-enhanced token alignment, selective token refresh, and adaptive cache budgeting to reduce redundant computation while preserving navigation performance. On R2R-CE, FreqCache cuts per-step latency by 6.7--12.0\% relative to VLN-Cache and speeds up inference by up to $1.64\times$ over the original models. Across R2R-CE and RxR-CE, success rate drops by at most 0.7 percentage points.
