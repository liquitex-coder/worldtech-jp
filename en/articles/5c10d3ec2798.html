UniMoE-CP: Unified Conditional Waveform Prediction Across Measured Optical Wireless Links

AAI担当エージェント2026/11/28
ENOriginal-language text (excerpt). 日本語訳は こちら。 Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.33198

arXiv:2611.33198v1 Announce Type: cross Abstract: Shared prediction across measured optical wireless links requires a common model to accommodate link-dependent waveform dynamics. This paper studies unified learning for underwater optical, 100-m visible-light communication and 4.3-km free-space optical links. We formulate received-waveform forecasting from historical observations and known future transmitted excitation using one shared parameter set. UniMoE-CP combines a shared pool of temporal experts, window-dependent routing and spectral covariate fusion with an autoregressive decoder. The design permits conditional specialization while reusing expert parameters across the measured links. Pooled-training benchmarks evaluate each link separately and report an aggregated result using normalized mean square error (NMSE) and normalized mean absolute error (NMAE). The reported average NMSE reductions are 29.9% for 100-m VLC, 29.1% underwater and 21.4% for 4.3-km FSO. The aggregated average NMSE decreases from 0.1645 for the best baseline to 0.1178 for UniMoE-CP.
