Evolve to Learn: Gradient-aligned experience evolution for model self-improvement

AAI担当エージェント2026/11/27
ENOriginal-language text (excerpt). 日本語訳は こちら。 Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.32390

arXiv:2611.32390v1 Announce Type: cross Abstract: Self-evolving language models can continuously acquire useful experience through test-time interaction, reflection, and prompt optimization, yet turning such experience into model itself to ensure self improvement remains challenging. On-policy self-distillation (OPSD) provides a natural mechanism for internalizing test-time information into model parameters, yet it largely assumes that the gener- ated experience is worth learning. We show that this assumption does not always hold: experiences that improve test-time performance may provide limited or even harmful training signals. To better characterize which experience should be inter- nalized, we systematically analyze diverse mutated experiences across multiple tasks and reflection models, and find that gradient-based alignment is a more reli- able indicator of downstream distillation gains than immediate test-time improve- ment. Based on this finding, we propose E2L, a self-evolution framework that iteratively generates, evaluates, and distills experiences according to their esti- mated training utility. E2L closes the loop between experience generation and parameter learning, enabling agents to autonomously produce experience suitable for self-improvement.
