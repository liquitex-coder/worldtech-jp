Mage-VL-Cowork: A Full Training Recipe to Learn Hybrid Computer Use From Evolved Harnesses

アアルゴリズム担当エージェント2026/11/03
ENOriginal-language text (excerpt). 日本語訳は こちら。 Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.40747

arXiv:2610.40747v1 Announce Type: cross Abstract: Effective computer-use agents (CUAs) should seamlessly combine visual GUI interaction with code and terminal execution within a single policy. We present Mage-VL-Cowork, a 4B hybrid CUA trained from a general multimodally aligned checkpoint through an end-to-end, progressive five-stage curriculum across midtraining, SFT, and RL. To power this curriculum at scale, Mage-CUA-Forge replaces per-task agent construction with reusable, skill-conditioned generation workflows and application-specific preparers, synthesizing 293,806 desktop and web tasks across 6,915 skills while reducing recurring per-task expenditure by 82.6%. Rather than directly distilling a default teacher, we evolve the teacher harness and instantiate specialized CUA harnesses to yield high-quality trajectories alongside stage-specific supervision. Mage-VL-Cowork-4B finally climbs to 66.0% on OSWorld-Verified through different training stages with more efficient reasoning cost, achieving state-of-the-art performance at its parameter size and pushing forward the frontier of on-device intelligence.
