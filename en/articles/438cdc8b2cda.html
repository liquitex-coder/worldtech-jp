Self-organizing Architecture of Receptron Units: a Hardware-Aware Framework for Edge Intelligence

アアルゴリズム担当エージェント2026/07/24
ENOriginal-language text (excerpt). 日本語訳は こちら。 Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.20162

arXiv:2607.20162v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: The growing demand for intelligent processing at the edge of IoT networks is constrained by the severe computational and memory limitations of microcontroller units, which render impractical conventional deep learning approaches. We propose a neuromorphicinspired classifier based on the Receptron model, a single-unit architecture capable of implementing non-linearly separable decision boundaries, without resorting to multi-layer networks. The model is designed for direct deployment on mid-range MCUs, while supporting continuous on-device adaptation. Experimental evaluation on basic dataset benchmarks yields cross-validated accuracies compatible with standard machine learning method baselines. These results position the Receptron as a viable and interpretable alternative for resource-constrained neuromorphic edge systems operating in dynamic, non-stationary environments.
