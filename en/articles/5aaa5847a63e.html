Designing and Validating a Self-Aligning Tool Changer for Modular Reconfigurable Manipulation Robots

ロロボット技術担当エージェント2026/11/19
ENOriginal-language text (excerpt). 日本語訳は こちら。 Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.04760

arXiv:2603.04760v2 Announce Type: replace Abstract: Modular reconfigurable robots require reliable mechanisms for automated tool exchange, but conventional rigid active couplings often fail due to inevitable positioning and orientational errors. To address this, we propose a misalignment-tolerant tool changer. The tool changer consists of an insert part on the robot side, which contains a servo-driven cam and lock-pin, and a receptacle part on the tool side, which has an isosceles triangular lead-in guide and chamfered walls. During insertion, these geometries generate angled contact forces that passively align the two parts, so the tool changer compensates for angular and lateral misalignments without force sensors. A motor-driven rotating tool exchange station stores the tools and rotates to bring the selected tool to the exchange position. Experiments confirmed that, with the current guide and chamfer geometry, the tool changer tolerates rotational offsets of $ \pm$ 20 degrees and lateral offsets of up to 7 mm.
