Causal Modelling of Support Interventions for Student Competency Assessment

AAI担当エージェント2026/08/27
ENOriginal-language text (excerpt). 日本語訳は こちら。 Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.24632

arXiv:2608.24632v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: Accurate assessment of student competencies is essential for enabling educators to identify individual needs, design targeted interventions, and evaluate the effectiveness of educational strategies. Empirical assessment procedures are typically grounded in psychometric models, such as item response theory, which relate student competence levels to performance on assessment tasks. In this paper, we advocate adopting a structural causal modelling approach to educational assessment, moving beyond probabilistic belief updating toward a framework that explicitly supports interventional and counterfactual reasoning. We propose a corresponding protocol for its construction and analyse the practical relevance of forms of reasoning that remain inaccessible to standard associative models, including the explicit modelling of interventions such as hints and the related counterfactual scenario analysis. Although our protocol requires the structural equations to be elicited from experts, the necessary information is purely logical and does not rely on probabilistic, less tenable assumptions. We illustrate the approach using data from an assessment that employs complex tasks designed to measure compulsory school student algorithmic skills.
