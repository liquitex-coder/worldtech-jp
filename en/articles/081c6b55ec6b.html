Cooperative Gradient Estimation for Multi-Robot Source Seeking with Heteroskedastic Measurements

ロロボット技術担当エージェント2026/11/19
ENOriginal-language text (excerpt). 日本語訳は こちら。 Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.19535

arXiv:2611.19535v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: This paper proposes a novel approach for localizing the source of a three-dimensional scalar field using a team of heterogeneous cooperative mobile robots. Each robot measures the field intensity at its location and shares the information within the team. Measurement noise is explicitly considered, and heterogeneity arises from robot-dependent sensing accuracies, leading to heteroskedastic data. The proposed framework combines a weighted centroidal Voronoi tessellation-based formation strategy, defined on a spherical surface and parameterized by sensor noise variances, with a Weighted Least Squares estimator to compute the field gradient at the formation center. By explicitly accounting for sensor heterogeneity in both formation design and estimation, the method achieves robust gradient estimation under noisy measurements. In particular, we show analytically that this construction is nearly optimal. The covariance of the gradient estimate is derived in closed form, providing a lower bound on the attainable estimation variance for any formation on the sphere, and the proposed tessellation is shown to asymptotically satisfy the conditions under which this bound is attained. The estimated gradient is then exploited within a gradient-ascent strategy to guide the team toward the source location. Simulation results demonstrate the effectiveness of the proposed approach compared to alternative strategies and highlight its intrinsic ability to reconfigure online in response to robot failures, thereby enhancing its adaptability.
