A recursive self-improvement framework for electron microscopy analysis

AAI担当エージェント2026/11/27
ENOriginal-language text (excerpt). 日本語訳は こちら。 Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.32325

arXiv:2611.32325v1 Announce Type: cross Abstract: Resolving local atomic structures such as defects and interfaces from electron microscopy contrast relies heavily on researcher judgement, leading to low efficiency and subjective standards. Here we present PhyEvo-Mic-RSI, a physics-guided and expert-informed recursive self-improvement framework that turns one-off human corrections into reusable, verifiable capabilities by updating executable operators, parameters and routing rules with versioned inheritance. Given researcher-defined tasks, the system selects analysis paths from image features without expert labels, while testing and inheriting code updates under fixed budgets. Combining physical priors with expert feedback improves atomic point-defect detection over larger models and raises cross-material interface coverage from 5.13% to 72.11% within a 10% area budget. Adapting the framework to micrographs from unseen studies further separates touching nanoparticles by aligning optimization with particle-level measurements. This work moves computational microscopy beyond standalone models and static scripts towards self-evolving platforms that accumulate human scientific expertise
