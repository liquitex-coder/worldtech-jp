Dispersive Tree Agreements for Multi-Robot Coordination

ロロボット技術担当エージェント2026/11/03
ENOriginal-language text (excerpt). 日本語訳は こちら。 Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.31540

arXiv:2610.31540v2 Announce Type: replace Abstract: Multi-robot coordination of nonlinear systems is naturally formulated as joint-space optimal control subject to joint constraints. Existing methods that approach joint optimality remain largely centralized and offline, while decentralized, real-time counterparts target safe navigation without pursuing the joint optimum. This paper develops a decentralized, real-time planner that approaches the joint optimum as an anytime process. We extend a recent single-robot framework that searches dispersive trees of forward-propagated commands to coordinate a multi-robot team near-optimally. We first show that the inter-robot coupling can be effectively replaced by some local constraints, under which locally grown dispersive trees contain a near-optimal agreement. It is this structure that the robots pursue by a negotiation driven by decentralized column generation. In the quiescent limit, this negotiation is shown to settle on a near-optimal agreement. Our real-time protocol, WarpWeaver, runs the negotiation asynchronously over a receding horizon and closes on quiesced agreements even when deadlines cut it short. In simulation, WarpWeaver reduces total travel time by up to 22% against centralized offline planners on the Dynobench suite and by up to 16% against a decentralized real-time baseline, and outperforms a task-specific planner on networked navigation. Hardware experiments on six robots planning onboard demonstrate real-world implementability.
