Colombo: A Frontier-to-Frontier Autoresearch System for Oracle-Based Optimization

AAI担当エージェント2026/11/20
ENOriginal-language text (excerpt). 日本語訳は こちら。 Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.23080

arXiv:2611.23080v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: Science advances from frontier to frontier as discoveries reshape what is known and create the basis for further discovery. We present Colombo, a self-organizing AI researcher that drives this renewal, first instantiated in oracle-based optimization. Here, the central question is how efficiently a problem class can be solved through a prescribed information interface and what costs no admissible algorithm can avoid. The gap between achievable guarantees and unavoidable limits defines the frontier recorded in its Complexity Atlas, a living map of frontier settings and the current state of knowledge. Self-Organization turns unresolved gaps into adaptive research programmes, coordinating conjecture formation, mathematical investigation, and replanning as evidence accumulates. Adversarial Co-Ascent develops algorithms and hard families through reciprocal challenge, exposing mechanisms of information use and concealment that guide stronger methods and proofs of unavoidable costs. Independent review and Lean-based formal certification check mathematical claims, while the Long-Term Memory carries supported findings and research experience across settings. To date, Colombo has established optimal or near-optimal rates for more than fifty settings in the Atlas. Colombo thus takes a concrete step toward Autopoietic Science: a scientific process whose validated advances regenerate the conditions of its own progress, continually reshaping how inquiry is organized and what it can discover.
