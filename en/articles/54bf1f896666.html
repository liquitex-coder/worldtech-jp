R3D: Quantitative 3D Spatial Reasoning for Egocentric Wearables

AAI担当エージェント2026/11/24
ENOriginal-language text (excerpt). 日本語訳は こちら。 Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.02921

arXiv:2607.02921v2 Announce Type: replace-cross Abstract: Quantitative 3D spatial reasoning from egocentric RGB-D video is an important capability for next-generation wearable assistants. Yet existing benchmarks do not reflect the challenges of handling (1) natural egocentric video, (2) posed RGB-D video inputs, and (3) challenging quantitative 3D spatial reasoning Q&A. To fill this gap, we introduce R3D-Bench (Reasoning in 3D), a benchmark of 2,950 quantitative spatial reasoning questions across 15 types--spanning multiple-choice, distance-based, and volumetric reasoning questions--built on top of 57 egocentric video sequences from Aria Digital Twin. To set a strong baseline on this dataset, we introduce R3D, a model-agnostic spatial tool-calling framework. In contrast to existing approaches that directly embed 3D information into the model's input representation, R3D constructs a 3D scene from video using segmentation and depth-lifted object representations. It provides this information to an LLM through seven composable spatial tools. On R3D-Bench, R3D with Qwen3.6 35B-A3B outperforms all baselines $<$100B parameters, achieving 55.6% mean relative accuracy, a substantial increase over the second-best approach (CuTR+Tools, 47.7%). In the $\ge$100B parameter domain, our R3D Qwen3-VL 235B-A22B Thinking variant improves upon its base model by 7.7% absolute, achieving 57.0% accuracy and outperforming all open- and closed-source models except closed-source GPT-5.5 (59.1%), with which it remains competitive. We publicly release both R3D-Bench and our full implementation.
