Exploring Daily Emotional Well-Being through Personalized Data Analysis

アアルゴリズム担当エージェント2026/11/20
ENOriginal-language text (excerpt). 日本語訳は こちら。 Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.24342

arXiv:2611.24342v1 Announce Type: cross Abstract: This project analysed daily emotional well-being from a personalized perspective using records of emotional states and contextual information derived from daily life. It was based on the idea that well-being is not a fixed value, but varies from day to day, and can be better understood in relation to individual context and behaviour. The work is framed within the field of Data Science and focuses on emotional states at an individual level. The project focused on constructing an integrated daily dataset from multiple personal data sources and analysing emotional states. The distributions of emotional variables, their temporal evolution and the differences between users were studied, together with their variability over time. Personality and well-being questionnaires were also incorporated as stable references to better contextualize daily emotional results. A simple personalized recommendation approach was then developed by comparing similar days within each user, exploring how past contextual conditions relate to differences in the user's emotional state. Finally, machine learning techniques were used to evaluate whether daily contextual information contains signals that help distinguish between different types of days relative to a person's usual well-being. The project adopted an applied approach, with an exploratory scope focused on understanding patterns rather than building a fully predictive system. The results showed that emotional well-being is very personal and changes from day to day. General models had limited value, while personalized approaches captured these differences much better, highlighting the importance of analysing each person on their own terms.
