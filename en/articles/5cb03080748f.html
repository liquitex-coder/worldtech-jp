EDAGoat: An EDA-Aware Agent for Project-Level GPU Optimization

AAI担当エージェント2026/11/24
ENOriginal-language text (excerpt). 日本語訳は こちら。 Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.27196

arXiv:2611.27196v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: GPU acceleration has transformed electronic design automation (EDA), yet further improving expert-engineered GPU tools still demands substantial manual effort. Automating this work calls for coding agents that can trace bottlenecks across host and device code while preserving correctness and design quality. We introduce EDAGoat, to our knowledge the first EDA-aware GPU optimization agent for this task. Its optimization enhancement (OE) mode guides edits with four cross-project GPU optimization patterns. Its mandatory validation (MV) mode requires the agent to test the edited program against the EDA constraints its changes could affect. Our benchmark covers five GPU EDA projects across four task families and 55 circuit inputs, measuring complete-solve runtime and verifying the resulting designs. Each configuration runs three independent searches per project. A search qualifies only if its solver passes correctness and design-quality checks on all public and hidden inputs. With DeepSeek, generic prompting qualifies in 7 of 15 searches and OE in 6 of 15, despite a higher best speedup on four projects. Adding MV raises this to 13 of 15 and, on every project, exceeds both generic baselines in mean and best speedup and OE in mean speedup; the geometric mean of per-project mean speedups rises from 1.11x (1.36x with OE) to 1.53x. With GPT-6 Astra, EDAGoat qualifies in all 15 searches and improves both speedup metrics over generic prompting on every project, lifting the same geometric mean from 1.26x to 1.57x. These results show that agents can use EDA knowledge to find further speedups in expert-tuned tools while preserving design correctness and quality, establishing EDA-aware GPU optimization as a new direction for agent research.
