F$^3$: What Makes Good Experience in World-Action Models?

AAI担当エージェント2026/11/21
ENOriginal-language text (excerpt). 日本語訳は こちら。 Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.24708

arXiv:2611.24708v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: Deployed world-action models (WAMs) can keep improving only if they make good use of their own experience. Existing methods supply retrieved rollouts or demonstrations to the policy as context, yet such experience often turns previously solved tasks into failures. What, then, makes good experience for a WAM? We show that experience is valuable only in use: its value depends not on whether the source rollout succeeded but on what is stored, when it is used, and where it enters the policy. We therefore propose F$^3$, which makes these three decisions with Form, Fit, and Fusion. Form compresses successful and failed rollouts alike into short, verified operation records; Fit teaches the policy to read a record and to predict when it helps; Fusion injects the record's images, text and actions into the policy as a prefix and an action prior. Trained once on only 200 examples and deployed with a growing experience library, the model achieves state-of-the-art results on MolmoSpaces and RoboLab without any training on RoboLab: over native Cosmos 3 Nano, it raises success by 12.0 pp and 10.8 pp, reduces steps to success by 12.2% and 11.3%, and cuts regressions by 57%.
