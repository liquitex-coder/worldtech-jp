ShellBench: What Matters More for AI Agents, Models, or Harness for Everyday Tasks

AAI担当エージェント2026/11/27
ENOriginal-language text (excerpt). 日本語訳は こちら。 Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.32946

arXiv:2611.32946v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: The use of AI agents among the general public has been increasing exponentially throughout 2026, though it is often hard for users to determine which configuration is most effective for their personal use. To help provide clarity here we introduce ShellBench, 100 day-to-day tasks based on more than 10 million user requests, each set in a realistic workspace with messy files and competing data sources, and graded for both correctness and safety. We evaluate 12 models across 5 agent harnesses at 3 reasoning levels for a total of 53,100 rollouts, 20% of which are on tasks designated for public release. We find that model choice impacts performance more than harness choice, but the three leading models differ by only 1.1 percentage points. GPT-6.1 Sol passed 61.4% of attempts at about one-sixth of GPT-6 Astra's estimated token cost per attempt, while GPT-6 Astra led at 62.5%. We also found that harness choice had little effect on either performance or safety with all harnesses scoring between 42.9 (Claude Code) and 45.2 (OpenClaw) for pass@1.
