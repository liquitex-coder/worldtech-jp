A Generative AI Agent to Emulate Interactive Human-driven Vehicles for Automated Driving Testing in Urban Areas

AAI担当エージェント2026/11/27
ENOriginal-language text (excerpt). 日本語訳は こちら。 Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.31797

arXiv:2611.31797v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: Realistic modeling of human-driven vehicle (HDV) interactions is essential for automated driving safety testing in urban areas. Existing methods struggle to reproduce realistic safety-critical interactions involving high collision risk for the following reasons: they often assume full observability, overlooking interactions arising from occlusion. Driving styles are mainly characterized by vehicle maneuvers, limiting the diversity of safety-critical interaction behaviors. Moreover, learning signals from safety-critical interactions can be diluted by non-critical driving behaviors. To address these issues, we propose a generative AI agent to emulate interactive HDVs. The agent incorporates field-of-view modeling to account for driver visibility under occlusion and characterizes the interaction process using five metrics spanning its initial, intermediate, and final stages. It automatically learns diverse interaction styles through a dynamic mixture-of-experts network. Through residual reinforcement learning, the agent further refines generated trajectories to match the real-world distributions of safety-critical interaction characteristics. Experiments on the INTERACTION dataset show that our method reduces Jensen--Shannon divergence (JSD) by 39.9\%--87.1\% across five interaction characteristics compared with a state-of-the-art baseline. Our method achieves greater improvements of up to 87.1\% in final-stage interaction metrics, suggesting that it can reduce error accumulation in long-horizon trajectory generation. Ablation studies confirm the effectiveness of each proposed component, particularly highlighting the importance of residual refinement. Generalization experiments show that our method approaches the performance of full training on unseen intersections with only one epoch of fine-tuning.
