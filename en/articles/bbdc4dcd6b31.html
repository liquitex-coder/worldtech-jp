DeepOpen: Decision-Oriented Intent Classification on BANKING77 and CLINC150

AAI担当エージェント2026/10/27
ENOriginal-language text (excerpt). 日本語訳は こちら。 Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.31104

arXiv:2610.31104v2 Announce Type: replace Abstract: We present DeepOpen, a decision-Oriented intent-classification framework with dataset- specific systems for BANKING77 and CLINC150. On BANKING77, a single English classi- fier continued with sharpness-aware minimization (SAM) achieves 94.1234% test accuracy (2,899/3,080). This is the best observed result among three fixed SAM seeds, selected for display after test evaluation; their mean is 94.0368%. On the 150 in-scope CLINC150 classes, probability fusion of one Laya specialist and three DeBERTa-v3-large classifiers achieves 98.0222% (4,411/4,500). We describe the two configurations, reproduce the reference com- parisons displayed in the accompanying evaluation explorer, and separate headline scores from evidence about improvement. Under a uniform BANKING77 evaluation path, the SAM mean exceeds the historical checkpoint by 0.0433 percentage points, with a shared-row 95% interval of [-0.2273, +0.3247]. Thus, the continuation does not establish a reliable accuracy gain. The report documents strong observed task-specific performance, selection effects, and unresolved historical execution-path differences, rather than a new backbone or a unified algorithmic advantage.
