Spatial Heuristic Strategy based Diffusion Reinforcement Learning for Online 3D Bin Packing

AAI担当エージェント2026/10/27
ENOriginal-language text (excerpt). 日本語訳は こちら。 Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.32241

arXiv:2610.32241v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: Traditional heuristic methods have accumulated mature experience in core objectives, such as space utilization and layout compactness of 3D bin packing, relying on artificially designed rule-based logic. However, they exhibit obvious limitations in generalization ability and adaptability to dynamic scenarios. Diffusion reinforcement learning, which integrates the spatial search capability of reinforcement learning and the data distribution modeling capability of diffusion learning, has achieved certain progress in action distribution modeling and decision compliance constraints. Nevertheless, there remain issues including insufficient perception of the spatial topological structure in 3D bin packing and limited layout optimization capability. To address these problems, we propose a diffusion reinforcement learning method integrated with spatial heuristic strategies, which combines classical bin packing heuristic rules with a diffusion reinforcement learning framework. Specifically, it compresses the action space through heuristic action pre-screening, introduces a graph neural network to model the spatial structure, and designs a composite reward function incorporating flatness to suppress spatial fragmentation, thereby realizing the organic integration of prior knowledge and adaptive optimization.
