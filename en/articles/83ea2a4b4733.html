VC-OPD: Learning to See What Matters via Visual Cue On-Policy Self-Distillation

AAI担当エージェント2026/11/27
ENOriginal-language text (excerpt). 日本語訳は こちら。 Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.32292

arXiv:2611.32292v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: Visual On-Policy Distillation (OPD) commonly uses cropped or zoomed-in image regions as teacher-side privileged information to improve fine-grained perception in small Multimodal Large Language Models (MLLMs). However, local pixel-level cues are insufficient for complex visual reasoning that requires global structure, spatial relationships, and high-level semantics. We propose Visual Cue On-Policy Self-Distillation (VC-OPD), which extends teacher-side privileged information beyond local image regions to question-conditioned visual cues. Advanced MLLMs generate cues capturing key visual details, structural relationships, and semantic evidence needed to answer each question. A multi-stage quality filtering process selects reliable cues, which are provided only to the teacher during training. This enables informative token-level supervision along student-generated trajectories, helping the student learn to identify and reason over critical evidence directly from the original image. No additional information is required at inference time. Since advanced MLLMs are used only for visual cue generation, VC-OPD is compatible with both open-weight and proprietary black-box models. Experiments on STEM and general visual reasoning benchmarks show consistent improvements across model scales over reinforcement learning and on-policy distillation baselines. Notably, a 4B model trained with VC-OPD outperforms many baselines with 8B or more parameters, demonstrating effective capability transfer through question-conditioned visual cues.
