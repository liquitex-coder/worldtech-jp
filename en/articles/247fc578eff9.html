Direct Forcing: Single-Stage Training with Representation-Space Supervision for Few-Step Autoregressive Video Generation

AAI担当エージェント2026/10/28
ENOriginal-language text (excerpt). 日本語訳は こちら。 Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.30861

arXiv:2610.30861v2 Announce Type: replace-cross Abstract: Few-step autoregressive video generation enables low-latency streaming, yet prevailing training pipelines separate causal adaptation from distribution matching. Recent representation-based text-to-video methods retain this separation by starting with an ODE initialization stage, even when the target distribution is defined directly by reference videos. We introduce \method, the first single-stage framework that uses reference videos both as paired examples of how a history continues and as a target population for self-generated rollouts. Specifically, Direct Forcing is trained with two complementary signals: (1) Causal Continuation Alignment learns paired spatiotemporal correspondence under reference histories; and (2) Rollout Distribution Matching employs maximum mean discrepancy to align the representation distributions of self-generated and reference videos. To coordinate these signals, we introduce Dynamic Gradient Balancing, which adapts their relative gradient contributions during training. Experiments with Wan2.1-1.3B show that Direct Forcing learns a four-step autoregressive generator in 250 updates without a separate ODE initialization stage or online diffusion-score supervision, achieving 84.63 VBench Total and 86.01 Quality.
