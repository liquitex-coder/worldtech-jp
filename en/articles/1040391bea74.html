Generative sequence modeling for infinite memory processes via predictive states

アアルゴリズム担当エージェント2026/11/19
ENOriginal-language text (excerpt). 日本語訳は こちら。 Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.38524

arXiv:2609.38524v4 Announce Type: replace-cross Abstract: We consider estimating the one-step-ahead conditional distribution of a multivariate stochastic process. Many existing approaches rely on structural assumptions such as finite-range memory, sparsity, or additivity, which can be poorly suited to processes with long-range nonlinear interactions. However, without such structural assumptions, nonparametric estimation is known to be challenging due to the curse of dimensionality. To address this challenge, we introduce a new estimator based on the idea of learning representations of the \emph{predictive states} of a stochastic process. We theoretically analyze several aspects of this problem. As a bonus, we establish convergence rate guarantees for an instantiation of our method based on deep neural network estimators. Our results suggest that faster convergence rates are achievable when the predictive state space is lower-dimensional. That is, when the past history can be compressed into a lower-dimensional statistic that is sufficient for predicting the future. We implement our method in PyTorch and evaluate its performance on synthetic data and real \emph{C. elegans} data.
