A minimal dynamical model of human-AI cognitive coevolution

AAI担当エージェント2026/11/07
ENOriginal-language text (excerpt). 日本語訳は こちら。 Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.06928

arXiv:2611.06928v1 Announce Type: cross Abstract: The rapid deployment of generative AI raises the question of whether pervasive cognitive offloading drives a measurable, potentially irreversible decline in unassisted human reasoning. We examine this question with a phenomenological dynamical model based on the Cattell-Horn-Carroll distinction between fluid intelligence ($G_f$) and crystallized intelligence ($G_c$). Starting from the discrete-time Bivariate Latent Change Score formalism used in cognitive-aging research, we derive its continuous-time linear counterpart and introduce a fast third variable representing composite machine-intelligence capability. The machine variable enters the human subsystem through Hill-function modifications of the self-feedback and cross-coupling coefficients. We emphasize that our phenomenological model captures both the positive and negative effects of AI on human cognition. By formalizing the qualitative concepts of positive and negative impacts, we introduce definitions for AI-induced cognitive decline and AI-induced cognitive augmentation. A singular-perturbation reduction, justified via Tikhonov's theorem, collapses the resulting three-dimensional system onto a two-dimensional slow manifold. On this manifold we locate two bifurcations of the equilibrium. In one parameter regime, weakening the contribution of fluid reasoning to crystallized knowledge produces a saddle-node bifurcation that eliminates the stable positive equilibrium. In another, reversing the contribution of crystallized knowledge to fluid reasoning creates opposing feedback; increasing fluid self-feedback then reduces damping and produces a supercritical Hopf bifurcation with a stable periodic orbit. We discuss limitations and future directions, interpret our results as a phenomenological account of a coupled socio-technical system, and state the conditions under which each dynamical regime applies.
