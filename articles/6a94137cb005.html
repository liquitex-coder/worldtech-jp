ORIGINAL · ENarXiv:2611.32193v1 Announce Type: cross Abstract: Solar filaments are cool, dense chromospheric plasma structures held by magnetic field lines above polarity inversion lines. Their destabilization triggers coronal mass ejections and severe space weather hazards. Segmenting individual filaments from ground-based GONG H-alpha observations (2048 x 2048 pixels) is challenging due to low contrast, turbulent fibrils, delicate barb structures, and annotator boundary variance. In this technical report, we describe SolarFilNet-V4, a targeted segmentation framework developed for the MAGFiLO 1.0 benchmark (Solar Filament Segmentation Challenge 2026). Our method couples a lightweight Pyramid Vision Transformer (PVT-v2-B1) backbone with anisotropic curvilinear strip convolutions (k=15) and dual-frequency feed-forward blocks (DFFN) to follow extended flux ropes while capturing thin central spines. A shallow full-resolution convolutional branch operating at 1024 x 1024 preserves high-frequency edge details and mitigates transformer downsampling. We train the network using multi-task supervision over masks, centerline spines, barb boundaries, and pixel-contrastive representations. At test time, we apply 8-fold dihedral test-time augmentation (D4), bilinearly upscale predictions to the native 2048 x 2048 grid, and apply an activity-adaptive post-processing routine that calibrates morphological joining parameters for quiet versus active solar disks. On the official benchmark, SolarFilNet-V4 achieves an out-of-fold validation Panoptic Quality of 0.4340 and a Public Leaderboard score of 0.380. The model contains 15.46M parameters and requires 224.34 GFLOPs per forward pass, reducing parameter count by 36.8% compared to standard ResNet-34 U-Net baselines.
