ORIGINAL · ENarXiv:2611.33985v1 Announce Type: cross Abstract: Autoregressive generation over adaptive 3D octrees (Uni-3DAR) offers an intrinsic $O(N \log N)$ sparsity advantage over dense $O(N^2)$ equivariant diffusion and flow models on large drug-like molecules, yet lags in geometric equilibrium, closed-shell valence stability, and property fidelity due to Morton-seam coordinate jumps, leaf-heavy compute with root-count trapping, and target-shortcut collapse in internal critics. We introduce MolGPAO (Molecular Generative Pre-trained Autoregressive Octree), a standalone 3D octree architecture and single-output speculative tree-decoding engine using zero external surrogates, zero reinforcement learning, and zero inference-time MLIP relaxation. MolGPAO unifies: (1) non-trapping 6-D ScaleFreeBudgetEmbedding with L5-to-L1 covalent packing priors; (2) a FlashAttention-2 pause-and-gather speculative octree funnel ($m_{s1} \to m_{s2} \to 1$, $M_{\mathrm{out}} = 1$); (3) an anti-shortcut ground-truth TwoStageOctreeProcessCritic with 64-RBF covalent edge gating and $SO(3)$-invariant 12-channel multipole norms (blind QM9 $r = 0.9867$--$0.9989$); (4) a $<0.5$ ms 4-layer $SE(3)$ PostTreeGraphRefiner for Morton-seam healing, expected-XYZ bonded supervision, selective CFG, and zero-heavy-displacement valence reconciliation; and (5) bubble-free stride-2 GPU leaf decoding. Across GEOM-Drugs (0.49 GPU-h), disjoint 50k/50k QM9 (0.43 GPU-h), and PBE0/def2-TZVP-verified OE62 (0.56 GPU-h), MolGPAO lifts drug-scale valid-connected yield 6.78x (12.7% to 86.2%, 99.83% atom stability), runs 7.3x faster (or 1.65x faster at 2.0x yield) than 100-step FlowMol at $N=100$, beats 156 GPU-h PropMolFlow on QM9 HOMO (-12.7%) and dipole (-21.0%) at 362x lower compute, and doubles live DFT hit rate on OE62 (33.3% vs. 16.7%) at 92.8x lower compute.
