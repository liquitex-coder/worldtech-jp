ORIGINAL · ENarXiv:2611.31463v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: This paper presents a rigorous geometric accessibility and controllability analysis for a fixed-wing Uncrewed Aircraft System (UAS) actuated solely by an internal moving mass, a momentum wheel, and external thrust. This configuration eliminates traditional control surfaces, which in turn reduces the combinatorial set of computational fluid dynamics (CFD) studies necessary for high-fidelity modeling of flight through unsteady flow fields. Using Lie bracket computations and the strong Lie Algebra Rank Condition, it is shown that the full nonlinear dynamics are generically small-time locally accessible, with singular configurations explicitly characterized and shown to be immediately escapable by direct mass actuation. The stronger property of small-time local controllability is shown to hold for the attitude subsystem at equilibrium points, using Sussmann's condition. A key contribution is the explicit treatment of the time-varying inertia tensor via the Huygens-Steiner Theorem, which introduces nontrivial coupling between the control vector fields. The momentum wheel provides direct yaw authority that, combined with inertia variation from the moving mass, generates a full-rank accessibility Lie algebra. A nonlinear Model Predictive Controller (MPC) is derived with consideration of flight envelope constraints and is applied to track different trajectories under unperturbed and perturbed flow field conditions.
